How to use the result

The result estimates cash outlay over the period you choose. It excludes resale value, financing interest, usage-based charges, and possible returns. Enter any known mandatory one-off charges in the tax and fees field. If an offer requires an annual upfront payment, include it in the item price rather than as a monthly fee.

For example, a $100 item with a 10% discount and $8 delivery costs $98 before tax. Add a $5 monthly service for 12 months and the total becomes $158. If that service is optional, run the comparison both with and without it.

Do not count cashback until its conditions are satisfied. Confirm renewal rates and cancellation deadlines separately. A lower calculated total does not account for differences in quality, compatibility, return rights, or warranty coverage.
