Buy with a plan, not a countdown

A lower sticker price is only useful if the item fits your needs and the final bill stays lower. These guides explain the checks you can make yourself, with worked examples rather than product rankings or unverified savings claims.

Start with the decision

Write down your maximum delivered budget, the features you must have, and when you need the item. This makes it easier to reject a heavily discounted product that solves the wrong problem. For furniture, measure the doorway as well as the room. For electronics, check the exact model suffix and regional warranty. For subscriptions, check the renewal price before comparing the introductory offer.
