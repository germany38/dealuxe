Flavors without Diacetyl

Diacetyl-free flavors refer to extracts, emulsions, oils, and other flavorings that do not use a widely speculated health-adverse chemical called Diacetyl (more commonly found in pop corn) during its formulation. Flavorings that are non-diacetyl instead rely on natural and USDA-certified organic ingredients to create the irresistible flavor notes that people love. Our diacetyl-free flavors are crafted to give you peace of mind and great tasting products, no matter if you’re developing a popcorn seasoning, a nutty cream sauce, or a clean-label creamy dessert.
