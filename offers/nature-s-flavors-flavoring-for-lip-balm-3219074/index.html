Flavoring for Lip Balm

At Nature's Flavors, our flavor experts have created lip balm flavor oils with natural or organic flavor oils that are safe for use on delicate lips. These flavoring oils for lip gloss are great for adding scents to lip balms and more lip products. Feel free to experiment and customize them with your favorite scent. You can create your own natural lip gloss with our flavor oils for lip balms with a few ingredients: coconut oil, beeswax, and your favorite lip gloss flavor oil. Melt the beeswax, add the coconut oil, and mix them together. Then, add your favorite lip flavor oil. Simply pour the mixture into a container, and allow it to cool for at least ten minutes.
