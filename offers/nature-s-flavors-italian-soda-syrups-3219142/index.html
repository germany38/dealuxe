Italian Soda Syrups

Our flavor experts have developed a line of organic and natural flavored Italian soda syrups used to make refreshing Italian sodas - the perfect summer beverage. The Italian soda syrup flavors are mixed with sparkling water or carbonated soda to create a tasty drink. At Nature’s Flavors, we offer some of the best syrups for Italian sodas. All of our Italian soda flavor syrups are created with organic and natural plant-derived ingredients. The Italian soda syrups are kosher, gluten-free, and vegan. The syrups for Italian sodas do not contain any alcohol, but they can be added to cocktail mixes to flavor alcoholic beverages.
