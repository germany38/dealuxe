Deep Dive: Inside the Customer Success Engineer Role at Mercor

Thousands of users and top-tier professionals join the Mercor platform every week to interview for cutting-edge roles. As a Customer Success Engineer focused on Latin America, you are the frontline champion and technical problem-solver ensuring that every interaction runs smoothly, professionally, and efficiently.

Unlike traditional customer support roles that rely strictly on pre-written macros, this position requires technical fluency. You will work directly with Mercor's core engineering team, investigating bug reports, analyzing logs, inspecting networks, and running database queries to diagnose complex system behavior.

Key Responsibilities:

  • End-to-End Investigation: Reproduce user-reported bugs, isolate root causes, and clearly differentiate between user interface friction, AI model edge cases, and genuine system defects.
  • Full-Stack Debugging: Investigate issues across an advanced AI + SaaS tech stack using telemetry, logs, and network inspection tools.
  • Smart Triage & Escalation: Apply sound judgment to escalate true engineering issues while resolving user friction through platform configuration, prompt refinement, or clear instructions.
  • Product Feedback Loop: Surface systemic patterns, failure modes, and product risks directly to engineering and product leadership.
  • Technical Documentation: Create concise runbooks and help documentation to prevent repeat issues and accelerate resolution times.

💡 Tone and Communication: Because your conversations are often the first introduction users have to Mercor, maintaining an enthusiastic, upbeat tone while writing at a native English proficiency level is essential. The communication style balances casual warmth with absolute professional precision.

Candidate Qualifications: What Does Mercor Look For?

To thrive in this high-growth environment, candidates must demonstrate a solid mix of technical debugging skills, AI familiarity, and pristine English communication:

  • Technical Background: A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field from a top-tier institution, or proven experience at a fast-growing tech startup. Alternatively, 2-5 years of hands-on experience supporting customers on modern web applications (React, Node, Flask, Next.js).
  • AI Systems Familiarity: Comfort with large language models (LLMs), AI agents, generative models, fine-tuning, prompt chains, or API integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic, LangChain).
  • Geographic Eligibility: Must reside within Latin America (countries including Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, Chile, Peru, Uruguay, Costa Rica, etc.).
  • Schedule Flexibility: Ability to work a 6-day per week schedule with substantial schedule overlap with Pacific Time (PT/PST) working hours.
