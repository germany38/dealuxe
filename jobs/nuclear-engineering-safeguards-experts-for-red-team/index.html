The Critical Need for Nuclear Experts in AI Safety

As large language models and frontier AI architectures become more advanced, they increasingly process complex technical jargon across scientific domains. However, increased capability brings significant dual-use risks. Ensuring these models can accurately differentiate between benign academic research, safe educational queries, and dangerous information requests requires domain-specific human oversight.

Mercor connects world-class specialists with top-tier artificial intelligence labs to bulletproof model alignment. In this role, your core responsibility is to simulate real-world threat vectors—a process known as red-teaming—to see where boundaries hold and where models might inadvertently reveal sensitive pathways.

Core Responsibilities of the Panel:

  • Prompt Generation: Write challenging, single-turn prompts in your technical domain, categorized across three strict tiers: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation: Review model responses against precise, predefined policy standards to judge whether the model handled the query safely and correctly.
  • Reference Answer Composition: Write detailed reference solutions outlining what a correct model response looks like, backed by thorough technical reasoning.

💡 Confidentiality Assurance: Your work with Mercor will not involve, and must not draw upon, classified or export-controlled information, or anything bound by active NDAs or prepublication review obligations. Candidates with past government or national lab experience are welcome, and workflows are scoped cleanly to remain entirely unclassified.

Ideal Candidate Backgrounds and Professional Profiles

Mercor is seeking deep subject-matter expertise paired with strong written communication skills. The strongest applicants come from specialized backgrounds where material management, regulatory oversight, and technical analysis intersect. Relevant professional signals include:

  • Safeguards Analysts & Inspectors: Former IAEA or Euratom inspectors with deep familiarity with material accountancy, inspection regimes, and Additional Protocol protocols.
  • Nuclear Engineers: Specialists focused on the fuel cycle, enrichment processes, reprocessing, criticality safety, and end-to-end nuclear engineering applications.
  • Nuclear Security Engineers: Professionals from national laboratories specializing in MPC&A (Materials Protection, Control, and Accounting), insider threat analysis, and physical protection design.
  • Nuclear Forensics Scientists: Experts skilled in radioxenon monitoring, radioactive attribution, detection, and material characterization.
  • Nonproliferation Analysts: Authorities on export controls, Nuclear Suppliers Group (NSG) trigger lists, and illicit acquisition or smuggling pathways.

Why Technical Writing Matters Here

This is intensive, writing-driven work. Every evaluation, prompt classification, and compliance judgment requires a clear, written rationale that non-specialists and developers can follow. Demonstrating prior technical writing experience, published research, or expert witness background serves as a major advantage during selection.

Compensation Structure and Contractor Benefits

Mercor’s payment ecosystem is designed to reward high-caliber technical expertise fairly and efficiently:

  • Task-Based Earnings: Earn between $65 and $75 per completed task, allowing high efficiency to scale your earnings.
  • Flexible Pacing: Work on your own schedule with complete location independence. You can pause or step away from sensitive topics at any time without penalty.
  • Referral Bonus Opportunities: Expand your income by sharing unique referral links, earning bonuses for every successful peer or colleague hired into the network.

How to Navigate the Application Process Successfully

Because positions on specialized red-team panels require elite credentials, presenting your professional history clearly is vital to passing initial screenings:

  1. Highlight Specialized Domain Experience: Clearly detail your background in nuclear safeguards, nonproliferation, or fuel-cycle engineering on your CV or profile.
  2. Provide Writing Samples: If available, include links to published research, technical articles, reports, or whitepapers that demonstrate your capacity for precise technical writing.
  3. Commit to the Screening Process: Complete Mercor’s streamlined interview and assessment steps promptly to secure your placement on the active panel.
