About Turing and the Mission

Turing supports the global AI ecosystem through two primary pillars: first, by accelerating frontier research with high-quality data, advanced training pipelines, and elite researchers specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and multimodality; and second, by helping enterprises translate AI proofs of concept into proprietary, production-ready intelligence.

For engineering professionals, this creates a unique avenue to monetize decades of specialized technical experience without leaving the drawing board or abandoning rigorous analytical work. Through structured projects like the PAA-based engineering benchmark initiative, consultants directly dictate how accurately future AI models simulate and validate physical systems.

The Role: CAD & Simulation Consultant

Turing is actively engaging experienced Aerospace, Mechanical, and Electrical Engineers to design, solve, and review simulation-led engineering tasks. This role is ideal for senior professionals who want to apply their deep design acumen to artificial intelligence training datasets. You will be responsible for translating real-world engineering constraints into structured problem statements that test an AI agent's deductive and computational limits.

Key Responsibilities & Deliverables:

  • Model Development: Develop precision CAD models and perform comprehensive simulation analysis for aerospace, mechanical, and electrical components.
  • Design Validation: Validate architectural designs using industry-standard tools and analytical sanity checks.
  • Problem Statement Authoring: Develop realistic engineering scenarios, complete with boundary conditions, operating constraints, variables, and objective acceptance criteria.
  • Peer Review & Quality Assurance: Review submissions from other engineers for technical correctness, completeness, and adherence to rigorous standards.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with research leads to optimize design trade-offs and document complex engineering methodologies.

You Are a Fit If You Possess:

This is an elite engagement tailored for seasoned technical practitioners. To thrive in this role, candidates must demonstrate:

  • Extensive Tenure: A minimum of 10+ years of professional experience working in CAD engineering and simulation-focused organizations.
  • Strong Fundamentals: Deep mastery of aerospace, mechanical, or electrical engineering principles (circuit analysis, power systems, control systems, thermodynamics, or structural dynamics).
  • Advanced Education: PhD or master's degrees in Mechanical, Electrical, or Aerospace Engineering are highly preferred, alongside prior tenure with leading aerospace or heavy engineering firms.
  • Tool Proficiency: Professional mastery in domain-specific suites including FreeCAD, ANSYS, MATLAB/Simulink, KiCad, OpenFOAM, COMSOL, LTspice, or Altium Designer, complemented by Python or MATLAB scripting capabilities for automation and data processing.

A Day in the Life

A typical workday combines rigorous analytical design with creative problem formulation. You might start your morning scripting an automated validation script in Python or MATLAB, transition into building complex multi-layer PCB schematics in KiCad or finite element models in ANSYS, and spend your afternoon reviewing peer-submitted simulation runs to ensure they meet strict industrial tolerances.

  • Competitive Contracting: Structured 8-week engagements with flexible weekly hour commitments (20 to 40 hours per week).
