The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Radiological Safety Practitioners

As modern large language models ingest vast libraries of scientific literature, nuclear engineering texts, and emergency response manuals, they sit on a delicate razor's edge known as the dual-use dilemma. The exact same isotope underpins vital cancer therapies in modern medicine and industrial radiography in manufacturing; the identical shielding calculation that protects a field worker from exposure can theoretically be misapplied if security boundaries fail.

Generalist evaluators or standard software developers cannot reliably distinguish between a benign technical inquiry made by a licensed technician and a probe attempting to extract dangerous methodologies. An AI model that unnecessarily refuses a standard, routine dose calculation or waste storage query fails in its core utility. Conversely, a model that over-shares sensitive deposition data fails in its safety mandate. Finding, defending, and encoding that exact operational line is why Mercor is turning directly to seasoned field professionals rather than generalists.

Your Key Responsibilities on the Panel:

  • Adversarial Prompt Engineering: Write challenging, single-turn prompts rooted firmly in your professional domain, systematically categorized across three distinct tiers: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy-Driven Evaluation: Review model-generated outputs against rigorous safety standards, rendering definitive judgments on whether the system correctly evaluated the request.
  • Authoring Reference Solutions: Formulate authoritative reference answers outlining what a correct, secure response looks like, alongside the underlying technical reasoning.

💡 Compliance Note: All work performed through Mercor must strictly avoid drawing on classified data, export-controlled information, or details bound by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication reviews. If you maintain such obligations, disclose them during onboarding so the scope can be appropriately tailored.

Ideal Backgrounds and Qualifications

To maintain absolute data integrity and high-fidelity evaluation standards, Mercor looks for candidates who possess rigorous professional credentials, command experience, and deep institutional knowledge. Ideal applicants typically come from backgrounds such as:

  • Radiological Emergency Planning: Professionals who have designed, tested, or executed emergency plans at licensed nuclear facilities, or at state, regional, or national government levels.
  • Field Response and Monitoring Teams: Field veterans from organizations such as the Federal Radiological Monitoring and Assessment Center (FRMAC), Radiological Assistance Program (RAP), CBRN units, or national emergency response teams.
  • Consequence & Dispersal Modeling: Specialists experienced in atmospheric plume and deposition estimation, dose projection modeling, and protective action decision-making.
  • Incident Command & Coordination: Leaders who have managed incident command structures or directed full-scale emergency exercises for radiological incidents.
  • Medical & Public Health Response: Public health officials and medical professionals specializing in radiological triage, medical countermeasures, and population monitoring.

Beyond Technical Depth: The Power of Written Communication

While deep technical expertise is the baseline requirement, this role is intensely writing-centric. Candidates with prior technical writing portfolios, published research papers, training manuals, or expert witness backgrounds will find their expertise translates exceptionally well to this work.
