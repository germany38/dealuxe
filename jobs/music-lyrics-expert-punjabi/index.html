Detailed Breakdown of Responsibilities and Expectations

Artificial intelligence models are learning to compose melodies, harmonies, and lyrics. However, machines lack cultural context and emotional depth. Your expertise will ensure that AI-generated Punjabi tracks capture the true essence of folk, pop, bhangra, and contemporary sub-genres.

Core Tasks:

  • Compare AI-generated lyrics against published commercial songs to detect copyright similarities and evaluate originality.
  • Rate lyrics based on artistic quality, creative depth, prompt adherence, and linguistic nuance.
  • Assess natural phrasing, vocabulary choice, regional idioms, and modern street or traditional slang.
