🇬🇧 English Overview

We're looking for audio engineers with strong experience editing and quality-checking voice recordings for speech and text-to-speech (TTS) applications, specifically for French-language audio. This role focuses on preparing high-quality audio datasets for machine learning systems, ensuring clarity, consistency, and technical compliance across large volumes of French speech data.

Key Tasks:

  • Edit and process French voice recordings for TTS pipelines.
  • Perform audio cleanup (silence trimming, de-clicking, noise reduction).
  • Evaluate pronunciation accuracy, naturalness, and fluency.

🇫🇷 Aperçu en Français

Nous recherchons des ingénieurs du son possédant une solide expérience dans l'édition et le contrôle qualité d'enregistrements vocaux pour des applications de synthèse vocale (TTS), spécifiquement pour l'audio en langue française. Ce rôle se concentre sur la préparation de jeux de données audio de haute qualité pour les systèmes d'apprentissage automatique.

Tâches Clés :

  • Éditer et traiter des enregistrements vocaux français pour les pipelines TTS.
  • Effectuer le nettoyage audio (recadrage du silence, dé-clic, réduction du bruit).
  • Évaluer la précision de la prononciation, le naturel et la fluidité.

Technical Responsibilities & Daily Workflow

Unlike traditional music production or sound design for film, audio engineering for artificial intelligence text-to-speech (TTS) requires extreme precision, consistency, and adherence to strict machine learning data standards. Your daily work ensures that AI models learn from natural, crystal-clear human speech patterns.

What You Will Be Doing Day-to-Day:

  • Dialogue Restoration & Cleanup: Execute meticulous audio cleanup tasks including silence trimming, breath reduction or removal, de-clicking, de-essing, and background noise reduction.
  • Quality Assurance Audits: Conduct rigorous technical inspections to detect clipping, distortion, phase issues, and inconsistent loudness levels.
  • Technical Compliance: Format and balance recordings to meet rigorous machine learning training pipeline specifications.
  • Linguistic Quality Control: Assess voice recordings for authentic French pronunciation, natural cadence, emotional neutrality (or appropriate affect), and native fluency.
  • High-Throughput Processing: Manage large batches of voice data while maintaining flawless quality and consistency.

💡 Pro Tip for Applicants: Highlight your hands-on proficiency with specialized audio restoration software like iZotope RX and standard digital audio workstations (DAWs) like Pro Tools in your application profile.
