Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

Stepping into the Project Coordinator role means anchoring yourself right at the core of the AI revolution. You will serve as the crucial operational bridge between visionary program leadership and a rapidly expanding workforce of elite domain experts, researchers, and technical specialists.

Your day-to-day operations will span multiple dynamic domains:

  • Operational Workflow Management: Act as the primary, day-to-day point of contact for complex AI data projects, ensuring that operational pipelines, annotation milestones, and evaluation cycles run with maximum efficiency.
  • Translational Leadership: Take broad, high-level inputs from executive program leads, distill them into precise, actionable operational guidelines, and distribute them cleanly to expert contributor teams.
  • Talent Onboarding & Support: Facilitate the seamless onboarding and ongoing enablement of new domain experts as project capacities scale to meet aggressive engineering timelines.
  • Business Review Execution: Participate actively in weekly business reviews, document vital notes and strategic action items, and occasionally lead operational calls when required.
  • Quality Assurance & Root Cause Analysis: Systematically spot quality bottlenecks or annotation anomalies, dive deep into root causes, and share diagnostic findings transparently across cross-functional teams.
  • Deliverable Tracking: Maintain absolute clarity over project milestones and deliverables utilizing advanced data tracking tools including Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel.

Qualifications & Ideal Candidate Profile

Because this role sits at the intersection of operational project management and cutting-edge machine learning research, the qualifications demand a unique blend of structural organization, technical fluency, and leadership capacity.

Essential Requirements:

  • Geographic Location: Candidates must be legally based within the United States (USA).
  • Educational Background: A strong academic or practical background in STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) fields is heavily preferred.
  • Professional Experience: A minimum of 3+ years of hands-on experience in project coordination, technical program management (TPM), or executive project management (EPM), showcasing the proven ability to coordinate large, cross-functional teams.
  • AI Ecosystem Fluency: Prior practical exposure to AI training-data pipelines or human-in-the-loop data projects—whether as an EPM, project lead, team coordinator, or expert contributor.
  • Technical Stack Proficiency: Strong data management capabilities (Excel, SQL) alongside working foundational knowledge of coding concepts.
  • Leadership Track Record: Demonstrated ability to lead groups effectively, manage stakeholder expectations, and steer large teams toward unified milestones.

🌟 The Competitive Edge: Preferred Backgrounds

Candidates who possess prior project coordination experience on AI/data programs at major tech giants (such as Meta, Amazon, or TikTok), or those who have successfully led high-visibility data and training projects through platforms like Mercor, will find themselves uniquely positioned for rapid advancement within this ecosystem.
