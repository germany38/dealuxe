Core Qualifications & Technical Requirements

Because these roles contribute directly to training the next generation of enterprise AI infrastructure, the evaluation standards require genuine production-grade expertise rather than theoretical knowledge.

Must-Have Technical Qualifications:
  • 4+ Years of Professional Experience: Dedicated background in cloud infrastructure, DevOps, site reliability engineering (SRE), or platform engineering at a recognized organization.
  • Deep Kubernetes Mastery: Hands-on production experience operating Kubernetes, including actively diagnosing and repairing cluster failures—not just managing static control planes or manifests.

  • AWS Integration Expertise: Direct, hands-on experience integrating essential AWS services in production environments, including Lambda, API Gateway, and DynamoDB.
  • CI/CD Ownership: Proven track record of building, maintaining, and scaling modern continuous integration and continuous deployment pipelines.

Additional Expectations: Demonstrable career progression, strong written communication skills, and the availability to commit 40 hours per week during standard US weekdays.
