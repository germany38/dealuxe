Comprehensive Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For management consultants, business analysts, corporate strategists, and executive leaders seeking high-yield, remote contract work, understanding the expectations of this role is crucial. The position demands senior-level professional judgment to review, audit, and refine complex AI-generated work products, ensuring that automated business outputs meet the highest standards of corporate rigor.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and audit AI-generated spreadsheets, business strategy documents, financial models, and executive slide decks focused on market expansion, competitive positioning, and operational efficiency.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify factual errors, strategic omissions, flawed business logic, and presentation mistakes in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written feedback to help train and align frontier AI models with elite enterprise-grade business standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex management frameworks, swot analyses, and strategic roadmaps generated by artificial intelligence align with proven industry best practices.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these positions directly train state-of-the-art AI systems relied upon by major global enterprises, the qualification standard is intentionally calibrated to attract seasoned specialists rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Demonstrated professional background in general business strategy, management consulting, corporate development, or executive operations.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication and analytical skills.
  • Software Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific emphasis on advanced slide presentation tools (Google Slides and PowerPoint) and spreadsheet modeling.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher, such as an MBA) from a reputable academic institution.
