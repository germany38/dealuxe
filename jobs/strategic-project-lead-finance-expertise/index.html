The Mission: Bridging Advanced Finance and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

Traditional software engineering talent alone cannot teach an AI model how to evaluate complex financial derivatives, parse nuanced SEC filings, or execute corporate valuation models. Artificial intelligence labs require specialized domain intelligence delivered at scale. This is where the Strategic Project Lead (SPL) comes in. You will act as the vital bridge between top AI lab customers and elite global networks of finance experts.

In this high-visibility core team role, you own projects from initial pipeline framing and scoping through rapid execution and final delivery. You will turn ambiguous customer requirements from major AI labs into structured, high-accuracy data pipelines that train next-generation AI reasoning engines.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • End-to-End Project Ownership: Take full ownership of projects with leading AI lab customers, driving a tight execution cadence from scoping to delivery.
  • Pipeline Design & Optimization: Design, launch, and continuously refine finance-focused data pipelines optimized for usability, accuracy, data quality, and scalability.
  • Expert Network Alignment: Align operations teams and senior Finance domain experts to ensure rapid deployment and high-quality contributions.
  • Client Communication: Maintain clear, direct, and consistent communication with premier AI lab stakeholders, providing daily internal updates covering progress, blockers, and explicit next steps.
  • Quality Management: Oversee finance domain experts and project leads, ensuring deliverables adhere to stringent accuracy and compliance standards.

💡 The Startup Advantage: micro1 operates with an intense bias toward execution and startup agility. Successful Strategic Project Leads thrive in high-velocity environments where autonomy, structured problem-solving, and rapid scaling are paramount.

Preferred Qualifications and Background

micro1 maintains rigorous evaluation standards to curate exceptional core teams. Candidates applying for the Strategic Project Lead position typically demonstrate:

  • Educational Foundation: A robust background in Finance, Financial Engineering, Economics, Accounting, or a closely related quantitative/analytical field.
  • Project Leadership Experience: 2+ years of demonstrated experience leading complex projects, operational workflows, data-intensive initiatives, or finance-related programs.
  • Executive Communication: Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills, particularly when interfacing with senior finance practitioners and AI researchers.
  • Technical Familiarity: Working knowledge of financial modeling, investment analysis, financial markets, accounting workflows, and data pipelines or human-in-the-loop AI training operations.
  • Bias for Execution: An entrepreneurial, startup-oriented mindset capable of turning ambiguous technical requirements into crystal-clear operational plans.
