The Role Overview: Python & Full-Stack (JS) Developer

Turing is actively engaging talented developers proficient in Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript to join an ambitious engineering team dedicated to pushing the boundaries of AI technology. This engagement is tailored for professionals who thrive on developing innovative solutions, writing modular code, and auditing complex software architectures.

As a developer on this project, you will collaborate with premier US companies and research labs, helping them evaluate, refine, and optimize commercial and research AI systems.

Core Technical Stack & Tools Required:

  • Languages: Python, JavaScript, TypeScript.
  • Backend Frameworks: Node.js, Nest.js.
  • Frontend Frameworks: React.js, Vue.js, Angular.
  • Infrastructure & DevOps: Docker (Mandatory).
  • Methodologies: Modular software architecture, test-driven development (TDD), code reviewing, and automated testing.

What Does Your Day-to-Day Look Like?

The daily routine of a Python & Full-Stack Developer at Turing is intellectually stimulating, combining rigorous software engineering with advanced AI benchmarking. Your key responsibilities include:

  • Code Creation & Maintenance: Designing, developing, and maintaining efficient, high-quality code to train and optimize AI models.
  • Model Evaluations (Evals): Conducting comprehensive benchmarks to measure model performance and analyzing results for continuous architectural improvement.
  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): Leading efforts in SFT, including creating and maintaining high-quality, task-specific datasets.
  • RLHF Collaboration: Working alongside researchers and annotators to execute Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback and refine reward models.
  • Peer Code Review: Conducting thorough peer reviews of code and documentation, providing constructive feedback, and enforcing pristine coding standards.
  • Tool Integration: Continuously exploring and integrating new tools, techniques, and methodologies to enhance AI training and evaluation pipelines.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Experience

To succeed in this rigorous environment, applicants must demonstrate a strong foundation in modern software development and engineering best practices:

  • Educational Background: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical industry experience.
  • Core Proficiency: Demonstrable mastery of Python, JavaScript, and TypeScript syntax, conventions, and asynchronous patterns.
  • Full-Stack Fluency: Solid understanding of JavaScript ES6+, Node.js or Nest.js, and modern frontend frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue.js).
  • Containerization: Mandatory knowledge and practical experience working with **Docker**.
  • Code Quality: Proven ability to write readable, reusable, maintainable, and secure code with robust testing protocols.
  • Communication: Excellent spoken and written English communication skills for effective cross-functional collaboration.

The Interview Process & Onboarding Strategy

Turing maintains a streamlined yet rigorous evaluation process designed to identify top-tier engineering talent efficiently. The entire evaluation takes approximately **75 minutes** and consists of two focused rounds:

  1. Technical Round (60 minutes): A deep dive into your Python and JavaScript/TypeScript proficiency, architectural knowledge, and problem-solving skills.
  2. Cultural & Offer Round (15 minutes): Discussion regarding team alignment, availability, and contract terms.

Crucial Onboarding Tip for Long-Term Success: Once accepted, ensure you complete your onboarding promptly and commit to completing your first **10 hours of work** rapidly.
