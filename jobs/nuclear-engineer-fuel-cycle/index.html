The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Nuclear Fuel Cycle Specialists

Large language models ingest millions of pages of scientific documentation, research papers, and technical specifications. However, raw ingestion does not equal safe contextual understanding. In the nuclear fuel cycle, the line between standard engineering utility and proliferation pathways is razor-thin. For instance, the exact material-balance calculations required to close an inspector's account can theoretically be manipulated to obscure inventory discrepancies; the exact processing steps utilized to fuel a commercial power reactor form the baseline of proliferation considerations.

An artificial intelligence model that unnecessarily refuses a routine, professional criticality-safety query is failing as severely as one that inappropriately answers a restricted prompt. Getting this boundary right requires specialists who have worked inside the fuel cycle—engineers who instinctively know which parameters represent routine industrial operations and which require rigorous scrutiny. This is the exact problem space you will address.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Drafting Targeted Prompts: Create intricate, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific nuclear engineering expertise, systematically categorized across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Evaluating Model Responses: Audit responses generated by AI models against defined policy standards, rendering precise, justifiable decisions on whether the system navigated the prompt correctly.
  • Writing Reference Answers: Author the authoritative reference answer detailing what an accurate, safe response looks like, accompanied by the underlying technical reasoning.

💡 Important Compliance Note: Your work with Mercor must strictly avoid drawing on classified, export-controlled data, or information restricted by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. If you maintain such clearances, specify them in your application so project scopes can be tailored compliantly.
