Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

As artificial intelligence models grow exponentially in scale and capability, technology labs and enterprise developers require sophisticated human oversight. Raw computing power cannot replace human critical thinking, cultural nuance, and safety guardrails. This is where your bilingual linguistic expertise becomes invaluable.

As a Bilingual Portuguese Generalist Expert at Mercor, your primary mission is to evaluate, audit, and strengthen how frontier AI models handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use topics in the Portuguese language. You will bridge the gap between machine outputs and human common sense.

Key Daily Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Engineering & Creation: Draft expert-level, intricate prompts in Portuguese across a diverse array of specialized and sensitive subject areas.
  • Structural Classification: Apply structured, rigorous guidelines to classify model-generated responses, conversations, and reasoning pathways.
  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: Identify and flag adversarial phrasings, edge cases, and escalation patterns, meticulously documenting the analytical reasoning behind your judgments.
  • Cultural Context Tuning: Ensure that idiom usage, regional slang, syntax, and cultural sensibilities align perfectly with standard Portuguese language standards.

💡 Note on Experience: You do not need a background in software engineering or machine learning. Mercor provides complete workflow training. What matters most is your critical thinking, high attention to detail, and command of both Portuguese and English.

Preferred "Nice-to-Have" Experience:
