Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For creative leads, art directors, brand managers, and senior copywriters looking to diversify their revenue streams or pivot toward flexible high-yielding remote work, understanding the day-to-day expectations of this role is crucial. Evaluators serve as the quality benchmark for AI-generated artifacts, ensuring that automated visual and text assets meet the rigorous standards expected by top-tier global brands.

Key Responsibilities in Detail:

  • Artifact and Deliverable Grading: Systematically review and assess complex AI-generated work products, including strategic documents, executive slide decks, marketing plans, and brand architecture layouts.
  • Aesthetic and Rhetorical Quality Audits: Identify subtle presentation errors, brand voice inconsistencies, tone-deaf messaging, visual misalignment, and stylistic discrepancies in machine outputs.
  • Constructive Evaluation and Feedback: Provide clear, highly structured, and actionable written commentary to help training pipelines fine-tune generative models for enterprise deployment.
  • Cross-Functional Domain Application: Apply rigorous professional judgment across various facets of brand storytelling, campaign collateral development, and creative direction rubrics.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because artificial intelligence models require nuanced, expert-level feedback to master complex human aesthetic and commercial standards, this role is explicitly curated for industry veterans rather than novices.

Must-Have Professional Qualifications:
  • 5+ Years of Industry Experience: A proven, demonstrable career background in brand strategy, creative direction, art direction, or marketing collateral creation.
  • Language and Communication Mastery: Native or professional fluency in English, with exceptional written communication skills required for detailed critique generation.
  • Technical Platform Proficiency: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific advanced expertise in slide presentation platforms like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) in marketing, fine arts, communications, or business administration from a reputable academic institution.
