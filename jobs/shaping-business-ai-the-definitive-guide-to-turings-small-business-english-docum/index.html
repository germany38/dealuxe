Artificial Intelligence & Business Operations

Discover how small business owners and operations experts are partnering with Turing to train next-generation AI models for enterprise problem-solving.

Artificial intelligence has moved past theoretical models and experimental chatbots. Today, enterprises rely on advanced AI systems to solve mission-critical priorities, from financial forecasting and market analysis to day-to-day operational logistics. However, ensuring these systems accurately understand complex business realities requires human intelligence, domain expertise, and rigorous real-world evaluation.

Turing stands at the forefront of this technological wave as one of the world's fastest-growing AI companies. By partnering with leading AI labs and global enterprises, Turing builds foundational intelligence layers that power reasoning, multi-modality, and agentic behavior. For entrepreneurs, small business owners, and operations specialists, Turing offers an exceptional remote engagement: the Small Business English Document project.

About Turing and the Mission

Turing accelerates the advancement and deployment of powerful AI systems through a dual approach: working with world-class AI labs to advance frontier model capabilities, and deploying those models to solve complex corporate challenges. The Small Business project bridges practical entrepreneurial experience with cutting-edge machine learning research.

Role Overview & Core Responsibilities

As a participant in this project, your primary mandate is to evaluate and compare the quality of responses generated by multiple AI chatbots across real-world small business use cases. Rather than performing routine administrative tasks, you act as a critical evaluator of artificial intelligence reasoning.

Your Daily Responsibilities Include:

  • Prompt Engineering: Creating realistic business-related prompts based on defined user goals and operational scenarios.
  • Multi-Chatbot Interaction: Interacting with multiple AI chatbots (up to 5 turns per conversation) to test contextual adaptability.
  • Quality Assessment: Assessing response quality across clarity, usefulness, and accuracy.
  • Structured Evaluation: Providing comparative feedback and submitting detailed conversation transcripts.
  • Data Analysis: Reviewing files such as spreadsheets, PDFs, and images as part of financial and operational workflows.

You Are a Ideal Fit If:

This project is specifically tailored for individuals who understand the nuances of running and scaling a business. You are a strong candidate if you possess:

  • Direct experience as a small business owner or a profound operational understanding of small business environments.
  • Business-related documents available in English to ground prompts in authentic scenarios.
  • Strong analytical and critical thinking skills to evaluate multi-layered business dilemmas.
  • Comfort navigating AI tools and adhering to structured evaluation guidelines.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

Working on this project offers dynamic variety. Your typical workday involves analyzing specific business problems—such as cash flow optimization, marketing campaign strategies, or customer retention challenges—and testing how well AI models interpret these scenarios. You will review AI-generated responses, cross-reference them with data tables or documents, and submit structured feedback.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Engaging with Turing provides unique advantages for independent professionals:

  • Work at the forefront of AI applications in accounting, finance, and business management.
  • Enjoy a fully remote and flexible work environment that adapts to your schedule.
  • Collaborate on high-impact global projects with industry-leading scope.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Best Practices

The project is structured around project-based evaluation tasks spanning a duration of 16 weeks. Each task involves multi-chatbot comparisons and final assessments.

To ensure long-term stability within the platform and unlock access to additional tasks, complete your onboarding thoroughly and commit to completing your first 10 hours of work successfully. Establishing early momentum guarantees a smooth workflow and positions you for sustained success.
