About Turing: Powering the Frontier of AI Intelligence

Based in San Francisco, Turing operates at the vanguard of artificial intelligence development. The organization supports global enterprises and frontier AI labs in two vital ways: first, by accelerating research through top-tier human experts specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and complex domains; and second, by helping enterprises transform AI proofs of concept into robust, reliable proprietary intelligence.

By engaging specialized domain experts, Turing builds the rigorous human feedback loops and evaluation benchmarks necessary to eliminate hallucinations and secure high-stakes AI deployment.

Role Overview: Politics Domain Expert

As a Politics Domain Expert at Turing, your primary mission is to evaluate and refine Large Language Models on political science, governance, elections, public policy, and international relations. You will act as an intellectual architect for AI training datasets, ensuring that models reason accurately across complex geopolitical landscapes.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Design: Create advanced, nuanced prompts covering political systems, governance structures, elections, and public policy challenges.
  • Response Evaluation: Assess AI-generated outputs for factual accuracy, logical consistency, reasoning depth, and neutral balance.
  • Error Detection: Identify hallucinations, logical flaws, outdated information, and edge-case vulnerabilities in model outputs.
  • Benchmark Creation: Develop robust benchmark datasets and adversarial test cases to push AI reasoning capabilities further.
  • Evidence-Based Feedback: Provide clear, referenced justifications and authoritative corrections to improve model performance over time.

You Are a Perfect Fit If You Possess...

Turing maintains exceptionally high standards for its domain expert network. Ideal candidates bring a potent mix of academic rigor and professional analytical experience:

  • Educational Background: A Master’s degree or higher in any field, with preferred degrees in Political Science, Public Policy, International Relations, Government, Public Administration, Law, Economics, Journalism, or related disciplines.
  • Subject Matter Mastery: Deep knowledge of political institutions, comparative politics, electoral systems, policy analysis, and global political developments.
  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of relevant experience in government, public policy, political research, consulting, think tanks, academia, or international affairs.
  • Analytical Rigor: Exceptional written English, research skills, and meticulous attention to detail when evaluating complex political data.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

A typical workday as a Politics Domain Expert at Turing is intellectually stimulating and dynamic. Rather than performing monotonous administrative tasks, you engage directly in high-level analytical evaluation:

  • Drafting complex prompt scenarios that test an AI's comprehension of constitutional law, legislative processes, or diplomatic negotiations.
  • Comparing multiple AI-generated policy briefs and ranking them based on factual precision and neutrality.
  • Consulting authoritative academic and governmental sources to justify grading decisions.
  • Collaborating asynchronously with AI research leads to calibrate task complexity and refine evaluation rubrics.

Perks and Engagement Details

Contracting with Turing provides unique professional advantages for elite researchers and subject matter experts:

  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Work at the absolute forefront of generative AI and large language model development.
  • Complete Remote Flexibility: Enjoy location independence while collaborating with world-class engineering teams.
  • Intellectual Engagement: Apply your hard-earned academic and professional expertise to solve futuristic technical challenges.

Engagement Terms: This is an 8-week remote contractor assignment. It requires a 40-hour weekly time commitment with at least 4 hours of overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST). Note that as a contractor position, medical and paid leave benefits are not included.

The Interview Process & Onboarding Strategy

Turing’s selection process is designed to identify top-tier analytical talent efficiently:

  1. Assessment: Complete the specialized domain assessment shared by the evaluation team.
  2. Review: Turing’s expert team rigorously evaluates your submission for analytical accuracy and depth.
  3. Onboarding & Stability: Selected candidates are onboarded directly into active projects. To ensure long-term stability, steady task flow, and access to advanced projects, experts should complete their onboarding and successfully log their first 10 hours of project work promptly.
