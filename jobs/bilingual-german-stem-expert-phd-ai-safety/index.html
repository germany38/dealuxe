Introduction: Redefining AI Safety Through Advanced Scientific Expertise

Artificial intelligence is evolving at an unprecedented pace, transforming industries from healthcare and pharmaceuticals to quantum computing and green energy. However, as frontier AI models become more autonomous and complex, ensuring their safety, accuracy, and ethical alignment has never been more critical. Leading AI laboratories are facing complex challenges when models encounter highly technical, specialized subjects. To bridge this gap, high-level human expertise is urgently required to test, evaluate, and train these systems.

Mercor, a pioneering platform partnering with top-tier AI labs and global enterprises, is meeting this challenge head-on. By connecting elite professionals with cutting-edge training workflows, Mercor is transforming how frontier models acquire reasoning and safety boundaries. One of their most sought-after opportunities right now is the Bilingual German STEM Expert (PhD) — AI Safety role. Designed for top-tier scientific minds who possess native or near-native fluency in German and deep academic research backgrounds, this remote position offers exceptional compensation, flexible hours, and the unique chance to directly influence the safety mechanisms of tomorrow's artificial intelligence systems.

Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

If you have spent years mastering a complex scientific discipline and want to apply your analytical prowess outside the traditional academic or laboratory setting, this role offers an intellectually stimulating environment. You will work as an independent contractor, tackling specialized tasks that require both technical mastery and rigorous linguistic precision.

As a Bilingual German STEM Expert focused on AI safety, your day-to-day responsibilities will include:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Crafting advanced, nuanced, and technically precise prompts in German across specialized scientific domains (chemistry, biology, nuclear physics, and related fields).
  • Model Response Evaluation & Annotation: Rigorously reviewing and grading model outputs for scientific accuracy, logical consistency, contextual helpfulness, and safe handling of sensitive or dual-use technical information.
  • Structured Classification: Applying structured evaluation frameworks and detailed guidelines to categorize prompts, dialogues, and model behaviors.
  • Red-Teaming Technical Content: Proactively probing model limitations to identify vulnerabilities, factual errors, or potential risks in specialized German-language technical queries.

💡 No Prior AI/ML Coding Experience Required: You do not need a background in machine learning engineering or software coding. Your value lies entirely in your domain-specific scientific knowledge and linguistic fluency. Comprehensive workflow training is provided from day one!

Ideal Candidate Profile & Qualifications

To succeed in this role, applicants must meet rigorous academic and communication standards. Mercor is searching for individuals who combine deep specialization with exceptional clarity of thought.

Required Qualifications:

  • Advanced Academic Background: A completed or ongoing PhD in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific field. You must possess the depth required to author and evaluate prompts covering complex chemical, biological, or radiological and nuclear subject areas.
  • Bilingual Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in German, paired with business-level written and spoken English.
  • Subfield Familiarity: Deep, up-to-date familiarity with modern laboratory protocols, experimental procedures, and computational techniques relevant to your specific subfield.
  • Analytical Rigor & Safety Judgment: Impeccable judgment regarding scientific safety protocols, ethical standards, and the responsible management of sensitive or dual-use information.

Preferred & Nice-to-Have Attributes:

  • Geographic Preference: Based in Germany or the broader Western Europe region (while preferred, this is a flexibility preference rather than a strict restriction; exceptional global applicants are welcome).
  • Specialized Subfields: Demonstrated depth in focused niches such as organic/inorganic synthesis, molecular biology, microbiology, virology, genetics, or energetic materials.
  • Radiological & Nuclear Knowledge: Coverage from a chemistry perspective—including radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, or radiation physics—is highly valued.
  • Security & Safety Awareness: Academic research, coursework, or professional experience touching upon chemical safety, biosecurity, biosafety, or hazardous materials handling.
  • Review Experience: Prior experience in peer review, academic grading, technical writing, or red-teaming complex information systems.
