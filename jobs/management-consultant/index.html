The Convergence of Strategy Consulting and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

For years, training large language models relied heavily on general-purpose internet data and basic coding datasets. However, as artificial intelligence systems scale to handle enterprise-level strategic execution, generalist models consistently stumble when confronted with nuanced financial modeling, ambiguous market dynamics, structured business frameworks, and multi-variable corporate decision-making. In executive strategy, a flawed premise or a poorly structured deduction can derail enterprise investments.

To eliminate these reasoning gaps, micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By collaborating with elite management consultants who have spent years navigating complex strategic transformations within top-tier firms, micro1 transforms real-world business acumen into high-fidelity training data, rigorous evaluations, and interactive feedback loops. No prior software engineering or machine learning background is required; your hard-earned strategic intuition and structural problem-solving abilities are what drive the technology forward.
