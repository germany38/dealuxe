Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

The Finance Specialist role is designed for senior-level practitioners who want to apply their deep domain knowledge to cutting-edge technology. Working alongside premier AI research and engineering teams, your primary mission is to inject real-world financial rigor, accuracy, and judgment into automated model outputs.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Bridging Knowledge Gaps: Guide research and engineering teams to resolve complex knowledge deficits in financial reasoning, corporate finance, market analysis, and high-level decision-making.
  • Task Design: Create challenging, domain-relevant financial tasks and write accurate, meticulously reasoned solutions grounded in established financial practice.
  • AI Output Evaluation: Evaluate AI model outputs against structured evaluation rubrics, providing clear and comprehensive written feedback on correctness, commercial judgment, and underlying analytical quality.
  • Rubric Development: Design, iterate, and refine evaluation guidelines and scoring metrics specifically tailored for advanced finance tasks.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate closely with fellow subject-matter experts (SMEs) to guarantee consistency, data integrity, and uncompromising accuracy across training datasets.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these foundational AI models serve institutional and enterprise users, the qualification criteria are rigorous. Mercor and its partnering entities look for proven track records at top-tier organizations.

Core Qualifications & Experience:
  • 8+ Years of Professional Experience: Dedicated background in finance, including investment banking, asset management, corporate finance, private equity, or financial advisory.
  • Top-Tier Background: Prior professional tenure at recognized institutions such as Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley, BlackRock, Fidelity, Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG, or equivalent organizations.
  • AI Evaluation Experience: Prior hands-on experience evaluating LLM or AI model outputs against structured rubrics and scoring criteria (mandatory; must be highlighted in your application).
  • Career Progression: Demonstrable, steady career advancement through roles such as Analyst, Associate, VP, or Director.
  • Reliable Availability: Ability to commit and engage reliably for at least 35 hours per week during standard weekdays.

Employment Structure, Compliance, and Employer of Record Details

  • Employer of Record (W-2 Employment): Cincinnatus LLC acts as the employer of record, providing formal W-2 employment status, comprehensive payroll management, statutory benefits, and compliance oversight while placing you directly within elite AI lab teams.
  • Remote Autonomy: Enjoy the freedom of a fully remote schedule while collaborating on high-impact initiatives from anywhere across the United States.
  • Reliable Payment Systems: Compensation is distributed seamlessly on a weekly basis via Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
