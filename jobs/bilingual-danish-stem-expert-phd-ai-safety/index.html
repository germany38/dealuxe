Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For PhD-level researchers, postdocs, and subject-matter experts looking to transition into high-impact remote consulting or supplement their academic endeavors, this role combines high financial compensation with intellectually stimulating work. No prior machine learning or AI engineering experience is required; Mercor provides complete onboarding and workflow training. Your primary focus will be applying your expert scientific judgment to evaluate and refine AI outputs.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Author sophisticated, professional-grade prompts in Danish across specialized scientific topics, challenging the model's depth of reasoning.
  • Model Annotation & Evaluation: Rigorously review and annotate model responses for absolute scientific accuracy, contextual helpfulness, and appropriate handling of sensitive or dual-use content.
  • Structured Classification: Apply structured evaluation guidelines to categorize and classify model prompts and conversation paths accurately.
  • Safety & Compliance Auditing: Ensure that complex technical discussions surrounding chemistry, biology, and related fields adhere strictly to safety parameters and responsible information sharing.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these roles directly influence the safety parameters of advanced AI models slated for global deployment, the candidate vetting standards are exceptionally high.

Essential Requirements:
  • Educational Background: A PhD in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific field (either ongoing or completed) with sufficient depth to craft prompts across chemical, biological, or radiological/nuclear subject areas.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in spoken and written Danish, combined with business-level written English communication skills.
  • Technical Rigor: Deep familiarity with modern laboratory protocols, advanced scientific literature, and computational techniques within your specialized subfield.
  • Safety Judgment: Sound, ethical judgment regarding scientific safety, biosecurity, and the responsible handling of dual-use technical information.

Preferred & Nice-to-Have Backgrounds: Based in Denmark or the broader Western Europe region (though global applicants are welcome); background in radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, energetic materials, or radiation physics; specialized focus in organic/inorganic synthesis, molecular biology, microbiology, virology, or genetics; prior experience with scientific red-teaming or technical content grading.
