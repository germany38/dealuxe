It relies on massive quantities of structured telemetry, per-frame synchronized video data, keyboard/mouse input matrices, and event-driven timestamps to learn tactical reasoning, navigation, and strategy.

As a Professional Gamer participating in this remote initiative, your primary responsibility is to act as the cognitive blueprint for these systems. By playing selected PC game titles under specialized recording parameters, you provide the pristine, real-world data input that teaches frontier agents how to analyze in-game states, react to dynamic obstacles, and execute high-level strategies.

This engagement is tailored specifically for individuals with a proven track record in competitive, high-level, or professional PC gaming environments. While formal AI experience is not required, candidates must fulfill strict operational criteria:

Detailed Scope of Work

Contractors selected for this role are entrusted with structured testing protocols that require both high-level mechanical skill and strict adherence to data collection standards. Daily operational duties include:

  • Gameplay Recording & Telemetry: Playing selected PC game titles while capturing per-frame synchronized gameplay video alongside precise keyboard and mouse telemetry.
  • Signal & Event Collection: Collecting and submitting detailed engine-level signals and in-game event datasets precisely according to project guidelines.
  • Scenario Annotation: Performing high-level, context-aware annotation of complex gameplay scenarios and unexpected in-game events.
  • Quality Assurance & Bug Tracking: Conducting hands-on quality assurance testing to uncover, document, and report gameplay anomalies, software bugs, or data collection errors.
  • Commitment & Punctuality: Maintaining consistent daily engagement with dedicated 2–3 hour gaming blocks and timely delivery of structured outputs.
  • Protocol Compliance: Adhering strictly to security, privacy, and data handling protocols mandated by project stakeholders.
