Bridging Real-World Hardware Engineering and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

Training advanced AI systems to reason correctly about physical electronics is vastly different from training standard language models. When an engineer designs a circuit, they must balance intricate constraints across voltage, current, power dissipation, signal integrity, thermal behavior, and manufacturing tolerances. A single flawed assumption in component selection or parasitic modeling can lead to catastrophic hardware failure.

Because simulation-only academic experience falls short when evaluating real-world physical constraints, micro1 recruits seasoned engineers who have taken designs through tape-out, fabrication, and physical laboratory bring-up. In this role, your primary mission is to transform your hands-on engineering wisdom into structured training data, benchmark evaluations, and automated verification checks that teach AI models how to think like master hardware architects.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Circuit Synthesis & Schematic Design: Design, analyze, and validate analog, digital, power, mixed-signal, or RF circuits, translating electrical specifications into netlists, models, HDLs, and simulation inputs.
  • Programmatic EDA Workflows: Run circuit simulations, synthesis, and verification workflows using command-line-accessible tools and text-based inputs.
  • Python Automation: Utilize Python (including libraries like PySpice, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, and Matplotlib) to generate inputs, automate simulations, run parameter sweeps, control laboratory instruments, and analyze waveforms.
  • Rigorous Electrical Analysis: Evaluate gain, bandwidth, impedance, noise, timing, stability, thermal behavior, and perform corner, worst-case, transient, frequency-domain, or Monte Carlo analyses.
  • Debugging & Validation: Diagnose complex engineering problems involving component selection, parasitics, PCB layout, power integrity, and manufacturing defects.
  • AI Solution Auditing: Review AI-generated circuit solutions, identify incorrect technical assumptions or invalid configurations, and develop reproducible reference solutions with automated verification scripts.

💡 Maximizing Your Engagement: To ensure long-term project success and secure your standing on high-priority engineering panels, selected experts are encouraged to fully participate in onboarding and complete at least 10 hours of active project work. Meeting this milestone validates your workflow efficiency and ensures seamless output integration.

Preferred Qualifications and Background

micro1 maintains rigorous technical screening standards to ensure data integrity for its enterprise AI clients. Candidates applying for this position should meet the following baseline professional criteria:

  • Educational & Professional Foundation: An MS or PhD in Electrical Engineering or a closely related discipline; OR a BS in Electrical Engineering paired with at least 1–2 years of relevant professional circuit-design experience.
  • Fabrication & Tape-Out Experience: Meaningful, hands-on experience with real circuit designs that were physically fabricated, integrated, shipped, or taped out (simulation-only experience is insufficient).
  • Practical Python Proficiency: Strong scripting skills in Python for automation, data analysis, and instrument or simulator control.
  • Command-Line EDA Tool Familiarity: Practical experience with tools supporting CLI, scripting, or programmatic APIs (e.g., ngspice, Xyce, Yosys, Verilator, KiCad scripting, or SPICE-compatible simulators).
  • Strong Analytical Problem-Solving: Deep understanding of circuit behavior, failure modes, design trade-offs, hardware bring-up, and laboratory validation.
