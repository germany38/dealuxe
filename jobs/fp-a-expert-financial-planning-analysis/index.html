Comprehensive Role Overview & Dual Tracks

Unlike standard administrative or entry-level data labeling tasks, this engagement is designed specifically for seasoned financial authorities. Contributors operate across two distinct strategic tracks, allowing experts to leverage their specialized regional accounting and reporting experience:

  • US Track: Focuses heavily on US GAAP-based corporate financial reporting, domestic market conventions, and U.S. corporate finance frameworks.
  • International Track: Centers on IFRS-based reporting standards, global financial conventions, and multi-national cross-border corporate frameworks.

Qualified experts who possess mastery in either or both tracks are encouraged to apply, building complex evaluation tasks that mirror the actual decision-making stakes, forecasting complexity, and organizational scope found within major global corporations.

Key Responsibilities for FP&A Contributors

Stepping into this role means moving beyond traditional spreadsheet maintenance and stepping into the design of sophisticated AI testing environments. Your daily responsibilities will revolve around architecting realistic business simulations:

Core Tasks & Deliverables:

  • Scenario Construction: Design complex FP&A scenarios spanning annual budgeting cycles, rolling forecasts, variance analyses, business partnering discussions, and major capital allocation decisions.
  • Task Multi-Faceted Buildout: Develop rigorous testing vectors across financial planning, financial modeling, performance reporting, and capital budgeting investment analyses.
  • Platform Integration Scenarios: Incorporate workflows involving industry-standard enterprise tooling, including specialized FP&A platforms (such as Anaplan, Adaptive Insights, and Planful), business intelligence suites (Tableau, Power BI), and enterprise resource planning (ERP) architectures.
  • Methodology Application: Apply advanced financial methodologies—including driver-based forecasting, sensitivity modeling, and variance drill-downs—tailored precisely to the target reporting basis (US GAAP or IFRS).
  • Rubric Authoring: Write meticulous grading rubrics that definitively distinguish authentic, high-level corporate FP&A judgment from generic textbook answers or superficial spreadsheet templates.

Ideal Candidate Qualifications

Because the AI systems being trained are deployed across major enterprise environments, the qualification criteria are tailored to attract senior industry leaders who have direct, hands-on operational experience at scale.

Key Requirements & Preferences:
  • Executive Experience: 5+ years working as a senior FP&A leader, Director of FP&A, or Chief Financial Officer (CFO) at a major corporate entity.
  • Direct Ownership: Proven track record of owning full corporate budgeting cycles, complex multi-year forecasting models, or high-value capital allocation decisions.
  • Technical Fluency: Deep mastery of modern FP&A software tools, corporate finance principles, and advanced financial modeling best practices.
  • Professional Credentials: Possession of recognized credentials such as a CFA, MBA, or CPA is considered a distinct professional advantage, alongside prior experience in training design or rubric authorship.
