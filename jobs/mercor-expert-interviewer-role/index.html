Inside the Role: What Does an Expert Interviewer Actually Do?

Understanding the day-to-day mechanics of the position is vital to crafting a winning application that stands out among hundreds of candidates. Frontier AI labs rely heavily on human expertise to train, refine, and evaluate next-generation machine learning systems. However, before these technical experts can be deployed on specialized projects, they must be rigorously vetted by professionals who understand how to extract signal from noise.

As an Expert Interviewer on this project, your core responsibilities will be structured around precision, efficiency, and clear communication:

  • Conducting Short-Form Structured Interviews: You will lead streamlined, ~20-minute structured interviews with shortlisted technical candidates. These candidates are typically experts in high-demand fields such as GPU kernel development, security and vulnerability research, and ML-compiler or accelerator engineering.
  • Assessing Against Rubrics: Rather than relying on generic conversational interviewing, you will evaluate each candidate's technical depth, communication clarity, and overall fit against a precise, provided evaluation rubric.
  • Verifying Compliance & Logistics: Your conversations will also touch on practical parameters, including confirming each expert's availability, work authorization status, and professional exclusivity, while proactively spotting any potential red flags.
  • Tiering and Reporting: Following each interview, you will categorize candidates into clear performance tiers (advance, hold, or pass) and draft a concise, high-signal summary report for the core program team.
  • Managing Schedule Dynamics: You will coordinate scheduling smoothly within standard weekday business hours, maintaining professional punctuality and a warm, welcoming demeanor.
Why This Role Stands Out: Unlike massive bureaucratic tech roles that require endless meetings and bureaucratic oversight, this part-time engagement is cleanly scoped at roughly 10 hours per week. It provides a phenomenal supplemental income stream without requiring you to sacrifice your primary schedule or career commitments.

What Are the Qualifications and Requirements?

Mercor looks for specific attributes when matching evaluators with high-priority AI evaluation pipelines. To succeed in this role, you should possess:

  1. Prior Interviewing or Vetting Experience: A solid background in technical recruiting, technical program management, engineering management, or rigorous interviewing where you have demonstrated experience assessing senior-level engineers.
  2. Exceptional Communication Skills: The ability to conduct a professional, engaging, and empathetic conversation while keeping strictly on time. Structured judgment is critical here.
  3. Core Availability: Reliable availability during weekday business hours to accommodate candidate schedules smoothly.
  4. Reliable Remote Setup: A quiet home office environment with high-speed internet, professional microphone/camera equipment, and a dedication to disciplined time management.

Please note: Independent contractor terms apply to this role. Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States, as the platform is currently unable to support H1-B or STEM OPT applicants for this specific engagement.
