Job Highlights & Salary Structure

This placement offers an attractive balance of direct classroom teaching hours and dedicated prep time, allowing teachers to maintain work-life balance while exploring China:

  • Monthly Salary: 18,000 ~ 24,000 CNY per month (paid reliably via monthly bank transfer).
  • Student Age Demographic: Young learners aged 3 to 12 years old (Kindergarten & Elementary levels).
  • Working Hours: 5 working days per week, with 30 to 40 weekly hours allocated across teaching and lesson planning.
  • Location: Major urban centers across Guangdong province (Guangzhou, Shenzhen, Dongguan).
  • Teaching Subject: English Language Arts, Phonics, Spoken English, and Immersion Learning.

Candidate Qualification Checklist

To qualify for educational work visa eligibility in China, applicants must meet the following standard criteria:

  • Education: Bachelor’s Degree or higher in any academic field from an accredited institution.
  • TEFL Certification: 120-hour TEFL, TESOL, or CELTA certification (or 2+ years of documented full-time teaching experience).
  • Passport Eligibility: Passport holders from native English-speaking countries (USA, UK, Canada, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand).
  • Background Check: Clean criminal record and healthy physical condition for visa clearance.
