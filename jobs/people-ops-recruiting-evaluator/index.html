Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For human resources directors, talent acquisition leads, and people operations specialists seeking to diversify their income or transition into flexible remote consulting, understanding the exact scope of this role is critical. The position centers on auditing, grading, and refining complex AI-generated work products—ranging from executive compensation slide decks to organizational restructuring documents and hiring policy evaluations.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, assess, and grade AI-generated documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks focused on recruitment strategies, HR policies, performance management systems, and workforce planning.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to uncover factual inaccuracies, tone inconsistencies, structural flaws, and presentation errors in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback Generation: Formulate clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to help train and align frontier AI models with enterprise-grade HR standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex personnel frameworks, interview rubrics, and organizational policies generated by AI reflect best practices in employment law, equity, and talent optimization.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these positions directly influence the performance and reliability of enterprise-grade AI systems, the vetting standards are designed to attract seasoned domain authorities rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven professional track record in people operations, talent acquisition, human resources management, or executive recruiting.
  • Language Fluency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills.
  • Software Competency: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in presentation tools like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) from a reputable academic institution in human resources, organizational psychology, business administration, or a related field.
