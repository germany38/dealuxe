Frontier AI Research & Science

Discover how PhDs and Master's-level chemists are leveraging their expertise to train next-generation large language models in advanced STEM reasoning.

As artificial intelligence transitions from basic automation into complex analytical reasoning, the demand for high-tier human domain expertise has reached an all-time high. General-purpose language models often struggle with the rigorous multi-step logic required in specialized academic fields like physical, organic, and inorganic chemistry. To bridge this gap, elite scientific minds are stepping forward to shape how machines understand molecular structures, chemical equilibrium, and thermodynamic equations.

Turing stands at the epicenter of this technological shift. Operating as the world’s leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs, Turing partners with global enterprises and advanced labs to build high-quality data pipelines and train AI models in coding, reasoning, and specialized STEM fields.

About Turing and the Mission

Based in San Francisco, California, Turing supports customers through two primary pillars: accelerating frontier research with high-quality training data paired with top-tier AI researchers, and helping global enterprises transform AI proofs of concept into reliable, scalable proprietary intelligence. For scientists and researchers, Turing provides a unique platform to future-proof careers in an AI-first world.

Role Overview: Chemistry Expert (PhD / Master's)

In this remote contractor role, you will work directly on advanced projects designed to fine-tune large language models (such as ChatGPT) using your strong analytical capabilities and structured English comprehension. Ideal candidates possess a robust foundation in chemistry, comparable to postgraduate research standards, and the ability to break down complex molecular problems into clear, logical explanations.

You Are a Perfect Fit If You Have:

  • Advanced Academic Background: Candidates pursuing or holding a PhD or Master's degree in Chemistry, Chemical Engineering, Biochemistry, or closely related fields.
  • Analytical Rigor: Exceptional research and problem-solving skills across physical, organic, and inorganic chemistry.
  • Communication Mastery: The ability to articulate intricate scientific concepts clearly using simple language, visual aids, and structural diagrams.
  • Remote Independence: Self-motivation, attention to detail, and a reliable laptop/desktop setup with a stable internet connection.

What a Day-to-Day Look Like

Your daily routine will center on designing, solving, and annotating advanced chemistry problems that test the boundaries of artificial intelligence reasoning capabilities. Typical tasks include:

  • Organic Reaction Mechanisms: Analyzing and explaining multi-step organic reaction pathways, providing detailed text alongside annotated chemical structures.
  • Equilibrium & Energy: Solving complex chemical equilibrium problems accompanied by visual representations of reaction progress and energy diagrams.
  • Thermodynamics & Stoichiometry: Addressing challenging engineering entrance exam or postgraduate-level problems using a combination of textual, visual, and diagrammatic explanations.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

Contracting with Turing offers flexibility and high-impact engagement without the constraints of traditional institutional roles:

  • 100% Remote Flexibility: Work from anywhere in a fully autonomous virtual environment.
  • Cutting-Edge AI Exposure: Collaborate on projects at the absolute forefront of generative AI and LLM development.
  • Career Longevity: Gain hands-on insight into how AI systems interpret science, future-proofing your professional skillset.
  • Contract Extension Potential: High-performing contractors enjoy opportunities for ongoing project extensions based on performance and client demand.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Process

This opportunity is structured as a flexible contractor/freelancer assignment. To ensure stability and unlock full access to diverse tasks within the platform, candidates must successfully complete the initial onboarding program and fulfill their first 10 hours of active project work. This foundational period establishes operational readiness and integrates you smoothly into Turing's elite network.
