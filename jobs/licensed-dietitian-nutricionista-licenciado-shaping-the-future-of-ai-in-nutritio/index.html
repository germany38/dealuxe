As a Licensed Dietitian contractor with micro1, your primary objective is to supply rigorous nutritional insights that train machine learning models to reason, advise, and evaluate dietary data safely and accurately. Your day-to-day work involves:

  • Nutrition Counseling Insights: Providing detailed, evidence-based nutrition counseling insights to support AI model development.
  • Dietary Assessments: Conducting comprehensive dietary assessments and reviewing case-based scenarios for clinical accuracy and relevance.
  • Meal Planning & Validation: Creating, evaluating, and refining meal planning templates and recommendations aligned with established clinical guidelines.
  • Clear Written Feedback: Delivering concise, well-articulated written feedback and explanations on diverse nutrition topics and client interactions.
  • Scientific Alignment: Assessing and validating nutritional content to ensure absolute consistency with the latest scientific research and industry best practices.
  • Asynchronous Collaboration: Working seamlessly with project coordinators through written and verbal communication channels while maintaining top-tier data quality standards.
