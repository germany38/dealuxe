Deep Dive: What Does a Customer Success Engineer Do at Mercor?

Mercor connects top-tier global talent with leading artificial intelligence research labs and enterprise clients. Thousands of users visit the platform weekly to interview for high-end roles through advanced AI video interviews. Ensuring a frictionless, delightful end-to-end user journey is critical to the company's hyper-growth.

As a Customer Success Engineer based in India, you will serve as the technical backbone of Mercor's talent support operations. You won't just answer emails; you will investigate complex system logs, debug web applications, collaborate closely with core software engineering squads, and solve high-priority technical roadblocks.

Key Responsibilities of the Role:

  • End-to-End Bug Investigation: Reproduce reported user errors, isolate system defects from UX friction, and identify root causes across modern web stacks.
  • Full-Stack & AI Debugging: Utilize telemetry data, browser network inspections, server logs, and database queries to inspect production behavior across AI and SaaS applications.
  • Strategic Triage: Apply sound engineering judgment to escalate genuine system bugs to product leadership while resolving standard support inquiries via prompt configuration, platform setup, or precise user guidance.
  • Systemic Optimization: Surface product risks and usage patterns to engineering teams, creating internal runbooks and user documentation to eliminate repeat friction.
  • Client Communication: Maintain an energetic, informal yet professional tone, communicating technical solutions with absolute clarity, precision, and empathy.

💡 Work Schedule Note: This is a demanding, fast-paced 6-day per week remote role that requires substantial working hour overlap with Pacific Time (PT/PST) to support global operations seamlessly.

Candidate Qualifications: What is Mercor Looking For?

Because this position sits precisely at the intersection of customer support, modern software engineering, and artificial intelligence, applicants must demonstrate strong technical aptitude and execution capabilities:

  • Technical Education or Background: A degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related technical discipline from a top-tier university, or equivalent startup experience.
  • Web Development & Support Experience: Hands-on experience building modern web applications (using React, Node, Flask, Next.js, etc.) OR 2 to 5 years of professional technical support experience handling SaaS web applications.
  • AI Systems Familiarity: Comfort and familiarity with modern generative AI tools, Large Language Models (LLMs), agent frameworks (LangChain, AutoGPT), API integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic), or chain-of-thought debugging.
  • Communication Excellence: Native-level English written and verbal proficiency with an upbeat, professional, and empathetic support voice.
