Comprehensive Overview of the Role

For practicing accounting specialists, audit managers, and senior financial officers seeking to transition their expertise into the high-growth tech sector, understanding the core mandate of this position is paramount. Rather than performing routine bookkeeping, you will act as a foundational architect of financial AI reasoning.

Working directly with research and program management teams at a premier AI laboratory, your core responsibility is to elevate how artificial intelligence interprets and executes real-world accounting and audit tasks. Your day-to-day work centers on establishing absolute factual correctness, identifying subtle discrepancies in reasoning, and crafting golden standard datasets that serve as the gold standard for model calibration.

Key Responsibilities & Scope of Work

The scope of this role moves far beyond simple prompt engineering. It requires active, senior-level professional judgment to audit, test, and refine complex financial models against established industry standards:

1. Data Quality Assurance & Critical Reviews

You will rigorously vet accounting and audit knowledge work tasks and model-generated outputs. This involves spotting missing behaviors, superficial logic, misapplied international or domestic accounting standards, unsupported financial treatments, and plausible-sounding answers that would fail inspection by a reviewing audit partner.

2. Instruction Specifications & Golden Datasets

You will author precise instruction specs, produce airtight golden solutions to intricate accounting problems, and design entirely new task frameworks that accurately mirror how high-level financial work is performed in institutional settings.

3. Benchmark Design & Domain Depth

Collaborating side-by-side with research scientists, you will construct challenging accounting and audit evaluation sets. These benchmarks directly determine whether frontier models are genuinely improving in financial reasoning capabilities.

4. Cross-Disciplinary Calibration

Translating tacit accounting judgment into explicit, teachable criteria requires close collaboration with researchers and adjacent specialists, ensuring that model evaluation metrics remain consistent, robust, and industry-aligned.

Core Qualifications & Eligibility Requirements

Because the AI systems being trained are deployed across high-stakes corporate environments, the candidate criteria are intentionally rigorous, welcoming only established professionals with verified industry credentials.

Key Qualification Checklist:
  • Active Professional Credentials: Must hold an active certification such as CPA, CA, CIA, CFE, or CMA, accompanied by an undergraduate or graduate degree in accounting or a closely related discipline.
  • Substantial Experience: Minimum of 4+ years of substantive accounting or audit experience at a recognized institution (public accounting firms, corporate controllerships, internal audit departments, or regulatory bodies). Note that bookkeeping-only roles do not fulfill this requirement.
  • Domain Specialization: Proven deep expertise in at least one key pillar: external audit and assurance, technical accounting (US GAAP or IFRS), internal audit and SOX controls, tax compliance, forensic accounting, or revenue recognition.
  • Seniority & Ownership: Clear career progression to senior roles such as Audit Manager, Senior Manager, Accounting Manager, Controller, Director of Accounting, or Partner.
  • AI Literacy & Judgment: Hands-on familiarity using large language models in daily professional workflows, possessing the critical eye necessary to distinguish between genuine analytical rigor and confident hallucinations.
  • Commitment & Location: Full-time availability (40 hours per week) for an initial 6-month engagement. This role is a hybrid position based in the Bay Area, California, requiring on-site collaboration multiple days per week (relocation expenses are the candidate's responsibility).
