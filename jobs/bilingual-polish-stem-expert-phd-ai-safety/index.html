Introduction: The Intersection of Advanced Science and AI Safety

Artificial intelligence is evolving at an unprecedented pace, stretching across complex scientific domains like biology, chemistry, and nuclear physics. However, as language models become more powerful, ensuring their absolute safety, accuracy, and technical reliability becomes critical. Tech labs and frontier AI organizations are racing to solve the problem of safe dual-use information handling, requiring top-tier human minds to test, validate, and train these systems.

Enter Mercor’s specialized hiring initiative for a Bilingual Polish STEM Expert (PhD). This unique role bridges the gap between high-level academic research and next-generation artificial intelligence. If you hold or are pursuing a PhD in a rigorous scientific discipline, possess native or near-native fluency in Polish, and want a flexible remote career with competitive hourly rates, this opportunity provides the ultimate platform to impact global tech innovation.

  • Position Title: Bilingual Polish STEM Expert (PhD) — AI Safety
  • Compensation Rate: $50 to $54 per hour
  • Employment Type: Independent Contractor / Part-time
  • Work Arrangement: Fully remote, complete schedule flexibility
  • Regional Preference: Based in Poland or the wider Eastern Europe region preferred, though exceptional global applicants are welcome
  • Project Start Date: Immediate

Detailed Job Description & Daily Responsibilities

What does an AI safety expert actually do day-to-day? You will not need prior coding or software engineering experience. Instead, your deep domain expertise in science and languages forms the core of the workflow. The responsibilities are explicitly structured around evaluating technical accuracy:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Write rigorous, expert-level prompts in Polish spanning specialized scientific topics, testing how advanced models process complex technical jargon.
  • Response Evaluation and Annotation: Rigorously analyze and annotate model outputs for scientific precision, overall helpfulness, and proper adherence to safety parameters regarding sensitive content.
  • Classification Protocols: Apply structured, standardized evaluation guidelines to classify model conversations and prompts accurately.

💡 Note on Workflow: No prior AI/ML experience is required. Mercor provides complete, structured training on the workflow so you can seamlessly apply your academic brilliance straight out of the gate.

Candidate Qualifications: What Mercor Is Looking For

Because this position deals with frontier technology and sensitive topics, the vetting standards are high. To qualify successfully for this role, candidates must meet the following baseline requirements:

  • Academic Background: A PhD (completed or ongoing) in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific field, bringing deep expertise across chemical, biological, or radiological and nuclear subject areas.
  • Linguistic Fluency: Native or near-native proficiency in Polish, combined with strong business-level written English skills.
  • Subfield Depth: Deep familiarity with modern laboratory techniques, computational methods, and specialized subfields (such as organic or inorganic synthesis, molecular biology, microbiology, virology, or genetics).
  • Safety Judgment: Sound, professional judgment regarding scientific ethics, biosafety, biosecurity, and the responsible handling of dual-use technical information.

Preferred Nice-to-Haves

While the above qualifications are essential, candidates with the following background characteristics will find themselves exceptionally well-suited for the position:

  • Residency in Poland or broader Eastern Europe.
  • Radiological and nuclear coverage derived from a chemistry foundation (e.g., radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, energetic materials, or radiation physics).
  • Prior academic research, coursework, or professional experience touching on chemical safety and security, hazardous-materials handling, or red-teaming technical documents.
