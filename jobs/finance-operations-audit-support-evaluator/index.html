Job Overview & Summary

Mercor is actively seeking expert Evaluators specializing in Finance Operations and Audit Support. In this role, you will review, critique, and assess AI-generated work outputs—such as complex financial documents, spreadsheets, and professional slide decks—ensuring absolute precision, rigor, and industry-standard domain quality. By leveraging your deep subject-matter expertise, you will grade AI models, correct analytical discrepancies, and help pioneer the future of AI-driven business intelligence.

Quick Job Facts:

  • Compensation: $80 to $120 per hour (paid weekly via Stripe or Wise)
  • Work Arrangement: 100% Remote, independent contractor setup
  • Schedule: Complete on your own flexible schedule
  • Platform: Mercor (connecting elite professionals with leading AI labs)

Core Responsibilities

  • AI Artifact Evaluation: Rigorously assess AI-generated documents, financial models, and presentations against strict domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Error Identification: Spot and flag factual inaccuracies, numerical mistakes, presentation flaws, and aesthetic inconsistencies in financial output.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, professional-grade written feedback to guide AI model improvements.
  • Quality Assurance: Maintain top-tier quality standards for deliverables tied to financial analysis, operations, and corporate auditing structures.

Candidate Requirements (Must-Haves)

  1. Professional Background: A minimum of 5+ years of relevant hands-on professional experience working directly in finance operations, corporate finance, or audit support.
  2. Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills.
  3. Software Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office suite and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in presentation tools like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Advanced educational degrees (such as a Master’s degree, MBA, or higher) from a reputable, accredited institution.
  • Prior experience in tech-adjacent consulting, financial compliance, or automated reporting systems.

Contract & Payment Terms

Engaged strictly as an independent contractor, this role offers immense flexibility. You can balance your commitments easily while working remotely on your own schedule. Payments are issued reliably on a weekly basis via Stripe or Wise based strictly on services rendered. Please note that Mercor is currently unable to support H1-B or STEM OPT candidates for this specific opening.

Comprehensive Deep-Dive: The Evolution of Finance Operations & AI Integration

The modern financial landscape is undergoing a massive paradigm shift. For decades, finance operations, auditing, and accounting compliance relied entirely on manual entry, exhaustive spreadsheet cross-checking, and grueling review hours. Today, artificial intelligence has entered the corporate workspace, promising to accelerate workflows and automate repetitive data tasks. However, AI models do not inherently possess domain intuition; they hallucinate, miscalculate complex financial ratios, and misinterpret regulatory nuances unless guided by seasoned human experts.

This is where senior professionals enter the picture. The role of a Finance Operations / Audit Support Evaluator is not merely about reviewing code or checking basic spelling. It is about applying decades of cumulative corporate wisdom to teach frontier AI systems how to think like elite financial analysts. When an AI model generates a comprehensive audit report or a strategic capital allocation slide deck, it takes a trained eye to recognize whether the underlying logic holds up against GAAP standards, internal controls, and risk management best practices.

Why Transition into AI Training & Evaluation?

You are no longer just keeping up with industry trends—you are actively building them.
