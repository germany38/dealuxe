Deep Dive into the Role & Responsibilities

AI labs are aggressively advancing voice-activated artificial intelligence assistants. To ensure these models understand natural human speech patterns, cadence, and contextual nuances in Korean, human experts are needed to test, record, and evaluate interactions.

English Overview

You will utilize a specialized desktop app on your Mac to execute everyday tasks entirely by voice. Your responsibilities include dictating emails, managing notes, prompting the AI to generate images, format spreadsheets, or summarize dense documents, followed by rigorous quality transcription and evaluation.

한국어 개요

Key Responsibilities / 주요 업무

  • Voice Interaction / 음성 상호작용: Use your voice to compose messages, emails, and notes across everyday productivity apps. (일상적인 생산성 앱에서 음성으로 메시지, 이메일, 메모 작성)
  • AI Task Direction / AI 작업 지시: Direct the AI to generate images, apply spreadsheet formulas, format text, or summarize complex documents. (이미지 생성, 스프레드시트 수식 적용, 문서 요약 등 AI 지시)

  • Evaluation / 평가: Rate the AI's contextual responses to maintain top-tier training benchmarks. (AI 응답 품질 평가 및 피드백 제공)
