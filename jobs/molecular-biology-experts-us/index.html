Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

Understanding the architecture of this role is crucial for life science professionals considering a transition into AI training and model evaluation. Unlike generalized data annotation jobs, this position requires deep, hands-on mastery of nucleic acid design, genetic engineering protocols, and advanced biochemical reasoning.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Model Troubleshooting & Gap Analysis: Work directly with leading research and engineering teams to identify exact breakpoints where an AI model's biological reasoning falters, formulating strategies to close those gaps.
  • Ground Truth Task Design: Construct rigorous, domain-relevant benchmark tasks and author meticulous, well-documented solutions. This spans precise sequence designs including primers for PCR, qPCR, and cloning; plasmids and expression vectors; gRNA and sgRNA for CRISPR editing; mRNA constructs; and HDR repair templates.
  • Output Auditing & Rigor Assessment: Review complex molecular biology tasks and model-generated outputs against expert-quality solutions, delivering clear written feedback regarding biological correctness, thermodynamic accuracy, and logical rigor.
  • Rubric & Guideline Creation: Author foundational guidelines and evaluation rubrics utilized to judge sequence design quality—covering parameters such as target selection, homology arm length, primer melting temperature (Tm), specificity, codon optimization, and regulatory elements.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner alongside other interdisciplinary subject matter experts to ensure training datasets maintain uncompromised consistency, accuracy, and scientific validity.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these frontier models power next-generation biotech and computational biology research, the qualification requirements are tailored for elite practitioners in the life sciences.

Core Qualifications:
  • Advanced Academic Degree: An advanced degree in molecular biology, genetics, biochemistry, synthetic biology, bioengineering, or a related life science field (PhD is strongly preferred).
  • Hands-On Sequence Design Experience: Proven practical experience designing nucleic acid constructs prior to experimental execution across several modalities: primers, plasmids and vectors, gRNA/sgRNA, mRNA, and HDR repair templates, paired with mastery of cloning strategies (Gibson, Golden Gate, Gateway, restriction digests) and codon optimization.
  • Publication Record: A strong peer-reviewed publication record, weighted toward first-author contributions in prominent scientific venues such as Nature and the Nature family, Cell, eLife, Nucleic Acids Research, PNAS, and the EMBO Journal (applicants are encouraged to include publication links).
  • Time Commitment: Ability to reliably commit to a minimum of 20 hours per week during standard weekdays.
  • Communication Skills: Exceptional written communication abilities, with a proven talent for articulating complex scientific reasoning and design justifications with absolute precision.
