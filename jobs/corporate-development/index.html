The Modern Frontier of Strategic Finance: Monetizing In-House M&A Expertise

The traditional corporate career trajectory for strategic finance, mergers and acquisitions (M&A), and corporate development professionals has historically been tethered to high-pressure office environments, grueling travel schedules, and rigid corporate hierarchies. While the intellectual rigor of deal-making, valuation modeling, and strategic negotiation remains deeply rewarding, elite operators increasingly seek alternatives that offer autonomy, superior hourly compensation, and freedom from internal corporate politics.

At the same time, the artificial intelligence revolution has created an unprecedented demand for specialized, real-world human expertise. Frontier AI laboratories and advanced enterprise systems require pristine training libraries built from authentic corporate development work products to master complex financial reasoning, transaction structuring, and valuation analysis. Platforms like Mercor are bridging this gap, offering seasoned in-house corporate development practitioners the opportunity to monetize their hard-earned deal experience at an elite rate of $100 per hour on a completely flexible, remote schedule.

Deep Dive: Job Description & Core Responsibilities

This specialized engagement is tailored specifically for seasoned in-house operators rather than sell-side bankers or external advisors. The objective is to build a premier, high-quality library of corporate development deliverables by completing self-contained M&A exercises derived from realistic mock files (including Confidential Information Memorandums, diligence reports, term sheets, and board decks).

What You Will Do:

  • Valuation & Merger Modeling: Construct comprehensive financial models from scratch, including three-statement projections, Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) analyses, trading and transaction comps, accretion-dilution analyses, capitalization tables, and complex equity waterfalls.
  • Transaction Term Structuring: Draft, review, and compare critical deal terms across Indication of Interest (IOI) letters, Letters of Intent (LOI), definitive term sheets, bid comparisons, deal structure mechanics, and consideration economics.
  • Process & Board Materials Production: Author executive-ready management presentations, comprehensive data packs, prospective buyer universes, board and committee decks, as well as closing mechanics and detailed funds flow documentation.
  • Screening, Diligence & Integration Execution: Drive inbound deal screens, formulate exhaustive diligence request lists, synthesize findings, build synergy models, execute carve-out analyses, and design structured 100-day post-merger integration plans.

Candidate Profile: Who Qualifies for This Role?

Because the AI systems trained through this project are designed to assist top-tier enterprise decision-makers, the qualification standards are rigorous. Mercor is actively searching for hands-on practitioners who have executed live transactions from conception to close from an in-house perspective.

Key Requirements & Preferences:
  • Core Requirement: 4+ years of professional experience working in an in-house corporate development or M&A role directly inside an operating company.
  • Direct Ownership: Proven track record of hands-on ownership of live transactions end-to-end, rather than supporting advisory work.
  • Preferred Background: Prior foundational experience in investment banking (IB) or private equity (PE) before transitioning in-house, or Big 4 transaction advisory/financial due diligence experience.

Understanding the Assessment Structure

To ensure alignment with the rigorous standards required for frontier AI training, applicants must successfully navigate a practical evaluation. Rather than relying on generic trivia or theoretical questions, the assessment tests real-world operational execution:

  • Building a complete merger model extracted from a mock target file.
  • Conducting a thorough term sheet comparison.
  • Drafting a board-ready recommendation memorandum synthesizing financial and strategic factors.
