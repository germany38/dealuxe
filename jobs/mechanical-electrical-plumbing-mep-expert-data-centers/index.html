  • Strong command of data-center systems and relevant codes/standards; PE or recognized commissioning credential preferred.
  • Experience at a leading multidisciplinary, mission-critical, or consulting engineering firm strongly preferred.
  • Able to interpret large document sets, drawings, schedules, calculations, equipment submittals, and machine-readable data.
