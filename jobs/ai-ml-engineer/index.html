The Micro1 Ecosystem: Building the Human Intelligence Layer for Frontier AI

To understand why micro1 has become a dominant force in AI data labeling and agent evaluation, one must look at how modern artificial intelligence is trained. While raw compute power and massive datasets are foundational, they often contain blind spots, hallucinations, and reasoning gaps. micro1 solves this by connecting elite human domain experts—including PhDs, professors, and industry leaders from top global institutions—directly into the training loops of advanced AI systems.

As an AI/ML Engineer on the core team, you won't just be building standard web applications; you will be architecting the infrastructure that measures, routes, and improves AI performance at massive scale. Your code will directly support mission-critical government projects and power micro1’s proprietary AI platform, ensuring that next-generation models are secure, robust, and enterprise-ready.

Key Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Model Design & Optimization: Design, implement, and optimize machine learning models with a specialized focus on Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, and advanced prompt engineering for production environments.

  • Data Pipeline Architecture: Build robust data pipelines, manage complex ETL processes, and develop comprehensive metadata catalogs and ontologies to guarantee pristine data for model training and inference.
  • API & SDK Development: Create and maintain high-performance REST APIs and SDK integrations to ensure seamless interaction between models, datasets, and external applications.
  • DevOps & Secure Coding: Collaborate closely with product, security, and engineering teams to enforce best-in-class delivery standards, adhering to secure coding guidelines and robust CI/CD DevOps pipelines.
