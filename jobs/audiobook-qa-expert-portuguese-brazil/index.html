Comprehensive Job Breakdown: What Does an Audiobook QA Expert Do?

Text-to-speech technology has reached unprecedented milestones, but machines still struggle with subtle human cadence, emotional inflections, pronunciation variations, and rhythmic pacing. As an Audiobook QA Expert, your role is pivotal in auditing AI-synthesized speech.

You will listen to AI-narrated audiobooks in Brazilian Portuguese with an analytical, trained ear—identifying exact moments where the speech falters, mispronounces technical terms, handles numbers incorrectly, or sounds unnatural. Your feedback directly retrains and refines frontier AI models.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Active Listening & Evaluation: Listen to AI-generated audiobook chapters in Brazilian Portuguese, grading their natural cadence, accuracy, and overall listener immersion.
  • Error Detection & Logging: Spot and record specific speech anomalies, such as added, skipped, or mispronounced words, wrong number readouts, incorrect abbreviation expansions, and audio glitches.
  • Precise Timestamp Annotation: Categorize issues and tag exact timelines where audio quality breaks down using specialized review software tools.
  • Quality Reporting: Deliver structured assessments detailing listener satisfaction and highlighting areas where synthetic speech successfully mimics professional human narration.

💡 Work Flexibility: This position requires approximately 20 hours per week (roughly 4 hours daily) and can be executed completely on your own schedule from anywhere in Brazil.
