Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Red Team Expert Do?

Artificial intelligence models are rapidly transforming global industries, but deploying them securely requires rigorous human evaluation. Automated tests often miss subtle linguistic nuances, cultural idioms, and complex contextual vulnerabilities. This is where human red team experts step in to deliberately stress-test models.

As an AI Safety Expert at Mercor, your core mission is to act as an ethical adversary. You will systematically probe conversational AI agents, test for prompt injections, evaluate bias and misinformation handling, and document vulnerabilities in both English and Thai. Your insights ensure that frontier AI architectures remain robust, safe, and reliable before they reach end users.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Conversational Probing: Execute sophisticated jailbreaks, multi-turn manipulations, and misuse scenarios to test AI model boundaries.
  • Data Generation & Annotation: Classify model failures, map structural vulnerabilities, and record systemic risks cleanly.
  • Taxonomy Alignment: Follow structured evaluation benchmarks and playbooks to ensure testing consistency across diverse projects.
  • Reproducible Documentation: Produce comprehensive reports and attack cases that technical teams can immediately act upon to patch vulnerabilities.

💡 Flexibility & Safety: All project work is text-based and fully remote. Participation in sensitive topics is completely optional, guided by clear boundaries and robust wellness resources.
