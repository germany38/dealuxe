About the Role: Sports Domain Expert

As a Sports Domain Expert at Turing, your core mandate is to evaluate, challenge, and elevate the reasoning capabilities of frontier AI models. You will design intricate prompts, test factual accuracy across global sports leagues, and audit AI outputs for nuance, completeness, and logical consistency.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Engineering & Design: Create advanced prompts spanning global sports, leagues, athletes, tournaments, rules, and deep analytics.
  • Model Evaluation: Assess AI responses for factual accuracy, reasoning quality, and subtle narrative nuance.
  • Error Detection: Identify hallucinations, logical contradictions, outdated information, and edge-case failures.
  • Benchmark Creation: Develop rigorous benchmark datasets and adversarial test cases.
  • Evidence-Based Feedback: Provide precise, reliable references and documentation to guide AI optimization.

You Are a Fit If You Bring:

Turing seeks analytical minds with deep domain fluency. Ideal candidates should possess:

  • Educational Background: Master’s degree or higher in any field, with a preference for Sports Management, Sports Science, Journalism, Communications, Media Studies, or related disciplines.
  • Industry Experience: 3+ years of professional experience in sports journalism, media, content production, research, analysis, or reporting.
  • Analytical Rigor: Strong written English, meticulous attention to detail, and a proven ability to evaluate sports-related data accurately.
  • Preferred Traits: Familiarity with Generative AI, LLMs, prompt engineering, published research, or teaching experience.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

Your typical workday involves acting as an intellectual sparring partner for advanced AI models. You will simulate complex research tasks, test how well an LLM interprets obscure tournament regulations or historical statistical anomalies, and author comprehensive evaluation reports. Working independently, you maintain high annotation standards while coordinating with AI research leads to refine testing frameworks.

Perks of Working with Turing

Contracting with Turing offers unique career advantages for seasoned professionals:

  • Cutting-Edge Impact: Stand at the vanguard of generative AI development, leaving a permanent imprint on how machines process human knowledge.
  • Professional Growth: Deepen your understanding of AI evaluation methodologies and frontier technology systems.
