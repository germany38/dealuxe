Detailed Role Description & Daily Responsibilities / 工作詳情與日常職責

As a Bilingual Writer focusing on Traditional Mandarin Chinese, you will work directly with a specialized application running on your Mac. Your primary goal is to simulate realistic human workflows using voice commands and evaluate how effectively the AI responds.

作為專注於繁體中文的雙語寫作者，您將直接在 Mac 上運行專用應用程式。您的主要目標是使用語音指令模擬真實的人類工作流程，並評估 AI 的回應成效。

Core Duties Include / 核心職責包括：

  • Voice Composition: Use your voice to compose emails, messages, and notes across everyday productivity applications.
  • AI Task Direction: Instruct the AI to generate images, format text, apply complex spreadsheet formulas, or summarize extensive documents.
  • Transcription & Cleanup: Record precise transcripts of your spoken queries and produce cleaned-up text versions.
  • Quality Rating: Rigorously rate the AI's responses and log task data into structured tracking sheets while uploading recordings.
  • 語音創作：透過語音在各大生產力應用程式中撰寫電子郵件、訊息與筆記。
  • 指導 AI 執行任務：指示 AI 生成圖片、格式化文字、應用試算表公式或摘要文件。
  • 逐字稿與整理：記錄您口頭查詢的精確逐字稿，並產出清理後的文字版本。
  • 品質評分：嚴格評估 AI 的回應，將任務數據記錄至結構化追蹤表中並上傳錄音檔案。

💻 Hardware Prerequisite Note / 硬體設備特別提醒： This position strictly requires an Apple Mac equipped with an Apple Silicon chip (M1, M2, or M3) running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or newer. Please verify your system specifications before applying. / 本職缺嚴格要求須擁有搭載 Apple Silicon 晶片（M1、M2 或 M3）並運行 macOS Sequoia 15.0 或更新版本的 Mac 電腦。

To ensure high data integrity for frontier AI training, Mercor seeks candidates who combine native linguistic precision with technical tech-savviness. Review the qualifications below:

  • English Comprehension: Able to seamlessly understand, interpret, and follow instructions written in English.
  • Software Familiarity: Regular, everyday usage of common productivity apps including email clients, messaging platforms, document editors, and spreadsheets.
  • Detail Orientation: High comfort level with structured data entry, self-directed quality control, and rigorous review protocols.
  • 語言流利度：具備繁體中文的母語或近母語水準，具備台灣成長或母語背景者優先。

  • 注重細節：對結構化數據輸入、自主品質控制及嚴格審查規範具備高度適應力。
