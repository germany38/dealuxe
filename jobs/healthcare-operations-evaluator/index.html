Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For healthcare directors, clinic administrators, health informatics specialists, and operational leaders seeking a high-yielding remote consulting role, understanding the daily scope of this position is vital. The job demands senior-level analytical precision, requiring evaluators to review and critique sophisticated AI-generated work products designed to solve healthcare administration challenges.

What You Will Do on a Day-to-Day Basis:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, audit, and grade AI-generated spreadsheets, strategic documents, and professional slide decks focused on healthcare operations, patient flow optimization, and health system management.
  • Rigor and Quality Assurance: Apply rigorous domain-specific knowledge to identify factual inaccuracies, clinical workflow inconsistencies, aesthetic flaws, and presentation errors in automated deliverables.
  • Constructive Technical Feedback: Author clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to help train and align frontier large language models with real-world medical business standards.
  • Cross-Functional Alignment: Ensure that operational frameworks and administrative strategies generated by AI comply with industry best practices for efficiency, patient safety, and regulatory compliance.

Candidate Requirements: Qualifications Needed to Succeed

Because these evaluations directly train the underlying artificial intelligence systems deployed across major global enterprises, the qualification criteria are carefully tailored to attract seasoned industry experts rather than casual generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: A solid, verifiable professional background in healthcare operations, clinical administration, health systems management, or healthcare consulting.
  • Communication Skills: Native or professional fluency in English, paired with exceptional technical and professional writing capabilities.
  • Technical Proficiency: High fluency in standard corporate productivity suites, specifically Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with advanced proficiency in presentation tools like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced academic degree (such as an MHA, MBA in Healthcare Management, MPH, or higher) from a reputable institution.
