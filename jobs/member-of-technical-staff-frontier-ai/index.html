The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Demands Advanced Technical Ownership

As large language models and autonomous agents integrate deeper into enterprise software, medical diagnostics, financial analysis, and autonomous robotics, standard evaluation benchmarks (such as static academic datasets) have become saturated. Models can easily memorize test sets while failing catastrophically in unpredictable, open-ended real-world workflows.

Solving this crisis requires building sophisticated feedback loops, reinforcement learning (RL) environments, and rigorous validation rubrics. As a Member of Technical Staff at micro1, you will not just write code; you will act as a definitive quality gate and technical owner. You will collaborate directly with top-tier AI researchers, domain experts, and client-facing teams to ensure that experimental work produces clean, defensible research signal that directly translates into state-of-the-art improvements in deployed systems.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • End-to-End Evaluation Ownership: Own research and evaluation initiatives from conception to execution—encompassing problem framing, advanced data design, quality calibration, and robust signal validation.
  • ML-Oriented Data Architecture: Design specialized data systems, including complex task definitions, annotation schemas, scoring rubrics, incentive structures, and pipelines optimized for downstream model training.
  • Failure Analysis & Root Cause Identification: Deeply analyze model and system failures to isolate root causes, edge cases, and systemic opportunities for performance gains.
  • Translating Ambiguity into Structure: Take messy, unstructured real-world system behavior and translate it into clear evaluation frameworks and novel data categories.
  • Serving as a Quality Gate: Maintain uncompromising standards by blocking claims, pausing work, or forcing architectural scope changes when signal strength or data integrity falls short.
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Partner with cross-functional research and engineering squads to translate complex technical progress into clear, credible, evidence-based narratives.

💡 The Power of High Signal: In frontier AI development, data quantity takes a backseat to data quality. This role places you in the driver's seat of signal integrity, deciding where capital, compute, and human expertise should be deployed to maximize model reasoning capabilities.
