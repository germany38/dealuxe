Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For finance and accounting experts seeking premium remote work that respects their professional background, understanding the scope of this engagement is vital. The position requires senior-level analytical rigor to audit, grade, and refine complex AI-generated work outputs—ranging from intricate financial models and balance sheet summaries to executive presentation decks.

What You Will Do:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review and critique AI-generated work products, including financial spreadsheets, accounting reports, valuation models, and executive slide decks against rigorous professional quality rubrics.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep subject-matter expertise in accounting standards and financial analysis to pinpoint computational errors, presentation flaws, structural omissions, and technical discrepancies.
  • Constructive Written Feedback: Deliver clear, structured, and actionable commentary to help calibrate and train frontier AI models to perform at an institutional corporate standard.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that automated financial outputs mirror best practices in corporate finance, financial statement analysis, and regulatory compliance frameworks.

Essential Candidate Requirements

Because these specialized evaluation projects directly train enterprise-grade AI models relied upon by major global organizations, the screening criteria are intentionally tailored for experienced professionals.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: A proven, substantial professional background working directly in general finance, corporate accounting, auditing, financial planning and analysis (FP&A), or related financial disciplines.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English, paired with exceptional technical and professional writing capabilities.
  • Technical Software Literacy: Advanced proficiency across Microsoft Office and Google Workspace ecosystems, with specialized expertise in presentation applications (Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint) and dynamic spreadsheet architecture.

Preferred Qualifications: Holding an advanced academic degree (such as a Master of Science in Finance, Master of Accountancy, MBA, or higher) from a recognized, reputable institution.
