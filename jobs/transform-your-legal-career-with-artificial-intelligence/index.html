Unpacking the Role: What Does a Pro Bono Counsel at Mercor Do?

The core objective of this remote, independent contractor position is to evaluate, critique, and refine AI-generated legal analyses dealing with civil legal services and access-to-justice matters. Millions of Americans face civil legal crises—ranging from housing instability and unfair debt collection to family law disputes and foreclosures—without adequate representation. AI models have massive potential to help close this justice gap by providing clear, structured legal guidance and educational resources. However, automated systems can occasionally miss subtle statutory nuances, procedural nuances, or regional jurisdiction rules.

As a Pro Bono Counsel on Mercor, your day-to-day responsibilities directly impact the reliability and safety of these AI tools:

  • Review AI-Generated Analyses: Carefully analyze complex legal reasoning generated by advanced artificial intelligence models across diverse civil practice domains.
  • Evaluate Scenario-Based Legal Content: Assess real-world case simulations involving housing disputes, family law, consumer rights, foreclosure defense, and debt relief.
  • Assess Procedural Guidance: Ensure that the guidance provided by the AI is practical, accurate, and aligned with standard U.S. legal procedures.
  • Identify Inaccuracies & Gaps: Pinpoint legal errors, omissions, or blind spots concerning access-to-justice considerations.
  • Deliver Structured Evaluations: Provide concise, highly professional written feedback to help engineers and researchers upgrade AI model performance.

Professional Freedom: This remote position offers total schedule flexibility. You can execute evaluations around your existing practice, consulting commitments, or personal schedule while receiving reliable weekly disbursements via Stripe or Wise.

Qualifications and Ideal Candidate Background

Because these AI models assist with critical, sensitive civil law matters, the standards for admission into this project are rigorous. Mercor seeks candidates who combine comprehensive legal training with a proven dedication to public interest or civil practice.

Required Qualifications:

  • Juris Doctor (JD): A degree from an accredited U.S. law school is mandatory.
  • Active U.S. Bar Admission: Candidates must hold an active license to practice law in at least one U.S. jurisdiction.
  • Pro Bono Leadership: Demonstrated experience managing, coordinating, or actively participating in pro bono legal initiatives, legal aid clinics, or volunteer attorney programs.
  • Civil Practice Expertise: Deep familiarity with day-to-day civil legal challenges experienced by everyday individuals and vulnerable populations.
  • Exceptional Legal Writing: Superior analytical, writing, and editorial skills to clearly articulate legal feedback.

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Prior formal experience serving as Pro Bono Counsel, a Pro Bono Partner, or legal clinic director.
  • Experience organizing community legal clinics, supervising junior attorneys, or managing volunteer legal networks.
  • Multi-disciplinary expertise covering various civil practice areas (e.g., housing, landlord-tenant law, consumer protection, bankruptcy, family law).
  • Familiarity with emerging legal technologies, legal tech platforms, or generative artificial intelligence workflows.
