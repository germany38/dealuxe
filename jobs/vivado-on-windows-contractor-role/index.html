To qualify for this lucrative contract, candidates must meet rigorous technical prerequisites regarding hardware capabilities and software environment access:

  • Operating System: A dedicated personal computer running Windows with Vivado pre-installed and ready to launch.
  • Display & Screenshot Resolution Standards: You must be capable of capturing high-fidelity screenshots exceeding 2.5 megapixels via one of two approved options:

    • Option B (Dual Monitor Setup): Two displays positioned side-by-side and captured as a single sprawling image (minimum two 1920x1080 displays combined).
