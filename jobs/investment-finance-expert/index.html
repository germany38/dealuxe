The Critical Need for Real-World Financial Expertise in AI

As artificial intelligence systems evolve to handle corporate decision-making, automated auditing, and market research, generalist datasets fall drastically short. A foundational LLM can easily generate generic textbook definitions of a discounted cash flow (DCF) model or leveraged buyout (LBO), but when confronted with nuanced deal structures, aggressive valuation assumptions, capital stack optimizations, or distressed debt restructurings, standard models routinely hallucinate or produce economically flawed outputs.

In finance, a flawed calculation or weak analytical reasoning can cost millions. To solve this, micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By collaborating with professionals who have spent years inside prominent financial institutions executing live transactions, micro1 translates real-world market acumen into high-quality training data, interactive evaluations, and fine-tuning feedback loops. No prior software engineering background is required—your deep domain knowledge is the exact catalyst needed to train models to reason like top-tier investment professionals.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • AI Output Assessment: Rigorously review, test, and assess model outputs pertaining to complex company valuations, financial modeling, capital markets activity, and sophisticated investment analysis.

  • Error & Hallucination Flagging: Identify subtle technical errors, weak logical reasoning, and unrealistic financial assumptions in AI responses.
  • Domain Feedback Generation: Provide precise, actionable domain feedback that directly refines and shapes how AI agents process financial data.
