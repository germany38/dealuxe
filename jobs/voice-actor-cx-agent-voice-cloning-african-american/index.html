Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For voice actors, narrators, and individuals blessed with clear, expressive voices, this engagement offers a streamlined and rewarding project structure. The primary objective is to contribute high-quality vocal samples that allow an AI research lab to build a sophisticated customer experience agent voice model.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Script Execution: Record diverse voice samples across multiple script genres, including conversational dialogues, narrative pieces, and instructional customer service scenarios.
  • Vocal Expressiveness: Deliver clear, natural, and highly engaging speech with precise command over tone, pacing, emotional variance, and standard pronunciation.
  • Consistency Maintenance: Ensure rigid consistency in voice quality, accent, and delivery parameters across distinct recording blocks.
  • Guideline Adherence: Follow exact recording guidelines covering acoustic environment specifications, microphone placement, pop filter usage, and file formatting requirements.
  • Dynamic Variation: Perform multiple alternate takes with varying degrees of emotion, inflection, emphasis, and style as demanded by the client scripts.

Candidate Requirements & Setup Expectations

Because the output directly trains a production-grade enterprise voice model, applicants must meet specific professional standards regarding vocal quality and recording equipment.

Essential Requirements:
  • Regional & Cultural Background: USA-based voice actor with a natural African American English delivery.
  • Vocal Clarity: A naturally clear, expressive, and dynamic voice (prior professional voice acting or narration experience is a strong plus, though talented newcomers are welcome).
  • Recording Environment: Access to a professional or near-professional home recording setup, including a quality microphone, quiet acoustic environment, and pop filter.
  • Linguistic Precision: Superior command of intonation, diction, and emotional range.
  • Commitment: Availability for a single ~4-hour initial recording session, with potential for a follow-up session of similar length.
  • Consent to Cloning: Full awareness and agreement that your voice will be cloned exclusively for the client's internal CX AI Agent (with strict guarantees that recordings will never be sold, licensed, or reused outside this specific scope).

Flexibility and transparency are core hallmarks of contracting through Mercor. This project is structured to respect your time while providing elite compensation:
