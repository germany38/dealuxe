Understanding the Role: What Do Synthetic and Process Chemists Do at Mercor?

In chemical research and manufacturing, the boundary between safe, innovative scaling and high-risk applications can be razor-thin. The same synthetic route that scales up a vital pharmaceutical compound can underpin regulated structures; the same impurity profile that guarantees a clean synthesis batch also reveals precise parameters of production. For artificial intelligence models, recognizing this delicate line is critical.

If an AI model encounters a technical question regarding reaction kinetics, impurity control, or scale-up optimization, it faces a major challenge. A model that refuses a routine professional question is failing as badly as one that incorrectly answers a query it should reject. Because generalist annotators cannot make these critical distinctions, Mercor relies on seasoned chemistry practitioners.

Your Core Responsibilities Include:

  • Prompt Authoring: Write challenging, single-turn prompts in your specialized domain, meticulously categorized across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Response Evaluation: Evaluate model-generated responses against established safety and technical policy standards, judging whether each output was handled with appropriate rigor.
  • Reference Answer Generation: Draft comprehensive reference answers showing what a correct, safe response looks like, complete with the detailed technical reasoning behind the decision.

💡 The Dual-Use Challenge: Your primary goal is ensuring that frontier models can distinguish between routine professional industrial questions and inquiries probing for dangerous or restricted methodologies.

Professional Backgrounds We Are Looking For

Mercor is not looking for generalist content moderators or theoretical science hobbyists. They need active or experienced chemists who have scaled reactions, run real routes, and worked hands-on across laboratory and industrial settings. Ideal backgrounds and experience areas include:

  • Synthetic Route Design: Extensive experience in route development within pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, or fine chemicals.
  • Process Chemistry & Reaction Engineering: Deep familiarity with optimization, reaction kinetics, and impurity control measures.
  • Scale-Up Operations: Practical experience moving processes from the bench to pilot plants and full industrial manufacturing, alongside associated safety reviews.
  • Regulatory & Precursor Chemistry: Knowledge regarding precursor sourcing, controlled-substance registration, or regulatory compliance frameworks.
  • GMP Environment Troubleshooting: Method development and troubleshooting within Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) or equivalent quality-controlled environments.

Beyond Technical Depth: The Demand for Rigorous Written Reasoning

Having brilliant lab experience is only half the equation. This role is exceptionally writing-intensive. Every single judgment, classification, and grading decision you make must be accompanied by a clear, structured written rationale that can be easily followed by non-specialist auditors and engineers.

Candidates who possess a documented history of technical writing, published peer-reviewed research, or expert witness experience will find their background translating seamlessly to this workflow. Additionally, because the project involves reviewing misuse scenarios in your field for sustained periods, Mercor provides full briefings and allows experts to pause or step away at any point without penalty.
