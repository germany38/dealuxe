Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For clinicians, medical researchers, pharmaceutical scientists, and biomedical experts seeking high-yield, flexible remote income, understanding the core expectations of this role is crucial. The position demands senior-level professional judgment to audit, grade, and refine complex AI-generated work products—ranging from clinical trial documents and research spreadsheets to comprehensive slide decks—ensuring that automated systems meet the highest standards of scientific and medical integrity.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and benchmark AI-generated documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks focused on clinical trial workflows, biomedical research, and pharmaceutical developments against stringent quality rubrics.
  • Scientific Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify factual errors, methodological flaws, statistical misinterpretations, and presentation mistakes in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Clinical Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to train and align frontier AI models with medical best practices and regulatory standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex clinical and pharmacological terminology, data analyses, and scientific arguments generated by artificial intelligence are precise, objective, and clinically sound.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these roles are engineered to train state-of-the-art AI systems utilized by leading healthcare and research organizations worldwide, the qualification standards are intentionally set to attract seasoned domain authorities rather than generalist contractors.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Demonstrated professional background in clinical practice, biomedical research, pharmaceutical development, or a related healthcare/life sciences discipline.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills.
  • Software Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific emphasis on advanced slide presentation tools (Google Slides and PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s, PharmD, PhD, MD, or equivalent) from a reputable academic or medical institution.
