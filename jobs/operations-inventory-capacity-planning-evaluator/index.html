Comprehensive Breakdown of Job Details and Responsibilities

For operations professionals looking to diversify their income streams or transition into flexible consulting, understanding the exact scope of this role is crucial. Evaluators do not perform manual data entry or basic support tasks; rather, they serve as senior auditors of machine-generated intelligence, ensuring that AI systems adhere strictly to rigorous enterprise standards.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, audit, and grade complex AI-generated documents, operational spreadsheets, inventory optimization models, and executive slide decks.
  • Rigorous Accuracy Checking: Scrutinize outputs against domain-specific rubrics to catch mathematical errors, flawed logistics frameworks, infeasible production schedules, and presentation inconsistencies.
  • Structured Feedback Delivery: Formulate clear, concise, and professional written feedback explaining why specific AI-generated recommendations succeed or fail from an operations management perspective.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that automated workflows reflecting supply chain planning, EOQ (Economic Order Quantity) modeling, throughput optimization, and capacity forecasting match real-world best practices.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these engagements directly shape artificial intelligence architectures utilized by major global enterprises, the selection criteria are stringent. Mercor seeks out verified domain specialists rather than generalist freelancers.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • 5+ Years of Professional Experience: Demonstrated career history working directly in operations management, inventory control, capacity planning, supply chain analytics, or manufacturing logistics.
  • Communication Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English, with exceptional technical writing and analytical expression.
  • Digital Tool Mastery: Advanced proficiency across Microsoft Office and Google Workspace ecosystems, with heavy emphasis on presentation tools like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Advantage: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s, MBA, or Ph.D.) from a reputable academic institution in supply chain management, industrial engineering, operations research, or business administration.
