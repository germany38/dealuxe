Comprehensive Overview: What Does a PDF Annotation Expert Do?

Artificial intelligence models struggle significantly with document understanding in regional languages like Bengali, especially when confronted with complex layouts, handwriting, multi-column newspapers, and dense tables. Mercor bridges this gap by gathering human-authored structural maps and transcriptions.

As a Bengali PDF Annotation Expert, your work ensures that frontier AI models learn to parse real-world Bengali literature, exams, forms, and newspapers accurately.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Structural Bounding: Identify, bound, and classify every meaningful component on a PDF page (titles, paragraphs, tables, diagrams, equations, and question-answer fields).
  • Reading Order Mapping: Assign exact indices that reflect the true reading flow across columns and document sections.
  • Faithful Transcription: Transcribe text character-for-character in authentic Bengali script, avoiding transliteration and ensuring diacritic precision.
  • Peer Review: Conduct thorough second-expert quality reviews on teammate task submissions to maintain pristine dataset accuracy.

💡 Earnings Protection & Performance Bonus: When you successfully refer candidates or scale your working hours, you unlock substantial earning multipliers. Note that you get paid once workers are hired, and you earn a 20% bonus for each hour they work through structured partner networks!
