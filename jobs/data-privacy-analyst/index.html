Detailed Scope of Work

As a Data Privacy Analyst, your daily contributions directly safeguard sensitive information while training advanced AI models. Your primary responsibilities include:

  • Spot-Checking Datasets: Evaluating transformed datasets to identify and validate proper redaction, anonymization, and safeguarding of PII.
  • Document Auditing: Performing granular document reviews to verify that strict data privacy standards are uniformly maintained.
  • Identifying Vulnerabilities: Assessing data collections for missed or improperly handled sensitive details, providing clear, actionable feedback.
  • Reporting Discrepancies: Documenting observations and reporting anomalies accurately utilizing specialized evaluation platforms and templates.
