Medical AI & Clinical Expert Opportunity

An in-depth exploration of how seasoned dermatologists can bridge clinical expertise and frontier artificial intelligence models to drive safe, evidence-based healthcare innovation.

Artificial intelligence is no longer confined to theoretical computer science research; it is actively reshaping critical industries, with healthcare standing at the forefront of this transformation. From diagnostic imaging assistance to automated clinical summaries, large language models (LLMs) and generative vision models hold immense potential to augment medical professionals. However, deploying AI systems in clinical environments demands extraordinary precision. Medical miscalculations carry severe real-world consequences, making expert human validation indispensable.

Recognizing this critical need, Turing—the world’s leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs and enterprise AI solutions—is spearheading initiatives to align advanced machine learning models with strict clinical standards. Through specialized roles like the Medical Expert – Dermatology, Turing engages top-tier medical specialists to evaluate, critique, and refine AI-generated medical outputs. If you are an experienced dermatologist based in India looking to influence the trajectory of global healthcare technology, this comprehensive guide outlines everything you need to know about the opportunity, daily responsibilities, qualifications, and onboarding framework.

About Turing: Powering Frontier AI

Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Turing operates at the bleeding edge of artificial intelligence development. Turing supports customers across two major pillars:

  1. Accelerating Frontier Research: Equipping leading AI labs with high-quality domain data, advanced training pipelines, and elite human experts specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, multimodality, and medical sciences.
  2. Enterprise AI Transformation: Translating theoretical proofs of concept into robust, reliable enterprise intelligence systems that drive measurable impact on the P&L.

By partnering with Turing, medical specialists step outside traditional clinical walls to help train the next generation of safe, reliable diagnostic tools.

Role Overview: Medical Expert – Dermatology

The Medical Expert – Dermatology role is designed for seasoned specialists who want to evaluate and enhance LLM outputs within the dermatology domain. As an expert evaluator, you will assess AI-generated clinical content for dermatological accuracy, safety, and relevance.

Specifically, India-based dermatologists in this engagement will review dermatology images using a structured online evaluation tool. The work involves assessing image quality and diagnostic usability; identifying technical flaws such as blur, poor lighting, inaccurate color balance, and suboptimal framing; and—where applicable—identifying likely skin conditions, assigning confidence ratings, and documenting rigorous clinical rationales.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like:

  • LLM Text Review: Rigorously review AI-generated medical text for factual accuracy, harmfulness, and completeness, with a concentrated focus on dermatologic conditions.
  • Case Evaluation: Evaluate AI-suggested diagnoses, treatment plans, and clinical summaries for complex dermatologic cases.
  • Expert Feedback Provision: Provide structured, actionable feedback to improve the medical integrity and safety of model outputs.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Collaborate directly with AI researchers, engineers, and fellow clinicians to align model performance with evidence-based dermatology standards.
  • Image Annotation: Examine clinical dermatology images via web-based annotation platforms, utilizing zoom and diagnostic tools to complete structured evaluation questionnaires.

You Are a Fit If: Required Skills & Experience

To maintain the highest caliber of scientific integrity, Turing enforces strict qualification standards for this engagement. Candidates must meet the following mandatory criteria:

  • Educational Background: MBBS degree with a successfully completed residency in Dermatology.
  • Clinical Experience: A minimum of 5 years of post-residency professional experience actively practicing dermatology.
  • Geographic Location: Must be based in India (mandatory).
  • Attention to Detail: Exceptional visual attention to detail, capable of discerning subtle colorations, skin textures, and morphological nuances.
  • Content Resilience: Comfortable reviewing sensitive, graphic, or varied clinical medical images.
  • Technical Proficiency: Technically savvy—capable of seamlessly navigating web-based annotation platforms, utilizing image manipulation tools (zoom, contrast adjustment), following intricate annotation guidelines, and completing structured questionnaires with precision.
  • Preferred Background: Prior experience in medical content review, clinical research, data annotation, or AI/ML model evaluation is highly advantageous.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

Engaging with Turing offers unique professional advantages that extend far beyond traditional clinical practice:

  • Frontier Innovation: Work directly at the intersection of medicine and artificial intelligence.
  • Remote Flexibility: Enjoy a fully remote work environment that accommodates flexible scheduling.
  • Industry Insights: Gain rare exposure to advanced large language models and firsthand insight into how state-of-the-art AI systems are trained and fine-tuned.

Engagement Details & Commitment

Before applying, candidates should review the structural commitments and contract terms of this 12-week assignment:

  • Engagement Type: Contractor assignment / freelance role (note that this contract does not include traditional medical or paid leave benefits).
  • Commitment Required: At least 4 hours per day and a total of 40 hours per week, with a mandatory 4-hour daily overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST).
  • Duration: 12 weeks of structured collaboration.

Onboarding, Stability, and Task Progression

Upon acceptance into the program, successful candidates undergo a comprehensive onboarding process designed to familiarize them with Turing's proprietary evaluation guidelines and software tools. To ensure platform stability and unlock long-term task access, contractors are required to complete initial onboarding and successfully execute their first 10 hours of work. Meeting this initial milestone stabilizes your contractor profile within the system and grants you full access to ongoing task queues and advanced project streams.

The Interview & Evaluation Process

Turing maintains a competitive selection process to ensure only top-tier medical talent joins their AI acceleration network. The evaluation process consists of:

  1. Initial Shortlisting: Screening based on professional qualifications, resume review, and academic credentials.
  2. Assessment Scores: Completion of domain-specific evaluation assessments testing clinical accuracy and attention to detail.
  3. Interview Round: One comprehensive interview round with Turing's clinical and operational leads.
