Technical Requirements: Hardware and Display Specifications

Because visual AI training involves high-resolution assets and detailed screen capture verification, Mercor enforces clear, strict technical minimums for applicants. Review these requirements carefully before submitting your application:

  • Operating System & Application: A Windows PC with Adobe Photoshop installed and ready to launch immediately.
  • Screenshot Capability: You must be able to capture screenshots exceeding 2.5 megapixels. This requirement can be satisfied via two options:
  • Option A (Single High-Resolution Monitor): A single monitor meeting standard aspect ratios at or above specified thresholds, such as 2560x1440 (QHD), 3840x2160 (4K), 3440x1440 (Ultrawide), or matching equivalents across 16:9, 16:10, 3:2, and 4:3 ratios.
  • Option B (Dual-Monitor Setup): Two displays side-by-side captured as a single spanning image, where two 1920x1080+ monitors qualify.
