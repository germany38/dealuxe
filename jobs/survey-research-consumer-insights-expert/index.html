Role Overview & Key Responsibilities

Mercor is partnering with a leading AI research organization to engage experienced professionals in survey research, market research, consumer insights, and behavioral science. The project focuses on evaluating and improving sophisticated survey-based research for cutting-edge AI systems.

What You’ll Do:

  • Review and evaluate surveys for methodological quality, clarity, and fitness for purpose.
  • Assess questions for issues such as leading language, ambiguity, double-barreled items, and response bias.
  • Evaluate screeners, survey flows, skip logic, scales, and overall questionnaire structure.
  • Help evaluate AI-generated survey content and research reasoning against professional research benchmarks.

Ideal Qualifications:

  • 3+ years of professional experience in survey research, market research, consumer insights, behavioral science, UX research, or related fields.
  • Strong understanding of questionnaire design, sampling strategies, and common sources of error/bias.
  • Familiarity with platforms like Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, Alchemer, or Forsta is helpful.

Contract & Payment Terms:

Why Survey Research and Consumer Insights Matter More Than Ever

When organizations deploy questionnaires, public opinion polls, or customer feedback loops, the margin between actionable market intelligence and costly misinformation is razor-thin. A poorly designed survey littered with leading language, double-barreled questions, or improper scaling can derail a multi-million-dollar product launch or distort political forecasting. As artificial intelligence models begin generating, interpreting, and synthesizing survey data autonomously, verifying methodological integrity becomes paramount.

Professionals with backgrounds in quantitative market research, UX evaluation, and polling are uniquely positioned to audit these workflows. They possess the critical eye needed to spot sampling anomalies, evaluate screener efficacy, and analyze respondent burden. By stepping into advisory and evaluation roles within tech ecosystems, these experts ensure that next-generation automated reasoning systems adhere to rigorous scientific standards. It is intellectual work of the highest caliber—one that demands genuine professional judgment rather than routine administrative execution.
