Why This Role Exists: Training AI in Under-Resourced Languages

English

Document understanding breaks down fastest in regional languages that parsing and vision-language models rarely see. This project builds high-grade training datasets specifically for Odiya and other Indic scripts. Each task takes real, publicly available PDF pages—drawn from newspapers, textbooks, examinations, forms, manuals, and brochures—and builds a complete structural map paired with faithful human transcription.

Detailed Responsibilities of an Odiya Annotation Expert

English
  • Review Task Pages: Check pre-uploaded PDF pages to ensure they are in Odiya, legible, contain real content, and show no personal details.
  • Annotate Structure: Identify and bound meaningful regions (titles, paragraphs, tables, diagrams, formulas) and assign component types and reading-order indexes.
  • Record Relationships: Link each region to its corresponding figure, table, or parent component identifier.
  • Faithful Transcription: Reproduce all text exactly as it appears in Odiya script, including handwritten entries.
  • Peer Review: Participate in end-to-end reviews of colleague submissions to guarantee absolute quality control.
