Mastering Managed Care Contracts and Underpayment Recovery in the Era of Artificial Intelligence

The financial sustainability of modern healthcare providers heavily depends on meticulous revenue cycle management, particularly when dealing with complex managed care agreements. Hospitals, health systems, and large physician practices lose millions annually due to subtle payment variances, overlooked contractual carve-outs, and unrecovered underpayments from commercial insurers, Medicare Advantage, and Medicaid managed care organizations. Untangling these multi-page contracts requires profound institutional knowledge and sharp financial acumen.

Today, artificial intelligence is transforming how healthcare organizations audit claims and monitor contract compliance. Leading AI research labs are actively developing advanced machine learning models designed to automate payment variance detection and optimize net revenue. To make these systems robust, accurate, and clinically viable, tech innovators partner with elite domain experts through platforms like Mercor. The open role of Underpayment & Managed-care Contract Specialist offers seasoned healthcare finance professionals an unparalleled opportunity to shape frontier AI systems while earning an exceptional $85 per hour on a fully remote contract basis.

Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For revenue cycle veterans, contract analysts, and managed care negotiators seeking high-impact advisory work, this role bridges deep healthcare finance operations with cutting-edge artificial intelligence development. Your primary function is to evaluate, validate, and train AI models designed to detect payment discrepancies and enforce complex payer contract terms.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Variance Discovery and Recovery: Identify, analyze, and recover underpayments and payment variances across commercial, Medicare Advantage, and Medicaid managed care contracts.
  • AI Output Evaluation: Assess AI-generated underpayment detection alerts, contract compliance outputs, and payment variance analyses for absolute precision.
  • Contract Interpretation: Deeply interpret intricate payer contract provisions, including fee schedules, carve-outs, outlier clauses, and specialized payment methodologies to validate claim adjudications.
  • Modelling and Reconciliation: Conduct thorough contract modeling and payment reconciliation to identify systematic underpayment trends and patterns.
  • Claims Submission & Correspondence: Formulate and submit formal underpayment recovery claims and correspondence to insurance payers.
  • Cross-Departmental Collaboration: Partner directly with managed care contracting teams to uncover contract language gaps and identify future negotiation opportunities.
  • KPI Tracking: Monitor critical underpayment recovery metrics, including identified underpayment dollars, overall recovery rates, and payer response efficiencies.
  • Regulatory and Contractual Compliance: Ensure full adherence to payer contract stipulations, HIPAA guidelines, and timely filing windows for underpayment appeals.
  • Dataset Annotation: Provide structured, expert feedback and annotate complex AI outputs to strengthen and refine AI training datasets.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because the artificial intelligence tools you evaluate will directly impact healthcare financial compliance and revenue recovery, rigorous professional qualifications are essential for applicants.

Essential Requirements:
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of dedicated experience in underpayment recovery, payment variance analysis, or managed care contracting, backed by proven contract interpretation expertise.
  • Reimbursement Knowledge: Comprehensive mastery of diverse payer payment methodologies, including DRG-based systems, per diem structures, percentage-of-billed charges, and complex fee schedules.
  • Technical Skills: Strong background in contract modeling, payment reconciliation, and payer dispute resolution mechanisms.
  • System Proficiency: Familiarity with managed care contract management software and specialized payment variance detection tools.
  • Analytical Fluency: Advanced proficiency with Excel-based financial modeling and overarching revenue cycle analytics platforms.
  • Communication Skills: Exceptional written and verbal English communication abilities, paired with meticulous attention to detail.

Preferred Qualifications: Relevant certifications such as CHFP, CRCR, or specialized managed care contracting credentials; hands-on experience with enterprise contract management systems (e.g., Experian Health, Recondo); executive-level presentation skills; and familiarity evaluating AI content.
