Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For finance professionals exploring alternative revenue streams or full-time remote contracting, understanding the precise day-to-day expectations of this role is crucial. Evaluators act as the final arbiters of quality for automated financial content, ensuring that artificial intelligence systems meet rigorous corporate, mathematical, and reporting standards.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation & Grading: Systematically review and assess AI-generated financial models, spreadsheets, financial statements, strategic corporate documents, and executive slide decks.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep subject-matter expertise in financial planning and analysis to identify mathematical errors, formula miscalculations, analytical omissions, and presentation flaws.
  • Constructive Written Feedback: Formulate clear, structured, and actionable critiques to guide frontier AI models toward enterprise-grade precision and clarity.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that automated outputs conform to modern corporate finance best practices, standard accounting principles, and rigorous valuation frameworks.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these positions are built to train state-of-the-art AI systems deployed by top-tier global enterprises, the qualification bar is set to attract seasoned domain specialists rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven professional background working in financial planning and analysis (FP&A), corporate finance, investment banking, strategic planning, or corporate accounting.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with pristine written communication skills.
  • Technical Software Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in financial modeling tools and professional slide presentation packages (Google Slides / PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications: Holding an advanced degree (Master’s in Finance, MBA, or higher) from a reputable institution.
