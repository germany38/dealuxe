About Turing: Driving the Global AI Ecosystem

Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Turing operates as a trusted partner for elite AI labs and Fortune 500 enterprises. The organization facilitates cutting-edge technological development through two distinct pillars:

  • Accelerating Frontier Research: Providing high-quality datasets, advanced training pipelines, and elite researchers specializing in coding, multi-step reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and AI agents.
  • Enterprise Transformation: Helping corporations translate raw generative AI models into secure, reliable proprietary intelligence that delivers measurable P&L impact.

Role Overview: AI Prompt & Policy Specialist

The Prompt & Verifier position sits at the vital intersection of prompt engineering, policy formulation, and digital safety. Modern AI systems do not operate in a vacuum; they interact dynamically with external tools, software plugins, APIs, and end-users across diverse geographies.

In this role, your objective is to design, evaluate, and optimize how artificial intelligence models handle complex tool workflows—ranging from communication apps like Slack to payment processing platforms like PayPal. Success demands a hybrid skill set: foundational coding and data literacy paired with sharp critical reasoning and comprehensive policy awareness.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

  • Prompt Design & Refinement: Crafting, reviewing, and optimizing structured instructions and operational prompts for AI systems interacting with external services.
  • API & JSON Evaluation: Reading API schemas and JSON arguments to verify parameter correctness and ensure seamless tool integration.
  • SQL-Based Verification: Applying basic SQL queries to validate data, investigate anomalies, and support behavior analysis.
  • Policy Creation & Maintenance: Documenting and maintaining guidelines governing content handling, tool workflows, and safety standards.
  • Safety & Compliance Filtering: Monitoring AI outputs to detect harmful, abusive, or policy-violating content using robust safety frameworks.
  • Multilingual Validation: Ensuring prompts and model responses maintain clarity, nuance, and accuracy across international locales.

Required Skills and Professional Experience

To thrive in this rigorous environment, applicants must demonstrate a blend of technical aptitude and operational policy experience. Key requirements include:

  • Professional Background: 3 to 6 years of cumulative experience in AI operations, content review, quality assurance (QA), product operations, technical policy, or advanced technical support.
  • API & JSON Literacy: Demonstrated capability to read technical schemas, interpret argument structures, and verify proper parameter passing.
  • SQL Competency: Ability to execute simple queries to extract data, investigate operational issues, or perform basic verification.
  • Prompt Engineering Experience: Proven background in authoring structured, clear instructions for automated systems or generative AI models.
  • Policy Authoring Skills: Experience drafting, interpreting, and applying operational guidelines within real-world environments.
  • Critical Reasoning & Attention to Detail: Exceptional judgment when evaluating ambiguous edge cases and checking compliance against strict technical schemas.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Engaging with Turing as a contractor unlocks unique professional advantages, including:

  • Complete Location Independence: Enjoy a fully remote work environment from eligible global regions.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Work alongside industry pioneers on high-impact projects featuring top-tier Large Language Model (LLM) companies.

Engagement Details & Commitments

Before applying, candidates should note the specific structural parameters of this assignment:

  • Time Commitment: Minimum of 4 hours per day and at least 20 hours per week, with a mandatory 4-hour overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST).
  • Contract Structure: Independent contractor assignment (structured without medical or paid leave benefits).
  • Duration: 3-month initial contract, adjustable depending on project performance and client scope.

Interview Process and Onboarding Stability

Turing maintains a rigorous evaluation standard to ensure optimal alignment between talent and client needs. The evaluation pipeline features a 60-minute technical interview designed to assess problem-solving skills, technical literacy, and critical reasoning.

Upon passing the interview stage, candidates undergo complete onboarding. To ensure long-term stability and unlock access to a broader volume of platform tasks, contractors must successfully complete an initial 10 hours of work. This stabilization period verifies workflow compatibility and sets the foundation for a successful, long-term engagement.
