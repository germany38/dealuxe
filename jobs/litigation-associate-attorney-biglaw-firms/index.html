The Evolution of Legal AI: Why Your Expertise Matters

For decades, machine learning models were trained on publicly available web content, books, and general articles. However, when deployed in professional settings, generalist models frequently stumble over nuanced legal doctrines, contractual ambiguities, and multi-jurisdictional regulatory frameworks. In high-stakes litigation, corporate restructuring, and capital markets transactions, errors carry substantial commercial and legal repercussions.

micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By engaging litigation associates and senior practitioners from prominent law firms, micro1 transforms real-world legal experience into structured training data, benchmark evaluations, and iterative feedback loops. No prior background in software coding or machine learning is necessary—your deep, practical command of legal principles is what drives the technology forward.
