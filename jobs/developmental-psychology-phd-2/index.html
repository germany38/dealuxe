The Intersection of Developmental Psychology and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

For years, training artificial intelligence relied primarily on vast corpora of unstructured internet text. However, as AI systems are deployed in educational tools, interactive agents, and psychological support frameworks, generic models frequently fail when confronted with the nuances of human cognitive development, emotional regulation, and linguistic acquisition. Without expert human guidance, AI models generate outputs that lack developmental validity or psychological integrity.

micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI by connecting leading laboratories with elite academic and research professionals. In this role, no prior coding or software engineering experience is required. Your doctoral-level training in developmental psychology is the exact domain knowledge needed to teach AI how humans learn, grow, and interact across diverse environments.
