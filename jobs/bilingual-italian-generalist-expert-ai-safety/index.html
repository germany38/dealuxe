Comprehensive Breakdown: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

Artificial intelligence models are scaling at an unprecedented rate across the globe. However, raw computational horsepower alone cannot ensure these systems behave safely, logically, and ethically. Leading AI research labs and enterprise technology companies require sophisticated human oversight to evaluate complex models, flag potential risks, and ensure that responses align with human values and cultural realities. This is precisely where your specialized linguistic talents become indispensable.

As a Bilingual Italian Generalist Expert working with Mercor, your primary responsibility is to review, audit, and strengthen how advanced AI architectures handle sensitive, technical, or multi-layered topics in Italian. You will act as the crucial bridge between raw machine learning output and human common sense.

Core Daily Responsibilities:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Author intricate, high-level prompts in Italian across a diverse variety of sensitive and technical subject domains.
  • Structured Output Classification: Apply rigorous, standardized classification frameworks to evaluate model responses, conversations, and decision paths.
  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: Detect adversarial phrasings, edge cases, and escalation patterns, documenting your analytical reasoning clearly.
  • Cultural Alignment Tuning: Guarantee that idioms, colloquialisms, regional variations, and cultural nuances in Italian match authentic human conversation standards.

💡 No Technical Background Needed: You do not need a background in computer programming or machine learning. Mercor equips every contractor with thorough workflow training. Critical thinking, exceptional writing skills, and native fluency are your most valuable assets.
