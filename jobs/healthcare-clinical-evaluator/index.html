The Rise of AI in Medicine: Why Healthcare Evaluators Are in Massive Demand

Artificial intelligence models are increasingly being deployed across healthcare systems, pharmaceutical research labs, and clinical diagnostics. However, raw algorithms cannot possess true clinical judgment. To ensure that AI-generated patient education material, treatment summary documents, and medical slide decks adhere to rigorous scientific standards, leading AI labs rely on elite human domain experts.

As a Healthcare and Clinical Evaluator at Mercor, you stand at the vanguard of health technology. Your mission is to audit automated medical work products, identifying subtle clinical inaccuracies, logical gaps, or presentational flaws that software alone cannot detect. You are safeguarding the integrity of digital healthcare intelligence.

Core Daily Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Auditing: Evaluate complex AI-generated documents, data spreadsheets, and slide presentations against stringent domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Factual & Scientific Verification: Scrutinize outputs for clinical accuracy, evidence-based reasoning, and adherence to established medical and healthcare protocols.
  • Structural & Aesthetic Review: Identify presentation errors, visual formatting issues, and structural inconsistencies in medical slide decks (Google Slides / PowerPoint).
  • Actionable Feedback Delivery: Draft clear, structured, and professional written feedback to refine and train the underlying machine learning architectures.

💡 Professional Autonomy: This is a fully remote, independent contractor position that accommodates your existing professional schedule. Whether you prefer working early mornings, late evenings, or specific blocks during the week, you maintain complete control over your hours.

Because the stakes are exceptionally high in clinical and healthcare data evaluation, Mercor maintains rigorous vetting standards to ensure client deliverables meet world-class standards. To qualify for this prestigious role, applicants must fulfill the following criteria:
