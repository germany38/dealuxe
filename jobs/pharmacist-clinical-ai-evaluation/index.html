Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For pharmacists looking to pivot into health tech advisory, clinical data annotation, or flexible remote contract work, this position offers an intellectually stimulating environment. As a clinical AI reviewer, your primary objective is to evaluate, correct, and annotate AI-generated outputs, ensuring that automated systems meet the highest standards of safety, accuracy, and clinical soundness.

Core Daily Responsibilities Include:

  • Clinical Inquiry Resolution: Answer complex medication-related clinical questions by synthesizing patient context, authoritative source materials, and strict drug labeling guidelines.
  • AI Output Auditing: Critically review AI-generated responses and recommendations for clinical accuracy, safety, clarity, completeness, and evidence support.
  • Correction & Annotation: Correct inaccurate or incomplete algorithmic outputs, providing structured annotations and precise written feedback to refine model training datasets.
  • Safety Escalation: Identify, flag, and escalate cases involving insufficient clinical evidence, conflicting guidelines, ambiguous patient context, or potential patient safety hazards.
  • Quality Calibration: Apply complex project rubrics and quality standards consistently while actively participating in ongoing calibration and feedback peer sessions.

Representative Clinical & Pharmacological Topics

The workload spans a diverse spectrum of everyday and specialized pharmaceutical scenarios, requiring versatile clinical expertise across disciplines:

  • Medication indications and off-label evaluations
  • Complex dosing recommendations and adjustments
  • Contraindications and severe drug-drug interactions
  • Comprehensive patient treatment histories
  • Prior authorization criteria and payer rule structures
  • Review and interpretation of supporting clinical documentation

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these positions involve evaluating safety-critical medical outputs destined for real-world healthcare integration, the qualification criteria are designed to ensure absolute professional rigor.

Essential Requirements:
  • Licensure: Active, unencumbered US Pharmacist license (PharmD or equivalent degree).
  • Clinical Competency: Strong, demonstrated experience with medications, pharmacy operational workflows, and advanced clinical decision-making.
  • Expertise: Superior understanding of pharmacological drug interactions, contraindications, and evidence-based prescribing guidelines.
  • Communication Skills: Exceptional written communication capabilities with extraordinary attention to detail.
  • Methodology: Comfort following structured annotation guidelines and evaluation rubrics.
  • Availability: Ability to commit to 30–40 hours per week during the project lifecycle.

Preferred Backgrounds: Prior authorization or specialty pharmacy experience; managed care or medication access operations; familiarity with Epic, Cerner, or other major Electronic Health Record (EHR) platforms; background in clinical quality assurance; experience with AI evaluation or healthcare data annotation; and 340B or medication assistance program knowledge.
