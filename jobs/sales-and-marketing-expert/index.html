The Evolution of Go-To-Market Strategy: How Enterprise Sales and Marketing Experts Are Training Frontier AI Models

The landscape of enterprise software, go-to-market (GTM) strategy, and revenue operations (RevOps) is undergoing a massive paradigm shift. As artificial intelligence models expand their capabilities into complex business reasoning, corporate decision-making, and strategic enterprise sales, technology companies face a unique bottleneck: generic models lack the tactical nuance required to navigate Fortune 500 boardrooms, complex multi-stakeholder buying committees, and high-stakes procurement cycles. Training these models demands elite human practitioners who have spent years in the trenches closing million-dollar deals and executing enterprise-scale marketing campaigns.

Platforms like Mercor are bridging this critical gap by connecting world-class enterprise professionals with leading AI research labs. Roles such as the Sales and Marketing Expert provide seasoned GTM leaders, account executives, and marketing directors with a high-paying, flexible opportunity. Offering an impressive compensation package of $60 to $70 per hour on a fully remote contractor basis, this position allows you to monetize your executive-level operational background while directly helping to shape the future of artificial intelligence in commerce.

Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For senior revenue operators, enterprise account managers, and demand generation leaders seeking a lucrative intellectual pivot, understanding the core responsibilities of this role is essential. Contributors are tasked with building sophisticated evaluation workflows, designing realistic enterprise GTM scenarios, and formulating rigorous grading rubrics that reflect authentic executive judgment.

Key Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Enterprise GTM Scenario Design: Construct intricate enterprise sales scenarios spanning $1M+ Annual Contract Value (ACV) deals, complex multi-stakeholder buying committees, and lengthy procurement cycles typical of Fortune 500 accounts.
  • Strategic Marketing Frameworks: Build comprehensive marketing tasks addressing Fortune 500 brand strategy, enterprise Account-Based Marketing (ABM), large-scale demand generation, customer lifecycle management, and category positioning.
  • RevOps Integration: Develop advanced RevOps and go-to-market scenarios integrating industry-standard enterprise stacks such as Salesforce Enterprise, Marketo, 6sense, Gong, and Outreach.
  • Methodology Application: Apply proven enterprise sales methodologies including MEDDIC, Challenger, and Force Management to produce reference deal strategies, account plans, and executive narratives.
  • Rigorous Rubric Authorship: Author detailed scoring rubrics that reliably distinguish authentic enterprise go-to-market judgment from superficial, generic playbook recall.

Candidate Qualifications and Professional Background

Because these projects directly influence how AI systems interpret and execute high-level corporate strategies, the background requirements reflect senior-level professional accomplishment.

Must-Have Professional Requirements:
  • Industry Experience: 5+ years of active experience selling, marketing, or running Revenue Operations at a Fortune 500 enterprise software vendor (e.g., Salesforce, Oracle, ServiceNow, SAP, Workday, Microsoft, AWS) or inside an established Fortune 500 brand/marketing organization (e.g., P&G, JPMorgan, Unilever, PepsiCo).
  • Direct Accountability: Documented ownership of major F500 accounts, large-scale brand campaigns, or enterprise-wide demand generation programs.
  • Tool & Methodology Fluency: Deep working fluency in enterprise GTM software stacks and established sales methodologies, coupled with a practical understanding of how corporate budgets, legal reviews, and procurement workflows operate.
  • Curriculum & Training Background: Prior experience designing sales-enablement curricula, training content, or evaluation rubrics is considered a strong professional advantage.
