The Paradigm Shift: Why AI Evaluation Needs Enterprise Leadership

Building frontier artificial intelligence models requires immense collaboration between theoretical researchers, machine learning engineers, and specialized domain practitioners. As generative models and autonomous agents are deployed into mission-critical corporate environments, generic benchmarking is no longer sufficient. Organizations require rigorous evaluation systems, bespoke RL environments, and continuous human data feedback loops to ensure safety, alignment, and optimal performance.

micro1 bridges this gap by operating the premier human intelligence layer for artificial intelligence. By vetting elite experts and structuring scalable evaluation programs, micro1 enables labs to train models that reason effectively. However, scaling these high-value data pipelines requires sophisticated commercial leaders who can speak fluently with machine learning researchers, understand complex engineering constraints, and structure multi-million-dollar enterprise agreements. This is precisely where the Enterprise Client Partner steps in.

Core Responsibilities and Day-to-Day Scope:

  • Partnership Development: Source, cultivate, and close high-value commercial partnerships with leading AI labs, research organizations, and ML-driven enterprises.
  • Technical Discovery: Lead intricate technical discovery sessions across model evaluation workflows, reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) pipelines, and continuous model improvement loops.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Engage directly with research leads, machine learning engineers, and procurement executives to comprehend system-level constraints and specific evaluation metrics.
  • Translating Ambiguity into Programs: Take ambiguous research and engineering requirements and translate them into well-scoped human data generation, annotation, and evaluation programs.
  • Complex Deal Negotiation: Structure and negotiate complex, high-value commercial agreements involving custom data pipelines, benchmarking frameworks, and annotation at scale.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with product, research, and operations teams to guarantee that customer delivery aligns with technical expectations and roadmap milestones.
  • Market Intelligence: Provide clear signal on pipeline quality, deal risk, and market dynamics, feeding customer insights directly back into product direction and go-to-market strategy.

💡 Operating in High-Velocity Environments: Success in this role demands high agency and comfort operating in fast-moving, ambiguous settings. You will collaborate with some of the brightest minds in artificial intelligence, making structured, hypothesis-driven problem solving an essential daily habit.

Required Qualifications and Ideal Background

Because micro1 partners with top-tier AI institutions, vetting standards for core team roles are exceptionally high. Ideal candidates bring a proven track record of commercial success combined with technical fluency:

  • Technical Fluency: Ability to communicate fluently with machine learning engineers and research scientists. While you do not need to write production code, you must be able to hold your own in technical architectural discussions regarding model evaluation and RL environments.
  • Specialized Sales Experience: Demonstrated experience selling into AI/ML, developer tools, infrastructure, or deeply technical enterprise products.
  • Complex Deal Execution: A strong, verifiable track record of closing intricate, multi-stakeholder commercial deals that require both technical depth and commercial acumen.
  • Domain Familiarity: Working knowledge of model evaluation methodologies, RLHF, reinforcement learning environments, or human-in-the-loop data pipelines.
  • Analytical Rigor: A structured, hypothesis-driven approach to sales pipeline management, prospecting, and strategic problem-solving.
  • Preferred Backgrounds: Prior experience with human data pipelines (labeling, synthetic data generation, benchmarking systems), direct exposure to frontier model development organizations, or familiarity with enterprise procurement in high-value technical deals.
