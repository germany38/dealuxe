Role Responsibilities & Daily Tasks / Trách Nhiệm Công Việc & Công Việc Hằng Ngày

English

As an AI Safety Generalist Expert, your core objective is to audit, evaluate, and strengthen how artificial intelligence models process sensitive or complex prompts in Vietnamese. Your daily activities will challenge your critical thinking and writing capabilities:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Craft intricate, high-level prompts in Vietnamese across diverse specialized and sensitive topic domains.

  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: Pinpoint adversarial phrasings, edge cases, and potential escalation vectors, carefully recording your analytical reasoning.
