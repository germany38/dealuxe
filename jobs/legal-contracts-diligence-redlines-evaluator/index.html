Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For legal professionals seeking autonomy, intellectual engagement, and high-yield remote income, understanding the precise scope of this role is crucial. The position centers on rigorous quality assurance and analytical evaluation of AI-generated work products, ensuring that automated legal documents meet the highest standards of professional excellence.

Key Responsibilities:

  • AI Artifact Evaluation: Rigorously review, grade, and audit AI-generated legal documents, agreements, spreadsheets, and presentation slide decks focused on contract analysis and due diligence workflows.
  • Legal Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify subtle legal oversights, structural inconsistencies, presentation errors, and conceptual flaws in automated outputs.
  • Structured Constructive Feedback: Provide precise, actionable written feedback to help machine learning engineers and researchers fine-tune frontier AI models for enterprise-grade legal precision.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex legal concepts, indemnity clauses, liability limitations, and diligence frameworks generated by AI align with industry best practices and legal standards.

Candidate Requirements: Qualifications Needed to Succeed

Because these evaluations directly influence the safety and reliability of AI tools deployed across major enterprises and law firms, the qualifications for this role reflect a high degree of professional accomplishment.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Relevant Experience: Demonstrated professional background working directly with legal contracts, corporate due diligence, contract redlining, or transactional law.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication and analytical skills.
  • Software & Presentation Literacy: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in presentation tools (Google Slides and PowerPoint) used for executive briefings.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master of Laws, J.D., or higher) from an accredited and reputable academic institution.
