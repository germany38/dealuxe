Candidates must satisfy this requirement through one of two approved configurations:

Display & Resolution Compliance Options:

Option A — A Single High-Resolution Monitor: Any monitor meeting or exceeding the specified resolutions at standard aspect ratios:

  • 4:3 Aspect Ratio: 1826 × 1370 or 2048 × 1536
  • 16:9 Aspect Ratio: 2109 × 1186, 2560 × 1440 (QHD), 3840 × 2160 (4K), or 5120 × 2880 (5K)
  • 16:10 Aspect Ratio: 2000 × 1250, 2240 × 1400, 2560 × 1600, or 2880 × 1800

Option B — A Dual-Monitor Setup: Two displays positioned side by side and captured as a single spanning image. Both monitors must individually meet or exceed 1920 × 1080 resolution (a single 1080p screen paired with a lower-resolution monitor will not qualify).
