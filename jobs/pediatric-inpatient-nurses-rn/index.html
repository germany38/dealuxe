Bridging Clinical Bedside Expertise with Next-Generation Artificial Intelligence

For decades, the standard path for a Registered Nurse has meant long shifts on hospital floors, managing fast-paced environments, navigating complex family dynamics, and charting mountains of documentation under tight constraints. While frontline care remains the heartbeat of healthcare, many clinicians look for alternative ways to utilize their expertise—seeking better schedule flexibility, remote work opportunities, or parallel projects that minimize physical burnout while maximizing intellectual contribution.

Enter health tech informatics and AI model training. Modern machine learning models designed for healthcare applications cannot learn effectively from generic data sets or textbooks alone. They require nuanced, real-world context, careful calibration, and validation against rigorous nursing documentation standards. This is where pediatric inpatient nurses step in. By reviewing AI-generated clinical outputs, validating medical accuracy, and annotating data sets, nurses directly impact how medical technologies assist future providers.

Detailed Job Description and Core Responsibilities

Contractors hired for this role will engage in analytical, detail-oriented work designed to stress-test and refine clinical artificial intelligence models. Key responsibilities include:

  • AI Output Review: Systematically review and evaluate AI-generated clinical responses and documentation outputs based on realistic nursing flowsheet data.
  • Standard Validation: Validate the clinical accuracy, completeness, and adherence of model outputs to current pediatric inpatient documentation standards.
  • Data Annotation: Annotate and structure inpatient pediatric nursing assessment data to serve as high-quality training datasets for machine learning models.
  • Expert Clinical Feedback: Provide granular expert feedback regarding nursing assessments, patient evaluation notes, and everyday documentation workflows.
  • Risk & Inconsistency Mitigation: Identify subtle gaps, data inconsistencies, or safety risks embedded in AI-generated medical responses.
  • Guideline Refinement: Ask clarifying questions when annotation guidelines are ambiguous and actively contribute to refining documentation standards over time.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Collaborate closely with technical engineering and data science teams to optimize overall model performance and benchmarks.

Basic and Preferred Qualifications

To ensure high standards of safety and clinical validity, candidates must meet specific criteria before stepping into this specialized role:

  • Licensure: Active, unencumbered RN license in the United States (excluding California residents due to specific regional contracting policies).
  • Clinical Experience: Recent pediatric acute care inpatient bedside experience, ideally within a non-procedural inpatient unit. Candidates should have worked in an inpatient bedside role within the past 10 years, with current or recent documentation experience prioritized.
  • Assessment Skills: High comfort level performing and documenting comprehensive pediatric nursing assessments.
  • Guideline Adherence: Ability to meticulously follow detailed annotation instructions and deliver consistent output.
  • Communication: Excellent written communication skills, responsiveness to editorial and technical feedback, and self-directed time management.

Preferred Assets: Familiarity with Epic EHR systems (which heavily align with standardized healthcare workflows), previous experience with data annotation, chart abstraction, quality improvement reviews, clinical informatics, or utilization review processes will heavily benefit applicants during the evaluation phase.

Important Contracting & Payment Terms

  • Engagement Structure: Fully remote independent contractor role that can be scheduled flexibly around personal commitments.
  • Compensation Schedule: Weekly payouts disbursed reliably via Stripe or Wise based on services successfully rendered.
  • Visa Limitations: Mercor is currently unable to support H1-B or STEM OPT visa candidates for this specific opening.

Why This Role Offers Exceptional Professional Value

Transitioning into a role like this offers a rare blend of autonomy and high-impact intellectual work. Rather than spending hours commuting or adhering to rigid hospital shift allocations, contractors enjoy total location independence. Furthermore, working with cutting-edge AI labs provides a window into the future of medicine. You are not just observing technology evolve; you are actively authoring the guardrails that make healthcare AI safer, more reliable, and more deeply aligned with human clinical judgment.

Additionally, the compensation structure—ranging from $55 to $65 per hour—reflects the specialized value of advanced nursing credentials. When combined with weekly disbursements, this creates a predictable, rewarding income stream that respects your professional expertise.
