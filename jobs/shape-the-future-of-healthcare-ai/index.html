Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

The Certified Medical Coder role hosted on Mercor places you at the forefront of healthcare technology development. As part of a specialized multilingual clinical documentation cohort, your primary objective is to review, annotate, and evaluate clinical notes and AI-generated text outputs. Below is a detailed breakdown of what the day-to-day responsibilities entail:

1. Documentation & Coding Review

  • Accuracy & Completeness: Thoroughly inspect clinical documentation to guarantee that every diagnostic statement and treatment record meets rigorous clinical accuracy benchmarks.
  • AI Output Evaluation: Systematically audit AI-generated clinical notes, summaries, and patient records by comparing them directly against baseline medical documentation and established industry coding rules.
  • Code Validation: Confirm that the clinical documentation perfectly aligns with assigned medical codes, verifying that no documentation gaps or logical discrepancies exist.

2. Annotation & Structured Feedback

  • Detailed Guidelines Compliance: Apply comprehensive annotation frameworks to label and evaluate clinical text artifacts.
  • Error Identification: Actively flag omissions, logical errors, inaccuracies, and documentation gaps within automated or AI-generated outputs.
  • Actionable Communication: Deliver structured, clear, and written feedback designed specifically for product engineering and machine learning teams to rapidly iterate and improve model performance.

3. Knowledge Contribution & Guideline Refinement

  • Expert Consultation: Offer expert-level insights on complex documentation standards and help shape evolving annotation protocols.
  • Clarification & Resolution: Raise targeted clarifying questions when baseline guidance is ambiguous, actively participating in refining instructions for the broader team of evaluators.

Required Qualifications and Candidate Profile

Because this project directly influences clinical tools and models, candidate selection standards are high. To qualify for consideration, applicants must meet the following baseline requirements:

  • Active Professional Certification: Must hold at least one active certification among CCDS, CHC, CCS, or CPC.
  • ICD-10 Expertise: Hands-on, professional proficiency with ICD-10 coding guidelines and applications.
  • Current Industry Experience: Actively working—or recently active—within medical coding, clinical documentation improvement (CDI), or a closely related healthcare documentation environment.
  • Geographic Eligibility: Must be based in the United States (US-based).
  • Multilingual Proficiency: In addition to fluent English, candidates must demonstrate advanced (C1 or above) proficiency in speaking, listening, and writing in at least one additional language.
  • Time Commitment: Availability to commit a minimum of 10 hours per week.

Preferred Qualifications

While the requirements above are strict, candidates possessing the following secondary credentials and experiences will find themselves exceptionally well-positioned during the review process:

  • Secondary or advanced certifications such as CRC or CDIP.
  • Familiarity with enterprise electronic health record (EHR) platforms, particularly Epic.
  • Experience with Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) coding or risk-adjustment models.
  • Prior experience in AI/ML model annotation, prompt engineering evaluation, or content moderation workflows with strong performance feedback.

Compensation, Flexibility, and Contract Terms

One of the most compelling aspects of this opportunity is the combination of top-tier compensation and absolute schedule autonomy. Operating as an independent contractor provides the flexibility to manage your hours around an existing clinical job, consulting business, or personal lifestyle.

Competitive Pay Scale: Contractors for this role earn between $45 and $65 per hour, depending on qualifications, test performance, and project tier. Furthermore, payments are processed swiftly and reliably on a weekly basis via Stripe or Wise.
