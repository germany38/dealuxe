Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For UK-based speakers with a neutral, standard accent and an ear for cadence, this project offers a focused, high-yielding engagement. The primary objective is to contribute pristine voice recordings that help train and evaluate text-to-speech engines designed specifically for internal enterprise CX AI agents.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Script Execution: Record diverse voice samples across multiple script genres, including conversational dialogue, narrative arcs, and instructional prompts.
  • Expressive Delivery: Maintain crisp articulation, natural pacing, and strong emotional command over tone, intonation, and pronunciation.
  • Acoustic Consistency: Uphold rigorous consistency in voice timbre, accent preservation, and stylistic delivery across continuous recording sessions.
  • Technical Compliance: Strictly follow professional recording guidelines concerning environmental noise control, microphone distance, and proper digital file formatting.
  • Dynamic Variation: Execute multiple takes reflecting subtle variations in emotional inflection, emphasis, and conversational style upon request.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these voice models form the foundation of enterprise customer experience applications, quality and precision are non-negotiable. Mercor has established clear criteria for applicants interested in participating in this initiative.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • Location & Accent: Native English speaker currently based in the United Kingdom (GBR) possessing a neutral, standard UK English accent.
  • Vocal Clarity: Naturally clear, expressive, and pleasant voice. While professional narration or voice-acting experience is a strong advantage, newcomers with clear delivery are welcome.
  • Recording Setup: Access to a professional or near-professional home recording setup (high-quality microphone, pop filter, and a quiet, sound-treated environment).
  • Commitment & Scope: Availability for an initial ~4-hour recording session, with potential for a follow-up session of similar length.
  • Voice Cloning Consent: Willingness to have your voice cloned exclusively for the client's internal CX AI Agent (strictly restricted; recordings are never resold or licensed externally).

Contract Terms, Flexibility, and Global Payment Infrastructure

Contracting through Mercor provides independent professionals with unmatched administrative freedom and financial transparency. This remote engagement is structured around absolute flexibility:

  • Independent Contractor Status: Complete your recording sessions on your own schedule from your home studio in the UK.

  • Data Protection & exclusivity: Rest assured that your vocal assets are protected—used solely for the designated internal CX AI agent with strict protocols against unauthorized redistribution.
