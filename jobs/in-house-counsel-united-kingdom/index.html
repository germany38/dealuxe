What Does the Role Involve?

Roles like the In-House Counsel (United Kingdom) position at Mercor offer a fascinating departure from day-to-day corporate grind while capitalizing directly on your core competencies. Rather than drafting contracts from scratch or managing endless litigation, expert reviewers act as quality assurance architects for legal AI systems.

The day-to-day responsibilities are structured, intellectually stimulating, and completely remote:

  • Prompt Execution: Run standardized sets of complex legal prompts across two distinct AI (LLM) platforms.
  • Comparative Analysis: Evaluate outputs side-by-side, analyzing accuracy, tone, completeness, and jurisdictional precision under UK law.
  • Rubric Scoring: Score each platform's response rigorously against a standardized, objective evaluation rubric.
  • Concise Feedback: Submit clear, well-reasoned written feedback justifying scores to help engineering teams refine model performance.

Candidate Requirements and Background

Because these AI models are being trained to process high-stakes queries, quality standards are exceptionally high. To qualify, candidates must bring substantial practical experience to the table:

  • Experience: 5+ years working as in-house legal counsel practicing actively within the United Kingdom.
  • Domain Expertise: Exceptional command of UK law, with specialized proficiency in commercial and contract matters.
  • Communication: Impeccable written English skills, acute attention to detail, and the ability to articulate legal reasoning clearly.
  • Autonomy: Ability to work independently, interpret structured evaluation rubrics accurately, and meet strict project deadlines.
