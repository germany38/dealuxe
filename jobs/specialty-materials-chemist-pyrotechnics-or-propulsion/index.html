Understanding the Core Mission: AI Red-Teaming in Energetic Materials

Artificial intelligence models trained on vast web corpora can easily regurgitate scientific text, but true comprehension requires expert validation. In fields like energetic materials, pyrotechnics, and solid-propellant design, the boundary between safe, legitimate industrial chemistry and hazardous misuse is exceptionally thin. The same sensitivity data required to establish safe production line limits can also be misconstrued if placed in the wrong context.

When an AI model evaluates a technical prompt, failing to answer a routine thermal stability question is just as catastrophic as answering a dangerous query it has no business addressing. Drawing that delicate line reliably is why Mercor is hiring practicing specialists rather than generalist reviewers.

What Your Daily Tasks Will Entail:

  • Prompt Generation: Write challenging, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific domain of chemistry, categorizing them across three distinct tiers: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation: Rigorously analyze model-generated responses against established safety policy frameworks, determining whether the model correctly balanced helpfulness and safety.
  • Authoring Reference Answers: Draft detailed technical justifications explaining what a correct response looks like, backed by precise scientific reasoning.

💡 The Practitioner Advantage: This domain lives on the dual-use line more than most. You are required to bring real-world laboratory, formulation, and synthesis experience to ensure models are anchored in practical chemical reality.

Professional Backgrounds We Are Looking For

Mercor maintains rigorous screening standards to ensure data integrity for AI labs. This project specifically targets the chemistry side of energetic materials and propulsion engineering. Ideal candidates have hands-on experience formulating, synthesizing, and testing materials in licensed commercial, academic, or government facilities rather than just modeling them from a distance.

Target Professional Backgrounds:

  • Formulation Chemists: Professionals experienced with pyrotechnics, display compositions, rocket propellants, or specialty energetic formulations.
  • Synthesis & Characterization Experts: Individuals with direct lab experience synthesizing and characterizing energetic compounds within certified facilities.
  • Testing & Safety Specialists: Experts versed in performance and sensitivity testing, including calorimetry, thermal stability assessments, small-scale safety testing, scale-up, and process safety.
  • Propulsion Engineers: Specialists focused on solid or liquid rocket propellant development, encompassing binders, oxidizers, and burn-rate modifiers.
