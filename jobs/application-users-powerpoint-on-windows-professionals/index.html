Understanding the Technical Display & Resolution Rules

Because the work involves evaluating and interacting with high-fidelity visual outputs, technical hardware standards are strictly enforced during the onboarding pipeline. Candidates must satisfy the display prerequisite via one of two supported configurations:

Option A: Single High-Resolution Monitor

Your single monitor must operate at or above specific minimum pixel dimensions depending on its aspect ratio:

  • 4:3 Aspect Ratio: Minimum resolutions such as 1826 × 1370 or 2048 × 1536.
  • 16:9 Aspect Ratio (Widescreen): Minimum 2109 × 1186, including standard QHD (2560 × 1440), 4K (3840 × 2160), or 5K (5120 × 2880) displays.
  • 16:10 Aspect Ratio: Minimum 2000 × 1250, including 2240 × 1400, 2560 × 1600, or 2880 × 1800 screens.
  • 3:2 Aspect Ratio: Minimum 1937 × 1291, including 2160 × 1440, 2256 × 1504, 2736 × 1824, or 3000 × 2000.
  • 21:9 Aspect Ratio (Ultrawide): Minimum 2416 × 1036, including 2560 × 1080, 3440 × 1440, or 3840 × 1600. (Note: Built-in MacBook Pro 14" and 16" displays also qualify if tested within cross-platform environments).

Option B: Dual-Monitor Setup

Alternatively, you can utilize two displays positioned side by side, captured as a single spanning image. Two side-by-side 1920 × 1080 (or higher) monitors fully satisfy this criterion. (Please note that a single 1080p screen out of that pair on its own does not meet the threshold).
