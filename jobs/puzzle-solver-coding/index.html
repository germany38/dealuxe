micro1 bridges this gap by building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By partnering with skilled programmers who excel at solving complex technical puzzles, micro1 transforms real-world coding acumen into structured evaluation datasets and feedback loops. In this role, you aren't just writing code for a traditional software product; you are actively auditing, testing, and refining how artificial intelligence reasons through code.

  • Solving Technical Puzzles: Tackle self-contained coding, logic, and algorithmic challenges designed to push the boundaries of current AI models.
