Detailed Scope of Work & Responsibilities

As a Generalist Writer on this specialized project, your daily contributions directly influence how language models reason, structure arguments, and maintain factual reliability. Key responsibilities include:

  • Content Assessment: Reviewing and evaluating written content and AI-generated responses for accuracy, coherence, completeness, and overarching quality.
  • Instruction Adherence: Systematically comparing model outputs against detailed operational guidelines and strict evaluation benchmarks.
  • Constructive Feedback: Delivering clear, actionable written feedback emphasizing precision, consistency, and objective appraisal.
  • Discrepancy Identification: Pinpointing subtle ambiguities, factual errors, logical inconsistencies, or areas requiring enhancement in written output.
  • Documentation & Reporting: Systematically documenting findings using designated evaluation software, structured guidelines, and reporting templates.
