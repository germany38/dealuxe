Artificial intelligence has evolved past basic text generation. Today, frontier models are entering complex multi-step reasoning, autonomous agent execution, and intricate software development tasks. However, before large language models can autonomously architect code, debug memory leaks, or optimize asynchronous threads, they require rigorous guidance from seasoned software engineers.

About Turing and the Core Mission

Turing helps top-tier AI labs in two vital ways: first, by advancing frontier model capabilities across reasoning, coding, multimodality, and STEM knowledge; and second, by leveraging that work to build mission-critical enterprise AI systems. This specific project places you directly within an engagement alongside a foundational LLM company.

Your primary objective is to help these labs enhance their Large Language Models through high-quality proprietary data generation. This data serves two critical functions: as foundational material for Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and as rigorous evaluation sets (Evals) to benchmark model performance against competitors.

Understanding SFT and RLHF Data Workflows

  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): You will review provided prompts containing code snippets and technical questions, generate optimal model responses, and write precise, production-grade Python code to solve complex queries.
  • Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF): You may create or utilize evaluation prompts, query competing versions of LLMs, analyze model outputs, and provide granular technical feedback used to refine reward models. Note that this role focuses on evaluation and data engineering rather than training models from scratch.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a Senior Python Developer on this engagement, your daily routine mirrors that of a high-level software architect and technical auditor. Responsibilities include:

  • Designing, developing, and maintaining efficient, high-quality Python code to train and optimize AI capabilities.
  • Conducting rigorous evaluations (Evals) to benchmark model performance and analyzing results for continuous improvement.
  • Evaluating and ranking AI model responses to complex user queries, ensuring strict adherence to software engineering best practices.
  • Developing comprehensive explanations and rationales for evaluations, showcasing elite technical reasoning.
  • Conducting thorough peer reviews of code and documentation, providing constructive feedback and identifying subtle regression risks.
  • Collaborating with cross-functional research teams to continuously integrate new tools and methodologies.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Experience

This engagement demands deep technical fluency. Successful candidates typically exhibit:

  • 3+ years of strong, professional experience with the Python programming language.
  • Thorough understanding of code quality, formatting, and industry software development best practices.
  • Extensive experience with Python’s testing ecosystem, including unit, integration, and property-based testing.
  • Solid knowledge of multi-threading and asynchronous programming in Python.
  • Demonstrated ability to work with complex architectural patterns and refactor code without introducing regressions.
  • Strong debugging capabilities, including the ability to resolve memory and concurrency issues.
  • Fluent conversational and written English communication skills.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Traditional engineering roles often trap talent in rigid office environments and bureaucratic silos. Turing liberates your career by offering:

  • Full Remote Autonomy: Work from anywhere in the world in a flexible virtual environment.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Direct collaboration with foundational LLM companies shaping the future of global technology.
  • Flexible Commitment Options: Choose between 20 hours, 30 hours, or 40 hours per week, with a minimum 4-hour daily overlap with PST.

The Interview Process & Onboarding Roadmap

To ensure high standards of technical excellence, Turing utilizes a streamlined, efficient evaluation process lasting approximately 75 minutes total:

  1. Technical Round (60 minutes): A deep dive into advanced Python concepts, debugging, testing, and system architecture.
  2. Cultural & Offer Discussion (15 minutes): Alignment on project expectations, availability, and onboarding details.

Maximizing Your Success: Once onboarded, successfully completing your initial onboarding milestones and your first 10 hours of work is crucial. This establishes operational stability, ensures long-term assignment security, and unlocks access to more advanced tasks and higher-tier projects within the ecosystem.
