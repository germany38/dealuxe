Demystifying the Role: Teaching Machines Physical Dexterity

When engineering advanced robotic systems, developers encounter a massive data bottleneck: teaching machines how to handle unpredictable everyday physical objects. While coding logical pathways is straightforward, teaching a robot the subtle fine motor control needed to pick up fragile items, open containers, or navigate complex physical clutter requires vast datasets of authentic human motion.

As an AI Robotics Trainer on this initiative, your physical movements and precise manual handling become the blueprint for machine learning models. Operating on-site in a specialized San Francisco lab, you will work directly with advanced hardware interfaces, capturing high-fidelity training data that dictates how future robotic systems learn to reason, adapt, and perform physical work.

Comprehensive Scope of Work

Contractors selected for this on-site San Francisco project play an active, daily role in data capture and hardware testing. Daily tasks and operational responsibilities include:

  • Operating Hardware: Utilizing a handheld UMI gripper device on-site in the lab to execute repetitive manipulation tasks across diverse objects and simulated environments.
  • Executing Everyday Actions: Performing common physical motions such as picking up items, opening containers, and executing simple household tasks to train learning algorithms.
  • Protocol Adherence: Closely following detailed written instructions and experimental protocols to ensure absolute reliability and consistency across data sets.
