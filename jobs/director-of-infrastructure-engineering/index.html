The Critical Role of Infrastructure in Frontier AI Scaling

As micro1 scales its global expert network—mobilizing top-tier subject matter experts across finance, healthcare, software engineering, and STEM domains—the underlying technical infrastructure must maintain absolute reliability, security, and low-latency performance. AI training pipelines, automated evaluation agents, and large-scale data processing demand fault-tolerant cloud architectures that operate seamlessly across geographic boundaries.

The Director of Infrastructure Engineering is not merely a managerial position; it is a hands-on executive leadership role. You will operate at every level of the technical stack, bridging high-level strategic planning with hands-on incident response, systems architecture, and mentorship. If you relish the challenge of scaling environments where uptime, security, and developer velocity are paramount, this role represents a defining career milestone.

Key Responsibilities and Strategic Scope:

  • Multi-Cloud Strategy & Architecture: Own the comprehensive architecture, evolution, and optimization of micro1’s multi-cloud infrastructure across AWS and GCP, balancing cost-efficiency with uncompromising scalability, performance, and security.
  • Team Leadership & Culture: Lead, mentor, and aggressively scale a high-performing Infrastructure and Platform Engineering team, cultivating an engineering culture anchored in deep ownership, operational excellence, and continuous delivery.
  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Spearhead the adoption and expansion of version-controlled, reproducible, and auditable environments utilizing Terraform (or equivalent IaC frameworks).
  • CI/CD & Developer Velocity: Design, build, and refine cutting-edge CI/CD pipelines that empower engineering squads to ship secure code rapidly and reliably.
  • World-Class Observability: Implement comprehensive observability frameworks across metrics, structured logs, distributed traces, and intelligent alerting to preemptively detect and resolve production bottlenecks.
  • Reliability & Incident Management: Drive rigorous SRE best practices, including robust incident response protocols, on-call rotations, Service Level Objectives (SLOs), error budgets, disaster recovery drills, and blameless postmortems.
  • Security & Compliance Integration: Partner directly with security and executive stakeholders to embed security by default, maintaining rigorous compliance frameworks including ISO 27001, SOC 2, and CMMC.

💡 Maximizing Professional Impact: For technical leaders and engineers joining micro1 projects, committing fully to onboarding milestones—such as achieving at least 10 hours of active project collaboration and platform engagement—ensures seamless integration, unlocks higher-tier project responsibilities, and validates long-term operational success.

Preferred Qualifications and Professional Background

Because micro1 powers critical workflows for elite AI labs, the selection criteria for this executive role demand a proven track record of managing complex, large-scale technical environments:

  • Extensive Technical Tenure: 8+ years of professional experience building, scaling, and operating production infrastructure, platform engineering, DevOps, or SRE systems in high-growth environments.
  • Executive Leadership Experience: 3+ years leading and scaling high-performing engineering teams, demonstrating exceptional mentorship and cross-functional influence.
  • Cloud & Container Mastery: Deep, hands-on expertise with AWS, GCP, containerization, Kubernetes orchestration, and modern CI/CD deployment pipelines.
  • Risk & Security Governance: Solid understanding of infrastructure security, regulatory compliance frameworks, and operational risk management.
  • AI/ML Infrastructure Familiarity (Preferred): Prior experience supporting artificial intelligence or machine learning infrastructure, model training pipelines, large-scale data platforms, or regulated cloud environments like FedRAMP and GovCloud.
