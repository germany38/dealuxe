Comprehensive Role Overview & Responsibilities

For professional educators, private tutors, and language instructors looking to diversify their income streams, this project offers a refreshing shift from traditional tutoring models. Mercor is partnering with a prominent language-learning platform to curate high-quality conversational datasets via recorded, one-on-one virtual mentoring sessions. The primary aim is to facilitate authentic, substantive interactions with native English speakers at varying stages of language mastery.

Core Responsibilities and Expectations:

  • Engaging Instruction: Lead dynamic, structured one-on-one Spanish tutoring sessions designed to simulate natural conversational flow.
  • Proficiency Adaptation: Skillfully adapt lesson plans and conversational pacing to match each individual learner's unique proficiency tier and assigned daily topics.
  • Substantive Dialogue: Encourage meaningful back-and-forth communication, avoiding extended pauses, dead air, or off-topic tangents.
  • Protocol Adherence: Strictly follow provided project instructions and lesson frameworks to ensure data uniformity.
  • Session Duration: Execute focused sessions running for a minimum of 20 minutes, typically lasting between 20 to 30 minutes per engagement.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these recordings directly contribute to sophisticated machine learning datasets and language evaluation tools, applicants must meet stringent professional criteria to qualify.

Essential Qualifications:
  • Language Fluency: Native or near-native fluency in Spanish combined with exceptional verbal communication skills. Tutors from diverse Spanish-speaking regions—including Spain, Mexico, and Latin America—are warmly encouraged to apply.
  • Educational Background: Must hold a Bachelor’s degree or higher from an accredited institution.
  • Teaching Experience: A minimum of three years of professional Spanish teaching or tutoring experience.
  • Target Audience Familiarity: Proven background instructing native English speakers across diverse developmental milestones and language levels.
  • Adaptability & Punctuality: Demonstrated ability to pivot teaching styles dynamically while maintaining a strong record of reliability and punctuality.
  • Recording Comfort: Absolute comfort participating in and being recorded during online instructional sessions.
