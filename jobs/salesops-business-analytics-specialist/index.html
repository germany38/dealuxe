The Mission: Powering the Human Intelligence Layer for AI

micro1 operates at the intersection of human expertise and machine learning. By vetting global talent through an advanced AI recruiter and converting real-world professional knowledge into high-quality training datasets, micro1 enables frontier AI models to reason, learn, and perform with unprecedented accuracy. Behind this massive operational scaling is a high-velocity revenue engine that requires rigorous data governance, efficient sales workflows, and pristine pipeline visibility.

As a SalesOps & Business Analytics Specialist, you will work closely with the Sales Operations Senior Lead to architect, maintain, and optimize the operational infrastructure supporting micro1's sales and recruitment funnels. This is not a back-office administrative position; it is a strategic role where your data models and forecasting insights directly influence company-wide growth targets and business decisions.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • CRM Administration & Optimization: Support and enhance core CRM operations—with a strong emphasis on HubSpot—ensuring flawless data hygiene, workflow automation, custom reporting, and responsive user support across sales teams.
  • Pipeline Integrity & Forecasting: Maintain rigorous pipeline quality standards and provide dependable support for weekly, monthly, and quarterly revenue forecasting cycles.
  • Dashboard & BI Development: Build and scale advanced performance dashboards covering pipeline health, conversion metrics, seller productivity, and quota attainment.
  • Funnel Analytics & Cohort Performance: Analyze sales trends, conversion funnels, and cohort performance to uncover hidden growth opportunities and operational bottlenecks.
  • Predictive Modeling: Help improve predictive forecasting models by leveraging historical data patterns and behavioral signals.
  • Stack Implementation: Support the seamless rollout, integration, and user adoption of emerging sales technologies, CRM pipelines, and revenue tools.
  • Cross-Functional Storytelling: Translate complex technical datasets into clear, actionable business narratives and strategic recommendations for leadership and cross-functional stakeholders.

💡 Thriving in a High-Growth Environment: micro1's culture values intellectual agility, comfort with ambiguity, and proactive problem-solving. Success in this role requires connecting granular operational metrics directly to high-level revenue impact.

Preferred Qualifications and Professional Background

To succeed in micro1's fast-paced, remote-first environment, candidates should bring a blend of rigorous analytical training and hands-on operational experience:

  • Professional Experience: 2 to 4+ years of dedicated professional experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations (RevOps), Business Analytics, or a closely related analytical discipline.
  • CRM Mastery: Hands-on administrative experience with modern CRM platforms, with advanced HubSpot proficiency or certification strongly preferred.
  • Technical Analytics Toolkit: Advanced proficiency in Excel or Google Sheets, combined with practical experience utilizing SQL and business intelligence tools such as Tableau, Looker, or Power BI.
  • Commercial Acumen: Strong business judgment with the ability to connect underlying data structures to commercial outcomes and strategic revenue decisions.
  • Communication & Presentation: Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the proven ability to present intricate analytical insights to non-technical stakeholders and executive leadership.
  • Bonus Qualifications: Experience with predictive analytics, statistical modeling, sales compensation/quota analysis, or cross-functional analytics spanning Marketing, Customer Success, or Recruitment funnels.

micro1 supports a high-performing, remote-first workforce by providing competitive compensation packages designed to reward top-tier operational talent:

  • Comprehensive Health Coverage: Up to 100% reimbursement for health-insurance premiums, ensuring robust medical support for team members.
