Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

Understanding what the role entails is the first step toward transitioning your professional skillset into the thriving tech and AI evaluation landscape. As an Education / School Evaluator, you act as a senior academic quality controller, auditing and grading complex artifacts generated by artificial intelligence models.

What You Will Do on the Job:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review and meticulously assess AI-generated work products—including structured educational documents, lesson plans, pedagogical spreadsheets, and instructional slide decks.
  • Rigor & Quality Auditing: Apply your professional subject-matter expertise to grade outputs against rigorous educational rubrics, ensuring factual accuracy, developmental appropriateness, and cognitive rigor.
  • Error Identification: Spot and analyze factual inaccuracies, presentation flaws, structural gaps, and aesthetic errors within automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback: Deliver clear, structured, and actionable written critiques that help frontier AI systems learn human-like educational reasoning and presentation standards.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these evaluations directly influence the quality of state-of-the-art AI systems used by institutions worldwide, the qualification standards are designed to attract established education professionals who command complete mastery of their field.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: A solid, verifiable professional background working in education, school administration, curriculum development, or instructional design.
  • Communication Mastery: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written articulation.
  • Software & Presentation Proficiency: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in presentation software like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s, Ed.D., or Ph.D.) from a reputable academic institution.
