The Mission: Building Autonomous AI for High-Stakes Government Environments

Traditional software development paradigms fail when deployed into high-assurance, secure government settings. Standard models cannot simply be plugged into operational networks without rigorous adaptation, low-latency infrastructure, and domain-specific validation. Forward-deployed engineering requires a rare breed of technician: someone who possesses world-class software engineering fundamentals paired with the operational poise to work side-by-side with government partners.

At micro1, forward-deployed engineers operate at the intersection of applied research and production engineering. You are not writing code in an isolated silo; you are actively translating complex national security mission requirements into scalable, resilient technical systems. By turning raw operational data, frontier models, and specialized tools into real operational autonomy, you help shape the future of defense technology.

What You Will Work On (Core Responsibilities):

  • Agentic AI System Deployment: Build and deploy advanced agentic AI architectures that support high-impact U.S. Government missions, moving systems seamlessly from prototype to production.
  • LLM & RAG Integration: Design and integrate sophisticated Large Language Model applications, encompassing multi-agent systems, tool-using agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows, and human-in-the-loop autonomy.
  • Partner-Facing Operations: Collaborate directly with government partners in forward-deployed environments to understand operational bottlenecks and translate mission requirements into functional code.
  • Data Curation & Evaluation Pipelines: Design, build, and maintain large-scale data curation and evaluation pipelines dedicated to continuous model training and performance improvement.
  • Inference & ML Infrastructure: Develop robust infrastructure for model inference, real-time experimentation, performance evaluation, and deployment across frontier AI platforms.
  • Full-Lifecycle Ownership: Own systems across their entire lifecycle—including discovery, architecture, implementation, deployment, security hardening, reliability monitoring, and rapid iteration.

💡 Operational Excellence: Succeeding in a forward-deployed role demands complete technical ownership. Engineers who thrive here excel in navigating ambiguous partner environments, managing high-security constraints, and delivering bulletproof Python code under demanding timelines.
