Deep Dive into the Role: What You Will Actually Do

Advanced machine learning models require rigorous human-in-the-loop validation to operate safely and effectively across complex human disciplines. As a Bilingual Russian STEM Expert, your core objective will be to construct complex evaluations, challenge model limits through strategic red-teaming, and ensure that technical content translated or generated in Russian matches the highest tiers of academic and scientific reality.

Your day-to-day responsibilities will feature tasks such as:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Crafting high-level, sophisticated technical prompts in Russian spanning specialized scientific domains like chemistry, molecular biology, and material sciences.
  • Response Annotation & Evaluation: Critiquing, grading, and correcting model outputs for absolute factual precision, helpfulness, and proper risk mitigation regarding dual-use technologies.
  • Framework Compliance: Applying structured evaluation systems to categorize prompts, evaluate safety risks, and maintain the highest standards of responsible AI deployment.

Who Should Apply? Candidate Requirements

Mercor values deep specialization and real-world domain mastery. To succeed and thrive in this high-paying independent contractor position, you should align with the following criteria:

  • Educational Background: A completed or ongoing PhD in chemistry, biology, or an adjacent hard science discipline with substantial technical depth.
  • Linguistic Mastery: Native or near-native fluency in Russian, alongside business-level written and spoken English proficiency.
  • Laboratory Literacy: Practical, hands-on familiarity with modern laboratory protocols, computational techniques, or specialized subfields (such as organic synthesis, virology, genetics, or radiochemistry).
  • Ethical & Safety Judgment: A robust understanding of scientific ethics, biosafety guidelines, and safe dual-use information protocols.
