This is where seasoned legal minds enter the equation. As a legal domain expert embedded within a cutting-edge GenAI research group, your core responsibility is acting as the definitive arbiter of legal truth and reasoning quality. Your day-to-day engagement focuses on several high-impact pillars:

  • Benchmark Design: Construct sophisticated, adversarial legal tasks and evaluation frameworks that rigorously test whether frontier models are genuinely mastering substantive legal concepts or merely mimicking stylistic patterns.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Calibration: Work alongside machine learning researchers, engineers, and specialists in adjacent domains to translate tacit legal intuition and professional judgment into explicit, teachable evaluation criteria.
