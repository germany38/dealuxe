About Turing and the AI Revolution in Finance

Turing helps top-tier customers in two distinct ways: first, by partnering with the world's leading AI labs to advance frontier model capabilities in thinking, reasoning, multilinguality, and complex STEM disciplines; and second, by leveraging that work to build real-world enterprise AI systems that solve mission-critical operational challenges.

In the financial sector, accuracy, regulatory compliance, and nuanced strategic judgment are paramount. By engaging senior accounting professionals, Turing ensures that AI models learn how to interpret financial statements, navigate QuickBooks Online (QBO) workflows, and deliver trusted advisory recommendations to small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs).

Inside the Role: What You Will Do Day-to-Day

As a Senior Accounting & Finance Advisor, your responsibilities go far beyond routine bookkeeping. You will act as a cognitive architect for artificial intelligence systems, evaluating complex financial data and structuring expert guidance.

  • Financial Data Analysis: Analyze intricate financial reports, statements, and ledgers to provide authoritative accounting guidance.
  • Complex Query Resolution: Handle multi-layered accounting, bookkeeping, and finance inquiries that require sophisticated analytical and advisory capabilities.
  • Advisory-Based Recommendations: Provide structured, strategic recommendations derived from real-world financial metrics.
  • Omni Chat Support Integration: Provide expert-level responses and support involving QuickBooks Online (QBO) and interactive financial support channels.

You Are a Perfect Fit If You Possess...

This engagement is tailor-made for high-level finance practitioners who have spent years navigating corporate balance sheets, advising leadership, and optimizing fiscal health.

Required Experience & Background:

  • Senior Leadership Profile: Extensive background as a CFO, COO (Finance), Financial Controller, Financial Director, VP of Finance, or Senior Finance Expert.
  • SMB Advisory Experience: Proven history supporting or advising SMB clients and business owners on accounting and financial matters (particularly US-based SMBs).
  • Multi-Organization Exposure: Hands-on experience working with or advising three or more distinct corporate organizations.
  • Tenure: 5+ years of professional experience in accounting, bookkeeping, finance, financial consulting, or related fields.
  • Platform Mastery: Direct practical experience with QuickBooks Online (QBO) and Omni Chat support infrastructure.
