Detailed Job Description and Core Responsibilities

For instructional designers, corporate trainers, and talent development leaders seeking flexible, high-yield remote income, understanding the daily scope of this role is crucial. The position demands high-level analytical evaluation, requiring experts to grade and review AI-generated work products against rigorous professional standards.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and audit AI-generated spreadsheets, strategic training documents, and slide decks designed for employee onboarding and professional development programs.
  • Pedagogical & Structural Auditing: Apply deep subject-matter expertise to identify structural gaps, factual inaccuracies, instructional design flaws, and presentation errors in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Written Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable feedback to help train and align frontier AI language models with enterprise training standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that corporate education plans, learning frameworks, and onboarding procedures generated by AI reflect best practices in adult learning theory and corporate governance.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these roles directly train state-of-the-art AI systems utilized by leading global enterprises, the qualification bar is set to attract seasoned domain authorities rather than general practitioners.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Demonstrated professional background in training, employee onboarding, instructional design, or learning and development (L&D).
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication and editorial skills.
  • Software Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific emphasis on advanced presentation software (Google Slides and PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) in instructional design, education, business administration, or a related field from a reputable institution.
