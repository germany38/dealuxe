Why Clinical Domain Expertise is the Foundation of Frontier AI Safety

For years, technology companies attempted to govern AI safety through rigid keyword blocking and simplistic rule-based filters. However, natural human language is nuanced, contextual, and deeply complex. When an adolescent expresses emotional turmoil, a rigid filter either overreacts—shutting down supportive dialogue—or under-reacts, missing subtle indicators of acute self-harm risk. Building reliable AI safety guardrails requires deep, real-world clinical acumen that can only be provided by trained mental health professionals.

micro1 bridges this gap by functioning as the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By engaging specialists who spend their careers conducting clinical risk assessments, crisis counseling, and therapeutic interventions, micro1 transforms empirical clinical knowledge into high-quality training data, evaluation benchmarks, and fine-tuning feedback loops. No prior software engineering or AI coding experience is necessary; your clinical insight into adolescent mental health is what shapes safer, more responsive technology.

Detailed Scope of Work and Core Responsibilities:

  • Adolescent Case Review: Review complex adolescent mental health case materials and generate structured, written risk assessments for suicide and self-harm presentations.
  • Validated Instrument Application: Apply established clinical frameworks and validated tools (such as the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale - C-SSRS) alongside expert risk formulation to establish clear, actionable guidance.
  • Acuity & Intent Differentiation: Differentiate with precision between non-suicidal self-injury, passive suicidal ideation, and active acute intent, properly characterizing risk acuity levels.
  • Response Protocols & Safety Planning: Recommend evidence-based response protocols, including escalation pathways, safety planning frameworks (e.g., Stanley-Brown Safety Planning Intervention), and means-restriction strategies.
  • Comorbidity & Context Integration: Identify underlying comorbidities, safeguarding concerns, and psychosocial contextual factors influencing risk, incorporating them seamlessly into evaluation frameworks.
  • Research-Grade Documentation: Document clinical guidance in clear, research-grade English so non-specialist users and AI systems can interpret and implement recommended actions safely and responsibly.
