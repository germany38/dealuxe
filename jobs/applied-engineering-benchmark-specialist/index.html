Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For engineering researchers, professors, and specialized professionals looking to engage in high-impact consulting and AI training work, understanding the core structure of this role is essential. Specialists are assigned specific tasks centered around authoring and verifying rigorous academic assessment content across vital technical domains.

The Two Primary Task Streams:

  • Question Authoring: Crafting original, highly challenging multiple-choice questions grounded in specific engineering subdomains. Authors must rate the difficulty levels and submit complete assessment packages for verification.
  • Question Verification: Reviewing pre-written technical questions for absolute accuracy, clarity, and analytical rigor. Verifiers edit content where necessary, adjust difficulty ratings, and document all changes made.

Core Responsibilities Include:

  • Conceptual Rigor: Author original engineering problems that test deep analytical understanding rather than superficial recall or memorization.
  • Unambiguous Formulation: Ensure every question is entirely self-contained, clear, and precisely defined, embedding all vital parameters directly within the problem statement.
  • Granular Difficulty Calibration: Accurately rate each question across levels: Medium (introductory undergraduate), Hard (advanced undergraduate), and Expert (post-graduate and above).
  • Complex Distractor Design: Supply one correct answer alongside nine plausible, subtly incorrect alternatives specifically engineered to challenge expert-level problem solvers.
  • Chain-of-Thought Documentation: Write thorough, step-by-step intermediate calculation steps using clear markdown formatting.
  • Academic Sourcing: Provide one to five reputable academic references per question, drawing from peer-reviewed journals or accredited university repositories.
  • Editorial Verification: For verification tasks, rigorously check clarity, completeness, precision, and solvability while justifying any modifications.
