About Turing and the Core Mission

Turing empowers top AI labs by translating real-world human expertise into rigorous training data, evaluations, and RLHF feedback loops. In the Board Game Reasoning Expert role, your primary objective is to leverage your deep background in board games, game mechanics, probability, and formal logic to construct evaluation tasks that test an AI's tactical reasoning. You aren't just playing games; you are reverse-engineering cognitive strategy to build the foundational reasoning layer for next-gen models.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a Board Game Reasoning Expert, your daily responsibilities are intellectually stimulating and highly analytical:

  • Task Creation: Design sophisticated game-based reasoning scenarios and multi-step evaluation tasks to test AI capabilities.
  • Strategic Analysis: Dissect decision trees, board game states, probabilistic models, and rule-based systems.
  • Model Evaluation: Audit AI-generated responses for logical consistency, rule adherence, and strategic depth.
  • Rubric Development: Author comprehensive evaluation guidelines, prompts, and QA frameworks for strategy-focused tasks.
  • Collaboration: Work alongside AI research teams to refine datasets and elevate benchmarking methodologies.

You Are a Perfect Fit If You Possess:

Required Qualifications:

  • Educational Background: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Cognitive Science, Game Design, Philosophy, Economics, or a related analytical field.
  • Experience: 2+ years of professional or semi-professional experience in board game design, playtesting, tabletop gaming communities, or strategy environments.
  • Core Competencies: Exceptional command of formal logic, probabilistic reasoning, game theory, and complex rule interpretation.
  • Domain Familiarity: Deep interest in modern board games, trading card games (TCGs), tabletop RPGs, and competitive strategy systems.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Engagement Details & The Interview Process

Understanding the engagement parameters ensures a smooth transition into the project:

  • Time Commitment: At least 4 hours per day and a minimum of 20 hours per week, requiring a 4-hour daily overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST).
  • Interview Process: Candidates undergo a rigorous take-home assessment designed to test analytical reasoning and rule evaluation capabilities.
  • Onboarding & Stability: Once selected, successful completion of onboarding and clearing your first **10 hours of work** locks in your project stability, establishes your reliability within the network, and grants you access to extended tasks and future project pipelines.
