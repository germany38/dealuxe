English Description

As a French Linguistic Expert at Mercor, your work directly impacts how large language models (LLMs) interpret, generate, and reason in French. You will work side-by-side with cutting-edge AI labs.

  • Text Evaluation: Critically assess LLM-generated French text across multiple verticals, including translation, creative writing, summarization, and syntax correction.
