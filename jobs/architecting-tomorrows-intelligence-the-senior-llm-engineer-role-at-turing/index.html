Deep Dive Into the Role: What You Will Do

This is an intensive, hands-on engineering position. You will not merely write speculative scripts; you will design, build, and deploy production-grade Generative AI and LLM-powered solutions. Your daily scope of work includes:

  • System Architecture: Designing and developing complex GenAI systems utilizing advanced tools like Langchain and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines.
  • Prompt Engineering & Agents: Implementing sophisticated prompt tuning techniques and agent-based frameworks to deliver intelligent, context-aware outputs.
  • Roadmap & Strategy: Collaborating closely with engineering leads to shape the technical roadmap for ongoing LLM initiatives.
  • Business Alignment: Translating complex business requirements into scalable, production-ready AI applications, working directly with business Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) and data teams.
  • Code Quality & Optimization: Driving architecture discussions, conducting rigorous code reviews, and optimizing overall model performance.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Experience

To succeed in this high-visibility role, candidates must combine rigorous software engineering foundations with specialized artificial intelligence expertise. The core requirements include:

Technical & Professional Requirements:

  • Core Programming: Deep proficiency in Python, Langchain, and SQL.
  • LLM Internals: Comprehensive understanding of prompt tuning, embedding generation, vector databases, and agent workflows.
  • Background: 7 to 12 years of overall professional experience in machine learning or software engineering with a strong emphasis on system-level thinking.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Hands-on experience working with major enterprise cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
  • Soft Skills: Independent execution capacity paired with exceptional communication and stakeholder management abilities.
