Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

Unlike rigid technical roles that require years of niche coding or specialized software engineering, the Generalist Expert position values versatile intellect, rigorous analytical skills, and clear communication. If you excel at spotting inconsistencies, evaluating written arguments, and structuring professional feedback, this role is custom-built for your capabilities.

What You’ll Do Day-to-Day:

  • Content Review & Evaluation: Thoroughly review and assess everyday professional deliverables, including complex documents, slide presentations, data spreadsheets, and general written materials.
  • Detailed Written Feedback: Provide clear, constructive, and well-reasoned written feedback and ratings that explain why specific outputs meet or fail rigorous professional quality bars.
  • Comparative Ranking: Analyze, compare, and rank multiple AI-generated outputs side-by-side against precise evaluation metrics and quality criteria.
  • Error & Gap Identification: Systematically flag logical errors, factual inconsistencies, formatting flaws, and gaps in reasoning or completeness.
  • Cross-Format Adaptability: Seamlessly transition between diverse subject matters and document formats without needing deep specialization in any single technical domain.
