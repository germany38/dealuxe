About the Role: What Does a Music Domain Expert Do?

As a Music Domain Expert at Turing, your primary responsibility is evaluating and improving Large Language Models (LLMs) across diverse musical disciplines. You will design challenging prompts, assess factual accuracy and reasoning, and identify deep knowledge gaps within music theory, genres, composers, artists, production techniques, and instrumentation.

Your work directly impacts how artificial intelligence comprehends human creativity, ensuring that future technological systems respect historical accuracy, musicological principles, and professional industry standards.

Key Responsibilities & Scope of Work:

  • Advanced Prompt Creation: Design sophisticated prompts covering music theory, composition, genres, artists, and audio production instruments.
  • Model Evaluation: Assess AI-generated responses for factual accuracy, reasoning quality, completeness, and nuanced interpretation.
  • Error Detection: Identify hallucinations, logical inconsistencies, outdated information, and edge cases in model outputs.
  • Benchmark Development: Build comprehensive benchmark datasets and adversarial test cases to challenge AI limits.
  • Evidence-Based Feedback: Provide authoritative, well-researched feedback backed by reliable musicological references.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Qualifications

This role is designed for dedicated music professionals who combine academic rigor with practical industry insight. To succeed in this engagement, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Educational Background: Master’s degree or higher in any field, with a strong preference for degrees in Music, Music Theory, Musicology, Ethnomusicology, Music Education, Music Production, Audio Engineering, or Performing Arts.
  • Domain Mastery: Comprehensive knowledge of music theory, composition, historical eras, genres, composers, artists, notation, and modern production techniques.
  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of relevant professional experience in music education, performance, composition, journalism, research, content creation, or audio engineering.
  • Analytical Rigor: Excellent written English, research skills, and exceptional attention to detail when evaluating complex musical data.

A Day in the Life of a Turing Music Expert

A typical workday combines deep analytical research, creative prompt engineering, and rigorous qualitative assessment. You might spend your morning designing adversarial test cases that challenge an AI's understanding of counterpoint or modal jazz harmony. In the afternoon, you evaluate model outputs, dissecting whether the system correctly attributes historical musical developments or accurately explains mixing and mastering workflows.

You collaborate closely with AI researchers to calibrate task complexity, maintaining meticulous documentation and high annotation standards.
