Inside the Role: Investment Banking & M&A Domain Expert

The specific opportunity highlighted above—the Investment Banking & M&A - Finance Domain Expert role—is designed for practitioners who want to stand at the absolute bleeding edge of technological evolution. Working directly with a premier AI lab’s research and program management teams, you will play a foundational role in shaping how large language models comprehend, analyze, and execute financial reasoning tasks.

Unlike standard consulting gigs or surface-level freelance tasks, this is a structured, high-impact career move. Employed via Cincinnatus LLC on a full-time W-2 basis, you will be embedded within the client's extended workforce, working alongside top-tier AI researchers using cutting-edge tools. Your day-to-day responsibilities will leverage the exact strategic and analytical competencies you built throughout your professional journey:

  • Data QA and Rigorous Reviews: You will vet the quality of complex finance knowledge work tasks and model outputs. This involves spotting missing behaviors, thin reasoning, flawed financial assumptions, and answers that might read well to an amateur but would fail under professional scrutiny.
  • Instruction Specs and Golden Datasets: You will author high-quality instruction specifications, produce authoritative golden solutions to intricate financial problems, and define brand-new benchmark tasks that reflect real-world financial execution.
  • Benchmarks and Domain Depth: By designing challenging evaluation sets, you help build specialized finance skills and tools that dictate whether an AI model is genuinely improving over time.
  • Calibration and Mental Model Translation: You will work alongside researchers to translate tacit financial judgment—the kind of intuition developed over years of closing deals—into explicit, teachable criteria for machine learning systems.

Core Qualifications and Who Should Apply

Because this position requires training models that will ultimately assist or automate institutional-grade financial analysis, the qualification bar is intentionally high. This is not an entry-level gig; it is built for senior practitioners who possess undeniable domain depth.

Candidates are expected to meet several core professional benchmarks:

  • Professional Experience: A minimum of 5+ years of substantive, dedicated professional finance experience at a recognized institution. This includes investment banks, top-tier asset managers, private equity or credit funds, Big Four accounting firms, corporate finance divisions of major enterprises, or financial regulators.
  • Specialized Discipline: Genuine specialization in core financial pillars such as corporate finance, FP&A, investment banking, M&A, asset/wealth management, private equity, quantitative finance, risk management, treasury, or accounting and auditing.
  • Seniority: Clear career progression to senior individual contributor or leadership roles—such as Vice President, Director, Principal, Managing Director, Portfolio Manager, Controller, or CFO.
  • Credentials & Education: Advanced degrees from rigorous programs (MBA, MS, or PhD in finance, economics, accounting, or quantitative disciplines) alongside professional designations like CFA, CPA, FRM, or actuarial credentials are strongly preferred.
  • AI Fluency: Hands-on familiarity with large language models in daily professional workflows, combined with the critical eye necessary to evaluate automated outputs.
