Detailed Job Description: What Does a BI Dashboards Evaluator Do?

As a BI Dashboards / Performance Reporting Evaluator on the Mercor network, your core responsibility is to act as the ultimate quality assurance checkpoint for cutting-edge artificial intelligence systems. You will be tasked with reviewing, assessing, and grading AI-generated work products—including comprehensive business documents, complex data spreadsheets, and polished slide decks—against stringent quality rubrics.

The evaluation process goes far beyond simple proofreading. It requires a sophisticated understanding of how data storytelling drives executive decision-making. Your day-to-day responsibilities will include:

  • Rigorous Content Evaluation: Systematically testing and analyzing AI-generated artifacts against specialized domain-specific quality rubrics to ensure absolute factual accuracy and structural rigor.
  • Error Identification & Correction: Pinpointing factual inaccuracies, aesthetic presentation flaws, data formatting inconsistencies, and logical fallacies within automated spreadsheet models and visualization layouts.
  • Actionable Feedback Generation: Providing clear, structured, and constructive written feedback that AI engineers and model trainers utilize to fine-tune subsequent iterations of language and multimodal models.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Interacting with top-tier AI researchers and project coordinators to align evaluation benchmarks with evolving industry standards.
