Healthcare & Medical AI Innovation

Discover how clinicians and medical researchers are partnering with Turing to train, evaluate, and refine next-generation artificial intelligence systems in healthcare.

Artificial intelligence is rapidly expanding across clinical domains, moving from theoretical utility into active diagnostic support, medical research acceleration, and patient workflow optimization. However, building reliable medical AI models requires far more than generic data ingestion; it requires rigorous clinical reasoning, expert human judgment, and nuanced evaluation frameworks rooted in real-world practice.

For licensed physicians and doctoral-level medical researchers, this technological evolution presents a unique career frontier. Through remote opportunities with Turing, medical experts can directly guide how frontier AI models understand medicine, ensuring that automated systems meet the highest standards of safety, precision, and clinical efficacy.

About Turing: Accelerating Frontier AI

Based in San Francisco, California, Turing operates as the world’s leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs and a trusted partner for global enterprises deploying advanced artificial intelligence systems. Turing supports customers through two primary pillars:

  • Accelerating Frontier Research: Providing high-quality datasets, advanced training pipelines, and elite domain experts specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and healthcare.
  • Enterprise Transformation: Translating AI proofs of concept into robust, proprietary intelligence that delivers measurable impact and reliable performance on the P&L.

About the Role: Medicine Physician (MD/DO/Doctoral Study/PhD)

The Medicine Physician role at Turing places you at the vanguard of medical technology development. You will collaborate directly with top-tier AI research teams to evaluate and enhance how machine learning models process complex medical information, diagnose conditions, and navigate clinical dilemmas.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like:

  • Systematic Framework Design: Design structured evaluation frameworks tailored specifically for medical AI systems.
  • Clinical Scenario Creation: Craft intricate patient cases and clinical scenarios that rigorously test AI reasoning, differential diagnosis, and decision-making capabilities.
  • Nuance Capture: Build assessment methodologies that capture the subtle nuances of expert clinical practice.
  • Gap Analysis: Identify vulnerabilities, errors, and knowledge gaps in AI medical reasoning.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Partner directly with AI researchers to translate clinical feedback into tangible model performance improvements.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Experience

To succeed in this role and deliver high-value insights to AI research teams, candidates should meet the following professional criteria:

  • Licensure & Credentials: Licensed physician in active clinical practice (any specialty) or doctoral-level medical study/PhD.
  • Clinical Acumen: Extensive experience with clinical decision-making, differential diagnosis, and evidence-based medicine.
  • Technological Curiosity: A strong interest in how artificial intelligence can safely and effectively support clinical practice.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent analytical, writing, and communication skills to articulate complex clinical nuances clearly.

Perks of Working with Turing

Balancing clinical practice with external projects requires flexibility. Turing is designed to support medical professionals without disrupting their primary clinical commitments:

  • Remote & Flexible: Fully remote setup with flexible scheduling that adapts around your clinical hours and patient loads.
  • Cutting-Edge Impact: Directly shape the safety and effectiveness of medical AI tools used globally.
  • Professional Growth: Expand your career portfolio into health-tech, AI governance, and clinical informatics.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Process

Understanding the structure of your engagement helps set clear expectations for a successful collaboration:

  • Commitment & Schedule: Flexible remote engagement requiring up to 30 hours per week.
  • Duration: Initial 1-month term, with strong potential for extensions based on performance and mutual fit.
  • Onboarding & Initial Milestone: All new experts complete a structured onboarding process followed by your first 10 hours of work. This initial milestone ensures workflow stability, validates system access, and opens the door to ongoing, expanded task assignments.

Interview Process

Turing maintains a streamlined yet rigorous vetting process designed to evaluate your clinical expertise, analytical clarity, and suitability for AI evaluation tasks. Expect professional background verification, credential checks, and task-based evaluations that demonstrate your ability to articulate clinical reasoning.
