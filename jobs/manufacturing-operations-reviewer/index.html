Detailed Job Description & Core Review Responsibilities

Unlike standard administrative or production roles where you build products from scratch, this position is strictly focused on auditing AI work products. You act as the ultimate quality-control expert, evaluating whether an AI agent's attempt at a realistic industrial task holds up to real-world scrutiny.

When reviewing an item—which typically takes about an hour—you examine the agent's complete work package: the prompt, the agent's generated strategy, its produced files, and the corresponding AI grader scoresheet. Your objective is to apply your field expertise to answer four core questions:

1. Realism & Task Validity

Would this scenario actually happen on a real shop floor, warehouse, or engineering department, described this way? You evaluate whether the task is realistic, borderline, or completely contrived.

2. Domain Accuracy

Are the technical standards, industrial figures, regulatory guidelines, and structural constraints accurate and consistent? You catch subtle errors or impossible constraints that only a seasoned practitioner would spot immediately.

3. Grader Logic Validation

The automated AI grader evaluates the work across eleven specific yes/no criteria—ranging from formatting constraints and fabricated metrics to technical requirements. You review the files and document precisely where you agree or disagree with the grader's logic.

4. Defensible Scoring

You assign your own professional performance band to the work. Could a practicing engineer or floor supervisor actually use or accept this output? You then check the reported score against your expert band.

Key Operational Note: You never perform the task yourself or redo the agent's work. Your entire focus is applied judgment, auditing, and structured feedback based on your professional career.

Targeted Profiles: Who Mercor Is Looking For

Mercor is recruiting across five distinct professional categories. Candidates must apply to the track that precisely matches their actual career background:

  • Buyers and Purchasing Agents: 5+ years placing real purchase orders (POs) and negotiating directly with suppliers for which you were fully accountable.
  • First-Line Supervisors of Production and Operating Workers: 5+ years running an active shift or manufacturing line, owning operational schedules and personnel performance.
  • Mechanical Engineers: 4+ years designing structural parts or mechanical systems that were successfully fabricated and built (Professional Engineer / PE certification is a strong plus).
  • Industrial Engineers: 3+ years conducting capacity, throughput, facility layout, or time studies that materially changed how a plant operated.
  • Shipping, Receiving, and Inventory Clerks: 4+ years owning receiving pipelines, cycle counts, and inventory accuracy in a high-volume warehouse or plant environment.

Ideal candidates know their industry cold—having executed root-cause investigations, run Production Part Approval Processes (PPAP), managed first-article inspections, resolved cycle-count variances, or corrected erroneous method sheets. Candidates whose experience is limited solely to enterprise planning software or theoretical simulations are generally less suited for this role.
