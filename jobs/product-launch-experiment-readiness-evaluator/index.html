Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For seasoned product managers, growth leads, and operations professionals seeking flexible, high-yield remote income, understanding the scope of this role is crucial. This position requires senior-level professional judgment to audit, grade, and refine AI-generated work products, ensuring that artificial intelligence systems can accurately simulate, plan, and evaluate complex commercial releases.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and audit AI-generated spreadsheets, strategic documents, and slide decks focused on product launch plans, feature rollouts, and experiment design metrics.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify factual errors, logical flaws, analytical omissions, and presentation mistakes in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written feedback to help train and align frontier AI models with enterprise-grade product management standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex product launch frameworks and readiness rubrics generated by AI align with best practices in risk management, statistical significance, and milestone tracking.
