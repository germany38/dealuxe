Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For individuals looking to transition their analytical, editorial, or regulatory expertise into the artificial intelligence sector, understanding the day-to-day expectations is essential. AI Safety Practitioners operate at the intersection of policy and machine learning, meticulously reviewing complex, ambiguous, and high-stakes model outputs.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Rigorous Output Evaluation: Assess AI-generated responses for safety, factual accuracy, policy compliance, and overall qualitative excellence.
  • High-Stakes Content Review: Scrutinize content involving sensitive domains such as misinformation, political persuasion, self-harm, physical violence, cyber threats, and biosecurity.
  • Rubric Development & Application: Apply and refine complex evaluation rubrics supporting Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF), Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), and comprehensive AI safety benchmarking.
  • Error Identification: Pinpoint subtle unsafe outputs, factual hallucinations, flawed logical reasoning, and explicit policy violations.
  • Structured Feedback Provision: Deliver clear, actionable, and structured feedback to guide model alignment and elevate safety performance over successive training iterations.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration: Partner directly with leading AI researchers, engineers, and dedicated safety teams on ongoing evaluation initiatives.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because the decisions made by safety practitioners directly influence how artificial intelligence models interpret nuance and adhere to safety standards, the professional prerequisites are rigorous.

Required Qualifications:
  • Educational Background: A Bachelor's degree or higher in Journalism, Communications, Psychology, Sociology, Public Policy, Law, Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, or an equivalent discipline.
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of dedicated professional experience in AI Safety, Trust & Safety, journalism, public policy, academic or scientific research, security, or related fields.
  • Language & Analytical Mastery: Excellent written English proficiency coupled with superior critical thinking and analytical reasoning skills.
  • Nuanced Judgment: Demonstrated capability to consistently evaluate complex, ambiguous, and policy-sensitive scenarios without bias.

Preferred Qualifications: Direct prior experience with AI Safety, RLHF, SFT, Trust & Safety operations, or AI evaluation frameworks; familiarity with modern safety policies and content moderation structures.
