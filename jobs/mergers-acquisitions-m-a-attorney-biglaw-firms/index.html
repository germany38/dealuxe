The Evolution of Legal Practice: Why Frontier AI Needs M&A Experts

For years, early artificial intelligence models were trained on generalized internet text, public court dockets, and basic summaries. While sufficient for casual conversational tasks, general-purpose models routinely stumble when confronted with nuanced corporate structuring, intricate stock and asset purchase agreements, indemnification caps, and multi-jurisdictional regulatory compliance. In transactional law, an AI hallucination or a flawed interpretation of a representation and warranty provision is unacceptable.

To eliminate these risks, micro1 constructs the human intelligence layer for frontier AI systems. By partnering with legal professionals who have spent years managing high-stakes corporate transactions inside top-tier law firms, micro1 transforms real-world transactional experience into rigorous training data, evaluation rubrics, and feedback loops. No prior coding or software engineering background is necessary; your deep, practical command of corporate law is the core asset driving the technology.
