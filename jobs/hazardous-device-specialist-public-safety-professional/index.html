The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Public Safety Practitioners

As artificial intelligence systems become more advanced, they ingest vast repositories of technical literature. However, raw data ingestion creates a dangerous vulnerability: knowing the line between educational, professional utility and malicious misuse. Generalist evaluators lack the nuanced, hands-on framework required to evaluate complex technical safety domains where safety margins are razor-thin.

This is where seasoned public safety professionals step in. The same rigorous assessment that teaches a certified technician how to safely approach and render safe a device also describes its structural mechanics. The compatibility data that keeps industrial materials stable in storage shares technical overlap with misuse vectors. An AI model that unnecessarily refuses a routine, professional render-safe or storage inquiry is failing as fundamentally as one that provides instructions for harm. Drawing and defending that exact operational line requires real-world practitioners.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Challenging Prompt Generation: Craft intricate, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific domain expertise, categorized systematically across three levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation & Auditing: Review model-generated responses against established safety policy frameworks, rendering precise judgments on whether the model handled the scenario accurately.
  • Authoring Reference Answers: Formulate expert reference answers detailing what a correct, safe response looks like, alongside the rigorous technical reasoning underpinning your conclusions.

💡 Note on Compliance: Your work with Mercor must strictly avoid drawing on classified, export-controlled data or information bound by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. If you maintain such clearances, specify them in your application so the scope can be appropriately tailored.
