Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

Designed for quota-carrying individual contributors with a hunger for scaling territory from scratch, this role places you directly at the heart of commercial expansion. As an Enterprise Account Executive and B2B Sales Leader, you are tasked with creating authentic business value, evangelizing cutting-edge solutions, and steering complex multi-stakeholder negotiations across Fortune 500 prospects.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Pipeline Generation & Qualification: Proactively generate and qualify robust commercial pipelines through targeted outbound strategies, executive networking, industry events, and strategic partnerships.
  • Consultative Sales Cycles: Run rigorous, high-level consultative cycles with senior enterprise operators and technical leaders, constructing airtight business cases that secure executive budget approval.
  • Technical Scoping & Collaboration: Partner closely with internal engineering teams to scope technical deployments, orchestrate proof-of-concepts (POCs) with distinct success metrics, and negotiate multi-year agreements to signature.
  • Account Expansion & Hygiene: Expand existing accounts beyond initial deal closures, maintain pristine CRM data hygiene, and deliver highly accurate revenue forecasts.
  • Market Feedback Loops: Act as the vital bridge between the field and corporate headquarters, feeding actionable market signals back to product and go-to-market leadership teams.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these enterprise sales contracts involve high-stakes negotiations and deep technical products, the bar for qualification reflects a proven track record of elite commercial execution.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of enterprise B2B sales experience, ideally selling complex technical, data-driven, or software platform solutions directly to the Fortune 500.
  • Quota Performance: A demonstrable, consistent track record of hitting and exceeding quota while successfully closing complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise deals ($500K+ ACV).
  • Dual-Buyer Fluency: Exceptional comfort selling simultaneously to C-suite executives and technical engineering buyers, with the unique ability to translate ambiguous enterprise pain points into a concrete financial value thesis.
  • Core Sales Competencies: Master-level discovery execution, advanced commercial negotiation tactics, and sophisticated pipeline-generation skills.
  • Startup Agility: The ability to thrive seamlessly in a fast-moving, high-autonomy startup environment where initiative and self-direction drive success.

Nice-to-Have Backgrounds: Prior hands-on experience selling AI/ML models, deep data architectures, or professional-services solutions; history of operating as an early or founding AE responsible for building an enterprise territory completely from scratch.
