Role Overview: Korean Voice & Annotation Specialist

Turing is actively seeking skilled Korean voice actors and linguistic annotators to bring characters, narratives, and conversational agents to life. This role requires capturing the precise emotion, personality, nuance, and vocal tone necessary to train modern interactive AI models.

Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Record high-quality voice-overs from a professional home studio or dedicated recording sessions.
  • Perform script lines with absolute clarity, appropriate emotional resonance, and precise pacing.
  • Interpret narrative and conversational scripts while taking creative direction effectively.
  • Revise vocal performances dynamically based on evaluation feedback.
  • Deliver clean audio files on schedule and formatted according to precise technical standards.

Are You a Fit? Required Skills & Experience

To thrive in this role, candidates must demonstrate strong vocal versatility and solid linguistic capabilities in Korean. Review the core criteria below:

  • Voice Acting Experience: Proven background in voice-over work, performance arts, or media localization.
  • Vocal Control & Versatility: Strong range, clear speech articulation, and excellent emotional delivery.
  • Technical Setup: Access to professional-grade recording equipment suitable for remote audio delivery.
  • Communication: Outstanding communication and responsiveness to direction and feedback.

Preferred Qualifications: Experience with dubbing, automated dialogue replacement (ADR), professional demo reels, or familiarity with digital audio editing tools.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

Engaging with Turing provides unique professional advantages for independent experts:

  • Complete Location Flexibility: Work seamlessly in a fully remote environment.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Collaborate directly on innovative AI projects alongside top-tier LLM developers.
  • Contract Growth Potential: Opportunities for project extensions based on performance and ongoing client needs.

Engagement Details & Evaluation Process

This position operates as a flexible contractual engagement rather than a standard full-time or part-time employment structure. The evaluation and onboarding sequence includes:

  1. Initial Shortlisting: Review based on submitted qualifications and relevant professional experience.
  2. Delivery Review: Shortlisted candidates undergo a practical delivery evaluation.
  3. Onboarding & Stability Period: Complete the onboarding workflow and commit to the initial 10 hours of work to establish system stability, ensure smooth integration, and unlock access to advanced tasks.
