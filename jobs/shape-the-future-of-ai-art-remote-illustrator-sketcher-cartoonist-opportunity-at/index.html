Remote Career Opportunity • AI & Design

Combine your traditional drawing expertise with cutting-edge technology. Discover how creative professionals are accelerating AI model capabilities at Turing.

The intersection of artificial intelligence and creative design has opened up unprecedented avenues for artists worldwide. As artificial intelligence models expand their multimodal capabilities, understanding visual nuance, character consistency, and artistic style has become a paramount priority for top technology labs. For digital artists, illustrators, and cartoonists, this technological shift presents a thrilling opportunity to work at the bleeding edge of innovation.

Turing, one of the world’s fastest-growing AI companies, is spearheading this evolution. By partnering with leading AI research laboratories and building mission-critical real-world systems, Turing is redefining what machines can understand and create. If you have ever wanted your artistic portfolio to directly contribute to the next generation of frontier AI models, this remote engagement offers the ideal platform.

About Turing and the Mission

Turing helps customers in two primary ways: working alongside the world’s leading AI labs to advance frontier model capabilities across thinking, reasoning, multimodality, multilinguality, and frontier knowledge; and leveraging that engineering foundation to build real-world AI systems that solve complex priorities for enterprise clients.

Creativity and visual reasoning play a foundational role in training advanced multimodal systems. By introducing human artistic intuition into the training loop, artists help shape AI models that comprehend proportion, perspective, lighting, and character integrity with remarkable precision.

Role Overview: Illustrator, Sketcher & Cartoonist

Turing is actively looking for talented illustrators, sketchers, cartoonists, digital artists, and concept artists who can create high-quality artwork from reference images while introducing creative variations and maintaining strict character identity.

This opportunity is welcomingly open to interns, freshers, emerging artists, and seasoned industry professionals who possess strong visual skills and a compelling portfolio demonstrating versatility, creativity, and meticulous attention to detail.

Project Snapshot & Engagement Details

  • Project Duration: 10–12 weeks of collaborative remote work.
  • Time Commitment: Ideally up to 40 hours/week for full-time contributors, with part-time options up to 20 hours/week warmly welcomed.
  • Compensation: Fully remote positions paid securely in USD.
  • Start Date: Immediate start as soon as portfolio approval and onboarding are completed.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

Working as a remote creative professional on this project involves a dynamic mix of traditional illustration techniques and structured metadata documentation:

  • Digital Creation: Producing digital character illustrations and sketches using detailed reference images provided by the team.
  • Creative Variation: Generating diverse stylistic iterations while faithfully preserving core character identity and visual continuity.
  • Core Artistic Principles: Applying fundamental mastery of composition, color theory, lighting, perspective, and anatomical proportion.
  • Theme Adaptation: Seamlessly adapting artwork across diverse visual themes based on evolving project requirements.
  • Documentation: Recording creative decisions, style choices, and simple prompt metadata to assist model training pipelines.
  • Timely Delivery: Polishing and delivering high-grade artwork within assigned project timelines.

You Are a Fit If You Bring:

To thrive in this role, candidates should possess a robust background in visual arts alongside adaptability and technical proficiency:

  • Demonstrated experience in illustration, sketching, cartooning, concept art, or closely related creative fields.
  • A strong, diverse portfolio showcasing creative flair, versatility, and meticulous attention to detail.
  • Experience across multiple artistic styles, such as character design, stylized illustration, surrealism, cinematic art, vintage/retro aesthetics, steampunk, or glitch art.
  • Proficiency in industry-standard tools including Adobe Photoshop, Procreate, Adobe Illustrator, Clip Studio Paint, Affinity Suite, or similar digital software.
  • Independent work ethic, openness to feedback and revisions, and strict adherence to deadlines.

Perks of Working with Turing

Engaging with Turing provides unique advantages for creative professionals seeking flexibility and cutting-edge experience:

  • Global Collaboration: Work alongside top-tier talent and innovative minds shaping the future of global technology.
  • Complete Remote Freedom: Enjoy location independence with flexible scheduling options ranging from part-time (20 hours) to full-time (40 hours).
  • Competitive USD Compensation: Earn reliable, competitive pay while building a groundbreaking portfolio item.

Interview Process, Onboarding, and Stability Phase

Turing maintains a streamlined yet rigorous evaluation process to ensure mutual fit:

  1. Application & Portfolio Submission: Submit your professional profile and portfolio showcasing your artistic range.
  2. Portfolio Review: Shortlisted candidates undergo a thorough portfolio assessment by the curation team.
  3. Onboarding: Candidates with approved portfolios proceed directly with formal onboarding.
  4. Initial 10-Hour Stability Phase: Upon onboarding, contributors complete an initial 10 hours of work. This stability phase ensures alignment with project workflows, validates task quality, and unlocks access to further ongoing tasks.
