The Mission: Building Internal Developer Infrastructure for AI Scale

Scaling a global expert network to train and evaluate AI models requires massive operational throughput. As micro1 rapidly expands its network of specialized professionals across finance, healthcare, STEM engineering, and law, the internal systems powering vetting, data ingestion, evaluation loops, and task routing must scale seamlessly without friction.

As a Software Engineer on the Internal Platforms core team, you will not be writing isolated features in a silo; you will own critical operational infrastructure end-to-end. Your code directly empowers internal operations teams, product managers, and external expert contributors by removing technical bottlenecks and ensuring ultra-high platform reliability and observability.

What You Will Own and Build:

  • End-to-End Fullstack Architecture: Design, develop, and scale fullstack systems powering critical internal workflows, vetting pipelines, and platform capabilities.
  • Scalable Backend Services & APIs: Build clean, modular backend services with clear contracts, utilizing TypeScript, Node.js (with NestJS familiarity a strong plus), and secure RESTful APIs.
  • Performant Frontend Interfaces: Create intuitive, high-performance user interfaces in React that internal stakeholders and operations teams rely on daily.
  • Database Optimization & Schema Design: Participate actively in relational database modeling, schema design, and query tuning using PostgreSQL to maintain high data integrity under heavy loads.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with product, design, and operations teams to translate ambiguous requirements into concrete, highly scalable technical systems.
  • Reliability & Observability: Improve developer experience, platform reliability, and system observability by establishing robust testing standards, CI/CD pipelines, and proactive monitoring practices.

💡 The Engineering Philosophy: micro1 operates in a fast-moving, high-growth environment where engineers are expected to make pragmatic tradeoffs between speed, scalability, and maintainability. If you thrive on autonomy and love building internal developer tooling that multiplies team output, this role is built for you.

Required Qualifications and Technical Proficiencies

Because micro1 builds cutting-edge AI infrastructure, engineering standards are exceptionally high. Candidates are evaluated on both technical execution and problem-solving mindset:

  • Fullstack Engineering Experience: Demonstrated professional experience, internships, or robust personal project history building and maintaining production-grade fullstack systems.
  • TypeScript & Modern Frameworks: Strong TypeScript fundamentals spanning both frontend and backend development, alongside proficiency in React and Node.js ecosystems.
  • Relational Database Expertise: Solid understanding of relational databases, specifically PostgreSQL, alongside core data modeling and query optimization principles.
  • Cloud & Distributed Architecture: Familiarity with cloud infrastructure platforms such as AWS, RESTful communication patterns, and modern microservices or event-driven architectures.
  • Preferred Backgrounds: Prior experience building internal developer platforms (IDPs), operational dashboard tooling, CI/CD automation, or working within high-growth startup and AI-adjacent environments.
