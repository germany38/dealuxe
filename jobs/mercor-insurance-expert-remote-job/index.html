Detailed Job Description: What Senior Insurance Professionals Will Do

Modern artificial intelligence systems are rapidly evolving to handle complex business processes, but they require master-level human guidance to navigate the nuances of high-stakes corporate sectors. Mercor is seeking senior insurance professionals to build sophisticated evaluation tasks for AI systems operating in Fortune 500 enterprise insurance and risk contexts. These workflows are meticulously calibrated to match the underwriting complexity, stringent regulatory stakes, and vast claims scale characteristic of Fortune 500 and large public insurance carriers.

Contributors in this role do not perform standard data entry or basic customer service tasks. Instead, you will act as a high-level architect of cognitive testing scenarios. Your responsibilities include:

  • Enterprise Scenario Construction: Design complex commercial underwriting cases, multi-stakeholder claims adjudication challenges, and regulatory filing or compliance review cycles typical of top-tier carriers like State Farm, Allstate, Chubb, AIG, and Berkshire Hathaway.
  • Multi-Disciplinary Task Building: Build comprehensive insurance tasks spanning enterprise underwriting, risk assessment, advanced claims management, actuarial pricing and reserving, reinsurance strategy, and regulatory/compliance affairs.
  • Tool Integration: Incorporate workflows involving industry-standard platforms and enterprise stacks, including Guidewire, Duck Creek, actuarial modeling platforms (SAS, R), and enterprise policy administration systems.
  • Methodology Application: Apply enterprise insurance methodologies—such as actuarial risk modeling, loss reserving, Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) frameworks, and NAIC compliance standards—to produce professional reference underwriting guidelines and executive risk narratives.
  • Rubric Authoring: Write meticulous evaluation rubrics that distinguish authentic enterprise insurance judgment from generic textbook knowledge or basic licensing-exam recall.

Ideal Qualifications and Professional Background

Because these evaluation tasks directly influence cutting-edge enterprise AI frameworks, standards are exceptionally high. Mercor looks for candidates who can demonstrate true mastery of their craft. To thrive in this position, applicants should meet the following benchmarks:

  • Experience Threshold: 5+ years of hands-on professional experience working in underwriting, claims, actuarial science, or risk management at a Fortune 500 insurance carrier, reinsurer, or inside an enterprise risk/insurance organization.
  • Portfolio Ownership: Direct, verifiable ownership of large-scale underwriting portfolios, complex claims operations, or institutional actuarial pricing models.
  • Technical Fluency: Deep familiarity with enterprise insurance tooling, compliance frameworks, reinsurance treaties, and reserve practices.
  • Educational Background: Prior background in rubric design, professional training curriculum development, or advanced insurance documentation authorship is considered a strong advantage.

Important Contracting Terms to Keep in Mind

Engaged as an independent contractor, you retain total freedom over your schedule. Projects are fully remote, and payments are fulfilled weekly via Stripe or Wise.
