The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Rigorous Aerodynamic Modeling

Traditional machine learning training relies heavily on static text corpora and web data. However, when artificial intelligence systems are deployed to assist aerospace engineers, defense contractors, and automotive designers, generalist models frequently hallucinate or fail to respect fundamental conservation laws, Navier-Stokes equations, and boundary-layer physics.

micro1 is building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI by engaging top-tier practitioners whose daily work involves wind tunnel testing, CFD mesh generation, and flight-test data reduction. By translating real-world engineering intuition into high-quality training data, evaluation benchmarks, and interactive feedback loops, experts ensure that future AI models can accurately interpret, solve, and explain complex aerodynamic challenges spanning external aero, propulsion integration, and vehicle performance.

Detailed Scope of Work & Responsibilities:

  • CFD Workflow Simulation: Design, solve, and validate aerodynamic and fluid-dynamics problems using command-line-accessible tools, configuration files, and script-driven pipelines.
  • Model Setup & Meshing: Translate physical engineering requirements into computational models. Create or modify geometries, computational domains, and high-quality meshes while defining correct boundary conditions, fluid properties, and solver settings.
  • Python Automation: Leverage Python to automate preprocessing, simulation execution, parameter sweeps, post-processing, and verification routines.
  • Performance Analysis: Analyze pressure, velocity, forces, moments, lift, drag, pressure loss, and other relevant aerodynamic outputs to determine numerical and physical validity.
  • AI Output Auditing: Review and assess AI-generated technical responses for engineering correctness, precision, and adherence to established AIAA standards and verification protocols.
  • Technical Documentation: Author clear written rationales, documentation, and peer reviews to refine AI reasoning loops and enhance system reliability.

💡 Operational Expectation: Because tasks require reproducible, programmatic workflows (rather than exclusively graphical user interfaces), familiarity with script-accessible tools like OpenFOAM, ANSYS Fluent, STAR-CCM+, or custom Python scripting is essential for success in this role.

  • Communication Excellence: Professional English proficiency with exceptional written and verbal communication skills, enabling the clear articulation of nuanced engineering concepts.
