Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

Artificial intelligence models are scaling at an exponential rate, but raw computational power is not enough. Tech labs and enterprise developers require sophisticated human oversight to ensure these systems reason accurately, adhere to safety guardrails, and understand nuanced cultural contexts. This is where your specialized linguistic skill set comes into play.

As a Bilingual Thai Generalist Expert at Mercor, your primary mission is to evaluate, audit, and strengthen how advanced AI models handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use topics in the Thai language.
