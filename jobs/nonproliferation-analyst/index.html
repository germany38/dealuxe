The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Nonproliferation Experts

Modern language models ingest massive quantities of public data, including scientific papers, industrial handbooks, and international trade regulations. However, raw data ingestion creates a critical vulnerability: understanding the fine line between educational, professional utility and malicious circumvention. Generalist evaluators cannot reliably distinguish between a legitimate procurement query and an evasion attempt.

This is where specialized nonproliferation analysts step in. The same control list that stops an unauthorized shipment also informs a legitimate procurement agent what to avoid declaring; the pathway analysis used to design national policy can mirror illicit routes read in reverse. An AI model that unnecessarily refuses a standard export-classification inquiry is failing as fundamentally as one that provides dangerous instructions. Drawing and defending that exact operational line requires seasoned practitioners rather than generalists.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Challenging Prompt Generation: Construct intricate, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific domain expertise, systematically categorized across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation & Auditing: Review model-generated responses against rigorous safety policy standards, rendering precise judgments on whether the model handled each scenario correctly.
  • Authoring Reference Answers: Formulate expert reference answers outlining what a correct, secure response looks like, alongside the detailed technical reasoning supporting your evaluation.

💡 Note on Compliance: Your work with Mercor will not involve and must not draw upon classified information, export-controlled data, or materials bound by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. If you maintain such clearances, specify them in your application so the scope can be tailored accordingly.

Ideal Backgrounds and Qualifications

Mercor maintains exceptional vetting standards for its red-teaming panels to ensure maximum data fidelity. Candidates ideally possess professional experience, academic credentials, and practical backgrounds in fields such as:

  • Export Control Licensing & Enforcement: Expertise in dual-use lists, catch-all controls, classification standards, and end-user screening protocols.
  • Treaty & Agreement Implementation: Professional familiarity with the NPT, CTBT, IAEA safeguards agreements, and bilateral cooperation frameworks.
  • Proliferation Pathway & Programme Analysis: Background conducting assessments at national laboratories, governmental agencies, ministries, or independent research institutes.
  • Sanctions & Illicit Trade Analysis: Practical experience examining illicit procurement networks, financial flows, and trade anomalies.
  • Open-Source & Technical Analysis: Specialized skills in open-source intelligence (OSINT) and technical evaluations of nuclear or advanced industrial programs.

Beyond Technical Depth: The Demand for Clear Writing

This position is writing-intensive. Technical mastery alone is not enough; every judgment and classification you submit must be supported by a structured, articulated written rationale that a non-specialist project coordinator can easily follow. Candidates with prior technical writing experience, published research, whitepapers, or expert witness testimony backgrounds will find themselves strongly positioned during review.
