Artificial intelligence has evolved at a breathtaking pace, moving past simple chat interactions into complex reasoning, multi-step problem solving, and professional work creation. However, as large language models (LLMs) and generative tools take on sophisticated business documents, presentations, and spreadsheet analytics, they encounter a critical bottleneck: the need for rigorous, high-level human oversight.

For detail-oriented professionals, educators, writers, and multidisciplinary thinkers, this technological boom has unlocked a brand-new professional landscape. Platforms like Mercor are bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI labs and human expertise, offering lucrative remote roles that value critical thinking above all else. The Generalist Expert (US/Canada) position on Mercor pays an impressive $50 to $70 per hour, providing a flexible, fully remote contracting avenue for individuals who excel at analyzing information, identifying errors, and explaining complex reasoning clearly.

Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

You don't need a background in software engineering or machine learning to thrive in this role. In fact, tech labs specifically seek out sharp generalists who possess strong reading comprehension, exceptional written communication skills, and sound analytical judgment across varied subject matters. Your day-to-day work directly impacts how future artificial intelligence models reason about and produce real-world professional work products.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Content Review & Evaluation: Thoroughly review and assess diverse everyday professional artifacts, including complex documents, slide decks, financial spreadsheets, and written materials for quality, clarity, and correctness.
  • Written Reasoning & Feedback: Provide clear, meticulous, and well-reasoned written explanations and quantitative ratings justifying your evaluations.
  • Output Comparison & Ranking: Compare and rank multiple AI-generated responses against pre-defined organizational criteria, precision standards, and logic guidelines.
  • Error Detection: Flag logical inconsistencies, factual errors, reasoning gaps, or formatting flaws in model outputs to safeguard training datasets.
  • Adherence to Guidelines: Follow structured project rubrics and context guidelines provided by top-tier AI researchers to ensure consistency across model training rounds.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because the output of these evaluations trains frontier AI architectures, applicants must meet specific baseline criteria to qualify for review:

Essential Requirements:
  • Educational Foundation: Minimum requirement of a Bachelor’s degree in any academic discipline.
  • Geographic Location: Legally based in the United States or Canada.
  • Communication Mastery: Flawless reading comprehension and pristine written communication skills in English.
  • Attention to Detail: Strong analytical focus, meticulous eye for detail, and sound judgment across diverse subject domains.
  • Digital Tool Proficiency: Comfortable working independently across standard productivity suites, including word processors, presentation software, and spreadsheet applications.

Note: No prior artificial intelligence or machine-learning background is necessary. Mercor provides all necessary guidelines, rubrics, and onboarding context to help you succeed.
