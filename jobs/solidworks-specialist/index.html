Demystifying the Role: Translating Craft into Computational Intelligence

Traditional CAD software empowers engineers to visualize and iterate physical components with incredible precision. However, teaching an artificial intelligence model how to interpret design intent, manage feature trees, optimize geometric constraints, and balance strict Design for Manufacturing (DFM) requirements is an entirely different discipline. Models cannot learn structural logic or spatial mechanics through algorithms alone; they require the nuanced oversight of seasoned practitioners who understand the physical reality behind every tolerance callout and extrusion.

As a SolidWorks Specialist on this project, your primary objective is to translate professional mechanical design expertise into structured, machine-readable training data. Operating as a crucial contributor for an innovative client, you will construct, evaluate, and refine complex 3D parametric models that teach artificial intelligence systems how to reason, plan, and execute industrial design tasks flawlessly.

Core Competencies & Professional Requirements

This engagement is engineered specifically for accomplished CAD professionals who possess deep, practical knowledge of mechanical assemblies and manufacturing processes. Candidates looking to excel in this role should meet the following benchmarks:

  • CAD Fluency: Extensive background in 3D parametric CAD modeling, with proficiency in industry-standard packages such as SolidWorks, FreeCAD, Fusion 360, or Autodesk Inventor. Note: While primary evaluation leverages strong SolidWorks expertise, projects utilize FreeCAD, making familiarity easily transferable.
  • Experience Threshold: 4+ years of hands-on parametric CAD experience for editing models, and 10+ years for the creation and management of complex multi-part assemblies.
  • Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T): Practical application of GD&T in accordance with ASME Y14.5 or equivalent manufacturing standards.
  • Manufacturing Domain Knowledge: Proven track record supporting at least two or more primary manufacturing methods, including injection molding, sheet metal fabrication, CNC machining, or casting and forging.
  • Advanced Capabilities: Active engagement in industrial CAD modeling, with added advantages given to professionals skilled in reverse engineering (scan-to-CAD, point-cloud processing) or CAD workflow automation (Python, iLogic, VBA).

Detailed Scope of Work & Project Responsibilities

Contractors selected for this initiative are entrusted with core technical deliverables that shape how automated engineering systems understand structural design. Key responsibilities include:

  • Parametric Modeling: Creating fully-parametric 3D CAD models of intricate mechanical parts and sub-assemblies based on comprehensive written briefs, ensuring robust feature trees and precise geometric constraints.
  • Model Evolution: Editing and updating existing parametric models to accommodate shifting technical specifications while maintaining rigorous documentation of all geometric changes.
  • Auditing & Quality Assurance: Reviewing models for dimensional accuracy, correct tolerance stack-ups, and absolute manufacturability across diverse production pipelines.
  • DFM Integration: Applying deep foundational knowledge of Design for Manufacturing principles—covering draft angles in injection molding, bend allowances in sheet metal, tool paths in CNC machining, and draft requirements in casting and forging.
  • Data Structuring: Translating conceptual design intent and technical requirements into clean, structured CAD datasets suitable for high-performance AI training loops.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Communicating complex design logic clearly through detailed written reports and structured technical updates with project coordinators.
