To eliminate these vulnerabilities, micro1 constructs the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By partnering with attorneys who have spent years navigating high-stakes corporate transactions, private equity deals, and complex M&A negotiations within BigLaw environments, micro1 translates real-world legal acumen into high-fidelity training data, rigorous evaluation rubrics, and interactive feedback loops.
