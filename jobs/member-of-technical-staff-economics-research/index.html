Why Economic Reasoning is the Next Great Frontier for Artificial Intelligence

For years, natural language processing models were evaluated primarily on conversational fluency and general knowledge trivia. However, enterprise adoption requires AI agents capable of autonomous economic decision-making, policy impact simulation, forecasting, and complex resource allocation. When an AI model is deployed to analyze market trends, evaluate monetary policy, or optimize supply chain pricing, superficial patterns are insufficient; it requires genuine economic reasoning grounded in empirical methodology.

micro1 is building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI by engaging world-class quantitative researchers. As a Member of Technical Staff specializing in Economics Research, your work will directly shape how large language models and multi-agent systems understand market mechanics, consumer behavior, and macroeconomic shifts. You will transition economic theory into computable benchmarks that determine whether next-generation AI agents are ready for enterprise deployment.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Evaluation Framework Architecture: Design, build, and evolve advanced evaluation frameworks for AI systems operating in economic domains, establishing rigorous benchmarks, scoring methodologies, and quality rubrics.
  • Applied Economic Research: Conduct high-level research on economic reasoning and empirical analysis, translating complex findings into measurable, actionable improvements in AI model performance.
  • Dataset & Benchmark Development: Develop specialized datasets, test cases, and simulation environments that reflect real-world economic research, industrial organization, and public policy challenges.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with researchers, software engineers, data scientists, and product teams to evaluate and refine economics-focused AI models and agentic workflows.
  • Model Behavior & Failure Mode Analysis: Analyze complex model outputs and failure modes to pinpoint research opportunities, eliminate hallucinations, and enhance system reliability.
  • Econometric Methodology Application: Apply advanced econometric methods, causal inference, and statistical analysis to rigorously evaluate AI capabilities on multifaceted economic tasks.
  • Technical Thought Leadership: Produce comprehensive research papers, technical reports, and industry best practices that advance the standard of AI evaluation in economics.
