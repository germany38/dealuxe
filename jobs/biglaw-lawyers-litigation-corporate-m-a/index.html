Generic models frequently fail when confronted with the subtle nuances of U.S. law, statutory interpretation, and complex case law.

This is why micro1 is building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By engaging attorneys with rigorous training from top-tier U.S. law firms, micro1 transforms real-world legal expertise into high-fidelity training data, evaluations, and interactive feedback loops.
