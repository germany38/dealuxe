Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For writers, editors, communications directors, and media specialists seeking lucrative, flexible remote engagements, understanding the scope of this role is crucial. The position tasks senior-level professionals with grading, reviewing, and perfecting complex AI-generated work products—ranging from informative documents and press releases to structured slide presentations—to ensure they match absolute standards of accuracy and clarity.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact and Output Evaluation: Audit and assess AI-generated documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks against strict domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Editorial Rigor & Fact-Checking: Identify subtle factual inaccuracies, stylistic inconsistencies, structural flaws, and presentation errors in automated textual and visual assets.
  • Constructive Feedback Generation: Provide precise, structured, and actionable written guidance to align frontier AI outputs with professional journalistic and corporate communication standards.
  • Domain Quality Assurance: Ensure that complex communication frameworks, media strategies, and public relations deliverables produced by AI reflect industry-best practices in audience engagement and clarity.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

  • Language Mastery: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional grammatical and editorial command.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) in journalism, communications, English, or a related discipline from a reputable academic institution.
