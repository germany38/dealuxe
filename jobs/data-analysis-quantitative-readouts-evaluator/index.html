Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For data professionals seeking to diversify their income streams or transition away from rigid 9-to-5 desk jobs, this role offers a compelling blend of intellectual rigor and absolute schedule freedom. As an evaluator, your primary mission is to review, audit, and refine AI-generated analytical artifacts to ensure absolute accuracy, logical consistency, and professional rigor.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact and Spreadsheet Evaluation: Review complex AI-generated spreadsheets, statistical models, quantitative research documents, and slide decks designed for executive decision-making.
  • Methodological Rigor Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to detect mathematical errors, logical fallacies, interpretive flaws, and misaligned quantitative readouts in automated outputs.
  • Structured Feedback Delivery: Formulate clear, precise, and actionable written critiques that help frontier language models and autonomous analytical agents improve their computational and reporting logic.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex data science frameworks, data visualization layouts, and statistical summaries align with top-tier enterprise standards.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these specialized engagements directly influence the training sets of next-generation artificial intelligence systems, the qualification standards are tailored for experienced practitioners rather than novices.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven, hands-on professional background in data analysis, quantitative research, statistical modeling, or financial/business analytics.
  • Language Fluency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills for clear technical reporting.
  • Software Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in structuring presentation slides (Google Slides / PowerPoint) and advanced spreadsheets.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s, Ph.D., or equivalent) from a reputable institution in statistics, data science, economics, applied mathematics, or a quantitative business discipline.
