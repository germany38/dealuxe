Detailed Role Breakdown & Core Responsibilities

For professional electrical engineers and design specialists looking to take on high-impact consulting and technical problem-solving projects, this role offers intellectual stimulation and immediate professional rewards. Specialists are tasked with analyzing and refining complex system-level engineering problems across diverse industrial sectors.

What You Will Do in This Role:

  • Design Development & Evaluation: Develop, analyze, and rigorously evaluate electrical designs based on strict technical requirements, engineering specifications, and overall system performance metrics.
  • System & Component Review: Design and review electrical systems, circuits, equipment, and components, including power distribution systems, control panels, switchgear, and motor control systems.
  • Schematic & Documentation Analysis: Interpret and analyze electrical schematics, single-line and wiring diagrams, equipment layouts, and complex technical documentation.
  • Advanced Calculations: Perform critical electrical calculations, including load flow studies, voltage drop analysis, short-circuit calculations, protection coordination, and proper equipment sizing.
  • Trade-Off Assessment: Evaluate alternative design approaches, engineering trade-offs, and system performance benchmarks to identify potential design flaws or optimization opportunities.
  • Standard Validation: Review and validate designs, engineering assumptions, calculations, and technical submittals against recognized industry codes and project standards.

Candidate Requirements & Professional Qualifications

Because this position deals with high-stakes engineering systems and advanced infrastructure, applicants must meet rigorous professional and educational benchmarks.

Key Candidate Requirements:
  • Professional Background: Demonstrated hands-on industry experience as an Electrical Design Engineer, Electrical Engineer, or specialized Electrical Design Consultant.
  • Educational Foundation: A Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD degree in Electrical Engineering or a closely related technical discipline.
  • Core Knowledge: Strong, foundational understanding of electrical system design, power systems, low- and medium-voltage architecture, and fundamental engineering principles.
  • Practical Experience: Real-world experience designing or evaluating electrical systems across industrial facilities, infrastructure projects, manufacturing plants, energy systems, renewable energy farms, building services, or energy storage facilities.
  • Technical Proficiency: Familiarity with industry-standard electrical design and analysis tools such as AutoCAD Electrical, ETAP, SKM Power Tools, EPLAN, or Revit is considered a strong asset.

Preferred Qualifications: Advanced degrees or Professional Engineering (PE) licenses; advanced power system study experience (protection coordination, short-circuit analysis); expertise in specifying transformers, switchgear, and cables; background in industrial automation; and familiarity with IEC, IEEE, or NEC codes.
