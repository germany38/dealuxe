Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

As organizations and cutting-edge AI labs build sophisticated libraries of financial work products, structured accuracy and rigorous validation have become paramount. In this role, you will engage with complex, self-contained operations exercises derived from realistic mock files—including general ledgers, custodian statements, and bank notices—producing precise analyst deliverables graded against strict professional rubrics.

Key Duties & Workflow Deliverables:

  • Ledger & Custodian Reconciliations: Reconcile complex ledgers against custodian or bank statements with meticulous precision, isolating and explaining every residual difference to produce fully signed-off reconciliations complete with adjusting entries.
  • NAV Movement Attribution: Attribute Net Asset Value (NAV) movements, flag any components falling outside acceptable tolerances, and draft comprehensive variance commentary.
  • Trade Break Investigations: Investigate complex trade breaks between disparate systems, isolate root causes, classify break types, propose structural fixes, and draft detailed investigation narratives.
  • Corporate Action & Exception Processing: Process complex corporate action events, compute exact entitlements per account, handle payment and transfer exceptions (repair, return, or escalate), and match messy merchant or vendor descriptors to a canonical registry.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these high-compensating contracts directly support critical financial modeling and training architectures, the eligibility criteria are curated for seasoned professionals who bring tangible, hands-on operational acumen to the table.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of dedicated experience in fund administration or investment operations (including fund accounting, reconciliations, corporate actions, trade support/middle office, or payment operations) gained at a fund administrator, custodian bank, asset manager, or institutional banking ops department.
  • Technical Mastery: Proven, hands-on expertise with reconciliations, NAV calculations, trade breaks, corporate actions, and payment/settlement exceptions, paired with advanced Microsoft Excel proficiency.
  • Geographic Location: Based in the United States.

Preferred Nice-to-Haves: Possession of a CPA or IOC certification; familiarity with institutional fund-accounting platforms (such as SS&C, Geneva, Multifonds, or InvestOne); and fluency in SWIFT or enterprise settlement systems.
