About Turing

Turing stands at the vanguard of the artificial intelligence revolution, recognized globally as one of the fastest-growing AI companies accelerating the development, tuning, and deployment of powerful next-generation models. Operating on two major fronts, Turing empowers world-leading AI labs to push the boundaries of frontier model capabilities—spanning deep reasoning, advanced coding, multimodality, multilingual mastery, and complex agentic behavior. Simultaneously, Turing leverages this elite infrastructure to construct real-world AI systems that solve mission-critical operational challenges for top-tier enterprises.

Role Overview: Arabic Voice Actor & Trainer

As artificial intelligence systems evolve toward human-like interaction, natural inflection, emotional nuance, and regional linguistic accuracy become paramount. Turing is actively seeking skilled Arabic Voice Actors to breathe life into characters, scripts, and synthetic dialogue datasets across diverse domains. This role requires utilizing vocal range and emotive delivery to ensure AI voice models capture authentic personality and tone.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

  • Professional Recording: Record high-fidelity voice-overs and dialogue prompts directly from a professional home studio setup or designated studio sessions.
  • Expressive Delivery: Execute performance lines with precise clarity, emotional depth, appropriate pacing, and contextual awareness.
  • Script Interpretation: Closely analyze scripts and integrate creative feedback or directional changes seamlessly.
  • Quality Assurance: Review, refine, and deliver pristine audio files on schedule and adhering to strict technical format guidelines.

You Are a Ideal Fit If You Possess:

  • Demonstrated professional background in voice acting, theatrical performance, or vocal arts.
  • Exceptional vocal control, versatility, and broad emotional expression in Arabic.
  • Crisp articulation, pristine speech clarity, and flawless linguistic command.
  • Access to professional-grade home recording gear, microphones, and acoustic environments.
  • The discipline to meet tight deadlines and interpret complex creative direction.

Required Skills & Preferred Qualifications

Candidates thriving in this role combine technical literacy with artistic talent. Essential foundational skills include professional communication, script analysis, and adherence to production workflows. Preferred qualifications include prior experience in localization, dubbing, Automated Dialogue Replacement (ADR), and familiarity with standard audio workstation (DAW) editing tools.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

  • Complete Remote Flexibility: Work from anywhere in the world in a fully distributed, remote environment.
  • Cutting-Edge Innovation: Collaborate directly on pioneering large language model (LLM) and multimodal AI training pipelines.
  • Career Growth: Enjoy strong potential for contract extensions and ongoing project assignments based on sustained performance quality.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Process

This opportunity is structured as a flexible freelance agreement rather than a traditional employment position. To successfully stabilize your relationship with the platform, complete the comprehensive onboarding process and successfully complete your initial 10 hours of work. Achieving this milestone guarantees operational stability and unlocks access to expanded tasks and higher-tier projects.

Evaluation Process

Turing maintains a rigorous yet streamlined evaluation framework:

  1. Initial Shortlisting: Candidates are selected based on professional background, qualifications, and relevant voice demo reels.
  2. Delivery Review: Shortlisted applicants undergo a practical delivery assessment to verify studio quality and performance range.
  3. Active Engagement: Upon passing the delivery review, you are cleared to begin project onboarding and initial tasks.

نظرة عامة على الدور: ممثل صوتي ومدرب باللغة العربية

بينما يتطور الذكاء الاصطناعي نحو تفاعل شبيه بالبشر، تصبح النبرة الطبيعية والتلوين العاطفي والدقة اللغوية أمراً بالغ الأهمية. تبحث Turing عن ممثلين صوتيين مهرة باللغة العربية لإحياء الشخصيات والنصوص عبر مجالات مختلفة.

مهامك اليومية تشمل:

  • التسجيل الاحترافي: تسجيل تعليقات صوتية عالية الجودة من الاستوديو المنزلي الخاص بك.
  • الأداء التعبيري: أداء السطور بوضوح وعاطفة وإيقاع دقيق.
  • تفسير النصوص: تحليل النصوص وتطبيق التوجيهات الإبداعية والتعديلات بدقة.
  • ضمان الجودة: تسليم ملفات صوتية نقية في الوقت المحدد وضمن المعايير التقنية.

أنت مناسب تماماً إذا توفرت لديك:

  • خبرة مهنية سابقة في التمثيل الصوتي، المسرح، أو الفنون الأدائية.
  • تحكم صوتي استثنائي، مرونة، ومدى عاطفي واسع باللغة العربية.
  • نطق واضح وطلاقة لغوية تامة.
  • إمكانية الوصول إلى معدات تسجيل صوتي منزلية عالية الجودة.
  • الانضباط لتلبية المواعيد النهائية بدقة.

مميزات العمل الحر مع Turing

  • مرونة كاملة عن بُعد: العمل من أي مكان في بيئة عمل موزعة بالكامل.

  • النمو المهني: إمكانية تمديد العقود والمهام بناءً على جودة الأداء.
