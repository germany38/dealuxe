Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For PhD-level professionals in chemistry, biology, physics, or related technical fields who possess native or near-native fluency in Japanese and English, this role offers an engaging pivot toward AI safety and red-teaming. The primary mandate of this position is to evaluate, challenge, and refine how advanced artificial intelligence architectures comprehend, process, and respond to highly technical Japanese-language queries.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Expert-Level Prompt Engineering: Craft sophisticated, highly technical prompts in Japanese across specialized scientific, medical, and industrial domains to test model limits.
  • Rigorous Response Evaluation: Evaluate and meticulously annotate AI-generated responses for scientific accuracy, logical soundness, helpfulness, and appropriate safeguards around sensitive or dual-use content.
  • Structured Classification: Apply robust, structured guidelines to categorize complex user prompts and conversational interactions during safety audits.
  • Red-Teaming Technical Outputs: Proactively identify potential vulnerabilities, hallucinations, or compliance risks in AI models dealing with sensitive chemical, biological, or radiological concepts.
  • Cross-Lingual Quality Assurance: Ensure seamless fidelity between English safety standards and Japanese localized execution in high-stakes technological domains.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because the insights generated by professionals in this role directly inform the safety protocols of advanced AI models, the screening criteria are appropriately rigorous.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • Academic Credentials: A completed or ongoing PhD in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific field with deep specialization.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Japanese, combined with professional, business-level written and spoken English.
  • Laboratory & Research Depth: Deep familiarity with modern laboratory protocols, experimental procedures, and computational techniques within your specific scientific subfield.
  • Safety Judgment: Sound, ethical judgment regarding scientific safety, biosafety, biosecurity, and the responsible handling of dual-use technical information.

Preferred Qualifications: Based in Japan or the wider East Asia region (though global applicants are welcome); specialized background in radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, energetic materials, radiation physics, organic/inorganic synthesis, molecular biology, virology, or genetics; and prior experience reviewing or red-teaming technical content.
