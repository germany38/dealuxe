Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

Artificial intelligence models are scaling at an exponential rate, but raw computational power is not enough. Tech labs and enterprise developers require sophisticated human oversight to ensure these systems reason accurately, adhere to safety guardrails, and understand nuanced cultural contexts. This is where your specialized linguistic skill set comes into play.

As a Bilingual Dutch Generalist Expert at Mercor, your primary mission is to evaluate, audit, and strengthen how advanced AI models handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use topics in the Dutch language as it is spoken natively in the Netherlands. You will bridge the gap between human common sense and machine intelligence.

Key Daily Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Engineering & Creation: Draft expert-level, intricate prompts in Netherlands Dutch across a diverse array of specialized and sensitive subject areas.
  • Structural Classification: Apply structured, rigorous guidelines to classify model-generated outputs, conversations, and reasoning tracks.
  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: Identify and flag adversarial phrasings, edge cases, and escalation patterns, meticulously documenting the analytical reasoning behind your evaluations.
  • Cultural Context Tuning: Ensure that idiom usage, regional slang, syntax, and cultural sensibilities align perfectly with standard Netherlands Dutch usage.

💡 Note on Background: You do not need a background in software engineering or machine learning. Mercor provides comprehensive workflow training. What matters most is your critical thinking, meticulous attention to detail, and command of both Dutch and English.

Candidate Qualifications: Are You the Right Fit?

Mercor maintains exceptionally high standards for its AI training pools, ensuring that clients receive premium data quality. To qualify for this role, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Linguistic Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Dutch as spoken in the Netherlands, combined with business-level written and verbal English. (Note: Separate tracks exist for regional variations like Belgian Dutch).
  • Geographic Location: Must currently reside in the Netherlands.
  • Educational Background: A completed bachelor’s degree, or a bachelor’s degree currently in progress.
  • Cognitive Skills: Exceptional written reasoning capabilities, stellar analytical vision, and extreme attention to detail.
  • Sound Judgment: High ethical standards and steady judgment when evaluating sensitive or dual-use information categories.

Preferred "Nice-to-Have" Experience:

While not mandatory, candidates who possess previous experience in content moderation, trust and safety policy evaluation, academic research review, grading technical writing, or adversarial software testing will find themselves right at home.
