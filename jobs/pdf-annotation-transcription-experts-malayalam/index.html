Deep Dive: What Does a PDF Annotation & Transcription Expert Do?

English Details

Document understanding models frequently fail on complex, messy, or handwritten layouts like multi-column newspapers, examination papers, historical textbooks, forms, and diagrams. Your job is to correct this limitation by establishing complete structural maps and human-verified transcriptions.

  • Structural Annotation: Identify, bound, and classify meaningful page regions—titles, headings, tables, figures, captions, formulas, and paragraphs.
  • Relationship Mapping: Connect text blocks and captions to their corresponding tables or figures using parent identifiers.
  • Faithful Transcription: Transcribe text accurately character by character in Malayalam script, managing conjunct forms and diacritics without relying on flawed OCR parsing models.
  • Quality Peer Review: Participate in reviewing work submitted by fellow experts to maintain exceptional dataset accuracy.
