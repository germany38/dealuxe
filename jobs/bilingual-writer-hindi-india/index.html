The Core Mission: Shaping Voice-Driven AI in Hindi

As voice assistants transition from rigid command-and-response tools to fluid, conversational companions, AI labs face a major bottleneck: high-quality regional language data. Standard machine-translated data fails to capture cultural nuances, idiomatic expressions, regional dialects, and natural conversational cadence. That is why native Hindi speakers are in high demand.

In this role, you will test and interact with a specialized version of an AI application on your personal Mac computer. Your objective is to perform everyday tasks using your voice in Hindi, evaluate how accurately the model interprets your spoken commands, and log structured data regarding its performance.

English Overview

You will test advanced AI voice capabilities by executing normal daily digital tasks—such as drafting messages, managing calendar schedules, summarizing documents, and formatting text spreadsheets—entirely through spoken Hindi commands.

Detailed Job Responsibilities

To succeed as a Bilingual Writer at Mercor, you must maintain meticulous attention to detail and enjoy structured digital documentation. Your core daily tasks will include:

  • Voice-Powered Digital Tasks: Use your voice to compose emails, instant messages, and daily notes across everyday productivity software.
  • Directing AI Actions: Command the AI to generate images, format text documents, write spreadsheet formulas, and synthesize long-form texts.
  • Transcription & Quality Control: Record accurate transcripts of your spoken queries, clean up raw transcripts, and rigorously rate the AI's output.
  • Data Logging & Uploads: Maintain clean records of all task metrics in a structured tracking sheet and upload supporting audio recordings to a shared drive.

Strict Technical and Background Prerequisites

Because this project involves specialized software integration and high-performance audio processing, Mercor enforces strict hardware and language standards for applicants:

  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Hindi (candidates raised or living in India are strongly preferred), combined with the ability to read, write, and comprehend instructions in English.
  • Mandatory Hardware Requirement: You MUST own a Mac computer with an Apple Silicon chip (M1, M2, or M3) running macOS Sequoia 15.0 or newer. Standard Windows PCs or older Intel-based Macs are incompatible with the testing software.
  • Software Familiarity: Regular everyday use of modern productivity apps (email clients, chat software, office docs, spreadsheets, and web browsers).
  • Self-Directed Discipline: Comfortable handling detailed data entry, self-directed quality assurance checks, and independent task tracking.
