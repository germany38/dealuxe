The Mission: Engineering Autonomous AI for National Security

Traditional software development follows predictable release cycles and structured testing frameworks. By contrast, defense tech and forward-deployed engineering operate at the bleeding edge of ambiguity and operational urgency. When deployed in support of U.S. Government, defense, or intelligence missions, software must perform flawlessly under extreme constraints, processing vast quantities of disparate data into actionable operational autonomy.

micro1 is building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI, scaling expert networks and technical platforms that transform complex data into superior machine reasoning. As a Forward Deployed Engineer, you serve as the critical bridge between cutting-edge AI research and real-world government deployment. You will design, build, and deploy agentic AI systems that empower national security missions from initial prototype to production-grade reliability.

What You Will Work On (Core Responsibilities):

  • Agentic AI System Architecture: Build and deploy advanced agentic AI systems supporting high-impact U.S. Government missions, managing everything from prototype development to full production rollout.
  • LLM & RAG Integration: Design and integrate complex Large Language Model applications, including multi-agent architectures, tool-using autonomous agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) workflows, and human-in-the-loop control systems.
  • Forward-Deployed Collaboration: Work directly with government partners, defense organizations, and strategic stakeholders in partner-facing settings to translate complex operational needs into robust technical architecture.
  • Data Curation & Evaluation Pipelines: Build and maintain large-scale data curation, filtering, and evaluation pipelines to drive continuous model training and performance improvement.
  • Inference & Experimentation Infrastructure: Develop high-performance infrastructure for model inference, experimentation, evaluation, and seamless deployment across frontier AI platforms.
  • Full-Lifecycle Ownership: Own systems across their entire lifecycle, encompassing discovery, architecture, implementation, deployment, security hardening, reliability engineering, and iterative enhancement.

💡 Operational Autonomy: This role requires exceptional independence. You will frequently operate in ambiguous, high-stakes environments where technical leadership and operational ownership are vital to mission success.

What micro1 is Looking For: Qualifications and Technical Profile

Because this position sits at the intersection of advanced artificial intelligence and high-security government operations, micro1 maintains rigorous hiring standards. Ideal candidates bring a combination of rigorous software engineering expertise and operational adaptability:

  • Strong Python Engineering Foundation: Demonstrated experience as a senior Python engineer with end-to-end system ownership, writing clean, scalable, and maintainable code.
  • Deep LLM & Agent Expertise: Hands-on experience working with Large Language Models, fine-tuning, and creating production-ready agentic systems.
  • Infrastructure & Data Pipelines: Proven track record of building or maintaining robust data pipelines and machine learning infrastructure at scale.
  • Advanced Workflows: Familiarity with Reinforcement Learning (RL) workflows, simulation environments, and distributed computing architectures.
  • High-Security Comfort: Proven ability to thrive in high-security, high-reliability environments where data integrity and operational security are paramount.
