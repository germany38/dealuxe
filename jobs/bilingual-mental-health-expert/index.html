For AI to become a safe and effective support tool globally, it requires direct oversight from doctors, therapists, and certified mental health researchers who understand the intricacies of the human mind.

micro1 transforms real-world domain expertise into high-quality training datasets and evaluation feedback loops. By joining this specialized network, you are not merely doing typical freelance writing or gig work—you are establishing the ethical, clinical, and scientific guardrails for machine intelligence.
