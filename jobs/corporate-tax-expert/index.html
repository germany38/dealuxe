Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

The Corporate Tax Expert role on Mercor is meticulously structured for professionals who possess deep, specialized knowledge in corporate income tax. Rather than handling routine individual tax preparation or sales-tax-only compliance (which are strictly out of scope), contractors in this position build and evaluate high-level corporate tax work products derived from complex mock files including trial balances, intercompany agreements, and M&A deal documents.

What You Will Do:

  • ASC 740 Provisions: Construct quarterly and annual income tax provisions, executing precise current and deferred calculations alongside comprehensive rate reconciliations.
  • Compliance & Forecasting: Prepare sophisticated returns and supporting workpapers spanning federal, state apportionment, and international tax regimes while forecasting cash tax liabilities by jurisdiction.
  • Technical Computations & Studies: Execute complex calculations including GILTI, FDII, BEAT, Pillar Two provisions, Section 163(j) limitations, and detailed net operating loss (NOL) and attribute analyses.
  • Transfer Pricing: Build robust transfer pricing master file and local file documentation backed by rigorous intercompany agreement and policy reviews.
  • Transaction & M&A Tax: Conduct tax due diligence on exposures and attributes, draft strategic structuring memos, and formulate documented tax positions including FIN 48 reserves.

Candidate Requirements & Qualifications

Because these engagements directly shape and train frontier AI models used by top-tier financial and enterprise networks, the selection criteria are tailored exclusively for seasoned domain specialists.

Key Qualification Requirements:
  • Professional Experience: Minimum of 4+ years of dedicated experience in corporate income tax, gained either in-house at a corporation or within a Big 4 public accounting environment.
  • Specialized Depth: Proven proficiency in at least one core pillar: provision and compliance, international taxation and transfer pricing, or M&A tax structuring.
  • Location: Must be based in the United States.

Preferred Credentials (Nice-to-Have): Certified Public Accountant (CPA), Juris Doctor (JD) or LLM in Taxation, Enrolled Agent (EA), or Chartered Tax Adviser (CTA) designation, alongside practical experience in tax equity and HLBV modeling.
