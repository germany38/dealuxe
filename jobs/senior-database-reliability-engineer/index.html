Core Responsibilities and Architecture Scope

As a Senior Database Reliability Engineer at micro1, you will own the stability, scalability, and performance of large-scale database clusters. Your work directly enables the reliable operation of data-intensive workloads supporting global expert networks and AI data pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Architecture & Design: Design, implement, and manage highly available, resilient PostgreSQL database architectures leveraging AWS RDS and Aurora PostgreSQL.
  • Performance Optimization: Optimize database performance by deeply analyzing query execution plans, tuning complex indices, and implementing advanced table partitioning strategies.
  • Disaster Recovery & Replication: Ensure absolute disaster recovery readiness through robust backup routines, automated replication, and seamless failover processes.
  • Bottleneck Resolution: Diagnose and resolve slow queries, locks, deadlocks, and performance bottlenecks impacting large-scale databases with millions of rows.
  • Automation & Operations: Automate routine database maintenance tasks and promote operational excellence across all development, staging, and production environments.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with software engineering and product teams to support new feature rollouts, complex data migrations, and continuous application scaling.

💡 Workplace Culture & Benefits: micro1 fosters a high-performing, remote-first engineering culture. Full-time team members enjoy comprehensive benefits including up to 100% reimbursement for health insurance premiums, paid time off, a 401(k) plan with company match, equity compensation, and performance-based bonuses.

Required Skills, Qualifications, and Technical Background

To succeed in this rigorous engineering role, candidates must demonstrate deep technical mastery and proven production experience:

  • Experience Threshold: 5+ years of dedicated professional experience managing and optimizing PostgreSQL databases in high-traffic production environments.
  • Database Internals: Expert-level knowledge of PostgreSQL internals, query tuning, indexing methodologies, partitioning structures, and master-slave replication.
  • Cloud Ecosystem: Proven hands-on production experience with AWS RDS and Aurora PostgreSQL, including backup policies, point-in-time recovery, and multi-AZ high-availability design.
  • Troubleshooting Proficiency: Strong analytical abilities in diagnosing slow execution plans, lock contention, and complex concurrency deadlocks.
  • Scale Competency: Demonstrated experience maintaining large-scale, high-traffic databases containing tens or hundreds of millions of rows.
  • Communication & Independence: Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to operate autonomously while collaborating effectively within a distributed remote team.

Preferred Qualifications:

Candidates who bring additional exposure to modern data platforms, analytics workloads, ETL processes, Infrastructure as Code (such as Terraform), CI/CD pipelines, and advanced cloud-native monitoring tooling will find themselves exceptionally well-aligned with micro1's tech stack.
