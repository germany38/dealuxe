The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Nuclear Practitioners

As artificial intelligence models grow increasingly sophisticated, their capacity to comprehend specialized scientific workflows expands proportionally. However, this advancement introduces profound safety alignment problems. In nuclear materials science and safeguards, the line separating legitimate research from misuse is razor-thin. The very analytical signature used to attribute an isotopic sample is the detail someone might want to suppress; the detection threshold that verifies material containment can conversely be interpreted as a boundary to circumvent.

Generalist AI evaluators lack the nuanced, hands-on framework required to navigate these high-stakes technical boundaries. An AI model that unnecessarily refuses a routine measurement methodology or isotopic analysis query is failing just as critically as one that answers a prompt it shouldn't. Establishing and defending that operational threshold reliably is why leading AI laboratories rely on seasoned nuclear practitioners rather than generalist software testers.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Adversarial Prompt Design: Develop complex, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific nuclear materials expertise, categorized systematically across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Rigorous Policy Evaluation: Audit and assess model-generated responses against defined safety policy standards, rendering precise judgments on whether the AI handled each technical scenario correctly.
  • Authoring Expert Reference Answers: Formulate comprehensive reference answers detailing what a correct, scientifically accurate response looks like, alongside the rigorous technical reasoning behind your conclusions.

💡 Compliance Note: Your work with Mercor will not involve, and must not draw upon, classified or export-controlled information, or anything bound by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. If you maintain active clearances or review obligations, specify them in your application so project scopes can be tailored accordingly.

Ideal Backgrounds and Qualifications

Mercor maintains stringent vetting standards for its technical evaluation panels to ensure uncompromising data integrity. Candidates ideally possess advanced degrees, laboratory experience, and specialized careers in fields such as:
