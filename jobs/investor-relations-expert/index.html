Comprehensive Overview of the Role & Responsibilities

Unlike private equity or venture capital investor relations—which primarily focus on fundraising and limited partner (LP) relations—this specific engagement centers entirely on public-company investor relations. Qualified candidates will contribute to building a high-quality library of public-company IR work products by completing self-contained mock exercises derived from real-world scenarios, shareholder registers, earnings packs, and financial analyst models.

Core Deliverables & Key Responsibilities:

  • Earnings Cycle Management: Draft comprehensive earnings releases, meticulously crafted prepared remarks, and robust Q&A briefings tailored for quarterly results conference calls.
  • Guidance & Scenario Planning: Produce sophisticated guidance-setting and revision recommendations supported by detailed scenario analyses and consensus models.
  • Presentation Architecture: Build comprehensive investor day packages, equity story positioning decks, annual general meeting (AGM) materials, and high-stakes deal announcement kits.
  • Shareholder Analytics: Analyze complex shareholder registers and ownership bases, assess vulnerability to activist investors, and formulate actionable dividend and capital return strategies.

Candidate Requirements & Qualifications

Because these deliverables are used to train and calibrate advanced AI frameworks for financial precision, the qualification standards are intentionally rigorous. Mercor seeks established professionals who possess direct, hands-on operational experience inside major public companies.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • 4+ Years of In-House Experience: Demonstrated professional background working directly within an in-house investor relations department at a publicly traded company.
  • End-to-End Earnings Ownership: Proven track record of taking full ownership of an active corporate earnings cycle—from the initial press release and executive script to live analyst engagement and Q&A preparation.

Preferred Nice-to-Haves: Possession of the Investor Relations Charter (IRC) from NIRI, prior career experience in sell-side equity research before transitioning in-house, or specialized background at a dedicated investor relations agency.

The Assessment Process and Evaluation Standards

To ensure absolute excellence, applicants undergo a streamlined, performance-based evaluation process. Candidates are typically tested on core practical tasks, which include drafting an earnings release and prepared remarks package from a mock financial results pack, formulating a strategic guidance revision recommendation, and conducting a mock activist vulnerability assessment.

This transparent evaluation structure ensures that hiring decisions are based strictly on professional competence, making the process faster, fairer, and completely meritocratic compared to traditional corporate recruitment pipelines.
