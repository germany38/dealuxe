🇬🇧 English Overview

Artificial intelligence models are rapidly expanding beyond textual data into spatial intelligence, visual reasoning, and real-world physics comprehension. To build robust models that safely navigate physical environments, autonomous systems and vision models require hyper-realistic, third-person perspective training datasets.

micro1 is engaging specialized Exocentric Video Recorders equipped with 360° cameras (such as Insta360 or GoPro MAX series) to capture immersive, high-quality footage across diverse real-world environments. This contractor role places you at the forefront of spatial data acquisition.

Core Scope of Work

  • Third-Person Capture: Collect exocentric video clips focusing on natural human movement, navigation through changing scenery, and outdoor environments.
  • Real-World Activities: Record everyday activities, walking patterns, indoor/outdoor transitions, and vehicle navigation scenarios.
  • Spatial Consistency: Maintain continuous camera trajectories, ensuring appropriate elevation, framing, and angle stability throughout clips.

Technical & Equipment Requirements

Candidates must already own or have access to compatible hardware meeting strict capture standards:

  • Camera Specs: Minimum 170° field of view (FOV) and minimum 720p recording resolution.
  • Compatible Models: Insta360 (X3, X4, X5, X6), DJI Osmo 360, GoPro (MAX, MAX2), or equivalent action/360° cameras.
  • Mounting Rigs: Insta360 Third-Person Backpack Mount or equivalent harness-mounted extension systems. (Note: Handheld selfie sticks, chest mounts, shoulder clips, and stationary tripods are excluded).

Recording Guidelines

  • Each continuous clip must run for at least 60 seconds.
  • Full body and relevant surroundings must remain consistently visible in the frame during normal movement.
  • Strict adherence to safety guidelines and task-specific recording instructions is mandatory.

Requisitos Técnicos y de Equipamiento

Los candidatos deben poseer o tener acceso a hardware compatible que cumpla con los estándares estrictos de captura:

  • Especificaciones de Cámara: Campo de visión (FOV) mínimo de 170° y resolución de grabación mínima de 720p.

  • Sistemas de Montaje: Soporte de mochila de tercera persona de Insta360 u otros sistemas de extensión con arnés. (Nota: Se excluyen los palos selfie de mano, soportes de pecho, clips de hombro y trípodes estacionarios).

Directrices de Grabación

  • Cada clip continuo debe tener una duración mínima de 60 segundos.
  • El cuerpo completo y el entorno relevante deben mantenerse visibles de manera constante en el encuadre durante el movimiento normal.
  • Es obligatorio cumplir estrictamente con las normas de seguridad y las instrucciones específicas de cada tarea.
