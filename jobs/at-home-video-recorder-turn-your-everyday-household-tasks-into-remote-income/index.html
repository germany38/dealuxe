Understanding the Core Objective: Building the Human Intelligence Layer for AI

Artificial intelligence models are transitioning from digital assistants into physical world agents. To perform useful tasks safely in homes, these models need to observe how humans naturally perform routine operations—such as preparing meals, cleaning living spaces, organizing rooms, and executing standard household maintenance.

As an At-Home Video Recorder, your primary contribution involves capturing structured, synchronized video footage of your normal daily activities from a first-person point of view. Your video submissions act as critical training datasets that help machine learning models learn spatial awareness, action sequencing, and fine motor interaction logic in real-world domestic settings.

Required Qualifications & Technical Prerequisites

Because this project relies heavily on precise hardware compatibility and specific geographic availability, candidates must meet strict eligibility criteria before onboarding:

  • Geographic Eligibility: Must be legally eligible to work in the U.S. and reside in an approved state: Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Indiana, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, New York, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Mexico, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, and Wyoming.

  • Wearable Equipment Comfort: Must be comfortable wearing specialized head strap recording equipment while performing repetitive physical tasks safely.
  • Physical Capability: Able to safely conduct standard household cleaning and organization tasks with proper attention to detail.

Detailed Scope of Work & Daily Execution

Contractors on this initiative follow structured recording protocols to ensure the collected data meets the rigorous quality standards required by micro1's artificial intelligence lab customers. Key responsibilities include:

  • Chore Recording: Filming ordinary household tasks and chores from a first-person perspective using your smartphone secured on a head mount.
  • Synchronized Footage Capture: Ensuring that all recorded video data aligns with project guidelines regarding lighting, camera stability, and task duration.
  • Onboarding & Verification: Successfully completing an initial device compatibility check, an AI-enabled interview, and a prompt identity verification process within 48 hours of initial contact.
  • Independent Execution: Managing your recording schedule autonomously from your own home environment while adhering strictly to submission timelines.

Compensation Structure & Additional Perks

Transparency and predictable remuneration are cornerstones of micro1 contractor engagements. For this role, compensation is calculated directly based on the hours of qualified, approved video recorded and submitted in line with project guidelines.

Additionally, to support your setup, the platform includes equipment reimbursement incentives: contractors receive a $20 signing bonus and reimbursement for head strap purchase after successfully completing 20 hours of work on the project.
