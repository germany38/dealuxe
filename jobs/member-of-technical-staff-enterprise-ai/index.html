The Core Mission: Bridging Enterprise Workflows and Frontier AI Research

As enterprises race to integrate artificial intelligence into their core operations, they frequently encounter profound hurdles: brittle system architectures, unpredictable model hallucinations, and an inability to maintain consistent reasoning across complex, multi-step workflows. Generalist software engineering approaches fall short because enterprise AI requires a delicate synthesis of rigorous machine learning research and practical, forward-deployed problem-solving.

As a Member of Technical Staff for Enterprise AI at micro1, you will not operate in an isolated academic silo. Instead, you will act as a forward-deployed research partner embedded directly within enterprise environments. Your mission is to identify where models stumble in production, formalize those failures into structured research problems, and engineer high-signal data and evaluation protocols to permanently eliminate system weaknesses.

Core Responsibilities and Daily Impact:

  • Embedded Research Collaboration: Partner directly with enterprise domain experts, client engineering teams, and internal researchers to analyze live AI workflows.
  • Failure Mode Analysis: Surface, formalize, and prioritize system failure modes in real-world deployments, transforming messy operational feedback into rigorous research directions.
  • High-Signal Dataset Design: Design specialized datasets and evaluation protocols targeting identified weaknesses to train models for superior edge-case handling.
  • Rapid Experimental Loops: Execute tight experimental cycles to validate hypotheses, test model variations, and quantify performance improvements.
  • Agentic Workflow Benchmarking: Develop and benchmark complex agentic workflows, placing primary emphasis on system robustness, scalability, and deterministic reliability.
  • Tooling & Research Artifacts: Build lightweight internal tools supporting automated evaluation, data curation, and rapid iteration, while contributing to internal and external research reports and benchmarks.

💡 A Culture of High-Velocity Iteration: micro1 is built for engineers and researchers who thrive in high-ambiguity environments. The pace is fast, expectations are rigorous, and the opportunity to define how global enterprises interact with artificial intelligence is unmatched.

  • Bias Toward Action: Exceptional communication skills with a bias toward clear, actionable insight, rapid execution cycles, and cross-functional collaboration.
