Detailed Job Overview & Recording Objectives

For voice actors, narrators, and expressive speakers based in the northern UK who want to explore high-paying remote contracts, understanding the scope of this project is vital. The engagement centers around recording clean, high-fidelity voice samples to train an exclusive internal CX AI agent for a major tech innovator.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Diverse Script Recording: Execute clean voice recordings across a wide variety of scripts, including conversational dialogue, narrative prompts, instructional segments, and customer service simulations.
  • Expressive Delivery: Maintain exceptional control over tone, vocal pacing, diction, pitch variation, and pronunciation to ensure the synthetic voice sounds completely organic.
  • Accent Consistency: Deliver unwavering consistency in regional accent, timbre, and style throughout each recording block.
  • Technical Adherence: Strictly follow comprehensive acoustic guidelines, including optimal microphone placement, background noise management, and precise audio file formatting.
  • Dynamic Take Generation: Perform multiple vocal takes with nuanced variations in emotion, emphasis, and conversational style upon request.

Candidate Requirements: Who Qualifies for This Role?

Because these voice models directly power enterprise-grade customer interaction agents, the technical and linguistic standards are precise. Applicants must meet specific criteria to be considered for onboarding.

Key Requirements & Eligibility:
  • Regional Profile: Female voice actor possessing a natural, authentic Northern English accent (e.g., Yorkshire, Manchester, Newcastle, Liverpool), currently residing in the northern UK.
  • Vocal Clarity: A clear, pleasant, and expressive voice. Professional voice acting, audiobook narration, or broadcast background is considered a strong plus rather than a strict prerequisite.
  • Home Studio Setup: Access to a professional or near-professional acoustic setup, including a high-quality microphone, pop filter, and a quiet, echo-free recording environment.
  • Expressive Range: Exceptional command over intonation, emotional range, and articulation.
  • Project Commitment: Availability for a primary ~4-hour recording session, with potential for a follow-up session of similar length.
  • Voice Cloning Consent: Comfort with having your voice cloned exclusively for the client’s internal CX AI Agent under strict privacy guarantees (recordings will never be sold or reused outside this specific project).
