Comprehensive Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For career public servants, policy researchers, and governance specialists seeking flexible, high-yield remote income, grasping the foundational expectations of this evaluation role is vital. The position calls for senior-level critical thinking and domain expertise to audit and refine complex AI-generated work products, guaranteeing that artificial intelligence systems meet rigorous institutional standards.

Primary Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Assessment: Review, grade, and audit AI-generated spreadsheets, policy documents, and slide decks focused on government initiatives, regulatory frameworks, public policy analysis, and civic administration.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify factual errors, policy misalignments, structural omissions, and presentation flaws in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to help train and align frontier AI models with elite public sector standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that intricate policy proposals, public budget frameworks, and administrative workflows generated by AI align with best practices in civic governance and compliance.
