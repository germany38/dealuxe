Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For PhD holders and Master’s scientists specializing in applied mathematics, numerical computing, or quantitative finance, this project offers an intellectually stimulating escape from traditional administrative overhead. Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these benchmarks serve as the primary evaluation standard for frontier models developed by leading AI labs, the selection criteria demand elite academic credentials and robust technical execution skills.
