Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For seasoned compliance specialists, fraud investigators, and risk managers looking to diversify their professional impact or transition into flexible remote contracting, this role offers an intellectually stimulating environment. Rather than dealing with live proprietary institution servers or risking sensitive client data leaks, analysts work within controlled, self-contained libraries of realistic mock case exercises and files.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Transaction Monitoring Adjudication: Review detailed alert packets, KYC/CDD profiles, and historical transaction logs to document dispositions accurately and draft robust SAR narratives where warranted.
  • Onboarding & Remediation Audits: Inspect client onboarding and KYC packets for omissions, discrepancies across documents, and formulate comprehensive remediation checklists.
  • Beneficial Ownership Resolution: Deconstruct complex, multi-layered UBO (Ultimate Beneficial Owner) legal structures, correctly applying 10% and 25% threshold standards.
  • Sanctions & Adverse Media Screening: Adjudicate screening hits (determining true matches versus false positives or escalations) supported by clear, concise, defensible rationales.
  • Fraud & Queue Triage: Triage supervisory alert queues, resolve card fraud and dispute cases, and cross-examine flagged operational communications against underlying trade records.

Candidate Qualifications: Who Flourishes in This Role

Because these positions directly train and fine-tune state-of-the-art financial AI systems used across top-tier enterprise networks, the selection criteria are tailored to find experienced practitioners who possess a rigorous grasp of regulatory frameworks.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 3+ Years of Hands-On Experience: Proven professional background in AML, financial crime investigation, KYC/CDD compliance, sanctions screening, fraud dispute resolution, or transaction surveillance within a bank, fintech platform, payments processor, or MSB.
  • Regulatory Framework Mastery: Working familiarity with FinCEN, FATF, and OFAC guidelines, alongside standardized SAR and CDD documentation protocols.
  • Defensible Writing Skills: Exceptional ability to draft clear, evidence-backed, and logically sound compliance rationales.

Preferred Credentials & Skills: Holding a CAMS/ACAMS or CFE certification; experience with enhanced due diligence (EDD) or corporate entity unwinding; proficiency in SQL or complex transaction analytics.

Understanding the Assessment Process

To ensure alignment with high-level institutional standards, applicants undergo a targeted, timed practical assessment that mirrors daily compliance operations. This evaluation typically spans three core modules:

  1. Mock Alert Packet: Evaluating a simulated transaction alert to determine the proper disposition and drafting a professional SAR narrative.
  2. KYC Packet Review: Spotting cross-document discrepancies in a mock client file and compiling an accurate remediation list.
  3. Sanctions Batch: Rapidly adjudicating a batch of sanctions or adverse media hits with concise, policy-compliant rationales.
