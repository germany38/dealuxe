Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

Securing venture backing or institutional capital is an art form backed by rigorous quantitative and qualitative metrics. As an Evaluator on Mercor, your primary mission is to ensure that AI-generated investment materials meet the uncompromising standards of top-tier venture capital and corporate finance environments.

What You Will Do on a Day-to-Day Basis:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Thoroughly review and grade complex AI-generated work products, including investment memos, financial spreadsheets, executive summaries, and multi-slide fundraising decks.
  • Rigor and Quality Auditing: Apply deep professional expertise to detect factual inaccuracies, logical inconsistencies in financial projections, structural flaws in narratives, and aesthetic formatting errors.
  • Actionable Feedback Delivery: Provide clear, highly structured, and constructive written feedback designed to train frontier language models to produce elite-level venture materials.
  • Domain Excellence Maintenance: Ensure that every piece of AI output adheres strictly to professional standards of venture capital storytelling, valuation frameworks, and go-to-market strategies.

Candidate Requirements: Who Qualifies for This Role?

Because these positions directly influence the training data of advanced AI systems utilized across global enterprises, the qualification criteria are designed to attract senior practitioners with proven track records in capital markets and startup growth.

Must-Have Professional Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Relevant Experience: Demonstrated career background in venture capital, investment banking, private equity, startup fundraising, management consulting, or corporate development.
  • Exceptional Communication Skills: Native or professional fluency in English with pristine written communication abilities.
  • Software & Design Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specialized expertise in advanced presentation software (Google Slides and PowerPoint) to evaluate visual and narrative layout.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced academic degree (such as an MBA or Master’s in Finance) from a reputable, globally recognized institution.
