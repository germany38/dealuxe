Demystifying the Initiative: Bridging CAD Expertise with Machine Learning

Standard automated systems can read geometrical coordinates, but they lack true engineering intuition. They do not intrinsically understand why draft angles matter in injection molding, how GD&T stack-ups affect assembly tolerances, or how to restructure a flawed feature tree to maintain full parametric integrity. This is where seasoned design professionals step in.

As an Autodesk Inventor Specialist on this project, your primary mandate is to evaluate, construct, audit, and refine parametric 3D models and technical documents. Your expert interventions train AI architectures to understand mechanical intent, structural feasibility, and design-for-manufacturability principles.

Technical Core Competencies & Software Note

While this project specifically leverages core expertise in Autodesk Inventor, the underlying modeling methodologies translate directly across modern parametric ecosystems. Notably, the core focus of the project centers around FreeCAD, making prior FreeCAD exposure an exceptional advantage, alongside deep fluency in platforms like SolidWorks or Fusion 360.

Candidates should demonstrate mastery across critical technical domains:

Autodesk Inventor Parametric 3D CAD GD&T (ASME Y14.5) DFM Principles Technical Drawing Interpretation Assembly-Level Design Reverse Engineering CAD Model Auditing Python / iLogic Scripting

Detailed Scope of Work & Deliverables

Contractors selected for this role will engage in complex, multi-faceted mechanical design workflows. Your day-to-day contributions will directly shape how automated models parse and generate industrial documentation:

  • Parametric Modeling: Develop fully-parametric 3D CAD models of intricate mechanical components and multi-part assemblies based on rigorous written briefs, utilizing Autodesk Inventor or comparable parametric software.
  • Model Iteration & Revision: Edit and update legacy or existing models to reflect evolving engineering requirements while ensuring that feature trees remain fully parametric and adhere to industry best practices.
  • CAD Model Auditing: Conduct thorough audits and reviews of third-party or AI-generated CAD models for compliance with strict dimensional tolerances, constraints, and manufacturability criteria, documenting every gap or discrepancy.
  • GD&T & Technical Drawings: Create, interpret, and validate detailed technical manufacturing drawings, applying ASME Y14.5 or equivalent Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing standards with absolute precision.
  • Design for Manufacturability (DFM): Guarantee robust DFM oversight across diverse fabrication processes, including injection molding, sheet metal stamping, CNC machining, and casting or forging.
  • Rationale Documentation: Support advanced AI training loops by supplying crystal-clear written documentation, design rationale, and step-by-step logic for edits, audits, and model builds.
  • Multidisciplinary Collaboration: Communicate effectively in both written and verbal formats, delivering precise, solution-oriented feedback to project teams and stakeholders.

Preferred Qualifications & Professional Benchmarks

Because this project demands elite-level mechanical engineering judgment, micro1 looks for candidates who have distinguished themselves in professional industrial environments:

  • Experience Threshold: A minimum of 4 years of hands-on parametric modeling experience for edit-level tasks, scaling up to 10+ years for complex, ground-up component creation and advanced assembly work.
  • Software Mastery: Exceptional proficiency in Autodesk Inventor, complemented by transferable fluency in platforms such as FreeCAD, SolidWorks, or Autodesk Fusion 360.
  • GD&T Fluency: Demonstrated, real-world application and mastery of GD&T standards (such as ASME Y14.5).
  • Manufacturing Versatility: Proven hands-on DFM background covering at least two to three distinct manufacturing processes (e.g., injection molding, sheet metal, CNC machining, and metal casting).
  • Advanced Workflows: Experience managing complex assembly-level designs and mechanical systems rather than being restricted to single-part modeling. Familiarity with reverse engineering workflows (scan-to-CAD or point cloud-to-model reconstruction) is highly valued.
  • Automation & Scripting: Familiarity with CAD automation and scripting frameworks—such as Python, iLogic, VBA, or NX Open—to enhance workflow efficiency and model flexibility.
