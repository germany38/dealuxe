Understanding the Initiative: Training Spatial AI Systems

Artificial intelligence models designed to assist with physical environments must learn how humans navigate everyday spaces. How do we open doors, organize kitchens, clean living areas, or manage routine household chores? Algorithms cannot guess these dynamics; they require synchronized visual and inertial motion data recorded directly from a human point of view.

As a Data-Video Generalist, your primary responsibility is straightforward yet vital: you will capture high-quality, first-person video footage of your normal household activities and everyday chores using your smartphone and wearable mounting equipment. Your contributions directly supply the raw training datasets required to build smarter, safer, and more perceptive spatial intelligence models.

Technical Requirements & Eligibility Checklist

Because this project depends on synchronized hardware and precise video specs, applicants must meet specific technical and geographic criteria before onboarding:

  • Geographic Eligibility: Must reside within eligible US states (including Alabama, Alaska, Arkansas, Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Idaho, Indiana, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Mexico, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, and Wyoming).
  • Smartphone Compatibility: Must own a compatible device: iPhone 12 or later, Google Pixel 6 through 9, or Samsung Galaxy S21 or later.
  • Wearable Hardware: Comfort wearing specialized head strap equipment shipped directly to your home.
  • Physical Capability: Ability to safely perform repetitive physical movement tasks associated with routine daily chores.
  • Verification Standards: Ability to complete an identity verification process within 48 hours of initial contact prior to contract issuance.

Detailed Scope of Work & Responsibilities

Contractors accepted into the program follow a structured yet flexible daily workflow. Key duties and operational milestones include:

  • Chore Capture: Filming ordinary household tasks and physical chores from an immersive, first-person point of view using your smartphone.
  • Synchronized Recording: Ensuring video and IMU (Inertial Measurement Unit) data are captured accurately according to project guidelines.
  • Onboarding & Vetting: Completing a mandatory device compatibility check and an AI-enabled interview during initial setup.
  • Equipment Management: Utilizing shipped head-strap equipment to maintain a steady, first-person camera angle throughout tasks.
  • Quality Maintenance: Paying close attention to detail to ensure submitted video files meet clarity, stability, and duration standards.

Compensation Structure & Perks

Transparency and reliable compensation are central to the micro1 contractor experience. Generalists are compensated directly per hour of valid, approved video submitted in accordance with project guidelines.

Additionally, micro1 supports onboarding hardware expenses by offering a $20 signing bonus and equipment reimbursement after contractors successfully complete 20 hours of work on the platform. Work availability is immediate, with project start times synchronized with the shipping schedule of headstrap equipment directly to your residence.
