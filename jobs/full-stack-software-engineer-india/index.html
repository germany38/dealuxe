Deep Dive: What Does a GenAI Full Stack Engineer Do?

Artificial intelligence is no longer just about theoretical algorithms; it requires robust, scalable software scaffolding to deliver value in production environments. Leading AI labs and foundational model creators need elite developers to stress-test their models by building live, complex applications on top of them.

As a Full Stack Software Engineer at Mercor, your primary mission is to build, integrate, and validate real-world software components, services, and internal developer tools that exercise frontier AI capabilities end-to-end. You will work directly with engineering managers on fast-moving projects, onboarding quickly and contributing production-ready code from day one.

Key Daily Responsibilities:

  • Full-Stack Application Development: Build and ship robust applications and services in Python, Java, Rust, C#, C++, or TypeScript.
  • API & Model Integration: Integrate pre-release model APIs into working software, including building surrounding scaffolding, tool interfaces, evaluation harnesses, and telemetry pipelines.
  • Failure Mode Analysis: Diagnose and document model or integration failure modes, translating technical observations into clear, actionable written feedback for elite research teams.
  • Rapid Prototyping: Deliver working increments quickly against shifting requirements with minimal upfront specification.
  • Code Quality & Collaboration: Maintain high standards of code architecture, technical documentation, and consistency across engineering workflows.

💡 Why This Role Stands Out: Instead of working on routine legacy maintenance, you will be sitting at the core of the global AI revolution, shaping how next-generation foundational models interact with real software.

Candidate Qualifications: What Are They Looking For?

Mercor maintains rigorous standards to ensure that partner AI labs receive top-tier engineering output. To qualify for this role, candidates must demonstrate:

  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of dedicated professional experience building and shipping production software at recognized, high-performing organizations.
  • Multi-Language Competence: Hands-on production expertise in at least one core language (Python, Java, Rust, C#, or C++), plus working proficiency in a second ecosystem.
  • Full-Stack Fluency: Experience spanning backend services/APIs, modern frontend frameworks (React or equivalent), relational/document databases, and cloud deployments.
  • Adaptability: Demonstrated ability to onboard rapidly onto unfamiliar codebases and deliver working code with minimal ramp-up time.
  • Communication Skills: Strong written and verbal communication abilities to articulate complex technical decisions clearly.
