Deconstructing the Role: What Does an AI Evaluator Actually Do?

For operations managers, process consultants, and continuous improvement directors wondering how their skill sets translate into the tech and AI space, the workflow at Mercor is refreshingly straightforward yet intellectually demanding. Evaluators are presented with AI-generated work products designed to solve simulated or real-world business challenges. Their task is to grade, critique, and refine these artifacts against stringent quality rubrics.

Daily responsibilities typically include:

  • Rigor & Accuracy Assessment: Verifying that operational frameworks, workflow logic, and process steps outlined in AI documents are structurally sound and practically viable.
  • Error Identification: Spotting subtle factual inaccuracies, logical inconsistencies, workflow bottlenecks, and presentation errors in generated documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks.
  • Structured Feedback Generation: Providing clear, actionable, and professionally structured written assessments that help train and calibrate underlying AI models to think more like human industry experts.

Because the role demands high-level proficiency in productivity suites—specifically Google Workspace and Microsoft Office (PowerPoint/Slides)—consultants feel right at home using familiar tools while applying high-level strategic oversight.
