The Core Mission: Why AI Needs Military Mental Health Specialists

Artificial intelligence models are increasingly being deployed in support roles, ranging from mental wellness coaching assistants to automated clinical documentation triage. However, without rigorous human-in-the-loop training by licensed professionals, these models risk missing vital subtext, misinterpreting trauma responses, or failing to capture the unique cultural framework of military service members and veterans.

Military and veteran mental health encompasses a deeply specialized body of knowledge—from combat-related post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD) and moral injury to transition stress, operational culture, and specific therapeutic interventions like Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) and Prolonged Exposure (PE). General AI models cannot safely navigate these domains without direct guidance from clinicians who have spent years in the trenches of patient care.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities as an AI Trainer:

  • Case Study & Material Review: Evaluate clinical texts, mental health training modules, and simulated patient interaction logs tailored to military and veteran demographics.
  • Prompt & Scenario Curations: Build, edit, and refine structured prompts and responses that reflect real-world clinical hurdles, diagnostic procedures, and therapeutic modalities.
  • Diagnostic & Cultural Nuance Alignment: Provide expert feedback ensuring that AI outputs respect clinical ethics, maintain high accuracy, and recognize military-specific terminology and cultural sensitivities.
  • Collaborative Documentation: Document findings, recommendations, and clinical improvements in structured, accessible formats to guide engineering and product teams.

💡 Note on AI Background: No prior experience in artificial intelligence or machine learning coding is required. micro1’s platform is designed to harness your pure clinical and domain expertise, turning real-world knowledge into structured training data for frontier models.

Required Skills and Professional Qualifications
