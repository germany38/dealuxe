The Intersection of Corporate Law and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

General-purpose artificial intelligence models have historically struggled with legal reasoning. When tasked with analyzing multi-layered commercial agreements, identifying subtle liability gaps, or redlining complex Master Services Agreements (MSAs), generic AI systems often hallucinate or miss critical statutory and contractual nuances. In high-stakes corporate environments, these inaccuracies are unacceptable.

To bridge this capability gap, micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By partnering with seasoned in-house transactional lawyers who possess years of practical experience negotiating technology transactions, micro1 transforms real-world legal judgment into structured training datasets, objective grading rubrics, and interactive feedback loops. No prior coding or software development background is required; your practical corporate legal expertise is the core engine driving these models forward.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Simulated Negotiations & Redlining: Perform rigorous contract redlining exercises and simulated commercial negotiations to test artificial intelligence reasoning limits.
  • AI Output Evaluation: Review, assess, and score AI-generated responses to complex contract scenarios, providing expert, actionable feedback to improve output precision.
  • Objective Grading Frameworks: Create systematic evaluation rubrics and grading criteria to assess AI performance on contract review tasks with consistency and academic rigor.
  • Product & Research Collaboration: Collaborate directly with product and research teams to refine data guidelines, prompt structures, and best practices for automated legal review tools.
  • Risk Interpretation: Help machine learning systems understand how to accurately identify risk, interpret liability clauses, and balance commercial interests in technology transactions.

💡 Maximizing Your Success: To ensure long-term project engagement and steady workflow assignment on micro1, experts are encouraged to complete the full application process and commit to completing at least 10 hours of active project work. Fulfilling this milestone validates your expertise, establishes your reliability within the network, and unlocks higher-tier project assignments.

Preferred Qualifications and Professional Background

Because micro1 supplies top-tier AI labs, candidate screening is thorough. The ideal applicants for this transactional role possess deep, verified professional credentials:

  • Accredited Legal Education & Bar Admission: A J.D. from an ABA-accredited law school paired with an active bar admission in at least one U.S. jurisdiction.
  • In-House Experience: A minimum of 3 years of focused in-house counsel experience specializing in technology transactions, particularly negotiating Master Services Agreements (MSAs), Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and Data Processing Agreements (DPAs).
  • Exceptional Communication & Analytical Skills: Meticulous attention to detail with the ability to translate complex legal principles into clear, structured, and actionable feedback for technical teams.
  • Corporate Firm Background (Preferred): Prior experience at a corporate law firm handling mergers and acquisitions (M&A) or private equity fund formation is considered a strong plus.
  • Tech-Forward Mindset: Demonstrated enthusiasm for innovation at the intersection of law and technology, with past exposure to legal tech or AI training initiatives.
