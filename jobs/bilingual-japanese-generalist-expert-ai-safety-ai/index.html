Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

Artificial intelligence models are scaling rapidly, but raw compute is not enough. Tech labs and enterprise developers require sophisticated human oversight to ensure these systems reason accurately, adhere to safety guidelines, and master nuanced cultural contexts. This is where your specialized linguistic expertise becomes vital.

As a Bilingual Japanese Generalist Expert at Mercor, your primary mission is to evaluate, audit, and strengthen how frontier AI models handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use topics in the Japanese language.

Key Daily Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Engineering: Draft expert-level, intricate prompts in Japanese across diverse and sensitive subject areas.
  • Structural Classification: Apply structured guidelines to classify model outputs and conversational paths.
  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: Flag adversarial phrasings and escalation patterns, documenting analytical reasoning.
  • Cultural Context Tuning: Ensure syntactic naturalness, idiom precision, and cultural nuance in Japanese.
