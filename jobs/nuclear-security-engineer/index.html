The Core Mission: Why AI Safety Needs Nuclear Practitioners

Modern large language models ingest vast quantities of public documentation, technical manuals, and regulatory frameworks. However, raw data consumption without deep domain intuition creates critical vulnerabilities. In high-consequence fields like nuclear engineering, the boundary between professional compliance and safety risks is razor-thin.

Consider the dual-use nature of the field: the exact material-balance calculation used by an inspector to close an account can theoretically be manipulated to obscure discrepancies; the physical protection assessment designed to harden a nuclear facility also maps its potential vulnerabilities. An AI model that unnecessarily rejects a routine, professional accounting or safeguards inquiry fails as severely as one that answers a hazardous prompt. Establishing and defending that operational boundary reliably is why tech innovators rely on experienced practitioners rather than generalists.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities:

  • Developing Rigorous Prompts: Draft complex, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific domain, categorized systematically across three distinct tiers: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation & Auditing: Review model-generated outputs against strict safety policy benchmarks, rendering precise determinations on whether each scenario was handled correctly.
  • Crafting Reference Solutions: Formulate comprehensive expert reference answers illustrating what a correct, safe response looks like, alongside the technical logic supporting your conclusions.

💡 Compliance Note: Work on this project must strictly avoid drawing on classified, export-controlled data or information restricted by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. Candidates with active clearances can often still participate by aligning their scope accordingly—simply specify your status in your application.
