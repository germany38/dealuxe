Deconstructing the Role: What Does a Clinical Survey Expert Actually Do?

At the center of this movement is Mercor’s high-demand position: the Therapeutic Area & Clinical Survey Expert. Designed for individuals with deep knowledge across specific medical and pharmaceutical domains, this role tackles a critical bottleneck in healthcare market research and AI model training: ensuring that automated systems and research questionnaires accurately mirror real-world medical practice.

"An MD is not required. The focus is on practical, professional mastery of treatment pathways, disease terminology, and real-world provider-patient dynamics."

Professionals engaged in this project evaluate physician and patient research surveys for clinical and therapeutic-area accuracy. Key responsibilities include:

  • Validating Terminology & Pathways: Assessing whether disease terminology, treatment sequences, modern therapies, and clinical scenarios precisely match contemporary medical reality.
  • Filtering Ambiguities: Identifying inaccurate, outdated, or missing clinical considerations within survey questions and data instruments.
  • Reflecting Decision-Making: Ensuring that survey structures accurately account for genuine physician decision-making patterns and patient experiences.
  • Providing Actionable Insights: Delivering clear, evidence-based recommendations to refine survey instruments used by leading research labs.

Target Therapeutic Areas: Where Your Specific Background Counts

The value of this engagement lies in niche, specialized knowledge. Mercor is currently prioritizing experts with deep professional backgrounds across several core therapeutic domains:

  • Oncology & Urologic Oncology: Deep familiarity with prostate cancer staging, complex treatment sequencing, modern systemic therapies, and shifting clinical standards of care.
  • Gastroenterology & IBD: Expertise in ulcerative colitis, assessing disease severity, biologics, targeted therapies, remission criteria, and long-term treatment pathways.
  • Neurology: Comprehensive understanding of migraines, acute and preventive treatment regimens, chronic versus episodic presentations, and patient burden.
  • Pulmonology & Respiratory Medicine: Insight into chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), diagnostic protocols, and ongoing management strategies utilized by pulmonologists and primary care physicians.
