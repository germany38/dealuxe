Project Architecture: The Human Intelligence Layer for Spatial AI

Artificial intelligence models struggle to interpret dynamic physical environments without rich, continuous spatial references. By capturing structured, third-person perspective recordings across diverse everyday settings, vehicle transit routes, and dynamic navigation environments, contractors supply the vital training data that enables AI systems to perceive depth, movement, and physical interaction accurately.

Scope of Work (English)

Contractors will collect third-person video across several real-world capture types. Recordings must emphasize continuous movement, changing scenery, and natural navigation through indoor and outdoor environments.

  • Natural everyday activities
  • Indoor and outdoor pedestrian movement
  • Vehicle navigation and driving-related recordings
  • Maintaining stable, unobstructed third-person perspectives

Alcance del Trabajo (Español)

Los contratistas recopilarán videos en tercera persona en varios entornos reales. Las grabaciones deben enfatizar el movimiento continuo, paisajes cambiantes y navegación natural.

  • Actividades cotidianas naturales
  • Movimiento peatonal en interiores y exteriores
  • Navegación vehicular y grabaciones de conducción
  • Mantenimiento de perspectivas estables en tercera persona

Hardware Requirements & Camera Specifications

To qualify for this engagement, creators must own or have immediate access to compatible camera equipment meeting strict resolution and field-of-view parameters.

Technical Criteria (English)

  • Field of View (FOV): Minimum 170° requirement.
  • Resolution: Minimum 720p continuous recording.
  • Compatible Devices: Insta360 (X3, X4, X5, X6), DJI Osmo 360, GoPro MAX, MAX2, or equivalent action/360° cameras.
  • Clip Duration: Each continuous clip must be at least 60 seconds long.

Mounting and Third-Person Perspective Standards

Unlike standard handheld selfie stick videos or stationary vlogging angles, spatial AI model training mandates rigorous third-person framing where the subject's entire body and immediate surroundings remain fully visible and stable.

Mounting & Setup Guidelines / Directrices de Montaje

English: The camera must be mounted above and behind you, angled slightly downward, and positioned far enough back to keep your entire body consistently visible. Rigid backpacks, harness mounts with extension arms, or secure vehicle mounts are required. Handheld selfie sticks, chest mounts, and stationary tripods are strictly prohibited.

Español: La cámara debe montarse arriba y detrás de usted, con una ligera inclinación hacia abajo. Se requieren mochilas rígidas, arneses con brazo de extensión o soportes seguros para vehículos. Se prohíben los palos deselfie de mano, soportes de pecho y trípodes estacionarios.
