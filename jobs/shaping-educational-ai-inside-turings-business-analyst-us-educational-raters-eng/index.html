The Role Overview: What You’ll Do Day-to-Day

As a Business Analyst focused on US Educational Raters, your day-to-day work centers on leveraging your analytical mindset and research capabilities to evaluate AI performance. Rather than performing routine administrative tasks, you act as an intellectual auditor for artificial intelligence outputs.
