Elite Remote AI & Finance Opportunity

Discover how senior finance practitioners are leveraging their domain expertise to train frontier artificial intelligence systems with Turing.

Artificial intelligence has transcended simple text generation and basic data retrieval. Today, frontier AI labs are pushing boundaries into complex reasoning, multi-step problem solving, and specialized enterprise domains. However, for an AI model to provide accurate financial analysis, evaluate corporate mergers, or structure investment portfolios, it cannot learn from synthetic data alone. It requires the nuanced, real-world insight of seasoned finance professionals.

Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Turing stands as the world's leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs and a trusted partner for global enterprises deploying advanced artificial intelligence systems. Turing bridges the gap between raw research and production-grade reliability by mobilizing top-tier expert networks across coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and multimodal agents. For finance experts looking to apply their professional acumen to cutting-edge technology, Turing’s Business Analyst (Finance) engagement represents an unprecedented career milestone.

About Turing and the Mission Behind the AI Revolution

Turing supports global customers through two core pillars: accelerating frontier research with high-quality data and advanced training pipelines, and transforming AI proofs of concept into proprietary enterprise intelligence that drives measurable results on the P&L.

The Business Analyst (Finance) role sits directly at the vanguard of this movement. You do not need prior AI coding experience; your value lies entirely in your mastery of financial concepts, analytical rigor, and operational judgment.

Role Overview & What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a Finance Expert collaborating with Turing’s AI researchers, your primary objective is to evaluate and enhance the reasoning performance of artificial intelligence models. Your daily responsibilities span across high-level financial domains including corporate finance, investment banking, accounting, FP&A, private equity, and management consulting.

Day-to-Day Responsibilities Include:

  • Model Output Evaluation: Systematically review AI-generated responses in finance-related tasks to pinpoint precise logical gaps, calculation errors, or strategic oversights.
  • Rubric & Assessment Design: Develop structured evaluations and comprehensive rubrics for specialized financial scenarios, including deal analysis, M&A assessments, and valuation models.
  • Collaborative Benchmarking: Partner directly with AI researchers and fellow finance specialists to architect advanced training methodologies and testing benchmarks.

Required Skills, Experience & Qualifications

To succeed in this rigorous environment, applicants must demonstrate robust professional credentials and deep domain knowledge:

  • Professional Experience: Minimum of 2+ years of hands-on experience in investment banking, private equity, venture capital, FP&A, accounting, or financial consulting.
  • Preferred Credentials: CFA (Level I, II, or III) cleared, or CA / CPA / MBA in Finance. Candidates with exceptional, proven on-job experience will also be considered.
  • Core Competencies: A commanding grasp of corporate finance, investment analysis, asset management, and risk management principles.
  • Communication: Outstanding written and verbal English communication skills to articulate complex analytical frameworks and evaluation feedback clearly.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

Traditional corporate roles often lock financial analysts into repetitive workflows and rigid office hours. Partnering with Turing as a freelance contractor unlocks elite professional benefits:

  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Work at the absolute forefront of artificial intelligence, gaining insider perspective on how frontier LLMs are trained and evaluated.
  • Complete Flexibility: Enjoy a fully remote work environment with flexible scheduling tailored to your professional lifestyle.
  • Competitive Compensation: Earn lucrative hourly compensation reflecting your specialized expertise.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Strategy

The engagement is structured to offer optimal flexibility while maintaining high performance standards:

  • Commitment: Flexible commitment ranging between 10 to 30 hours per week.
  • Duration: Approximately 1 month, with strong potential for extension based on ongoing project needs and individual performance.
  • Employment Type: Freelance contract (remote position based in India).

Crucial for Success: To establish stability, unlock long-term task flow, and gain access to advanced project modules, all new contractors must successfully complete the full onboarding process and finish their initial 10 hours of work promptly. This initial milestone cements your status as a reliable core contributor within the network.

The Interview & Selection Process

Turing maintains rigorous quality standards to ensure its expert network comprises top-tier talent. The selection process evaluates your practical domain knowledge, analytical problem-solving abilities, and precision in communication. Once your credentials and initial assessments are reviewed, successful candidates move rapidly into onboarding and project deployment.
