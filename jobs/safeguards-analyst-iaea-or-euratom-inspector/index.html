The Core Mission: Why Frontier AI Needs Nuclear Safeguards Practitioners

As modern large language models ingest massive quantities of scientific papers, regulatory guidelines, and technical manuals, they absorb deep theoretical details regarding nuclear fuel cycles and material management. However, raw data ingestion creates critical security vulnerabilities. A model must understand the delicate boundary between providing legitimate educational or industrial assistance and generating actionable details that bypass non-proliferation norms. Generalist AI evaluators simply do not possess the granular context required to evaluate these high-stakes technical domains.

This is where seasoned safeguards professionals become indispensable. The exact material-balance calculation used by an inspector to close an account can theoretically be manipulated to conceal missing quantities; the specific fuel-cycle step that feeds a power reactor shares overlap with proliferation pathways. An artificial intelligence model that overly restricts routine, professional accountancy queries is failing as badly as one that answers prohibited questions. Drawing and defending that exact operational line requires real-world practitioners who have verified physical materials rather than merely reading about them.

Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities on the Panel:

  • Challenging Prompt Formulation: Design sophisticated, single-turn prompts rooted in your specific inspection and safeguards expertise, categorized systematically across three tiers: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation & Review: Assess model-generated responses against rigorous safety standard guidelines, rendering precise professional judgments on whether each query was handled correctly.
  • Drafting Reference Answers: Author precise technical reference responses detailing what a correct, secure output looks like, along with the deep technical reasoning supporting your evaluation.

💡 Compliance Notice: Your work with Mercor must strictly avoid drawing on classified or export-controlled information, or anything bound by active non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) or prepublication review obligations. If you maintain such obligations, mention them in your application so the scope can be appropriately customized.

Ideal Professional Backgrounds and Qualifications

Mercor maintains exceptionally rigorous vetting standards for its red-teaming panels to guarantee top-tier dataset fidelity. Candidates ideally possess extensive field experience, certifications, and operational backgrounds in areas including:

  • IAEA, Euratom, or State-System Inspectors (SSAC/RSAC): Direct career experience in inspection planning, on-site verification, and formal reporting.
  • Nuclear Material Accountancy: Specialized expertise in facility declarations, inventory change reports (ICRs), and Material Unaccounted For (MUF) evaluation and resolution.
  • Additional Protocol Implementation: Practical familiarity with state declarations, complementary access protocols, and advanced information analysis.
  • Safeguards Instrumentation & Containment/Surveillance: Experience with Non-Destructive Assay (NDA) measurements, seal verification, and unattended monitoring systems.
  • Safeguards-by-Design & Policy Analysis: Professional background in regulatory policy analysis, national laboratories, or government ministries focusing on non-proliferation frameworks.

Candidates with a proven history of technical writing, regulatory reporting, published research, or expert witness backgrounds will find themselves exceptionally well-suited for this work.
