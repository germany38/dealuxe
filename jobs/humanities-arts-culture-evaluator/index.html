Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For professionals grounded in the humanities, fine arts, cultural studies, philosophy, history, or literature, this role provides an intellectually stimulating outlet that transcends traditional academic or institutional constraints. Evaluators are entrusted with auditing, grading, and refining complex AI-generated work products, ensuring absolute factual rigor, creative authenticity, and structural coherence.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Thoroughly review, audit, and grade AI-generated documents, comprehensive reports, analytical spreadsheets, and sophisticated slide decks dealing with cultural, historical, and humanistic subjects.
  • Rigor & Depth Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify historical inaccuracies, shallow thematic interpretations, narrative gaps, and stylistic inconsistencies in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback Generation: Provide clear, articulate, and structured written critiques designed to guide and refine frontier AI models toward nuanced, academically sound human expression.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Standards Alignment: Ensure that complex educational and cultural frameworks synthesized by AI align with high-level pedagogical, aesthetic, and historical standards.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these positions directly influence how artificial intelligence perceives and communicates human history, culture, and art, the qualification standards are curated to attract genuine subject-matter authorities.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: A robust professional or academic background spanning humanities, arts, cultural preservation, education, publishing, or historical research.
  • Language Mastery: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional command over grammar, syntax, style, and academic or professional prose.
  • Software & Presentation Proficiency: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in structuring compelling visual presentations via Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s, MFA, or Ph.D.) from a reputable academic institution in a humanities or arts discipline.
