Detailed Job Overview & Recording Scope

Understanding the structure and scope of this unique gig is critical for applicants looking to optimize their performance and secure high earnings. Unlike ongoing open-ended employment positions, this engagement is designed as a focused, project-based recording collaboration tailored for voice artists and expressive speakers.

Key Responsibilities & Project Structure:

  • Studio-Quality Recording Sessions: Deliver high-fidelity voice samples across diverse script categories, including conversational dialogues, structured narratives, and instructional prompts.
  • Expressive Vocal Delivery: Maintain natural clarity, precise timing, and strong control over emotional range, tone, pacing, and pronunciation across multiple takes.
  • Accent Consistency: Ensure absolute stability in intonation, diction, and acoustic delivery throughout each recording session to meet strict TTS model requirements.
  • Guideline Compliance: Strictly follow detailed acoustic and technical instructions regarding recording environments, microphone positioning, pop filter usage, and raw audio file formatting.

The engagement centers primarily around a single initial recording session lasting approximately 4 hours. Depending on client performance metrics and evolving project needs, a secondary session of comparable length may follow, though subsequent hours are subject to project requirements.

Candidate Requirements & Technical Qualifications

Because artificial intelligence models require clean, studio-grade training datasets to function properly, the technical and vocal qualification standards are clearly defined.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • Regional Authenticity: Native Southern American English speaker currently residing in the United States with a genuine, authentic Southern accent.
  • Vocal Clarity: A naturally clear, expressive, and engaging voice. While professional voice acting, audiobook narration, or broadcast experience is a major asset, newcomers with great vocal talent are welcome to apply.
  • Acoustic Setup: Access to a professional or near-professional home recording setup, including a quality microphone, quiet recording environment, pop filter, and baseline acoustic treatment.
  • Technical Engagement: Ability to follow complex scripts precisely while sustaining dynamic emotional range and style variations.
  • Voice Cloning Consent: Comfortable with having your voice cloned exclusively for the client's internal CX AI Agent (recordings are strictly confidential and will never be resold, licensed, or repurposed outside of this specific internal use case).

Preferred Qualifications: Prior experience recording for text-to-speech datasets, IVR telephone systems, audiobooks, or familiarity with digital audio workstations like Audacity, Adobe Audition, and Reaper.

Contract Terms, Flexibility, and Global Payout Infrastructure

Contracting through Mercor offers independent creators maximum autonomy without the administrative headaches of traditional employment. Key terms include:

  • Independent Contractor Model: Complete your recording sessions on your own schedule from your home studio setup anywhere within the United States.

  • Prompt Weekly Compensation: Earnings are disbursed reliably on a weekly basis via secure global payment rails such as Stripe and Wise based on completed milestones and services rendered.
