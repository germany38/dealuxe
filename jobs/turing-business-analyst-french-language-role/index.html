Role Overview: Business Analyst (French Language)

The Business Analyst role at Turing is designed for sharp analytical thinkers with stellar English and French comprehension skills. The core mission is to help train large language models by reading, summarizing, and dissecting dense content into clear logical blocks, conducting rigorous online research, validating factual claims, and solving complex puzzles alongside LLMs.

This role not only directly contributes to fine-tuning state-of-the-art AI systems but also transforms you into an expert on how to leverage AI to maximize your own analytical output—future-proofing your career in an AI-driven economy.

You Are a Perfect Fit If You Have:

  • Mandatory Multilingual Fluency: Professional-working proficiency in both English and French.
  • Analytical Rigor: Outstanding research capabilities, data interpretation skills, and a strong foundation in logical reasoning and basic arithmetic.
  • Feedback Proficiency: The ability to provide precise, constructive feedback and detailed annotations for AI training loops.
  • Lateral Thinking: Creative problem-solving skills to construct scenarios that test model limitations.
  • Remote Independence: Self-motivation, excellent communication skills, and the discipline to thrive in a remote work environment.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a Business Analyst, your daily routine involves tackling complex analytical questions and designing scenarios that challenge LLMs to improve their reasoning. Models frequently stumble on nuanced logical and mathematical problems. Your job is to provide the correct answers and thorough explanations to guide their learning.

Example Scenario 1: Given a complex distribution of sales data across multiple geographic locations over a specific timeline, you analyze which location grew the most while accounting for sudden statistical variability at the beginning of the period.

Example Scenario 2: In a town with distinct neighborhoods (Oak, Pine, Maple, Elm) subject to rigid delivery rules (e.g., visiting Oak before Pine), you resolve routing puzzles based on initial delivery constraints.

Note: No specialized prior domain engineering experience is required; your analytical mindset and linguistic precision are your greatest assets.

Required Skills, Experience & Preferred Qualifications

While open to diverse professional backgrounds, successful candidates typically exhibit the following qualifications:

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or undergraduate coursework in Engineering, Literature, Journalism, Communications, Arts, Statistics, or related analytical fields. Candidates without a formal degree but with robust professional writing experience are also welcomed.
  • Professional Background: Experience in roles such as business analyst, research analyst, copywriter, journalist, technical writer, editor, or translator.
  • Technical Proficiency: Comfort using Excel, Google Suite, and digital research tools.
  • Hardware Setup: A reliable desktop or laptop computer paired with a stable, high-speed internet connection.

Perks and Benefits of Working with Turing

Working with Turing as a contractor unlocks unique professional advantages:

  • Competitive Compensation: Attractive pay rates based on your specific expertise and experience level.
  • Remote Flexibility: Enjoy complete location independence and flexible working hours.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Collaborate directly with top-tier AI labs and gain firsthand experience working with state-of-the-art language models.
  • Growth Potential: Opportunities for contract extensions and higher-tier projects based on consistent performance.

Engagement Details & Commitment

To ensure smooth project execution and team alignment, specific operational parameters govern this engagement:

  • Commitment Options: Choose between 20, 30, or 40 hours per week (minimum 4 hours per day).
  • Time Zone Overlap: Requires a 2 to 5-hour daily overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST / UTC-8).
  • Engagement Type: Contractor / freelance assignment (does not include traditional medical benefits or paid leave).
  • Contract Duration: Initial 2-month term with potential for extension.

Interview Process, Onboarding & Stability Phase

Turing utilizes a streamlined, performance-driven evaluation and onboarding pathway:

  1. Automated Challenge: Shortlisted candidates receive an automated analytical assessment to test logical reasoning.
  2. Writing & Language Assessment: Passing the analytical test unlocks a business writing assessment in English followed by a French language proficiency evaluation.
  3. Immediate Onboarding: Once assessments are cleared, you are cleared for onboarding!
  4. The 10-Hour Stability Milestone: Upon entering the platform, you will complete an initial 10 hours of work. This stability period verifies your workflow integration, ensures seamless technical access, and unlocks a broader array of incoming tasks and project streams.
