The Intersection of Advanced Spreadsheet Modeling and Generative AI Training

Microsoft Excel has been the silent engine driving global enterprise decision-making, financial modeling, and data analytics for decades. From complex balance sheets and multi-variable financial projections to operational dashboards and automated macro scripts, Excel remains the universal language of business logic. Today, however, Excel mastery is expanding beyond corporate boardrooms and financial institutions into an entirely new frontier: training next-generation artificial intelligence systems.

As leading technology companies race to develop sophisticated Generative AI models capable of complex reasoning, data manipulation, and spreadsheet generation, they face a critical bottleneck. AI models cannot learn elite spreadsheet design, advanced formula architecture, or nuanced problem-solving from standard public datasets alone. They require human experts who possess deep, cross-industry spreadsheet knowledge. The newly launched Excel Expert — General role hosted on Mercor bridges this gap, paying exceptional professionals between $70 and $100 per hour to shape how artificial intelligence understands and executes spreadsheet workflows.

Comprehensive Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

This engagement is tailor-made for versatile generalists who have applied Microsoft Excel across a broad spectrum of industries and functional domains. Rather than focusing on a single narrow niche, successful candidates bring genuine breadth—combining data modeling, dashboard creation, financial analysis, and VBA automation into a cohesive skill set.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Model Guidance & Research Collaboration: Guide engineering and AI research teams to identify knowledge gaps, address logic failures, and improve model performance on sophisticated Excel-based tasks.
  • Task Design & Creation: Design realistic, highly functional Excel challenges drawn directly from professional cross-industry experience, accompanied by clean, well-structured, and accurate solutions.
  • Evaluation & Feedback Auditing: Review, audit, and evaluate AI-generated spreadsheet solutions, providing clear, constructive written feedback to refine model accuracy.
  • Cross-Functional Quality Control: Partner closely with other subject matter experts and data annotators to maintain rigorous consistency, formatting precision, and functional integrity across all training datasets.

Essential Qualifications and Technical Requirements

Because these frontier AI models power tools utilized by top-tier global enterprises, the qualification standards are intentionally high. Applicants must demonstrate top-tier command over both basic and advanced spreadsheet functionalities.

Core Qualifications Checklist:
  • Advanced Technical Mastery: Proven, professional-level proficiency in advanced Excel formulas (such as XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, and dynamic arrays), complex PivotTables, data modeling, dynamic dashboards, Power Query, Power Pivot, and macros/VBA development.
  • Cross-Industry Breadth: Broad application of spreadsheet engineering across multiple business functions rather than a single isolated vertical.
  • Schedule Availability: Ability to engage reliably for 40 hours per week during weekdays through the end of September, transitioning to 20 hours per week thereafter.
  • Communication & Geography: Exceptional verbal and written communication skills paired with mandatory United States residency.

Preferred Enhancements: Prior teaching, tutoring, or academic experience (holding a PhD is a strong plus), alongside previous background in human data annotation or rubric training on Mercor or similar platforms.
