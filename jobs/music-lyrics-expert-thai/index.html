Inside the Role: What Does a Music & Lyrics Expert Do?

Generative AI audio models are learning to compose melodies, harmonies, and lyrical verses at unprecedented speeds. However, generating music that resonates culturally, respects copyright boundaries, and captures authentic human emotion requires sophisticated human critique. As a Music & Lyrics Expert, your primary job is to listen critically, analyze structure, and grade AI output against the highest standards of the Thai and global music scenes.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Similarity Auditing: Compare AI-generated lyrics and compositions against existing published songs to detect unintended overlaps, structural similarities, or copyright issues.
  • Quality & Creativity Scoring: Rate lyrics based on originality, emotional resonance, rhyming schemes, and overall prompt adherence.
  • Linguistic & Cultural Nuance: Evaluate whether lyrics sound natural in Thai, ensuring correct slang usage, colloquialisms, idioms, and regional expressions.
  • Critical Listening: Use professional headphones or studio monitors to assess audio fidelity, mixing elements, and sonic texture.

🎵 Earning Structure: Earn $18 per hour on a flexible schedule. Plus, when you refer peers who get hired, you earn up to $180 per successful referral, alongside bonuses tied to active project hours!
