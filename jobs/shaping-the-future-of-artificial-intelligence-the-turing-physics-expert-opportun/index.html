Company: Turing · Location: Remote — Global · Type: contractor · Posted: 2026-09-19

Frontier AI & Physics Research

Discover how graduate-level physicists and researchers are training next-generation large language models through rigorous scientific benchmarking and problem architecture.

As artificial intelligence systems transition from simple statistical pattern matchers into autonomous reasoning engines, the global tech industry faces a profound limitation: the scarcity of elite domain expertise. While models can synthesize text effortlessly, they frequently falter when confronted with advanced physics problems requiring multi-step mathematical derivation, deep conceptual abstraction, and symbolic manipulation.

To bridge this gap, Turing—the world's leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs—engages top-tier scientific minds to construct the foundational training data and evaluation benchmarks required for advanced AI systems. If you possess an advanced background in physics and a passion for shaping cutting-edge technology, the Physics Expert role represents an unprecedented career catalyst.

About Turing: Powering the AI Revolution

Headquartered in San Francisco, California, Turing serves as a trusted partner for global enterprises and premier AI labs deploying advanced machine learning systems. Turing empowers customers through two primary avenues:

  • Accelerating Frontier Research: Providing high-quality training data, sophisticated training pipelines, and elite researchers specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and multimodal agents.
  • Enterprise Transformation: Translating theoretical artificial intelligence proofs of concept into robust, reliable enterprise intelligence that drives measurable business impact.

Role Overview: Physics Expert

In this specialized role, you will work directly on advanced projects designed to fine-tune large language models (such as ChatGPT) using your strong analytical capabilities and superior English comprehension. The ideal candidate maintains a rock-solid foundation in physics—drawing from engineering entrance examination rigor up to graduate and PhD-level programs.

Your core mission is to deconstruct complex physics concepts into clear, articulate explanations while collaborating with top-tier AI researchers. This engagement not only contributes to the evolution of artificial intelligence but also equips you with deep insights into how to leverage AI tools effectively, future-proofing your career in an AI-first economy.

Key Required Physics Domains

  • Classical Mechanics: Advanced dynamics, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formulations, rigid body motion, and oscillatory systems.
  • Electromagnetism and Optics: Maxwell's equations, electrodynamics, electromagnetic wave propagation, and wave optics.
  • Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics: Laws of thermodynamics, phase transitions, partition functions, and quantum statistical mechanics.

What a Day-to-Day Looks Like

Working as a Physics Expert at Turing is intellectually demanding and dynamically structured. Your daily responsibilities encompass:

  • Problem Architecture: Designing and solving challenging, novel physics problems specifically engineered to probe the limitations of large language models.
  • Solution Engineering: Crafting clear, high-quality, step-by-step solutions with impeccable, well-articulated mathematical and physical reasoning.
  • AI Alignment: Collaborating with LLM research leads to align evaluation tasks with specific performance goals, targeting areas where models traditionally struggle—such as symbolic manipulation and multi-step deduction.
  • Benchmark Formulation: Helping define brand-new evaluation standards spanning early undergraduate to doctoral-level curricula.

Required Skills & Experience

To thrive in this rigorous remote environment, candidates must exhibit:

  • Analytical & Research Skills: Exceptional ability to conduct deep technical research and synthesize complex physical systems.
  • Constructive Feedback: Proficiency in providing detailed annotations, error analysis, and constructive feedback on model outputs.
  • Creative Thinking: Lateral and innovative problem-solving capabilities to generate unique edge-case scenarios.
  • Communication: Outstanding written and verbal English communication skills suited for asynchronous remote collaboration.
  • Independence: A self-motivated, proactive work ethic capable of operating autonomously.
  • Technical Setup: A reliable desktop or laptop computer paired with a high-speed internet connection.

Preferred Qualifications

Candidates currently pursuing or holding a Master’s, Ph.D., or Postdoctoral degree in Physics, Applied Physics, or a closely related quantitative field are strongly encouraged to apply. A demonstrated history of approaching complex physical problems with structured, logical frameworks is essential.

Perks of Freelancing with Turing

Engaging with Turing offers freedom, flexibility, and high-impact professional exposure:

  • Complete Remote Autonomy: Work from anywhere in the world in a fully flexible remote environment.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Collaborate directly on frontier AI projects alongside leading language model laboratories.
  • Career Growth: Enjoy opportunities for contract extensions based on exceptional performance and evolving project requirements.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Strategy

This position operates as a freelance contractor engagement (note that contractor assignments do not include standard medical benefits or paid leave). To ensure long-term stability and unlock access to a broader tier of advanced tasks, new contractors should complete the comprehensive onboarding process immediately and dedicate their initial 10 hours of work toward establishing high-quality output and platform familiarity.
