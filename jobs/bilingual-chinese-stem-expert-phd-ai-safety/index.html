Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For PhD-level scientists, academic researchers, and laboratory specialists seeking a high-impact transition into AI safety evaluation, understanding the exact scope of this role is essential. The position centers on evaluating, red-teaming, and strengthening how frontier AI models comprehend, process, and respond to advanced scientific queries written in Chinese and English.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Expert Prompt Engineering: Author rigorous, expert-level technical prompts in Chinese across complex scientific subfields, challenging the model's limits and reasoning capabilities.
  • Response Annotation & Evaluation: Evaluate and annotate model outputs for scientific accuracy, depth of reasoning, helpfulness, and appropriate adherence to safety boundaries regarding sensitive or dual-use content.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these positions directly dictate the guardrails of models deployed globally, the qualifications demand exceptional educational pedigree and domain mastery.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • Educational Background: A PhD in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific discipline (ongoing or successfully completed) with deep specialization in technical subject areas.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Chinese, coupled with professional, business-level written and spoken English.
  • Technical Depth: Comprehensive familiarity with modern laboratory protocols, computational techniques, and theoretical foundations within your specific scientific subfield.
  • Safety Judgment: Sound, mature professional judgment regarding scientific safety, biosecurity, chemical security, and the responsible handling of sensitive or dual-use technical data.

Preferred Qualifications & Preferences: Based in Hong Kong or the wider East Asia region (though global applicants are fully welcome); specialization in radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, energetic materials, radiation physics, molecular biology, virology, genetics, or organic/molecular synthesis; background in chemical or biological safety compliance; and previous experience in technical red-teaming or content peer review.
