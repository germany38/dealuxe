Elite Remote Software Engineering

An exhaustive look into Turing’s remote JavaScript / TypeScript Full-Stack Developer position, covering day-to-day responsibilities, technical stacks, evaluation processes, and strategies for long-term project stability.

The intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence represents the most lucrative and intellectually stimulating frontier in modern technology. As artificial intelligence models evolve from basic text generators into autonomous, reasoning agents capable of complex multi-step execution, the demand for elite software architects who can train, benchmark, and fine-tune these systems has skyrocketed.

Enter Turing, one of the world’s fastest-growing AI companies accelerating the advancement and deployment of powerful AI systems. Turing serves as a critical bridge between leading artificial intelligence laboratories and global enterprises seeking mission-critical technological solutions. For senior full-stack developers specializing in JavaScript and TypeScript, Turing offers an unprecedented opportunity to influence how next-generation AI models understand, write, and evaluate code.

About Turing: Pioneering the Human Intelligence Layer

Turing operates on a dual mission: collaborating with top-tier AI labs to advance frontier model capabilities across reasoning, coding, multimodality, and STEM domains, and applying those advanced systems to solve real-world corporate challenges. By leveraging an elite network of global engineering talent, Turing is building the foundational human intelligence layer required to make AI safe, reliable, and exceptionally accurate.

Working with Turing is fundamentally different from traditional software outsourcing. Instead of building standard client web applications, you become an expert evaluator, architect, and trainer, teaching AI models how to reason through complex programming bugs, architectural trade-offs, and security vulnerabilities.

Role Overview: JavaScript / TypeScript Full-Stack Developer

The JavaScript / TypeScript Full-Stack Developer engagement is tailored for seasoned engineers who thrive on deep technical challenges. In this role, you will work alongside US-based companies and premier AI researchers to evaluate, benchmark, and refine commercial and research AI systems.

You are an ideal fit for this role if you possess a rigorous background in modular web development, a deep understanding of JavaScript ES6+, and hands-on familiarity with backend frameworks like Node.js and Nest.js, as well as frontend libraries such as React, Vue.js, or Angular.

Core Technology Stack:

  • Languages: JavaScript (ES6+), TypeScript
  • Back-End: Node.js, Nest.js
  • Front-End: React, Vue.js, Angular
  • Infrastructure & Tooling: Docker, modular build systems, automated testing frameworks

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a contractor on Turing’s AI training and benchmarking projects, your daily responsibilities blend high-level software engineering with rigorous analytical evaluation:

  • Code Authoring & Optimization: Design, develop, and maintain efficient, high-quality code used to train and optimize advanced AI models.
  • Model Benchmarking (Evals): Conduct rigorous evaluations to test model performance across diverse programming paradigms and analyze results for continuous improvement.
  • Response Ranking & Curation: Evaluate and rank AI-generated code responses to user queries, ensuring strict alignment with functional correctness, readability, and security standards.
  • Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT): Lead initiatives in SFT by creating and maintaining pristine, task-specific datasets that teach models proper syntax and logic.
  • RLHF Collaboration: Partner with researchers to execute Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback, refining reward models and complex reasoning loops.
  • Peer Review & Quality Assurance: Conduct thorough peer reviews of code and documentation, providing constructive feedback and identifying subtle logical errors.

Required Skills and Qualifications

To succeed in this rigorous environment, applicants must demonstrate exceptional technical depth and communication skills:

  • Extensive, demonstrable professional experience working with JavaScript or TypeScript.
  • Solid command of modern JavaScript ES6 syntax alongside either Node.js, React, Nest.js, Angular, or Vue.js.
  • Strong background in modular web app development, scalable architectures, and rigorous testing methodologies (unit/integration testing).
  • Familiarity with containerization tools like Docker.
  • Prior software Quality Assurance (QA) and test planning experience is considered a strong plus.
  • Flawless written and spoken English communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex technical rationales clearly.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Traditional corporate software engineering often comes with rigid office politics, grueling commutes, and monotonous legacy maintenance. Turing redefines professional autonomy:

  • 100% Remote Flexibility: Work from any location worldwide with complete schedule autonomy.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Collaborate directly with leading LLM developers and gain early access to frontier AI technology.
  • Professional Growth: Sharpen your analytical, code-review, and systems-architecture skills by auditing state-of-the-art machine learning outputs.

Engagement Details & Commitment Options

This engagement is structured as a flexible contractor assignment (no medical benefits or paid leave included). To accommodate diverse schedules, Turing provides three commitment tiers:

  • Option 1: 20 hours per week (at least 4 hours per day)
  • Option 2: 30 hours per week
  • Option 3: 40 hours per week

Note: A minimum 4-hour daily overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST) is required. The initial contract duration is 1 month, with expected start dates as early as next week.

The Interview Process

Turing’s streamlined evaluation process takes approximately 75 minutes total:

  1. Technical Round (60 minutes): A deep-dive technical interview assessing your command of JavaScript/TypeScript, system architecture, and problem-solving abilities.
  2. Cultural & Offer Discussion (15 minutes): A brief discussion covering alignment, communication, scheduling preferences, and onboarding details.

Maximizing Success: Onboarding and Your First 10 Hours

Securing a contract is only the beginning. To achieve long-term stability, unlock continuous task streams, and establish yourself as an elite contributor on the platform, new developers must complete their platform onboarding thoroughly and successfully log their first 10 hours of active work without delay. Hitting this milestone promptly signals reliability, ensures account stability, and grants priority access to higher-tier tasks and project extensions.
