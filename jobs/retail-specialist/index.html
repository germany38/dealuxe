Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For senior category managers, merchandising directors, and retail operations executives looking to apply their strategic background to technology development, understanding the structural expectations of this role is vital. As part of a cutting-edge GenAI team, your primary function is to bring rigor, structured evaluation, and absolute realism to AI training datasets.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Bridging Knowledge Gaps: Guide elite research and engineering teams to close complex knowledge gaps concerning retail merchandising, category management, and enterprise operations reasoning.
  • Task Design: Design challenging, domain-relevant retail scenarios and write accurate, meticulously reasoned solutions grounded in authentic retail practices.
  • Model Evaluation: Evaluate complex AI model outputs against structured rubrics, delivering clear, written feedback covering correctness, commercial judgment, and reasoning quality.
  • Guideline Refinement: Develop, test, and refine evaluation guidelines and scoring rubrics tailored specifically to high-level retail tasks.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate closely with fellow subject matter experts (SMEs) to guarantee consistency, logical soundness, and superior accuracy across all training data.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these positions directly dictate the accuracy and capability of AI architectures destined for enterprise deployment, the qualification criteria are rigorous and demand verified professional distinction.

Mandatory Qualifications:
  • Professional Tenure: 8+ years of dedicated, hands-on professional experience in retail functions such as merchandising, category management, retail operations, buying, or planning.
  • Brand Pedigree: Experience gained at a recognized, top-tier organization (e.g., Amazon, Walmart, Target, Nike, Costco, Home Depot, or equivalent scale).
  • AI/LLM Evaluation Experience: Prior hands-on experience evaluating LLM or AI model outputs against rubrics or structured scoring criteria (candidates must detail this experience during application).
  • Career Progression: Demonstrable, upward career trajectory (e.g., advancing from Category Manager to Senior Manager or Director of Merchandising).
  • Time Commitment: Ability to engage reliably for at least 35 hours per week during standard U.S. weekdays.
  • Communication Skills: Exceptional verbal and written English communication, problem-solving, and interpersonal competencies.
