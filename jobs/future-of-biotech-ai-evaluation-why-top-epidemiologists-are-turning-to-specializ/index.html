Understanding the Role: Sizing Patient Populations for Modern Therapeutics

At the intersection of advanced technology and pharmaceutical development lies a critical bottleneck: ensuring that AI models and automated evaluation frameworks accurately interpret complex medical and commercial drug data. Real-world evidence (RWE) is notoriously tricky to standardize. When an AI system evaluates a drug development program, it requires precise calibration from domain specialists who understand how to translate raw data registries into concrete patient funnels.

In this specialized pilot role with Mercor, successful candidates will tackle key analytical responsibilities:

  • Rubric Creation and Critique: Designing and reviewing rigorous evaluation rubrics used to analyze commercial drugs and therapeutic development programs.
  • Patient Population Sizing: Quantifying target patient numbers for specific drugs and indications across the entire continuum—from prevalence and incidence down to diagnosed, treated, and addressable patient pools.
  • Data Integrity Validation: Auditing and judging whether third-party or automated patient-population estimates are scientifically sound, well-sourced, and free from dangerous over- or under-counting errors.
  • Cross-Indication Application: Executing these validation frameworks across a diverse portfolio of drugs and therapeutic indications featured in the pilot.

Requirements and Qualifications

Because this project demands precise, high-level analytical judgments, specific criteria must be met to ensure quality outcomes. This opportunity is ideally suited for US-based professionals who match the following background profile:

  • Advanced Education: A graduate degree in epidemiology, biostatistics, public health, or a closely related quantitative medical discipline.
  • Experience Threshold: A minimum of 5+ years of active, practical experience working as a professional epidemiologist.
  • Industry Knowledge: Deep familiarity with biotech and pharmaceutical drug development pipelines and lifecycle management.
  • Data Fluency: Proven expertise in converting complex real-world data sources—such as medical claims, electronic health records (EHR), and clinical registries—into actionable patient funnels and epidemiological metrics.
