Detailed Job Overview & Scope of Work

Understanding the exact deliverables and structure of this project is critical for applicants wanting to secure an engagement. This specialized recording initiative is designed to build an internal Customer Experience (CX) AI Agent for a premier AI research partner. Your voice recordings will be used exclusively for this designated application and will not be licensed, sold, or repurposed for any other dataset or commercial product.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Script Execution: Record high-quality voice samples across a diverse array of scripts, including conversational dialogue, narrative prompts, and instructional guides.
  • Expressive Delivery: Deliver crystal-clear, natural, and emotionally engaging speech while maintaining tight control over pacing, intonation, and pronunciation.
  • Vocal Consistency: Sustain absolute uniformity in accent, tone, and delivery style throughout the duration of the recording session.
  • Technical Compliance: Follow strict recording guidelines regarding acoustic environments, microphone placement, pop filtering, and professional file formatting.
  • Dynamic Variation: Perform multiple vocal takes with subtle or distinct shifts in emphasis, emotion, and conversational style whenever required by project directives.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because the output directly dictates the quality of cutting-edge speech models, candidates must meet specific geographic, linguistic, and technical prerequisites before onboarding.

Essential Candidate Requirements:
  • Geographic & Linguistic Profile: Native English speaker with an authentic Australian accent, currently residing in Australia.
  • Voice Quality: A naturally clear, expressive, and resonant voice. Prior professional voice acting, narration, or broadcasting experience is celebrated, though enthusiastic newcomers with exceptional vocal clarity are welcome.
  • Technical Setup: Access to a professional or near-professional recording setup, featuring a high-quality microphone, a quiet, treated acoustic environment, and standard accessories like pop filters.
  • Vocal Range: Strong command of intonation, diction, emotional nuance, and script interpretation.
  • Time Commitment: Availability for an initial ~4-hour recording session, with potential for a follow-up session of similar length based on client demand.
  • Voice Cloning Consent: Full comfort and agreement with having your voice cloned exclusively for the client's internal CX AI Agent architecture.

Preferred Qualifications and Technical Familiarity

While the primary requirement is an authentic Australian accent paired with pristine audio capture capabilities, certain backgrounds will help candidates stand out during the application and review process:

  • Prior professional recording experience tailored for text-to-speech engines, audiobooks, Interactive Voice Response (IVR) phone systems, or specialized AI voice datasets.
  • Hands-on familiarity with industry-standard audio editing software suites such as Audacity, Adobe Audition, or Reaper.
  • Demonstrated ability to effortlessly transition between multiple stylistic delivery formats, ranging from casual conversational tones to polished corporate narratives and energetic presentations.
