Comprehensive Breakdown of the Role and Core Responsibilities

Understanding the day-to-day expectations of an AI Evaluator in the IT GRC sector helps professionals gauge how their background translates into this unique contracting framework. Unlike traditional corporate consulting, which often involves endless slide decks, stakeholder alignment meetings, and traveling to client sites, this position focuses purely on high-level intellectual evaluation and precision auditing.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Rigorous Artifact Assessment: Review, analyze, and grade AI-generated documents, compliance spreadsheets, and security slide decks covering topics ranging from ISO 27001 and SOC 2 controls to NIST frameworks and GDPR compliance strategies.
  • Accuracy and Fact-Checking: Identify subtle technical inaccuracies, regulatory misinterpretations, policy gaps, and structural formatting errors in automated security deliverables.
  • Constructive Analytical Feedback: Write clear, precise, and structured feedback that retrains and aligns frontier AI models with enterprise-grade security standards and regulatory nuances.
  • Domain Quality Assurance: Ensure that the strategic risk assessments and governance models produced by artificial intelligence adhere strictly to industry best practices and legal compliance mandates.

Essential Requirements and Preferred Qualifications

Because these evaluations directly train the AI systems utilized by leading tech enterprises and financial institutions, the qualification criteria are designed to filter for seasoned domain authorities rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Professional Experience: Proven background in cybersecurity, information technology governance, risk management, compliance (IT GRC), or information security auditing.
  • English Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional technical and professional writing capabilities.
  • Software & Tool Mastery: High-level proficiency across Microsoft Office and Google Workspace ecosystems, with specialized expertise in presentation applications like Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint.

Preferred Qualifications: An advanced academic degree (Master’s or higher) from a reputable institution in cybersecurity, information systems, business administration, or a related discipline.
