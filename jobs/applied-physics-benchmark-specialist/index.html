Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For physicists, astrophysicists, and quantum researchers looking to pivot into AI training and model evaluation, understanding the core expectations of this position is critical. As an Applied Physics Benchmark Specialist, your mission is to author and review elite academic assessment content designed to push frontier models to their absolute limits.

Contributors are typically assigned to one of two primary task workflows:

  • Question Authoring: Crafting original, highly challenging multiple-choice questions within your specialized domain of physics, grading their difficulty accurately, and submitting them for technical review.
  • Question Verification: Auditing pre-written assessment questions for absolute clarity, mathematical precision, and technical rigor. Editors verify solutions, refine problem statements, and document all changes made.

Core Physics Domains Covered:

The scope of this benchmark initiative spans specialized, high-demand subfields of modern physical sciences:

  • Semiconductor Physics & Nanoelectronics / Spintronics
  • Photonics, Quantum Optics & Ultrafast Physics
  • Quantum Sensing & Metrology
  • Plasma Physics & Controlled Fusion Energy
  • Nonlinear Dynamics, Chaos & Fluid Turbulence
  • Geophysics & Advanced Reservoir Simulation

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Advanced Problem Design: Author original physics questions that test deep conceptual understanding and mathematical formalism rather than surface-level recall or rote memorization.
  • Unambiguous Formulation: Ensure every problem is self-contained and precisely defined, embedding all necessary boundary conditions and parameters directly in the prompt.
  • Difficulty Tiering: Rigorously rate each question according to standardized metrics: Medium (introductory undergraduate level), Hard (advanced undergraduate level), or Expert (post-graduate doctoral level and above).
  • Distractor Engineering: Formulate 1 correct answer accompanied by 9 plausible, subtly flawed alternative options specifically designed to challenge expert-level solvers and advanced AI algorithms.
  • Chain-of-Thought Documentation: Write comprehensive, step-by-step mathematical solutions featuring clear, concise intermediate derivations structured cleanly in markdown format.
  • Academic Citations: Supply 1 to 5 reputable academic references per question sourced directly from peer-reviewed journals or accredited university repositories.
  • Rigorous Verification: For verification assignments, systematically flag any ambiguity, logical gaps, or solvability flaws while providing clear justifications for edits.
