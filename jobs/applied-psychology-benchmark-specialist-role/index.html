Detailed Job Description & Responsibilities

The role is structured around two distinct yet complementary task types designed to test deep conceptual reasoning rather than surface-level recall:

  • Question Authoring: Specialists design original, highly challenging multiple-choice questions within their specialized psychology domains. Authors must accurately rate question difficulty, create one undeniably correct answer, and formulate nine plausible, subtly incorrect distractors that challenge expert-level solvers. Furthermore, authors write detailed, step-by-step Chain-of-Thought solutions in markdown format, backed by 1 to 5 reputable academic references.
  • Question Verification: Verification specialists meticulously review pre-written questions for accuracy, clarity, and logical rigor. They edit problem statements when necessary, re-verify difficulty ratings, and ensure every component meets the highest academic standards.

Did You Know? The expectations for this project go far beyond standard multiple-choice questions. Because these benchmarks are built to challenge advanced AI systems, your questions must be completely self-contained, unambiguous, and grounded in rigorous empirical literature.

Ideal Candidate Profile & Qualifications

Because these benchmarks serve as rigorous stress-tests for cutting-edge AI architectures, the selection criteria are intentionally selective. Mercor looks for candidates who combine academic rigor with stellar communication skills:

  • Educational Background: A PhD, PsyD, or active doctoral candidacy in Psychology or a closely related discipline is strongly preferred. Master's degrees will be considered for candidates demonstrating exceptional depth in a specific niche subdomain.
  • Theoretical Command: A master-level grasp of graduate psychological theory, experimental design, research methodology, and empirical literature.
  • Professional Add-ons: Clinical licensure, active research portfolios, or published papers in reputable peer-reviewed psychology journals represent significant advantages.
  • Written Communication: Exceptional command of written English to convey complex cognitive principles with complete clarity and conciseness.
