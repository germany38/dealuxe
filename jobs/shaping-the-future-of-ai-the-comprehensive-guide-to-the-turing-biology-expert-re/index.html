Achieving this breakthrough requires more than raw computing power—it demands the rigorous domain intelligence of world-class human experts.

By bridging elite human expertise with cutting-edge machine learning pipelines, Turing enables systems to perform reliably and drive measurable real-world impact. For advanced researchers in the life sciences, Turing's remote Biology Expert role offers a unique pathway to future-proof careers in an AI-first economy.

About Turing: Powering the Intelligence Layer

Turing supports the global AI ecosystem through two primary pillars. First, the company accelerates frontier research by providing high-quality data, sophisticated training workflows, and top-tier researchers specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, and autonomous agents. Second, Turing helps major enterprises transform experimental AI proofs of concept into robust, proprietary intelligence architectures that deliver lasting enterprise value.

Role Overview: Fine-Tuning LLMs Through Biological Rigor

As a Biology Expert at Turing, your core mission involves working on specialized projects designed to fine-tune large language models (such as ChatGPT) using your strong analytical capabilities and English comprehension. The ideal candidate possesses a rock-solid foundation in biology—particularly at the level expected in graduate, postgraduate, and PhD programs—and the ability to distill complex biological concepts into clear, precise explanations.

Required Skills & Core Qualifications:

  • Academic Background: Candidates pursuing or holding a Master’s, Ph.D., or Postdoctoral degree in Biology, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Molecular & Cellular Biology, or Microbiology.
  • Analytical Rigor: Exceptional research and problem-solving skills to approach complex biological challenges methodically.
  • Feedback & Annotation: The ability to provide constructive, detailed feedback and technical annotations.
  • Communication: Outstanding structured communication skills for seamless remote collaboration.
  • Autonomy: Self-motivated disposition capable of driving independent tasks in a distributed remote setting.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

Working as a contractor with Turing is dynamic and intellectually stimulating. Your daily activities bridge deep scientific inquiry with cutting-edge AI evaluation:

  • Challenging Problem Design: Construct intricate biology problems specifically engineered to test and expose the limitations of large language models.
  • Rigorous Solution Engineering: Develop comprehensive, step-by-step solutions featuring clear and faultless logical reasoning.
  • AI Researcher Collaboration: Partner directly with LLM researchers to align problem types with specific evaluation targets, particularly focusing on conceptual abstraction and data interpretation.
  • Benchmark Formulation: Contribute to establishing brand-new evaluation standards spanning early undergraduate to PhD-level biological curricula.

Perks of Freelancing With Turing

Contracting with Turing offers exceptional professional advantages tailored for modern knowledge workers:

  • Complete Location Independence: Enjoy a fully remote work environment operating from your own desktop or laptop setup.
  • Cutting-Edge Exposure: Collaborate directly with leading LLM development companies at the vanguard of artificial intelligence.
  • Career Longevity: Tap into strong potential for contract extensions based on consistent performance and ongoing project demands.

Engagement Details & Onboarding Best Practices

This position is structured as a flexible contractor/freelancer assignment (without medical or paid leave benefits). Because projects require immediate precision and commitment, completing your full onboarding process smoothly is essential.

To ensure long-term stability and unlock access to advanced, high-value tasks, experts are advised to complete their onboarding thoroughly and successfully log their first 10 hours of work. Meeting this initial milestone establishes your reliability within the platform and opens doors to continuous project assignments.
