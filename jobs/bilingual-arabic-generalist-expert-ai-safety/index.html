Understanding the Role: What Does an AI Safety Expert Do? / فهم الدور: ماذا يفعل خبير أمان الذكاء الاصطناعي؟

Artificial intelligence models are developing at breakneck speeds, but raw computer algorithms cannot understand cultural nuances, idioms, regional dialects, or complex ethical boundaries on their own. Tech labs and enterprise AI developers rely heavily on human intelligence to evaluate, refine, and secure their models.

As a Bilingual Arabic Generalist Expert at Mercor, your core mission is to evaluate how advanced AI systems handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use prompts in Arabic. You ensure that artificial intelligence models respect cultural sensitivities, maintain factual accuracy, and adhere to strict safety guidelines.

  • Expert Prompt Creation: Write sophisticated, intricate prompts in Arabic across diverse, sensitive subject categories.
  • Structured Classification: Apply rigorous evaluation frameworks to categorize and grade AI-generated responses and dialogues.
  • Adversarial Testing (Red-Teaming): Spot edge cases, adversarial phrasing, and escalation risks, documenting your analytical rationale clearly.
