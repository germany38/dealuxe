Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

Unlike traditional writing or coding gigs, this role is strictly evaluative. You are not tasked with generating AI models, writing prompts, or executing ground-up tasks. Instead, you act as the ultimate subject-matter authority, auditing an AI agent's complete attempt at a realistic workplace task.

When reviewing an item—which typically takes about an hour—you will examine the task description as written, the specific steps taken by the AI agent, the generated files, and the evaluation score sheet filled out by an automated AI grader. Your primary objective is to bring your practical, hands-on professional judgment and answer four fundamental evaluation pillars:

1. Is This a Real Task?

You assess whether the scenario would genuinely occur in your day-to-day professional life, described accurately. You determine whether the prompt is realistic, borderline, or completely contrived based on your field experience.

2. Is the Domain Content Right?

You verify whether the underlying standards, figures, regulations, constraints, and professional protocols are completely accurate and consistent, ensuring there are no subtle errors that only an active practitioner would catch.

3. Does the Grading Hold Up?

The automated AI grader typically answers eleven specific yes/no questions regarding the finished work, including format compliance, missing figures, unmet requirements, and terminology. You open the generated files and cross-examine these checkpoints, explaining precisely where you agree or disagree, and why.

4. Is the Score Defensible?

You independently assign a performance band to the work, deciding whether a working practitioner could accept or use it in the field. Finally, you compare your evaluated score against the automated report.
