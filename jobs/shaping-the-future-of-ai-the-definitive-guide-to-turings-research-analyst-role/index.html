About the Role: Research Analyst - Advanced Math

The **Research Analyst - Advanced Math** position places you at the forefront of generative AI development. In this role, you will work directly on projects designed to fine-tune large language models (such as ChatGPT) by applying strong analytical and comprehension skills. Rather than performing routine data entry, you will act as a cognitive architect for AI systems—reading, summarizing, breaking down intricate content, validating claims through web research, and collaborating with LLMs to solve challenging math and logic puzzles.

This engagement not only helps models overcome reasoning errors but also transforms you into an expert on leveraging artificial intelligence to supercharge your own analytical workflows. It represents a definitive pathway to future-proof your career in an AI-first economy.

What a Day-to-Day Looks Like

Everyday operations involve tackling diverse analytical challenges and constructing rigorous training scenarios designed to correct model blind spots. For instance, you might analyze sales distribution data across multiple locations to determine growth trends while accounting for early-period variability, or solve multi-step constraint puzzles involving distinct neighborhood routing rules.

When models generate incorrect conclusions, your responsibility is to articulate the accurate solution with clear, logical explanations, enabling the AI to learn and adapt. Interestingly, no prior specialized domain experience beyond solid foundational mathematics and critical thinking is required.

Core Requirements & Skills:

  • Mathematical Proficiency: Strong command of algebra, geometry, trigonometry, and calculus at high school or college levels.
  • English Comprehension & Writing: High-degree proficiency in reading, summarizing, and articulating complex solutions clearly.
  • Problem-Solving Rigor: Systematic and creative approaches to untangling multi-layered logic and math problems.
  • Time Management & Commitment: Ability to fulfill contract commitments (20, 30, or 40 hours per week) with daily overlap during PST working hours.
  • Technical Setup: Reliable laptop/desktop, stable internet connection, and familiarity with collaboration tools.

The Evaluation and Onboarding Process

Getting started with Turing involves a streamlined, skill-based evaluation designed to identify top-tier analytical talent. The process takes approximately 80 to 90 minutes total and consists of:

  1. Automated Analytical Challenge: A 30-minute test evaluating logical reasoning and problem-solving.
  2. Business Writing Assessment: A 30-minute evaluation testing English comprehension and written clarity.
  3. Language Proficiency Assessment: A 20-minute check verifying communication skills.

Once you clear these online assessments, you are ready to begin. To ensure long-term stability on the platform and unlock access to advanced tasks, contractors complete their onboarding and successfully finish their initial 10 hours of project work.
