Deep Dive: What Does an AI Safety Generalist Do?

Artificial intelligence models are scaling at an exponential rate, but raw computational power is not enough. Tech labs and enterprise developers require sophisticated human oversight to ensure these systems reason accurately, adhere to safety guardrails, and understand nuanced cultural contexts. This is where your specialized linguistic skill set comes into play.

As a Bilingual Hindi Generalist Expert at Mercor, your primary mission is to evaluate, audit, and strengthen how advanced AI models handle sensitive, complex, or dual-use topics in the Hindi language. You bridge the gap between human common sense and machine intelligence.

Key Daily Responsibilities:

  • Prompt Engineering & Creation: Draft expert-level, intricate prompts in Hindi across a diverse array of specialized and sensitive subject areas.
