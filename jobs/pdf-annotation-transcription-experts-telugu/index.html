Job Description & Purpose | ఉద్యen వివరాలు & ఉద్దేశ్యం

English

Document understanding breaks down fastest in languages that parsing and vision-language models rarely see. This project builds training data for exactly those languages: Telugu, alongside other Indic scripts. Tasks involve taking real, publicly available PDF pages and producing a complete structural map paired with faithful transcriptions.

What You'll Do: Core Responsibilities | మీ ప్రధాన బాధ్యతలు

English
  • Review Tasks: Confirm pages are in Telugu, legible, and contain real content.
  • Annotate Structure: Bound every meaningful region (titles, paragraphs, tables, figures) and assign reading-order indices.
  • Record Relationships: Link regions to respective tables or figures.
  • Transcribe Faithfully: Reproduce text exactly as it appears in Telugu script, including handwriting.
  • Capture Metadata: Note language, document type, dimensions, and layout features.
