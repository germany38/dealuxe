Core Responsibilities & Day-to-Day Tasks

English

As a Bilingual Writer for Italian, your primary objective is to test how accurately and naturally an advanced AI assistant understands and executes spoken Italian commands across everyday digital workflows. Your responsibilities include:

  • Voice Interaction: Use your voice to compose messages, emails, and notes across everyday productivity applications.
  • Directing AI Capabilities: Instruct the AI to perform complex actions such as generating images, formatting text, applying spreadsheet formulas, or summarizing lengthy documents.
  • Transcription & Cleaning: Record accurate transcripts of your spoken queries and produce clean, polished text outputs.
  • Performance Rating: Systematically rate the AI’s responses for accuracy, tone, and contextual appropriateness.
  • Data Tracking: Log all task metrics in a structured tracking sheet and upload supporting audio recordings to a secure shared drive.
