About Turing: Driving the Frontier of AI Intelligence

To understand the weight of this role, one must examine Turing's unique position in the tech ecosystem. Turing helps customers in two primary ways: first, by collaborating directly with premier AI labs to enhance frontier model reasoning and agentic execution; and second, by leveraging that expertise to solve mission-critical operational priorities for global enterprises.

By joining a Turing engagement, you are not merely reviewing static content or performing standard data entry. You are directly training autonomous AI assistants to interpret human intent with nuance, empathy, and absolute accuracy.

Role Overview: Architecting Lifestyle AI Solutions

In the Lifestyle Expert position, your core mandate is to author domain-specific "recipes" that teach an AI assistant inside Google Sheets how to transform plain-text requests into fully functional, interactive applications. Focusing on tangible, real-world lifestyle scenarios—such as personal fitness tracking, comprehensive event planning, multi-city road trips, or specialized pet care—you own each recipe from conception to final execution.

You will write original domain guidance, draft intelligent discovery questions, build robust reference Google Sheets, and refine the assistant's visual app output. Because the client evaluates every single deliverable for authentic human voice, all content must reflect genuine, lived practice and cannot sound generic or AI-generated.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • End-to-End Recipe Ownership: Take full charge of assigned lifestyle scenarios from initial discovery parameters through final app iteration and sign-off.
  • Domain Guidance & Rule Creation: Draft original domain rules, edge cases, gotchas, and discovery parameters in your own words to ground the AI assistant's underlying logic.
  • Interview Workflow Design: Formulate step-by-step interview questions that an AI assistant must ask users to resolve ambiguity in their initial requests.
  • Golden Asset Construction: Build and verify reference Google Sheets that serve as the functional foundation for data models, formulas, and user structures.
  • Visual App Iteration & QC: Test and iterate on AI-generated visual apps, participating in daily check-ins, quality control reviews, and final sign-offs.

You Are a Perfect Fit If:

This engagement is tailor-made for meticulous analysts, structured thinkers, and subject-matter experts who possess a unique blend of technical literacy and practical domain experience. You will thrive in this role if you match the following criteria:

  • Lived Domain Expertise: You possess direct, hands-on experience managing complex lifestyle scenarios—such as maintaining rigorous workout logs, organizing weddings, or mapping intricate travel logistics—with deep familiarity with real-world edge cases.
  • Authentic Human Writing: You exhibit exceptional, plain-English writing skills that read like an experienced human practitioner. All initial text must be 100% human-authored before any light syntactic polishing.
  • Google Sheets Proficiency: You demonstrate strong technical capability in constructing clean, multi-tab Google Sheets using everyday formulas, clear data organization, and structured validation.
  • Assistive Logic & Workflow Design: You can effortlessly anticipate user intent and design natural interview paths that uncover hidden requirements.

Day-to-Day Look Like

A typical workday in this engagement is dynamic, collaborative, and fast-paced. You will begin with daily check-ins to review sprint goals with research leads. Much of your core hours will involve drafting domain guidelines, structuring formulas inside Google Sheets, and stress-testing AI assistant responses against complex edge cases. You will participate in quality control reviews, refine visual application outputs, and ensure that every workflow mirrors authentic human best practices.

Required Skills, Experience & Education

To ensure high standards across all deliverables, candidates must meet specific professional criteria:

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience in any academic or technical field.
  • Technical Background: Experience in AI evaluation, data annotation, content review, quality assurance, or related analytical roles is preferred.
  • Communication: Impeccable written and verbal communication skills.
  • Infrastructure: A personal desktop or laptop equipped with a stable, high-speed internet connection in a dedicated remote setup.

Engagement Details & Commitment

Clarity and transparency are essential for project stability. Review the core operational parameters of this contract:

  • Commitment Required: 40 hours per week, with 8 hours of required daily overlap with Pacific Standard Time (PST).
  • Engagement Type: Flexible Contractor agreement.
  • Duration: Up to 3 weeks of intensive sprint development.
  • Availability: Weekend on-call availability is mandatory (part-time engagement is acceptable).

The Interview & Onboarding Process

Turing maintains a rigorous yet streamlined vetting process to identify top-tier talent:

  1. Shortlisting: Shortlisted candidates receive a formal Job Interest Form to confirm availability and qualifications.
  2. Delivery Interview: Finalized talents participate in a targeted delivery interview to assess practical competencies.
  3. Onboarding & Stability: Once accepted, candidates complete a thorough onboarding process. Successfully completing your initial onboarding and committing the first 10 hours of work is vital to establishing stability, building rapport with project leads, and unlocking access to advanced tasks and future projects.
