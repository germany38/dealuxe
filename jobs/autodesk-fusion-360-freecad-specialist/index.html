Core Competencies & Required Qualifications

This engagement is crafted exclusively for practitioners with extensive, hands-on experience in 3D parametric CAD modeling and mechanical manufacturing. Successful applicants demonstrate a commanding blend of theoretical knowledge and practical execution:

  • Software Mastery: Extensive hands-on experience with parametric platforms, ideal proficiency in FreeCAD, with robust backgrounds in Autodesk Fusion 360, SolidWorks, or Autodesk Inventor fully welcomed.

Detailed Scope of Work

Contractors selected for this initiative are entrusted with meticulous modeling, auditing, and optimization duties. Daily responsibilities and project deliverables span several core technical domains:

  • Parametric Part & Assembly Development: Constructing fully parametric 3D models of mechanical parts and multi-component assemblies from detailed written briefs, placing heavy emphasis on robust sketching, relational constraints, and clean feature trees.
  • Model Evolution & Editing: Modifying and updating existing parametric CAD models to reflect evolving design changes while maintaining structural integrity and adherence to specifications.
  • CAD Auditing & Quality Control: Reviewing and auditing CAD files for dimensional precision, correct tolerance stack-ups, and strict alignment with core design intent, documenting any structural gaps or deviations.
  • AI Training Support: Delivering consistent, high-end CAD data packages accompanied by crystal-clear technical documentation explaining the underlying design rationale.
  • Manufacturing Optimization: Applying deep working knowledge of production processes—including injection molding, sheet metal fabrication, CNC machining, and casting or forging—to optimize geometry for fabrication.
  • Technical Communication: Communicating design rationale, constructive feedback, and engineering insights clearly in both written and verbal formats to drive continuous project improvement.
