The Intersection of M&A Expertise and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

For years, training artificial intelligence models relied primarily on generalized web data and basic arithmetic datasets. However, as enterprise AI evolves to assist financial analysts, private equity associates, and corporate development teams, generic models consistently fail when confronted with multi-variable valuation models, nuanced accounting adjustments, and sophisticated transaction structuring. In corporate finance, a calculation error or flawed valuation logic carries massive financial implications.

To eliminate these vulnerabilities, micro1 builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By collaborating with elite finance professionals who have spent years navigating live M&A transactions, capital markets advisory, and rigorous financial analysis within top-tier advisory firms, micro1 transforms real-world transaction acumen into high-fidelity evaluation datasets and interactive feedback loops. No prior coding or software engineering experience is required; your deep, battle-tested financial domain knowledge is what drives the technology forward.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Comprehensive Industry & Company Research: Conduct deep-dive company and sector research to inform data-driven AI training initiatives and market comps.
  • Screening & Comparable Analysis: Perform and review buyer or target screening decks, precedent transactions, and comparable company analyses (comps).
  • Excel Financial Modeling: Build, update, and troubleshoot intricate Excel-based valuation and transaction models, ensuring absolute methodological accuracy, dynamic sensitivity analysis, and logical consistency.
  • Pitchbook & Presentation Preparation: Prepare, edit, and critique client-facing presentation materials and pitchbooks, reflecting complex transaction execution scenarios and strategic rationales.
  • Analytical Auditing & Critique: Review sample financial materials to identify numerical errors, weak strategic positioning, or unclear logic, providing actionable feedback for model refinement.
  • Accounting & Valuation Explanation: Articulate complex accounting treatments, valuation adjustments, deal sensitivities, and trace how key operational assumptions influence ultimate valuation outcomes.
