Revolutionizing Healthcare Informatics: The Vital Role of CDI Leaders in Training Frontier AI

The landscape of modern healthcare informatics and clinical documentation is undergoing a massive paradigm shift. Ensuring that patient medical records accurately reflect clinical severity, resource utilization, and care complexity has always been foundational for hospital quality metrics, accurate reimbursement, and regulatory compliance. Today, artificial intelligence and machine learning models are being integrated into health systems to streamline documentation review and coding workflows. However, these advanced AI models require elite domain expertise to guide their development, safeguard accuracy, and maintain clinical integrity.

Through innovative platforms like Mercor, leading AI research laboratories connect directly with top-tier healthcare professionals. The remote Clinical Documentation Integrity (CDI) Leader position offers experienced CDI managers and healthcare directors an extraordinary platform to influence next-generation medical AI systems. Compensated at an impressive $84 per hour on a flexible, independent contractor basis, this role enables seasoned nurse leaders, health information managers, and CDI specialists to utilize their decades of clinical expertise in a cutting-edge technological environment.

Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For healthcare executives, CDI supervisors, and documentation specialists looking to transition their leadership experience into high-impact remote consulting, understanding the precise scope of responsibilities is vital. In this role, you will act as a critical bridge between clinical practice and artificial intelligence, auditing and refining automated tools designed to assist clinical documentation improvement workflows.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Program Leadership & Oversight: Lead clinical documentation integrity initiatives across inpatient and/or outpatient care environments, supervising both concurrent and retrospective review workflows.
  • AI Output Evaluation: Critically assess AI-generated clinical documentation improvement suggestions, automated physician queries, and diagnostic coding recommendations for absolute clinical accuracy and regulatory compliance.
  • Documentation Review & Auditing: Conduct thorough reviews of medical records to guarantee the precise capture of primary and secondary diagnoses, surgical procedures, severity of illness (SOI), and risk of mortality (ROM).
  • Query Management Standards: Oversee and develop compliant physician query processes adhering strictly to AHIMA and ACDIS guidelines.
  • KPI Tracking & Optimization: Monitor core CDI performance metrics, including physician query response rates, CC/MCC capture rates, case mix index (CMI), and overall DRG accuracy.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with coding, compliance, and clinical teams to resolve documentation discrepancies and streamline querying workflows.
  • Staff & Physician Education: Deliver targeted education to physicians, resident staff, and clinical teams regarding complex documentation requirements and best practices.
  • Regulatory Alignment: Ensure continuous compliance with Official Coding Guidelines, CMS regulations, and payer-specific mandates.
  • Dataset Annotation: Annotate complex AI outputs and provide structured, clinical feedback to train and enhance advanced healthcare AI datasets.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these frontier AI models directly impact clinical and financial workflows within healthcare systems, the professional standards for this role are exceptionally high.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • Professional Experience: 5+ years of dedicated experience in clinical documentation integrity or clinical documentation improvement, featuring at least 2 years in a managerial or leadership capacity.
  • Technical Mastery: Deep knowledge of MS-DRG methodology, CC/MCC hierarchies, and ICD-10-CM/PCS coding guidelines.
  • Query Expertise: Advanced understanding of physician query management aligned with AHIMA and ACDIS compliant standards.
  • Clinical Acumen: Strong clinical background with the analytical ability to interpret complex medical records and clinical narratives.
  • System Proficiency: Demonstrated proficiency with leading CDI software suites (such as 3M, Nuance, Optum360, or equivalent) and major Electronic Health Record (EHR) platforms.
  • Communication Skills: Exceptional written and verbal English communication abilities, coupled with rigorous attention to detail and independence.

Preferred Qualifications: Active CCDS (Certified Clinical Documentation Specialist) or CDIP (Clinical Documentation Improvement Practitioner) credentials; hands-on experience with HCC risk adjustment and outpatient CDI; professional background as an RN, RHIA, CCS, or similar credentialed expert; and familiarity with AI-assisted CDI tools.
