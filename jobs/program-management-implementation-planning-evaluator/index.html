Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For senior project leaders, implementation consultants, and program directors seeking high-yielding remote contracts, understanding the precise expectations of this role is crucial. The position demands high-level professional judgment to audit, grade, and refine complex AI-generated work products, ensuring that automated systems meet rigorous enterprise standards of logic, accuracy, and operational viability.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, audit, and grade AI-generated spreadsheets, program roadmaps, project timelines, strategic documents, and slide decks centered around implementation planning and program execution.
  • Rigor & Structural Auditing: Apply deep subject-matter expertise to identify factual gaps, operational misalignments, strategic omissions, and presentation flaws in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to train and align frontier AI models with elite corporate management frameworks.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that implementation frameworks and project lifecycles generated by artificial intelligence adhere to top-tier industry standards in risk mitigation, timeline forecasting, and resource allocation.
