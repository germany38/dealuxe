Comprehensive Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

To succeed as an Evaluator on Mercor, you must understand how large language models generate professional artifacts—such as financial spreadsheets, investment pitch decks, event planning schedules, and hospitality operational manuals—and how to rigorously audit them.

What Your Day-to-Day Responsibilities Will Look Like:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and assess AI-generated documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks focused on real estate underwriting, hospitality management strategies, and large-scale event logistics.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep subject-matter expertise to identify factual inaccuracies, compliance oversights, structural flaws, and presentation errors in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Feedback Generation: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written critiques that help train and align frontier AI models with elite corporate and commercial standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex asset valuation models, guest lifecycle frameworks, and experiential event production workflows generated by AI align with industry best practices.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these positions are directly tied to training mission-critical artificial intelligence models used by top-tier global enterprises, the qualification criteria are designed to attract established domain authorities rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Relevant Professional Experience: Proven career background working within real estate development, property management, hotel/hospitality operations, or large-scale event planning and production.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills.
  • Software Mastery: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in slide presentation tools (Google Slides and PowerPoint) and financial/operational spreadsheets.

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) from a reputable academic institution in business administration, real estate finance, hospitality leadership, or a related discipline.
