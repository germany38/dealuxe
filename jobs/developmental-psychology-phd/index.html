The Core Mission: Shaping Frontier AI Through Human Expertise

Large language models and AI agents are increasingly deployed in sensitive environments, including interactive tutoring platforms, children’s digital software, and personalized learning assistants. However, generalist AI engineers often lack the nuanced understanding of cognitive milestones, socio-emotional stages, linguistic acquisition, and behavioral psychology needed to evaluate whether an AI model’s output is developmentally appropriate.

micro1 bridges this gap by recruiting world-class domain experts to transform real-world academic knowledge into high-quality training datasets, evaluations, and robust feedback loops. No prior technical background in machine learning is required; your deep subject-matter expertise is what drives the model's reasoning capabilities.

Your Key Responsibilities on the Platform:

  • Data Evaluation & Annotation: Review, annotate, and evaluate extensive datasets specifically relevant to child development, learning patterns, and developmental psychology.
  • Behavioral Insight & Feedback: Provide granular, detailed feedback concerning the accuracy, cultural appropriateness, and developmental relevance of model responses.
  • Scenario & Test Case Development: Contribute expert analysis to complex scenario generation and real-world behavioral situations involving children’s learning, cognition, and interaction.
  • Interdisciplinary Collaboration: Work alongside diverse teams to inform structural model improvements, guaranteeing psychological integrity across all AI outputs.
  • Guideline Authoring: Create and refine comprehensive safety and performance guidelines helping AI systems address linguistic, cognitive, and psychosocial dimensions across diverse demographics.
