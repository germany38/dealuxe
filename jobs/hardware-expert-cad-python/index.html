Bridging Mechanical Engineering and AI Spatial Reasoning

Historically, training machine learning models in technical domains was bottlenecked by a lack of structured, high-fidelity engineering datasets. Standard language models struggle when asked to interpret geometric dimensions, understand tolerance stack-ups, or execute programmatic CAD generation. When an AI agent assists engineers with product design or automated prototyping, precision is non-negotiable.

micro1 bridges this gap by building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI. By engaging experienced hardware specialists, mechanical designers, and technical programmers, micro1 transforms real-world engineering intuition into rigorous training loops, evaluation benchmarks, and executable code. This role is specifically designed for professionals who possess an intuitive grasp of 3D space, technical schematics, and programmatic geometry.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Schematic Interpretation: Analyze dimensions, tolerances, and geometric relationships directly from complex technical drawings and engineering blueprints.
  • 3D Mechanical Modeling: Translate 2D schematics into accurate 3D solid models, decomposing intricate components into appropriate geometric features.
  • Advanced Geometry Execution: Model complex curved profiles, sweeps, lofts, fillets, chamfers, and Boolean operations with absolute precision.
  • Programmatic CAD & Automation: Leverage Python to generate, manipulate, or automate CAD geometry using libraries and frameworks such as build123d, CadQuery, trimesh, or OpenCascade.
  • Export & Topology Validation: Export valid, watertight solid models in universal formats like STEP files while validating dimensions, volumes, surface areas, and mass inertia properties.
  • Debugging & Troubleshooting: Diagnose subtle geometric discrepancies between expected outputs and generated models, producing reproducible solutions in clean technical environments.
