Detailed Job Description & Responsibilities

What does it actually mean to train and secure frontier AI models? Language models process billions of parameters, but they require continuous fine-tuning to ensure they handle cultural nuances, safety protocols, and sensitive data with accuracy. As a Bilingual Danish Generalist Expert, your core responsibilities will include:

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Crafting high-level, sophisticated prompts in Danish across a diverse spectrum of complex and sensitive subject areas.
  • Structured Classification: Applying structured guidelines rigorously to classify conversational outputs and model responses.
  • Adversarial Analysis: Flagging adversarial phrasing, potential safety violations, and escalation patterns while documenting clear, logical reasoning behind each judgment.

No prior software engineering or machine learning background is required. Mercor supplies comprehensive training workflows to transition your domain expertise directly into the tech sector.

Candidate Qualifications and Requirements

To thrive in this environment, candidates must demonstrate excellence across a targeted set of criteria:

  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Danish, coupled with business-level written and spoken English.
  • Educational Background: A completed bachelor’s degree or a degree currently in progress.
  • Analytical Rigor: Strong written reasoning capabilities and obsessive attention to detail.
  • Judgment: Sound, unbiased judgment when handling sensitive, technical, or dual-use informational contexts.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Haves)

  • Residency in Denmark or the broader Western Europe region (though applicants residing elsewhere are warmly welcomed).
  • Prior professional experience in grading, reviewing, or red-teaming written and technical outputs.
  • Background experience in content moderation, trust and safety, policy evaluation, or systemic adversarial testing.
