Scope of Work & Core Responsibilities

Alcance del Trabajo y Responsabilidades Principales

English Version

Contractors selected for this specialized initiative provide vital human intelligence oversight to customer projects centered around technology management, architecture design, and project execution. Key responsibilities include:

  • Initiative Planning & Execution: Guide and execute limited-scope technology initiatives, ensuring full-cycle ownership across multi-quarter timeframes.
  • Vendor & SLA Oversight: Coordinate closely with external technology providers, establish rigorous performance standards, and enforce Service-Level Agreements (SLAs).
  • Cross-Functional Leadership: Support project participants, foster effective team collaboration, resolve conflicts proactively, and keep deliverables on track.
  • Risk & Progress Reporting: Monitor project velocity, identify potential bottlenecks, and deliver concise, actionable reports to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
  • AI Model Optimization: Contribute real-world insights, operational feedback, and structural evaluations to train frontier AI systems on complex technology management scenarios.
Versión en Español

  • Planificación y Ejecución de Iniciativas: Guiar y ejecutar iniciativas tecnológicas de alcance limitado, asegurando la propiedad de ciclo completo en marcos temporales de múltiples trimestres.

  • Informes de Riesgos y Progreso: Monitorear la velocidad del proyecto, identificar cuellos de botella potenciales y entregar informes concisos y accionables a partes interesadas técnicas y no técnicas.
  • Optimización de Modelos de IA: Aportar perspectivas del mundo real, retroalimentación operativa y evaluaciones estructurales para entrenar sistemas de IA de frontera en escenarios complejos de gestión tecnológica.
