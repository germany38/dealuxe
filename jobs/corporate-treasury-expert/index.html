The Modern Transformation of Corporate Finance: Why Treasury Experts Are in Massive Demand for AI Development

The corporate finance landscape has always relied on precision, strategic foresight, and elite operational execution. Within operating companies, corporate treasury functions as the heartbeat of financial liquidity—overseeing cash management, mitigating foreign exchange risks, optimizing capital structures, and ensuring debt covenants remain safely within compliance parameters. For years, these complex responsibilities were confined within traditional corporate hierarchies or elite boutique financial consultancies. Today, however, a monumental shift is taking place across global technology sectors.

Artificial intelligence and frontier language models are expanding rapidly into specialized financial domains. Yet, AI models cannot autonomously learn the nuanced mechanics of rolling 13-week cash forecasts, cross-border FX exposure netting, or credit agreement covenant modeling without expert human guidance. Platforms like Mercor are spearheading this technological revolution by connecting senior corporate finance and treasury professionals with top-tier AI labs. Through high-compensating remote consulting contracts, experts can now leverage their hard-earned operational knowledge to train next-generation financial AI systems while commanding an elite hourly rate of $100.

Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For corporate treasurers, financial controllers, and treasury directors seeking flexible, high-yield remote advisory work, understanding the expectations of this role is crucial. The primary objective is to contribute to a comprehensive, high-quality library of corporate treasury work products by completing self-contained exercises based on mock financial files—such as bank statements, credit agreements, and exposure schedules—which are rigorously evaluated against professional rubrics.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Rolling Cash Forecasting: Build comprehensive, accurate rolling 13-week direct cash forecasts featuring itemized receipts, disbursements, and detailed variance explanations.
  • Risk Mitigation & FX Management: Identify and net foreign exchange (FX) exposures, analyze fixed versus floating rate risk, and design sophisticated hedging strategies aligned with corporate policies.
  • Hedge Accounting & Effectiveness: Produce meticulous hedge designation documentation accompanied by periodic effectiveness testing protocols.
  • Working Capital & Liquidity Optimization: Analyze working capital performance metrics (DSO, DPO, and DIO), benchmark bank fees against rendered services, evaluate trapped cash and repatriation options, and forecast debt leverage and covenant headroom.
  • Policy Development: Draft, review, and refresh corporate treasury policies covering liquidity management, FX exposure, counterparty risk, and short-term investments.

Candidate Requirements: Qualifications and Scope

Because these engagements directly train enterprise-grade financial AI systems, the qualification standards are tailored for seasoned professionals who possess deep, hands-on operational experience within corporate environments.

Must-Have & Preferred Requirements:
  • Professional Experience: Minimum of 4+ years working in a dedicated corporate treasury role at an operating company.
  • Hands-On Competency: Demonstrated expertise in cash forecasting, hedging strategies, bank relationship management, and liquidity planning.
  • Preferred Credentials: Professional certifications such as CTP (Certified Treasury Professional); ACT, AMCT, or MCT; and proficiency with advanced treasury management systems (TMS).

Note on Scope: This role strictly focuses on corporate treasury operations. Bank asset-liability management (ALM), funds transfer pricing (FTP), and regulatory liquidity reporting are outside the scope of this project.
