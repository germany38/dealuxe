Role Overview & Description

Mercor is conducting a specialized, high-impact research study in collaboration with a leading AI research lab dedicated to transforming and optimizing healthcare and member experiences.   • Submit all feedback accurately within the designated project timeline.

Candidate Requirements:

  • Based in the United States and Age 64+ / Medicare-eligible.
