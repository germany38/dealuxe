Detailed Scope of Work & Deliverables

Contractors selected for this initiative take ownership of constructing the complex testing suites that challenge and refine frontier models. Key professional responsibilities include:

  • Environment Architecture: Design, develop, and implement realistic cloud infrastructure environments that test an AI model’s proficiency in systems design, deployment, and troubleshooting.
  • Scenario Creation: Build intricate, reproducible scenarios encompassing distributed systems, networking topology, IAM policies, message queues, storage systems, and seamless rolling deployments.
  • Validation & Golden Solutions: Develop deterministic validation tests alongside golden reference solutions to guarantee absolute reliability and precision within the reinforcement learning framework.

  • Iterative Refinement: Collaborate closely with technical leads and project stakeholders to continuously elevate environment specifications and performance acceptance criteria.
