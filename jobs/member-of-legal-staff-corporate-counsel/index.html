The Role: Bridging Transactional Execution and Legal Automation

As micro1 scales its global expert network and builds the human intelligence layer for frontier AI, internal legal operations must match the velocity of its technological advancements. The Member of Legal Staff, Corporate Counsel will combine strong transactional execution with a genuine passion for legal technology, automation, and workflow design.

Rather than being bogged down solely by repetitive paperwork, this role empowers you to build the very systems that streamline legal processes. You will work within a collaborative, asynchronous, and Slack-driven environment, partnering closely with cross-functional teams to manage corporate matters while architecting self-service templates, playbooks, and AI-enabled intake workflows.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Venture Financings & Transactions: Support venture capital financings, secondary transactions, board and stockholder approvals, and other corporate matters, including drafting transaction documents and coordinating with outside counsel.
  • End-to-End Transaction Execution: Own transaction execution from signatures, checklists, and document circulation through closing sets and post-closing organization.
  • Data Room & Records Management: Maintain the company’s master data room, ensuring corporate records remain immaculate, organized, and current across all subsidiary entities.
  • Corporate Governance & Compliance: Manage entity administration, state filings, annual reports, minute books, and organizational documents.
  • Cap Table & Equity Administration: Administer cap tables, equity plans, and equity grants—including new grants, exercises, transfers, cancellations, and comprehensive record reconciliation.
  • Legal Workflow Automation: Design AI-enabled legal intake and triage workflows tailored for asynchronous operations, building self-service tools, templates, and automation for recurring legal processes like NDAs and approvals.

💡 Operational Excellence: Success in this role requires exceptional organization and attention to detail. By identifying repetitive legal bottlenecks and developing scalable automation systems, you will allow the legal team to focus on high-value strategic matters.

Preferred Qualifications and Background

micro1 seeks legal professionals who bring rigorous training from top-tier law firms or high-growth technology environments:

  • Educational & Professional Background: Juris Doctor (JD) degree combined with 3+ years of corporate counsel experience supporting venture capital transactions across prominent law firm and in-house environments.
  • Transaction Expertise: Significant hands-on experience supporting venture-backed or high-growth technology companies through complex financing rounds, M&A, and corporate governance.
  • Equity & Cap Table Mastery: Strong proficiency in managing cap tables, equity administration, state filings, and corporate entity maintenance.
  • Tech-Forward Mindset: Genuine interest in artificial intelligence, legal technology, workflow design, and building custom automations using contract management systems and Slack integrations.
  • Autonomous Operation: Comfort operating independently in a fast-paced, asynchronous environment with exceptional written communication and project management capabilities.
