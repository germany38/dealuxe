Understanding the Role: What is AI Red Teaming?

At Mercor, safety is achieved by proactive testing. Artificial intelligence models must undergo rigorous examination by human experts before deployment to ensure they do not produce biased, harmful, or misleading outputs. As an AI Safety Expert specializing in English and Malay, your core objective is to act as an ethical adversary—pushing conversational AI models to their absolute breaking points.

You will explore jailbreak prompts, prompt injections, multi-turn manipulation tactics, and bias exploitation cases. Your work directly safeguards future users by building structured datasets and attack reports that developers use to patch vulnerabilities.

Comprehensive Job Breakdown & Key Responsibilities

The position is structured around several critical pillars designed to enhance the safety and resilience of large language models:

  • Adversarial Probing: Invent creative scenarios, linguistic traps, and edge cases in both English and Malay to test model safety limits.
  • Data Annotation: Label system failures, categorize toxicity or misinformation vectors, and ensure strict adherence to safety frameworks.
  • Reproducible Documentation: Produce precise reports and testing datasets that outline how specific jailbreaks or behavioral anomalies occurred.
  • Cross-Cultural Nuance Application: Leverage your deep native understanding of cultural expressions, idioms, and regional variations in Malay and English to evaluate context sensitivity.

💡 Note on Wellbeing & Support: Reviewing AI outputs that touch upon sensitive topics (such as bias or safety edge cases) is managed with clear guidelines, advance topic warnings, and supportive wellness resources.
