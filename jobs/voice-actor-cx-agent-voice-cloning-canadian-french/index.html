Inside the Role: What is AI Voice Cloning for Text-to-Speech?

Advanced artificial intelligence labs are rapidly evolving beyond simple robotic text generation. To create seamless, natural, and emotionally engaging user interactions, next-generation text-to-speech (TTS) engines require high-fidelity human audio input. In this role, your voice will serve as the auditory blueprint for an enterprise client’s internal Customer Experience (CX) AI Agent.

Unlike traditional commercial voice-over work that syndicates across public media channels, your voice recordings for this project are bound by strict privacy terms. They will be used exclusively for the client's internal CX AI Agent and will never be sold, licensed, or reused for any other external product, dataset, or commercial purpose.

Key Responsibilities During Your Session:

  • Diverse Script Delivery: Record clean audio samples across a wide variety of scripts, including conversational dialogue, narrative descriptions, and instructional prompts.
  • Expressive Range: Maintain precise control over tone, pacing, intonation, and pronunciation while delivering natural, crystal-clear speech.
  • Acoustic Consistency: Ensure absolute consistency in voice texture, accent, and delivery parameters throughout the duration of the recording session.
  • Guideline Adherence: Strictly follow technical recording parameters covering microphone positioning, environmental noise isolation, and standardized file formatting.
  • Dynamic Variations: Provide multiple takes incorporating subtle shifts in emotion, emphasis, and conversational style whenever requested by the engineering team.

💡 Important Note on Voice Cloning: Because this project involves building a specialized AI voice model (CX Agent), you must be fully comfortable with your voice being cloned for this specific application. Security and data integrity are top priorities for Mercor and its enterprise partners.

Candidate Requirements: Do You Have What It Takes?

To maintain exceptional data standards for speech training models, Mercor looks for specific linguistic and technical qualities in applicants:

  • Linguistic Background: Native Canadian French speaker currently based in Canada, with the ability to speak neutral, accent-free international French without overly heavy regional slang.
  • Vocal Quality: A naturally clear, expressive, and pleasant voice. Prior professional voice acting, narration, or broadcasting experience is celebrated, though enthusiastic newcomers with great natural timbre are welcome.
  • Technical Setup: Access to a professional or near-professional home recording setup, including a high-quality microphone, pop filter, and a strictly quiet, echo-free recording environment.
  • Control & Precision: Strong command over diction, emotional range, and pacing, alongside the technical discipline to execute scripts line-by-line without deviation.
  • Availability: Commitment to complete a single ~4-hour primary recording session, with potential for a follow-up session of similar length based on project needs.

Preferred Qualifications:

Candidates who stand out during review often possess previous experience recording for audiobooks, IVR (Interactive Voice Response) telephone systems, AI speech datasets, or text-to-speech models. Familiarity with digital audio workstations like Audacity, Adobe Audition, or Reaper is also a distinct advantage.

  • Complete Autonomy: Fully remote setup allowing you to coordinate your recording sessions around your personal schedule.
  • Lucrative Referral Programs: Mercor offers expansive peer referral incentives, allowing you to earn additional bonuses when qualified professionals join through your network.
