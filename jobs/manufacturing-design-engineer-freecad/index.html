Demystifying the Mission: Training AI in Advanced Mechanical Design

Artificial intelligence models cannot instinctively grasp why a draft angle matters on an injection-molded housing, how tolerance loops behave across complex multi-part assemblies, or what separates a theoretically sound 3D model from a truly production-ready artifact. Bridging this gap requires senior practitioners who live and breathe mechanical engineering workflows.

As a Manufacturing Design Engineer within micro1's specialized network, your primary focus will be contributing to customer-driven projects centered on advanced mechanical design for manufacturing. You will apply your core technical knowledge to shape how automated AI models learn, reason, and execute spatial and structural evaluations. No prior background in artificial intelligence development is required—your practical, hands-on engineering wisdom is the exact asset being harnessed.

Candidate Profile & Preferred Qualifications

This engagement is tailored exclusively for dedicated mechanical and manufacturing professionals who possess robust, verifiable industry experience. Applicants are evaluated against rigorous technical standards:

  • Professional Experience: A minimum of 2+ years of hands-on experience designing mechanical components specifically for manufacturing environments.
  • Parametric CAD Mastery: Deep proficiency in FreeCAD or comparable parametric modeling platforms for complex assemblies, advanced surfaces, and drawing generation.
  • Manufacturing Exposure: Practical familiarity with diverse manufacturing processes, including sheet metal fabrication, CNC machining, plastics, and injection molding.
  • Technical Rigor: Strong command of Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T), tolerance analysis, and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) best practices.
  • Execution Capability: A proven track record of producing production-grade CAD files and validating them against stringent real-world manufacturability constraints.
  • Communication Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication abilities to articulate technical decisions and coordinate seamlessly with distributed teams.

Comprehensive Scope of Work

Contractors selected for this project undertake complex technical tasks that directly influence how machine learning frameworks interpret engineering blueprints and structural logic. Core responsibilities encompass:

  • Parametric Modeling: Creating and modifying detailed, highly structured 3D CAD models of mechanical parts and complex assemblies using FreeCAD or equivalent environments.
  • Engineering Documentation: Developing comprehensive manufacturing drawings complete with precise GD&T callouts and tolerance stack-ups ready for workshop handoff.
  • Design for Manufacturing (DFM): Optimizing component geometries for specific production pipelines such as sheet metal stamping, multi-axis CNC milling, and plastic injection molding.
  • Validation & QA: Performing rigorous CAD validation and quality assurance checks to guarantee dimensional accuracy and production viability.
  • Technical Content Creation: Preparing clear, concise technical documentation, guidelines, and assembly instructions that serve as benchmark reference materials for AI model training.
  • Iterative Refinement: Incorporating feedback from technical reviewers to systematically revise, enhance, and optimize CAD files and supporting documentation.
