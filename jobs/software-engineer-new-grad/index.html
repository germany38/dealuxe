Why Join micro1’s Core Engineering Team as a New Graduate?

Many traditional tech enterprises relegate new graduates to writing minor bug fixes or managing peripheral legacy scripts for years before granting any real autonomy. At micro1, the engineering culture is built around rapid growth, high accountability, and end-to-end ownership. As a core team software engineer, you will contribute directly to—and rapidly grow into owning—critical internal platform capabilities and user-facing features.

You will work across internal tools, automated services, complex asynchronous workflows, and AI-powered products that support micro1’s massive engineering, operations, and recruiting platforms. Because micro1 sits at the nexus of human expertise and artificial intelligence, your code directly influences how frontier models learn, reason, and perform at global scale.

What You Will Own and Build:

  • Fullstack System Design: Contribute to the architecture, design, and deployment of end-to-end fullstack systems that drive critical internal workflows and platform capabilities.
  • Scalable Backend Services: Build clean, maintainable backend services and robust APIs while mastering scalable engineering best practices.
  • Interactive Frontend Interfaces: Develop high-performance, responsive frontend user interfaces utilized daily by internal operations teams and global experts.
  • Data Modeling & Optimization: Participate actively in relational database design, schema optimization, and query performance tuning using PostgreSQL.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with product managers, operations teams, and designers to translate abstract product ideas into reliable, production-ready features.
  • Engineering Quality & Resilience: Elevate code quality through rigorous peer code reviews, automated testing, comprehensive documentation, and proactive troubleshooting of production systems.

💡 Accelerated Career Growth: Working in a fast-moving AI startup environment forces you to develop sound engineering judgment rapidly. You will learn how to make thoughtful technical tradeoffs between speed, scalability, and maintainability under the guidance of experienced senior engineers.

Required Qualifications and Technical Background

micro1 maintains high standards for its core engineering group, seeking candidates who demonstrate both strong foundational computer science knowledge and a demonstrated passion for building software:

  • Educational Background: A Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering, or a closely related technical field (recent graduate or graduating soon).
  • Practical Engineering Experience: Demonstrable hands-on experience through internships, co-ops, academic research, or substantial personal and open-source project development.
  • Frontend & Backend Fundamentals: Strong programming foundations in JavaScript and/or TypeScript, with working familiarity in React for frontend development and Node.js (NestJS experience is a strong plus) for backend services.
  • Database Competency: Solid understanding of relational database systems such as PostgreSQL, including core data modeling principles and relational design.
  • API & Architecture Understanding: Familiarity with RESTful APIs, asynchronous communication between distributed applications, and modern Git version control workflows.
  • Mindset & Drive: Exceptional problem-solving capabilities, natural curiosity, meticulous attention to detail, and an insatiable growth mindset.
