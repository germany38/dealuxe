The Core Mission: Solving the Data Bottleneck in Physical AI

While large language models benefit from massive text corpuses scraped from the internet, robotic systems require rich, multi-modal, physics-grounded data streams. From egocentric video feeds and teleoperation trajectories to sensor integration and simulation environments, training a robot to navigate, manipulate objects, and reason spatially demands rigorous dataset curation.

As a Member of Technical Staff in Robotics Research at micro1, you will sit at the exact intersection of robotics, embodied AI, and scalable data systems. Your work will directly define how frontier AI labs train robotic agents to interact safely and effectively with the physical world.

What You Will Do:

  • Data Needs Research: Investigate and map out the data requirements of frontier robotics, world models, and physical AI systems.
  • Dataset Architecture: Design innovative datasets and collection methodologies spanning egocentric video, teleoperation, Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI), robot trajectories, diverse sensor arrays, and simulation environments.
  • Schema & Evaluation Design: Define data schemas, fine-grained annotations, ground truth metrics, and rigorous evaluation frameworks for manipulation, navigation, spatial reasoning, and physical understanding.
  • Experimental Iteration: Execute empirical experiments to determine precisely which dataset structures and formats yield the highest performance improvements in model training.
  • Ecosystem Collaboration: Partner directly with leading AI and robotics labs, translating complex research challenges into scalable data pipelines and production-ready pipelines.
  • Strategic Foresight: Stay ahead of cutting-edge academic and industrial research, translating insights into micro1’s overarching robotics data strategy.

💡 Maximizing Professional Impact: To thrive in this core team role, candidates should excel at translating abstract research problems into concrete datasets, experiments, and engineering specifications. Networking with top-tier robotics professionals and encouraging peers in your network to explore micro1's ecosystem helps build the human intelligence layer for next-generation AI.
