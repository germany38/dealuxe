Unpacking the Role: Translating Operational Discipline into AI Excellence

Artificial intelligence systems require more than basic grammar rules to understand government reporting; they need deep exposure to real-world operational workflows. Whether drafting Weekly Activity Reports (WARs), Situation Reports (SITREPs), After-Action Reviews (AARs), or complex executive memoranda, military writing is characterized by absolute clarity, conciseness, and strict adherence to structural standards.

As a Military Writing Specialist, your core objective is to serve as a domain authority who trains next-generation AI systems to comprehend, evaluate, and replicate authentic federal documentation processes. No prior programming or machine learning background is required—your deep, firsthand familiarity with Department of Defense procedures and back-office documentation standards is the exact expertise powering this initiative.

Core Competencies & Professional Profile

This specialized remote engagement is tailored exclusively for experienced defense professionals who have spent years navigating federal reporting frameworks. To qualify, candidates should meet the following baseline professional criteria:

  • Professional Background: Former U.S. Department of Defense (Department of War) professional with at least 3 years of relevant operational or administrative experience.
  • Reporting Expertise: Demonstrated experience producing standardized federal artifacts, including Weekly Activity Reports (WARs), Situation Reports (SITREPs), After-Action Reviews (AARs), decision briefs, and executive summaries.
  • Branch Familiarity: Experience spanning two or more branches of the U.S. military is viewed favorably, offering a valuable breadth of stylistic perspectives.
  • Communication Mastery: Flawless written and verbal communication skills capable of conveying intricate technical and tactical concepts with pristine precision.
  • Compliance Acumen: Broad familiarity with federal documentation standards, administrative protocols, and compliance best practices.

Detailed Scope of Work & Deliverables

Contractors selected for this role will participate in a structured suite of evaluation, drafting, and quality assurance tasks designed to benchmark AI outputs against elite military standards. Daily responsibilities include:

  • Generating Status Reports: Creating realistic military and federal status reports, summaries, and executive updates based on complex supplied prompts and artifacts.
  • Building Golden Responses: Developing benchmark “golden responses” that illustrate top-tier, standards-aligned reporting for foundational AI model training.
  • Designing Grading Rubrics: Drafting rigorous rubrics and assessment guidelines that mirror best practices in federal and military writing evaluation.
  • Evaluating AI Outputs: Reviewing AI-generated reports, offering detailed, actionable feedback in both written and verbal formats.
  • Clarifying Stylistic Nuances: Sharing deep institutional context regarding individual approaches to common military documents (WARs, SITREPs, AARs, and action officer updates).
  • Collaborating with Peers: Interfacing with a diverse network of subject matter experts to guarantee broad stylistic representation and strict adherence to branch-specific terminology.
