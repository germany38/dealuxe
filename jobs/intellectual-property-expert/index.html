Comprehensive Role Overview & Core Responsibilities

For patent attorneys and IP specialists looking to diversify their income streams or transition toward flexible, high-yield remote consulting, understanding the expectations of this role is crucial. The position tasks senior legal professionals with constructing evaluation frameworks, drafting reference legal documents, and designing nuanced grading rubrics that test whether artificial intelligence systems can genuinely replicate senior-level legal judgment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Scenario Construction: Develop intricate IP scenarios spanning patent prosecution, strategic portfolio management, trademark and copyright disputes, and complex technology transfer or licensing processes.
  • Multi-Track Workflows: Build evaluation tasks across two specialized tracks: a US track (focusing on 35 U.S.C., MPEP guidelines, and USPTO procedures) and an International track (focusing on the European Patent Convention, PCT, and WIPO treaties).
  • Tool Integration: Design practical scenarios incorporating industry-standard legal tooling, such as patent search platforms (PatSnap, Innography, USPTO PatFT), IP docketing solutions (Anaqua, CPA Global), and complex claim-charting software.
  • Precision Rubric Authoring: Write strict evaluation rubrics that effectively distinguish authentic, high-level IP strategic judgment from generic law school or bar exam-level textbook recall.

Candidate Qualifications: Who Qualifies for This Elite Role?

Because these positions are structured to train frontier AI models deployed by top-tier global corporations and research laboratories, the qualification criteria are designed for senior practitioners rather than general legal novices.

Ideal Qualifications & Prerequisites:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Extensive professional background working as an IP attorney or patent agent at a premier intellectual property law firm (e.g., Fish & Richardson, Finnegan, Wilson Sonsini) or as in-house IP counsel within a major technology or pharmaceutical enterprise.
  • Direct Portfolio Ownership: Proven track record of managing patent prosecution portfolios, driving licensing negotiations, or spearheading complex IP litigation matters.
  • Procedural Mastery: Comprehensive fluency in USPTO or EPO administrative procedures, international treaty frameworks, and modern IP software stacks.
  • Professional Credentials: A Juris Doctor (JD) with active state bar admission and USPTO patent bar registration, or a recognized international equivalent such as a European Patent Attorney credential. A technical or STEM undergraduate background combined with prior rubric or legal training authorship is considered a strong advantage.
