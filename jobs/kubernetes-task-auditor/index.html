Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For cloud engineers and SRE professionals seeking to diversify their income streams or transition into flexible consulting, understanding the day-to-day expectations of this engagement is vital. As a Kubernetes Task Auditor, your primary objective is to evaluate the quality, correctness, and production-readiness of container orchestration tasks designed to train and benchmark state-of-the-art AI models.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Task and Artifact Evaluation: Critically assess complex Kubernetes task workflows, automation scripts, and simulated incident scenarios generated by frontier AI systems.
  • Cluster Operations Auditing: Review manifest correctness, spot security misconfigurations in Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and evaluate network policy implementations across various environments.
  • Failure-Mode Troubleshooting: Analyze sophisticated troubleshooting exercises involving classic production failure modes, such as CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled, persistent storage bottlenecks, and node eviction pressures.
  • Rubric-Based Feedback: Deliver clear, precise, and structured written feedback that helps top-tier AI researchers align their models with industry-standard engineering practices.

Candidate Qualifications: Basic and Preferred Requirements

Because the artificial intelligence models being trained are destined to assist enterprise-grade software development and cloud operations, the qualification bar is set to ensure absolute technical mastery.

Basic Qualifications (Must-Have):
  • 3+ Years Hands-On Production Experience: Extensive professional work managing Kubernetes in real-world environments (Amazon EKS, Google GKE, Microsoft AKS, or self-managed bare-metal clusters).
  • Deep Cluster Internals Knowledge: Comprehensive mastery of Container Network Interfaces (CNI), CoreDNS, Ingress controllers, Persistent Volumes (PV/PVC), RBAC, and complex pod scheduling failure modes.
  • Manifest & Incident Engineering: Proven background in authoring, linting, and reviewing Kubernetes manifests and Helm charts, coupled with a track record of debugging live cluster incidents.
  • Programming Proficiency: Working competence in at least one modern language utilized in cloud automation, such as Go, Python, or TypeScript.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice-to-Have): Possession of Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA) or Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD) certifications; direct experience with service meshes (Istio), horizontal pod autoscaling (HPA), and observability stacks like Prometheus and Grafana; and prior SRE, platform engineering, or technical grading experience.
