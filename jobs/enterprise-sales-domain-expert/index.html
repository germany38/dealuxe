Detailed Job Overview & Responsibilities

For sales leaders, individual contributors, and account executives looking for flexible remote engagements that respect their professional background, understanding the core expectations of this role is crucial. You are not being brought in as a generic data annotator or low-level telemarketer; rather, you are serving as a senior advisor and curriculum architect for advanced artificial intelligence models.

Core Responsibilities:

  • Establishing Benchmarks of Excellence: Review and critique complex, multi-step enterprise sales workflows to evaluate whether artificial intelligence models handle scenarios the way a seasoned, elite human seller would.
  • Building Golden Trajectories: Execute real-world sales operations from end-to-end—including high-level prospecting, pipeline management, CRM hygiene, revenue reporting, and customer-facing proposal generation—to build the foundational reference data that frontier models learn from.
  • Shaping Evaluation Rubrics: Pressure-test, refine, and optimize the grading rubrics used by engineers to measure AI outputs, ensuring clear delineation between mediocre execution and stellar enterprise selling.
  • Operating in Realistic Enterprise Sandboxes: Navigate and simulate workflows within authentic technical environments that mirror modern tech stacks, interacting seamlessly across CRM, communication, billing, and productivity tools.
  • Producing Executive-Level Artifacts: Draft polished, professional customer-facing documentation, pitch decks, and strategic summaries that meet the exacting standards of senior enterprise leadership.

Because these positions directly train cutting-edge generative AI models utilized by global enterprises, the selection criteria are intentionally rigorous. Mercor and its hiring partners seek out seasoned commercial practitioners rather than casual applicants.

  • Diverse Leadership Background: Sellers at all tiers are welcomed—whether quota-carrying individual contributors, sales managers leading high-performance teams, or VPs managing regional and global sales divisions.
