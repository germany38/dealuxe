Technical Requirements: Hardware & Monitor Specifications

Because this project requires precise visual evaluation and interface documentation, applicants must meet specific hardware standards. Specifically, your system must be capable of capturing screenshots exceeding 2.5 megapixels. You can satisfy this requirement using one of two approved setups:

Option A: Single High-Resolution Monitor

Your single monitor must meet or exceed one of the following standard aspect ratios and resolutions:

  • 4:3 Aspect Ratio: 1826 × 1370 or 2048 × 1536
  • 16:9 Aspect Ratio: 2109 × 1186, 2560 × 1440 (QHD), 3840 × 2160 (4K), or 5120 × 2880 (5K)
  • 16:10 Aspect Ratio: 2000 × 1250, 2240 × 1400, 2560 × 1600, or 2880 × 1800
  • 3:2 Aspect Ratio: 1937 × 1291, 2160 × 1440, 2256 × 1504, 2736 × 1824, or 3000 × 2000
  • 21:9 Ultrawide: 2416 × 1036, 2560 × 1080, 3440 × 1440, or 3840 × 1600

Option B: Dual-Monitor Setup

If you run a dual-monitor rig, your displays must sit side-by-side and be captured as a single spanning image. Two dual 1920×1080 (or higher) monitors qualify. (Note: A single 1080p screen out of that pair does not meet the standalone threshold).
