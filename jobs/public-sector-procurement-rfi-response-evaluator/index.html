Official Job Description & Requirements

Mercor is hiring expert Evaluators specializing in Public-sector procurement / RFI response to review and assess AI-generated work products (documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks) for accuracy, rigor, and domain quality. You will apply deep subject-matter expertise to grade outputs.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Evaluate AI-generated artifacts against rigorous, domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Identify factual, aesthetic, and presentation errors in documents, proposals, and slides.
  • Provide clear, structured written feedback to improve frontier artificial intelligence models.

Must-Have Requirements:

  1. 5+ years of relevant professional experience in Public-sector procurement / RFI response.
  2. Native or professional fluency in English.
  3. High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, especially Slides (Google Slides / PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications:

  • Advanced degree (Master's or higher) from a reputable institution in a relevant discipline.

Contract & Payment Structure:

  • Complete flexibility to work on your own schedule.
  • Weekly payments processed reliably via Stripe or Wise.

Your insights ensure that the resulting tools are rigorous, dependable, and aligned with industry best practices.
