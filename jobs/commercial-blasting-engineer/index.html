The Core Mission: Red-Teaming Frontier AI Models in High-Risk Domains

Artificial intelligence models are frequently pushed to their limits when processing queries that straddle the line between legitimate commercial engineering and potential hazard. For instance, the exact same geometric layout and explosive timing used to fracture rock for a highway construction cut involves principles of energy release. Similarly, inventory management, magazine transport protocols, and security standards that maintain job-site safety also map out material distribution pathways.

An artificial intelligence model that fails to distinguish between routine compliance inquiries and diversion risks poses a severe hazard. If a model refuses a basic vibration-compliance question, it fails its operational purpose. Conversely, if it answers a query it should withhold, it risks catastrophic misuse. Drawing that razor-thin line reliably is why Mercor relies on licensed practitioners rather than general AI testers.

Primary Responsibilities in the Role:

  • Challenging Prompt Design: Write sophisticated, single-turn prompts within your specialized domain, meticulously categorized across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy-Driven Evaluation: Review and grade model-generated responses against rigorous policy standards, judging whether the model handled the nuance correctly.
  • Technical Reference Authoring: Write comprehensive reference answers detailing what an accurate, safe response should look like alongside the technical engineering reasoning supporting your judgment.

💡 Writing-Intensive Nature: This role demands more than just field experience; it requires clear written articulation. Every judgment you make must be accompanied by a logical rationale that non-specialist reviewers can easily follow.

Target Backgrounds and Professional Qualifications

Mercor specifically targets licensed professionals who handle, store, secure, and transport specialty materials as a daily operational responsibility and who personally sign off on compliance records.
