Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For communications directors, health promotion specialists, epidemiologists with a communications focus, and public health educators seeking flexible, high-yielding independent contractor work, understanding the exact scope of this role is crucial. Evaluators operate as the ultimate quality assurance gatekeepers for AI-generated outputs.

What You Will Do Day-to-Day:

  • Artifact and Deliverable Review: Assess and grade AI-generated work products, including policy briefs, public health campaign documents, educational spreadsheets, and comprehensive slide decks.
  • Rigor and Accuracy Auditing: Apply expert subject-matter discernment to spot factual inaccuracies, misleading statements, tonal misalignment, or presentation flaws in automated content.
  • Constructive Evaluation Feedback: Deliver structured, meticulous written critiques and corrections that guide AI models toward producing medically sound, highly professional communications.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex public health concepts, campaign strategies, and risk communication frameworks adhere strictly to established scientific and professional standards.

Candidate Requirements: Qualifications You Need to Succeed

Because these engagements directly influence foundational AI systems utilized across global enterprises, the qualification standards are intentionally rigorous, welcoming seasoned professionals who have mastered their craft in the field.

Must-Have Qualifications:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Proven professional background working directly in public health communications, health promotion, medical writing, or public health education.
  • Language Fluency: Native or professional-level fluency in English with exceptional technical and professional writing capabilities.
  • Digital Tool Proficiency: High proficiency across Microsoft Office and Google Workspace ecosystems, with advanced expertise in slide presentation software (Google Slides and PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (such as an MPH, MSc, PhD, or DrPH) from a reputable institution focused on public health, communications, or medicine.
