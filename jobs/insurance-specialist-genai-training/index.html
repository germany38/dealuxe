Comprehensive Job Description & Core Responsibilities

For career underwriters, claims directors, and risk management veterans looking to pivot or supplement their expertise with high-impact technology consulting, understanding the scope of this role is essential. As an Insurance Specialist, your core mission is to inject rigorous, real-world underwriting judgment and logical frameworks into foundational AI training pipelines.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Guiding AI Research & Engineering: Work alongside top-tier technical teams to close complex knowledge gaps concerning underwriting judgment, claims lifecycles, and multi-layered risk-assessment reasoning.
  • Designing Domain-Specific Tasks: Construct challenging, real-world insurance tasks and write accurate, highly-reasoned solutions rooted in professional underwriting and claims practice.
  • Evaluating AI Model Outputs: Systematically evaluate AI-generated outputs against structured rubrics, delivering clear, meticulous written feedback regarding correctness, logical judgment, and reasoning quality.
  • Refining Scoring Rubrics: Develop, test, and continuously optimize evaluation guidelines and rubrics tailored specifically to high-level insurance scenarios.
  • Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner closely with other industry subject-matter experts to ensure total consistency, accuracy, and standardization across all training datasets.

Candidate Qualifications & Professional Requirements

Because these foundational AI models directly impact safety and reasoning standards in highly regulated domains, the qualifications for this position demand deep professional pedigree and hands-on technical evaluation experience.

Mandatory Qualifications:
  • Professional Tenure: 8+ years of dedicated, hands-on professional experience within the insurance sector (e.g., underwriting, complex claims, actuarial science, or risk management) at recognized, top-tier organizations (such as AIG, Chubb, Allstate, Progressive, MetLife, Marsh McLennan, or equivalent enterprises).
  • AI Model Evaluation Experience: Prior hands-on experience evaluating LLM or AI model outputs against structured rubrics and scoring criteria is strictly mandatory; applicants must be prepared to articulate this experience clearly.
  • Career Progression: Demonstrable, upward career trajectory (e.g., progressing from Underwriter to Senior Underwriter to VP of Underwriting or equivalent leadership milestones).
  • Time Commitment: Ability to engage reliably for at least 35 hours per week during standard U.S. weekdays.
  • Communication Skills: Outstanding verbal and written English communication skills, paired with sophisticated problem-solving and interpersonal abilities.

Employment Structure: This position operates as a W-2 employment role through Cincinnatus LLC, providing comprehensive payroll, compliance, and employment benefits while placing you directly within elite client tech teams.
