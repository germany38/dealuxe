What Is the Mercor Public Sector Practice Reviewer Role?

Unlike traditional writing or coding positions in the tech sector, this role is explicitly designed for working and seasoned public sector professionals who want to audit AI work products within their specific fields. You are not tasked with producing fresh content, generating administrative documents from scratch, or performing baseline operational work. Instead, you act as the ultimate expert evaluator—bringing the critical eye of someone who performs public sector work for a living to evaluate what artificial intelligence systems have produced.

When you log into your review queue, you will inspect an AI agent's complete attempt at a realistic, complex task tailored to your professional domain. This includes the task prompt as originally written, the concrete actions taken by the agent, all corresponding output files produced during the simulation, and an automated scoresheet filled out by an internal AI grader. Your objective is to apply your seasoned professional judgment and answer four fundamental evaluation pillars:

  1. Authenticity Verification: Is this a genuinely realistic task? Would this type of assignment realistically cross your desk in your public sector career, or is it contrived, superficial, or borderline?
  2. Domain Accuracy: Are the standards, figures, administrative regulations, and legal constraints completely accurate? Or is there a subtle structural or factual error that only an experienced practitioner would catch?
  3. Grading Integrity: The automated grader answers eleven specific yes/no questions regarding formatting, figures, requirements, and terminology. You review these metrics directly against the raw files to confirm or dispute the findings.
  4. Defensibility of Scores: You band the work yourself, determining if a real-world practitioner could utilize, accept, or act upon the output, and compare your findings against the reported scores.

Each item generally takes around one hour to review and process, offering incredible autonomy and flexibility around your personal calendar.

Who Is Eligible? Exploring the Five Core Professional Tracks

Mercor is looking for absolute subject matter experts who know their respective fields inside and out. To ensure high-quality evaluations, the opening targets five distinct public sector professions. You should apply to the background that precisely matches your verified career trajectory:

  • Compliance Officers: Candidates with 4+ years of hands-on experience in regulatory, licensing, or program compliance roles, specifically with a track record of writing compliance findings that real entities had to legally act upon.
  • Administrative Services Managers: Professionals with 5+ years of experience running operational facilities, managing public records, overseeing procurement, or handling general administrative services for a public agency or municipal institution.
  • Child, Family, and School Social Workers: Individuals holding active state licensure where required, with at least 4+ years of direct experience carrying and managing their own complex caseloads.
  • Recreation Workers: Practitioners with 4+ years of professional background running public programs for a municipal parks department, community center, summer camp, or school district, covering staffing logistics and safety plans.
  • First-Line Supervisors of Police and Detectives: Sworn law enforcement professionals with 4+ years of supervisory experience at the rank of sergeant or higher.

Who is less of a fit? Policy researchers who have historically analyzed programs from an academic or theoretical standpoint without ever directly administering them will find this role less aligned. Similarly, pure private-sector equivalents are typically not accepted because these tasks rely heavily on public procurement frameworks, mandated reporting standards, and specific public-records rules.

Additional non-negotiable requirements include near-native business English proficiency (since you will provide clear, written justifications for every single evaluation judgment) and standard comfort navigating spreadsheets, data reports, and multi-page PDFs to cross-reference figures.
