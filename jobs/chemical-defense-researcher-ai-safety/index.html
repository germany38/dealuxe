The Delicate Balance of Dual-Use Science in AI Models

To understand the gravity and intellectual stimulation of this role, one must examine the fundamental dilemma of modern AI safety: the dual-use line. In chemical research, toxicology, and defense, knowledge is inherently symmetrical. The exact same toxicological data required to formulate a protective medical countermeasure or establish a decontamination protocol also maps out the exposure pathways utilized in threat assessments.

When an artificial intelligence model encounters a technical query regarding hazardous compounds, personal protective equipment (PPE), or treatment protocols, it walks a razor-thin line. A model that refuses to answer a legitimate, routine inquiry about safe handling or professional PPE is failing just as catastrophically as a model that hands out instructions for manufacturing illicit agents. Drawing that line reliably requires real practitioners—individuals who have lived and breathed chemical defense from the defensive side.

Core Responsibilities of the Researcher:

  • Prompt Generation & Red-Teaming: Write challenging, single-turn prompts rooted in real-world chemical defense domains, categorized meticulously across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Response Evaluation: Analyze the outputs generated by frontier AI models against rigorous, predefined safety policy standards, issuing definitive judgments on whether the model responded appropriately.
  • Authoring Reference Answers: Draft comprehensive technical rationale documents outlining what a correct model response looks like, including the underlying scientific and chemical reasoning.

💡 An Intensive Analytical Environment: This position involves sustained engagement with misuse scenarios and technical safety evaluations. Mercor provides comprehensive project onboarding, and researchers retain complete autonomy to pause or step away from tasks as needed.

Who Qualifies? Backgrounds and Professional Experience Needed

Because this work demands true subject matter expertise, Mercor is looking specifically for professionals who have dedicated their careers to the defensive side of chemical safety. Generalist writers or basic content reviewers will not fit this profile. Ideal candidates typically possess background experience in institutional, governmental, or specialized corporate settings.

Relevant professional backgrounds include:

  • Chemical Defense Research: Experience gained within organizations such as USAMRICD, the Department of Defense (DoD), the CDC, or allied international national institutes.
  • Toxicological Science: Research focusing on the toxicology of chemical warfare agents, hazard mitigation, and medical countermeasure development.
  • Decontamination & Protection: Hands-on research in environmental detection, specialized personal protective equipment (PPE), and decontamination methodologies for chemical threats.
  • International Compliance & Verification: Work related to the Chemical Weapons Convention (CWC), including verification protocols, destruction oversight, and designated laboratory analysis.
  • Consequence Assessment: Clinical management, emergency response planning, and medical consequence assessment for chemical exposures.
