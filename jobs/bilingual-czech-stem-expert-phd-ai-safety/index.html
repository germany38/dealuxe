Detailed Breakdown of the Bilingual Czech STEM Expert Role

The Bilingual Czech STEM Expert (PhD) — AI Safety position is meticulously designed for high-caliber individuals who possess a deep mastery of scientific subfields coupled with native or near-native fluency in Czech and business-level proficiency in English. Because AI models increasingly process multilingual and multi-domain data, specialized evaluations in languages other than English are vital to mitigating regional vulnerabilities and ensuring global safety standards.

Candidates selected for this role will directly impact how frontier models process complex instructions, technical prompts, and safety-sensitive queries. No prior software engineering or machine learning background is required; Mercor provides comprehensive workflow training, allowing you to focus purely on your core scientific domain expertise.

Core Responsibilities

  • Expert Prompt Generation: Craft sophisticated, high-level prompts in Czech across rigorous scientific, technical, and laboratory topics to test model limits.
  • Response Evaluation & Annotation: Thoroughly review model outputs for scientific accuracy, logical consistency, helpfulness, and the safe handling of sensitive or dual-use information.
  • Structured Classification: Apply detailed, framework-driven guidelines to classify prompt-response dialogues, identifying potential safety risks or hallucination vectors.
  • Cross-Lingual Quality Assurance: Ensure that nuances, technical terminologies, and contextual meanings translate accurately between Czech and English technical specifications.

Candidate Profile & Qualifications

To succeed in this rigorous assessment environment, applicants must meet specific professional and academic criteria:

  • Educational Background: A completed or ongoing PhD in chemistry, biology, or a closely related scientific discipline.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or near-native fluency in Czech, complemented by strong written and verbal business English.
  • Subfield Specialization: Familiarity with modern laboratory frameworks, analytical methods, and computational techniques. Priority is given to deep focus areas such as organic/inorganic synthesis, molecular biology, microbiology, virology, genetics, or specialized material sciences.
  • Specialized Nuclear/Chemical Coverage: Candidates with backgrounds in radiochemistry, nuclear chemistry, energetic materials, or radiation physics are warmly welcomed to apply.
  • Risk Assessment & Judgment: Sound situational judgment regarding biosafety, biosecurity, chemical safety, and the handling of hazardous or dual-use data.

Preference Note: While applicants based in the Czech Republic or the broader Eastern Europe region are preferred, exceptional candidates located anywhere globally are encouraged to apply. H1-B or STEM OPT sponsorships are not supported for this contracting arrangement.
