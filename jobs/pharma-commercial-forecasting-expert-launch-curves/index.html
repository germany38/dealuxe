Role Overview

Mercor is partnering with an elite biotech and pharma research team on expert human-evaluation work—creating and critiquing rubrics that analyze commercial drugs and drug-development programs. We are seeking a seasoned pharma commercial / forecasting expert for a paid pilot project, with high potential for continuous engagement as the program scales.

Key Responsibilities

  • Create and critique evaluation rubrics that analyze commercial drugs and drug-development programs.
  • Build or thoroughly critique complex launch curves (including uptake trajectories, peak share estimates, and market adoption pathways).
  • Judge whether a commercial forecast's foundational assumptions (such as analogs, ramp velocity, peak sales, and loss of exclusivity) are fully defensible.
  • Apply strategic evaluation frameworks across the pilot's comprehensive set of upcoming drug launches.

Candidate Requirements

  • US-based professional with specialized background in pharma commercial forecasting, analytics, or strategy (pharma sales team veterans are also welcome to apply).
  • Deep functional familiarity with biotech and pharmaceutical development programs.
  • Proven hands-on experience building or critically evaluating drug launch and market uptake models.
  • 5+ years of dedicated professional experience in commercial forecasting or pharmaceutical market analytics.

Engagement & Compensation Details

  • Duration: Approximately 10–20 hours executed over a 1–2 week focused pilot program.
  • Work Style: Fully remote position completed entirely on your own flexible schedule.
  • Payouts: Weekly compensation disbursed reliably via Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
