Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For corporate lawyers seeking high-yield alternative revenue streams without the grueling billable-hour quotas of traditional big law firms, understanding the mechanics of this position is essential. The role centers on building sophisticated evaluation tasks, scenarios, and rubrics for AI systems operating in high-stakes corporate contexts.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Complex Transaction Scenario Building: Construct intricate corporate law scenarios spanning large-scale M&A transactions, multi-party deal negotiations, complex regulatory reviews, and multi-layered restructuring processes.
  • Cross-Disciplinary Legal Tasks: Build rigorous tasks across M&A deal structuring, securities and capital markets, corporate governance, commercial contracts, and corporate restructuring.
  • Tool-Driven Workflow Simulation: Design legal scenarios incorporating industry-standard tools and platforms, including Westlaw, Lexis, virtual data rooms (such as Intralinks and Datasite), contract lifecycle management platforms, and document automation systems.
  • Jurisdictional Framework Application: Apply robust corporate law methodologies across specific tracks—including US frameworks (Delaware General Corporation Law, SEC regulations, Model Business Corporation Act) or International frameworks (UK Companies Act 2006, EU company law directives)—to draft reference memoranda, transaction documents, and regulatory narratives.
  • Expert Rubric Authorship: Author sophisticated rubrics that distinguish authentic, expert-level corporate law judgment from generic law school or bar exam-level recall.

Candidate Qualifications: Who Qualifies for This Elite Role?

Because these positions are designed to calibrate state-of-the-art AI systems for enterprise-grade deployment, the candidate criteria are calibrated to attract senior legal authorities with direct industry experience.

Ideal Qualifications & Experience:
  • 5+ Years Experience: Extensive professional background working as a corporate attorney or general counsel at a major law firm, investment bank, or major corporation (e.g., Wachtell, Skadden, Cravath, Latham & Watkins, Kirkland & Ellis, Sullivan & Cromwell, or in-house legal at a large public company).
  • Direct Transaction Ownership: Demonstrated history of managing M&A transactions, complex governance matters, or SEC/regulatory securities filings from inception to closure.
  • Tool & Regulatory Fluency: Deep familiarity with corporate law tooling, regulatory approval workflows (SEC review, antitrust/HSR clearances, or international equivalents), and board-level oversight.
  • Professional Credentials: A recognized professional credential is strongly preferred (JD with active bar admission, or an international equivalent such as Solicitor or Qualified Lawyer in England & Wales). Prior experience authoring legal training curricula or transaction documentation is a distinct plus.

Contract Terms, Payment Structure, and Remote Flexibility

  • Independent Contractor Status: Complete evaluation milestones and authoring tasks entirely on your own schedule from any remote location globally (note: H1-B and STEM OPT visa support are currently unavailable).
