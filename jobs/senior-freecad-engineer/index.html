Machines do not inherently understand why a specific tolerance matters, how assembly constraints behave under stress, or how to translate conceptual engineering intent into production-ready technical drawings.

Core Competencies & Professional Requirements

This engagement is tailored for meticulous engineers who possess comprehensive command over open-source and parametric modeling environments. Candidates should meet the following benchmarks:

  • Professional Experience: 2+ years of focused experience in mechanical engineering, product design, industrial engineering, or closely related technical fields with heavy emphasis on parametric CAD modeling.
  • Platform Mastery: Advanced proficiency in FreeCAD or equivalent advanced parametric CAD platforms.
  • Design Principles: Deep fluency in mechanical design fundamentals, including rigorous Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) best practices.
  • Documentation Standards: Demonstrated history of producing comprehensive, pristine technical drawings and documentation suitable for downstream manufacturing and assembly.
  • Quality Assurance: Proven capability in conducting engineering QA, ensuring absolute model integrity, and verifying strict compliance with project specifications.
  • Automation Proficiency: Practical exposure to Python scripting for CAD automation—streamlining repetitive or complex modeling tasks (highly preferred, though not strictly mandatory).

Comprehensive Scope of Work

Contractors selected for this role undertake multifaceted technical responsibilities that directly shape how artificial intelligence systems interpret spatial designs. Core day-to-day deliverables include:

  • Parametric Modeling: Creating, modifying, and validating detailed parametric CAD models in FreeCAD that adhere strictly to custom project specifications.
  • Assembly Optimization: Developing and optimizing complex mechanical assemblies while leveraging best practices in component integration and mechanical hierarchy.
  • Applying Tolerancing Standards: Integrating rigorous GD&T and DFM principles into designs to guarantee ultimate manufacturability, structural efficiency, and quality control.
  • Technical Drafting: Generating clean, precise technical drawings and documentation packages designed for rigorous engineering review and downstream production workflows.
  • Engineering Quality Assurance: Conducting meticulous QA audits on CAD models and technical drawings to isolate, communicate, and resolve geometric or structural discrepancies.
  • Workflow Automation: Utilizing Python scripting to build automation pipelines that optimize intricate modeling tasks and scale efficiency (highly preferred).
  • Collaborative Feedback: Providing insightful, granular feedback regarding model accuracy, design intent, and continuous process improvements to the project team.
