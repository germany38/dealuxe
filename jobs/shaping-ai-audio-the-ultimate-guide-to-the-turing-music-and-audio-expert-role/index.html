Shaping AI Audio: The Ultimate Guide to the Turing Music and Audio Expert Role

Company: Turing · Location: Remote — Global · Type: contractor · Posted: 2026-09-19

AI Innovation & Music Expertise

Discover how professional musicians, producers, and audio engineers are leveraging their ears to train frontier artificial intelligence systems at Turing.

Artificial intelligence has transcended text and visual generation. Today, frontier AI labs are pushing the boundaries of synthetic audio, music composition, and sound synthesis. However, machines cannot learn the nuance of acoustic instruments, complex timbre, or professional audio production in a vacuum. They require elite human expertise to evaluate, annotate, and refine their outputs.

If you have formal music education paired with hands-on experience in performance, production, or audio engineering, a unique opportunity awaits. Turing is actively recruiting talented professionals for specialized remote projects focused on evaluating and improving AI-generated audio data.

About Turing

Based in San Francisco, California, Turing is the world’s leading research accelerator for frontier AI labs and a trusted partner for global enterprises deploying advanced AI systems. Turing supports customers in two vital ways:

  1. Accelerating Frontier Research: Providing high-quality data, advanced training pipelines, and top AI researchers specializing in coding, reasoning, STEM, multilinguality, multimodality, and agents.
  2. Enterprise AI Transformation: Applying that deep expertise to help enterprises transform AI from proof of concept into proprietary intelligence with reliable, high-impact systems that drive results on the P&L.

Role Overview: Music and Audio Expert

We are looking for music professionals with formal music education and hands-on experience in performance, production, or audio engineering to help evaluate and improve AI-generated audio data. As generative audio models evolve, human evaluation remains the gold standard for ensuring accuracy, realism, and high fidelity.

Project Snapshot

  • Flexibility: Flexible hours across a fast-moving 2-day project window.
  • Compensation: Earn up to ~$150–$160 with a full push across both days, paid securely in USD.
  • Location: Fully remote.
  • Growth Potential: Possible project extension or access to ongoing tasks for strong performers.

What Your Day-to-Day Looks Like

As a Music and Audio Expert at Turing, your daily workflow centers around focused listening and precise data annotation. You will listen to short audio clips and execute tasks such as:

  • Identifying the instrument family (Strings, Brass, Woodwind, Percussion, Keyboard, etc.).
  • Pinpointing the specific instrument heard within the recording.
  • Consulting provided audio references to guide your decisions and maintain objective standards.
  • Following clear annotation guidelines to deliver consistent, accurate work under remote conditions.

You Are a Fit If You Meet These Criteria

This role demands a highly trained ear and professional discipline. Ideal candidates possess the following background:

  • Formal Education: Bachelor’s degree in Music or a related field.
  • Professional Experience: 3+ years of relevant experience in music performance, production, audio engineering, or similar work.
  • Aural Skills: A trained ear and a robust ability to recognize and categorize instruments by sound instantly.
  • Communication: Strong English communication skills for interpreting complex annotation guidelines.
  • Focus: Comfortable with focused, repetitive listening tasks and independent remote work environments.
  • Bonus Qualifications: Experience with audio transcription, remixing, music information retrieval (MIR), or AI/data annotation.

Perks of Working with Turing

Joining Turing as an expert evaluator offers distinct advantages for music and audio professionals looking to supplement their income while exploring the tech industry:

  • Global Impact: Directly contribute to the training data that shapes next-generation generative AI audio models.
  • Remote Freedom: Work entirely from your home studio or workspace on a flexible schedule.
  • Competitive Pay: Earn strong compensation in USD for your specialized domain expertise.
  • Network Expansion: Gain exposure to cutting-edge artificial intelligence workflows and research pipelines.

Engagement Details & Interview Process

The onboarding journey is streamlined to get qualified experts working quickly:

  1. Application & Review: Submit your profile and background details.
  2. Qualifying Assessment: Complete a short ~15-minute qualifying test designed to evaluate your aural acuity and attention to detail.
  3. Onboarding & Stability Period: Once onboarded, experts complete an initial 10 hours of work to ensure operational stability, familiarize themselves with guidelines, and unlock access to more tasks and future project extensions.
