Deep Dive Into the Role: Personal Finance / Consumer Planning Evaluator

Mercor’s opening for a Personal finance / consumer planning Evaluator is tailored specifically for specialists who spend their careers advising clients, structuring investments, building budget models, and navigating complex consumer financial landscapes. In this capacity, contractors act as the ultimate quality-assurance gatekeepers for next-generation intelligence systems.

What does the day-to-day engagement actually entail? You will be tasked with reviewing and grading AI-generated work products—including comprehensive financial plans, complex analytical spreadsheets, and structured slide decks (PowerPoint/Google Slides). Your objective is to scrutinize these outputs for factual precision, logical consistency, aesthetic presentation quality, and domain-specific depth.

Core Responsibilities at a Glance:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Grade AI-generated documents and models against rigorous, domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Error Detection: Pinpoint subtle factual inaccuracies, calculation flaws, aesthetic formatting errors, and structural presentation gaps.
  • Constructive Feedback: Deliver clear, precise, and actionable written notes that guide the refinement of automated systems.
  • Independent Collaboration: Work autonomously on a remote schedule tailored around your professional life.

Qualifications and Prerequisites

Because these frontier models are expected to generate high-stakes professional output, the entry bar is calibrated for established practitioners:

  • Professional Background: A minimum of 5 years of verified, hands-on professional experience working directly in personal finance, wealth management, financial advisory, or consumer planning.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English, ensuring crystal-clear communication and advanced technical writing capability.
  • Tool Mastery: High-level proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific expertise in slide design and data presentation (Google Slides / PowerPoint).
  • Advanced Credentials (Preferred): Possessing an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) from a reputable academic institution serves as a strong advantage during the vetting process.
