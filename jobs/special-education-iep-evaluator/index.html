Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For educators and specialists seeking high-yield flexible income outside traditional school calendars, understanding the mechanics of this evaluator role is vital. The position requires senior-level professional judgment to audit, grade, and refine AI-generated work products, documents, spreadsheets, and slide decks, ensuring they meet the highest standards of accuracy, rigor, and pedagogical quality.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and assess AI-generated educational documents, accommodation plans, resource spreadsheets, and presentation slide decks against rigorous quality rubrics.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional domain expertise in special education and IEP frameworks to identify factual errors, presentation flaws, or gaps in accessibility standards.
  • Constructive Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and actionable written critiques to help train and align frontier AI models with expert-level educational practices.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that specialized instructional strategies, diagnostic language, and developmental frameworks generated by AI systems reflect authentic, real-world educational standards.

Candidate Requirements: What It Takes to Qualify

Because these evaluator positions directly shape the foundational data used by leading AI laboratories and global enterprises, the selection criteria are tailored to attract accomplished professionals rather than generalists.

Must-Have Requirements:
  • 5+ Years of Experience: Demonstrated professional background working directly in special education, IEP development, educational diagnosis, or student support services.
  • Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English with exceptional written communication skills.
  • Software Mastery: High proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Workspace, with specific emphasis on slide presentation software (Google Slides and PowerPoint).

Preferred Qualifications: Possession of an advanced degree (Master’s or higher) from a reputable academic institution in special education, educational leadership, educational psychology, or a related field.
