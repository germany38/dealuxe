Detailed Job Description & Core Responsibilities

As an IP / Trademark / Copyright Law Evaluator, your primary objective is to review, assess, and grade AI-generated work products—including complex documents, detailed spreadsheets, and professional slide decks—for factual precision, aesthetic polish, and domain quality. You are not writing code or building software; rather, you are acting as an elite legal reviewer applying your hard-earned subject-matter expertise to judge synthetic outputs against rigorous professional standards.

What You Will Do Day-to-Day:

  • Evaluate AI Artifacts: Systematically review AI-generated legal documents, memorandums, and presentations against strict domain-specific quality rubrics.
  • Error Identification: Spot and flag subtle factual, structural, stylistic, or legal presentation errors that require human insight to correct.
  • Provide Actionable Feedback: Formulate clear, structured, and constructive written feedback to help train and refine next-generation AI reasoning capabilities.
  • Maintain High Standards: Leverage your extensive background in intellectual property, trademark clearance, or copyright enforcement to ensure every output mirrors the rigorous quality expected from top-tier legal practice.

Candidate Requirements and Qualifications

Because this role sits at the bleeding edge of AI development and legal compliance, the selection bar is high. Mercor seeks seasoned professionals who bring authentic mastery to the table:

  1. Professional Background: A minimum of 5 years of relevant professional experience practicing, advising, or working intensively within IP, trademark, or copyright law.
  2. Language Proficiency: Native or professional fluency in English, with immaculate written communication skills necessary for detailed rubric evaluation.
  3. Technical Fluency: High proficiency in standard productivity suites, specifically Microsoft Office and Google Workspace (with an emphasis on PowerPoint and Google Slides formatting and presentation structure).
  4. Preferred Credentials: An advanced degree (such as an LL.M., Master’s, or equivalent terminal degree) from a reputable academic or professional institution is considered a strong plus.

Important Contracting Terms

This position is structured as a flexible, remote independent contractor engagement. Payments are processed weekly via Stripe or Wise based strictly on services rendered. Please note that at this time, Mercor is unable to support H1-B or STEM OPT visa sponsorships for this particular role.
