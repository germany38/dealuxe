The Mission: Shaping the Human Intelligence Layer for Frontier AI

As artificial intelligence systems evolve, they require more than raw computing power—they demand structured human intelligence, robust evaluation frameworks, and seamless developer workflows. micro1 connects elite domain experts with cutting-edge AI labs, creating high-quality training data and feedback loops that improve how models learn, reason, and perform at scale.

Behind micro1’s rapid scaling is a high-performing, remote-first engineering and product organization. As a Product Owner on the core team, you will act as the operational and strategic anchor between advanced machine learning research, software engineering execution, and end-user adoption. You will take ambiguous, complex technical challenges and transform them into streamlined, intuitive, production-ready software solutions.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • End-to-End Product Lifecycle Ownership: Own the complete product journey from initial discovery, scoping, and prototyping through execution, launch, and iterative refinement.
  • User-Centric Design & Experience: Build intuitive, high-quality customer-facing products with an uncompromising focus on user experience and workflow friction reduction.
  • Metric-Driven Strategy: Define, monitor, and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs), adoption metrics, and product outcomes to guide iterative development.
  • Ruthless Prioritization: Balance competing user needs, overarching business goals, and engineering constraints to triage scope and deliver high-impact releases.
  • Ambiguity Translation: Navigate fast-moving, high-ambiguity environments by converting abstract problems into focused, executable sprint goals.
  • Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner closely with software engineering, data science, and business stakeholders to maintain absolute alignment and project momentum.

💡 Operational Excellence: micro1 operates as a high-agency, remote-first organization. Success in this role requires exceptional async communication, technical fluency, and the ability to move quickly while maintaining an exceptionally high bar for software quality.

Preferred Qualifications and Background

Because micro1 builds infrastructure for frontier AI models, vetting standards for core team members are rigorous. Ideal candidates possess a robust blend of technical product management experience and startup agility:

  • Product Experience: Demonstrated track record in a Product Owner, Product Manager, or similar leadership role owning a live, customer-facing software product.
  • Tech & AI Familiarity: Hands-on experience with AI, machine learning, data platforms, developer tools, or technically sophisticated SaaS products is strongly preferred.
  • Design & UX Intuition: A proven history of building intuitive, delightful products that simplify complex workflows for technical and non-technical users alike.
  • Data-First Decision Making: Exceptional product judgment backed by practical experience using usage data, analytics dashboards, and direct customer feedback.
  • High Agency & Speed: Comfort operating in rapid, ambiguous environments with minimal oversight and maximum ownership.

Strong Technical Plus Points:

Candidates possessing an engineering background, strong technical fluency, experience with LLMs, autonomous agents, data infrastructure, or a history of taking products from 0 to 1 will receive strong consideration during evaluation.
