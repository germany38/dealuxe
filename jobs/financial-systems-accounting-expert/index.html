The Intersection of Corporate Finance and Frontier Artificial Intelligence

For years, training large language models relied heavily on general web crawling, leaving models entirely unequipped for domain-specific professional tasks. In enterprise finance, an AI hallucination, incorrect debit/credit treatment, or miscalculated accrual can lead to catastrophic compliance failures. Developing reliable AI agents requires feeding models pristine, human-verified accounting logic and structured expert feedback.

micro1 builds the foundational human intelligence layer for frontier AI models. By partnering with practicing accountants, controllers, FP&A professionals, and enterprise resource planning (ERP) specialists, micro1 transforms real-world accounting acumen into high-quality training datasets and evaluation benchmarks. No prior software coding or machine learning experience is required; your hands-on financial expertise is what makes the technology intelligent and reliable.

Core Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • AI Output Evaluation: Evaluate AI-generated accounting outputs for absolute technical accuracy, including proper accounting treatments, calculations, journal entries, and contextual explanations aligned with GAAP.
  • Financial Statement & Workpaper Review: Review, analyze, and annotate complex financial statements, account reconciliations, and close workpapers, identifying subtle misstatements and supplying correct treatments with full rationales.
  • ERP Workflow Assessment: Assess accounting workflows and financial systems logic across modern enterprise platforms such as NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, QuickBooks, Xero, and Microsoft Dynamics 365.
  • Rubric Application & Grading: Consistently apply structured written rubrics and grading guidelines when rating, ranking, and auditing model outputs, carefully flagging ambiguous cases.
  • Scenario Generation: Contribute authentic, real-world accounting scenarios, transaction test cases, and reference answers that reflect true operational practices in modern financial systems.
  • Actionable Feedback Delivery: Deliver clear, highly articulated written feedback to project teams and AI engineers to continuously refine model performance.
