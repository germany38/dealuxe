The Core Mission: Reimagining Legal Ops Through Artificial Intelligence

In high-growth technology companies, the legal department is often the central engine driving corporate scaling, investment rounds, and entity governance. However, traditional legal operations are frequently bogged down by repetitive manual tasks, fragmented communication channels, and administrative bottlenecks. micro1 is building the human intelligence layer for frontier AI, and that mission begins internally by engineering modern, scalable legal infrastructure.

As a Member of Legal Staff at micro1, you will not only manage core corporate transactions and entity governance but also actively design and build AI-enabled legal intake workflows, self-service tools, and automations tailored for a fast-paced, asynchronous, Slack-driven environment. You will bridge the gap between traditional legal rigor and cutting-edge software engineering.

Key Responsibilities and Scope of Work:

  • Venture Financing & Transaction Execution: Support venture capital financings, secondary transactions, and stockholder approvals. Own transaction execution from initial document circulation, signatures, and closing checklists through post-closing organization.
  • Corporate Governance & Entity Administration: Maintain master data rooms and ensure corporate records remain pristine. Manage state filings, annual reports, minute books, and subsidiary records.
  • Cap Table & Equity Administration: Administer cap tables, equity plans, and equity grants—managing issuances, exercises, transfers, cancellations, and meticulous reconciliation of equity records.
  • AI Legal Automation & Workflow Design: Design AI-enabled legal intake and triage workflows. Build self-service templates, playbooks, and automated solutions for recurring legal processes such as NDAs and signature requests.
  • Independent Legal Drafting: Independently draft, review, and manage routine corporate documents while exercising sound judgment on when to escalate complex matters to outside counsel.

💡 Why This Role Stands Out: This position offers a rare hybrid of core legal execution and product innovation. Rather than performing isolated administrative duties, you will actively engineer the automated systems that make micro1's legal function faster, smarter, and fully scalable.

Ideal Qualifications and Background

micro1 maintains rigorous hiring standards to ensure candidates possess both deep operational mastery and the adaptability required in a fast-moving AI lab:

  • Exceptional Organizational Acumen: Meticulous attention to detail with the autonomy to drive complex projects from conception to completion in a remote-first setting.
