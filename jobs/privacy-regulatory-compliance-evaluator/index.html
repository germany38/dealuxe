Comprehensive Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

For compliance directors, data protection officers, legal consultants, and regulatory analysts seeking to pivot toward high-yielding independent consulting or supplement their advisory income, this role offers intellectual stimulation paired with unmatched schedule autonomy. The position requires senior-level professional judgment to rigorously assess, audit, and refine AI-generated work products.

Key Responsibilities & Daily Tasks:

  • Artifact Evaluation: Review, grade, and audit AI-generated corporate documents, risk assessment spreadsheets, and executive slide decks focused on privacy regulations, compliance audits, and data governance frameworks.
  • Rigor & Accuracy Auditing: Apply deep professional subject-matter expertise to identify factual errors, legal ambiguities, regulatory omissions, and presentation mistakes in automated outputs.
  • Constructive Written Feedback: Provide clear, structured, and legally sound feedback to help train, correct, and align frontier AI models with enterprise-grade compliance standards.
  • Domain Quality Control: Ensure that complex regulatory guidelines, data privacy protocols, and corporate compliance strategies generated by AI strictly adhere to recognized legal frameworks and best practices.
