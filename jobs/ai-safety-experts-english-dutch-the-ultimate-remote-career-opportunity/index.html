Artificial Intelligence has evolved at a breakneck speed, moving from rigid automated algorithms into fluid, conversational systems, generative text models, and complex AI agents capable of reasoning, executing tasks, and interacting dynamically with humans. However, with this unprecedented technological expansion comes a massive escalation in risk. As AI systems become integrated into enterprise workflows, software development, customer service, and daily decision-making, ensuring their reliability, predictability, and safety is no longer optional—it is an absolute necessity.

Enter the discipline of AI safety and adversarial red teaming. Major frontier AI labs and multinational enterprises rely heavily on human intelligence to identify blind spots that automated benchmark testing simply cannot catch. This is where specialized linguistic and technical talent steps in. If you possess native fluency in both English and Dutch, combined with a sharp, analytical, and exploratory mindset, an extraordinary career opportunity awaits you at Mercor.

Understanding the Role: What Does an AI Safety Expert Actually Do?

The role of an AI Safety Expert at Mercor is multifaceted, dynamic, and deeply impactful. You are not merely reviewing text; you are actively stress-testing the boundaries of modern language models to uncover hidden flaws before malicious actors or unforeseen real-world conditions can exploit them.

Your primary responsibilities include:

  • Red Teaming Conversational Models: Actively probing AI models and autonomous agents using advanced adversarial methods, including complex jailbreaks, prompt injections, multi-turn manipulation, and edge-case scenario testing.
  • Human Data Generation & Curation: Creating high-quality, structured human datasets that highlight model failures, classify vulnerabilities, and categorize systemic reasoning risks.
  • Framework Adherence: Applying rigorous taxonomies, industry benchmarks, and evaluation playbooks to ensure testing methodology remains completely consistent, reproducible, and actionable.
  • Technical Documentation: Producing comprehensive reports, attack case breakdowns, and dataset evaluations that engineering teams and enterprise customers can immediately act upon to patch vulnerabilities.

Why Linguistic Nuance Matters: The Intersection of English and Dutch

One of the most fascinating aspects of modern large language models is their cross-lingual capabilities. However, models frequently exhibit distinct vulnerabilities when processing different languages, regional idioms, syntax structures, and cultural contexts. While English often serves as the primary training baseline for frontier models, low-resource or mid-resource languages like Dutch require dedicated scrutiny.

Bilingual experts fluent in both English and Dutch are uniquely positioned to evaluate how safety guardrails hold up when switching between languages, testing regional phrasing, or assessing localized misinterpretations, bias, and misinformation risks. Your native mastery ensures that subtle nuances—such as sarcasm, double entendres, cultural idioms, and socio-technical implications—are accurately identified and flagged.

The Importance of AI Safety in the Modern Enterprise Landscape

As artificial intelligence permeates financial systems, healthcare diagnostics, educational platforms, and corporate governance, the cost of model failure skyrockets. An unconstrained model that hallucinates misinformation, generates biased outputs, or falls victim to a clever prompt injection can cause catastrophic reputational and operational damage to an enterprise.

By engaging in red teaming, human safety experts act as the ultimate line of defense. Your work directly builds public trust and establishes industry standards for safe, ethical artificial intelligence development.
