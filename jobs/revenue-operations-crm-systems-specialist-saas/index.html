Redefining SaaS Architecture Through AI

The boundary between traditional enterprise software architecture and advanced artificial intelligence is rapidly dissolving. As modern frontier models learn to reason through complex multi-system workflows, billing structures, and CRM permissions, they require rigorous real-world guidance from elite practitioners who build and manage these environments daily.

micro1 is engaging seasoned Revenue Operations & CRM Systems Specialists (SaaS) for a high-impact customer initiative focused on complex systems alignment. In this role, your daily operational mastery over revenue tech stacks will directly train next-generation AI agents to navigate messy enterprise structures with human-like precision.

Core Scope of Work

  • Systems Auditing: Analyze complex CRM, billing, and ticketing data to pinpoint exact operational misalignments and friction points.
  • Policy Conflict Resolution: Surface, isolate, and document policy conflicts, outdated automations, duplicate entities, and permission mismatches.
  • Prompt & Recommendation Design: Draft precise operational prompts outlining correct handling procedures for ambiguous SaaS revenue scenarios.
  • Data Integrity & Alignment: Evaluate desired data states, correcting discrepancies to guarantee logical consistency across interconnected stacks.
  • Transparent Decision Trails: Record detailed rationale behind operational adjustments, outlining choices made and areas left untouched.
  • Cross-System Impact: Exercise rigorous operational judgment when handling cascading changes impacting billing, CPQ, and CRM environments.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience Threshold: 5 to 10 years of hands-on experience in RevOps, sales operations, customer success ops, or SaaS implementation.
  • Direct System Ownership: Proven ability to personally configure CRMs (fields, workflows, validation rules, permissions) rather than delegating tasks.
  • Tool Ecosystem: Direct familiarity with at least two adjacent SaaS revenue tools (subscription/billing platforms, CPQ, ticketing, or orchestration tools).
  • Industry Background: Deep exposure to SaaS environments, particularly fintech, healthtech, insurtech, or multi-product setups.
  • Communication Mastery: Professional English fluency with exceptional writing skills to articulate nuanced trade-offs.
