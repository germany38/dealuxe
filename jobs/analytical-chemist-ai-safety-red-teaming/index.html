The Role: What Does an AI Safety Red-Teaming Chemist Do?

As artificial intelligence models grow exponentially in computational capacity, leading tech labs face critical challenges regarding safety guardrails. While generalist prompts are easily handled, specialized technical domains—especially chemistry, pharmaceuticals, and hazardous material detection—require sophisticated human oversight. A model must be intelligent enough to answer legitimate educational or professional questions fully, while remaining robust enough to recognize and refuse genuinely dangerous misuse inquiries.

As an Analytical Chemist on Mercor's panel, your primary mission is to test, challenge, and refine how frontier models reason through technical scientific data. You are not writing code or building algorithms; you are applying your real-world laboratory judgment to establish where the boundaries of machine safety lie.

Key Responsibilities Include:

  • Challenging Prompt Creation: Write rigorous, single-turn prompts in your specialized domain, meticulously categorized across three distinct levels: benign, dual-use, and adversarial.
  • Policy Evaluation & Grading: Critically review model-generated responses against strict safety policy frameworks, determining whether the model correctly navigated the technical query.
  • Authoring Reference Answers: Draft precise reference answers detailing what a scientifically accurate response looks like, accompanied by the technical reasoning supporting your evaluation.
  • Navigating the Dual-Use Line: Address scenarios where the exact same analytical method used for environmental screening or trace detection intersects with boundary thresholds. You will ensure models do not fail by incorrectly refusing routine professional questions, nor pass harmful ones.

💡 Why Practitioners Matter: The nuances of trace-level analysis mean that generalist reviewers cannot safely draw the line. Mercor specifically seeks out experienced practitioners who know what a detection limit truly implies in practice.

Background and Qualifications: Who Are They Looking For?

Mercor maintains rigorous selection standards for its scientific panels to ensure elite data integrity for AI labs. Ideal candidates possess substantial hands-on experience building and validating analytical methods. Relevant professional and academic backgrounds include:

  • Advanced Instrumental Analysis: Detection and identification of hazardous compounds using GC-MS, LC-MS/MS, FTIR, or NMR spectroscopy.
  • Method Development: Designing and validating protocols for trace-level analysis, method optimization, and analytical validation.
  • Field and Emergency Sampling: Environmental, occupational hygiene, or emergency response sampling and chemical analysis.
  • Standards & Libraries: Working with reference standards, spectral libraries, and unknown compound identification.
  • Quality Assurance: Laboratory accreditation, QA/QC protocols, and inter-laboratory proficiency testing.

Beyond Technical Depth: The Writing Demand

This position requires strong technical writing capabilities. Every single judgment you make must be accompanied by a clear, structured written rationale that a non-specialist moderator can easily follow. Candidates with published research, peer-reviewed articles, prior technical writing portfolios, or expert witness experience possess a distinct advantage during screening.

Step-by-Step Guide to Completing Your Application Successfully

Because specialized scientific panels fill up quickly, submitting a polished, comprehensive application is vital. Follow these key steps to maximize your chances of acceptance:

  1. Highlight Specific Instrumentation: Clearly state your hands-on proficiency with analytical equipment (e.g., LC-MS/MS, GC-MS) and specific method validation experience in your resume or profile.
  2. Include Writing Samples: Because this role requires rigorous written rationales, provide links to published papers, technical reports, or documentation that showcases your scientific writing clarity.
  3. Address Compliance Transparently: Ensure your work does not violate any active classification or export-control obligations. If you hold prior NDA commitments, mention them upfront so the scope can be adjusted cleanly.
  4. Complete the Screening Promptly: Respond to interview or assessment steps quickly to secure your spot as an early applicant before cohort capacities are met.
