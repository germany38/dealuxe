Shaping the Future of AI: Inside the Senior Design Expert Study

Artificial intelligence is evolving at a breakneck speed, but true innovation relies heavily on human insight, craftsmanship, and critical thought. Major technology companies building next-generation AI design tooling are recognizing that automated systems cannot replicate high-level human intuition on their own. That is precisely why Mercor has launched an exclusive, high-paying research study tailored for seasoned design professionals.

Expert design thinking and rigorous evaluation form the backbone of modern AI development frameworks.

What the Role Entails

The core objective of this study is to capture how expert product, UX, brand, and specialized designers think through complex problems. The initiative explores how top-tier professionals differentiate exceptional craft from mediocre execution and how they determine when a project meets the stringent standards required to ship.

Participants engage in structured, recorded remote sessions lasting roughly 4 to 5 hours per task or interview (inclusive of preparation and short reviews). Depending on exact professional fit and alignment, participants will either act as the core design expert being interviewed or take charge of running the interview sessions.

Compensation & Terms: Earn between $150 and $250 per hour on a fully remote, flexible hourly contract. Weekly payouts are handled securely via Stripe or Wise.

Key Requirements for Applicants

  • Senior-Level Experience: Demonstrated background in product, UX, brand, or adjacent design disciplines.
  • Clear Critical Perspective: An articulate, well-defined point of view on design quality, critique methodologies, and rigorous shipping standards.
  • Portfolio Submission: A shareable public portfolio showcasing past work (strictly excluding confidential, unreleased, or NDA-protected material).
