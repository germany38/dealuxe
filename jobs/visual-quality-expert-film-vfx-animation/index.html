Detailed Job Overview & Core Responsibilities

If you have spent your career reviewing dailies, balancing color grades, supervising render farms, or capturing cinematic masterpieces, this role is tailored to your expertise. You will be at the core of the AI revolution, acting as the definitive visual judge for cutting-edge GenAI models.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Pixel-Level Evaluation: Scrutinize high-resolution video frames and stills to identify compression artifacts, digital noise, aliasing, color banding, inconsistent grain structure, and over- or under-sharpening.
  • Uncompromising Quality Auditing: Catch the intricate visual imperfections that typically bypass the average viewer, ensuring AI outputs adhere to elite cinematic standards.
  • Template & Guideline Design: Formulate and refine evaluation rubrics so that multi-reviewer teams maintain a consistent visual bar, even when assessing challenging edge cases.
  • Descriptive Translation: Translate complex, nuanced visual and aesthetic critiques into precise, structured language suitable for AI data captions and training sets.
  • Visual Sample Generation: Produce your own visual test samples for comparative analysis and targeted benchmarking against competing generative outputs.

Candidate Requirements & Discipline Specializations

Because these generative AI models are trained for professional enterprise environments, the qualifications demand verified seniority across high-end visual disciplines.

Core Qualifications:
  • 5+ Years Experience: Demonstrated professional background in high-end visual evaluation across film, VFX, animation, or professional photography.
  • Hardware Access: Reliable access to a true 4K-resolution monitor or display capable of precise, pixel-level visual inspection.
  • Schedule & Availability: Ability to commit reliably to approximately 20 hours per week with working hours overlapping standard U.S. time zones (PST to EST).
  • AI Familiarity: Working knowledge of modern AI tools and prompt workflows to generate targeted test samples.

Accepted Disciplines Include: VFX Supervisor, Rendering Supervisor, Lighting Supervisor/Artist, Colorist, Director of Photography (Cinematographer), Effects/Surfacing Artist, High-End Photographer, Director (especially crowd/background action), and Digital Projectionist.
