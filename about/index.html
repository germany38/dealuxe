About Dealux & our editorial standards

Dealux brings together shopping guides, comparison tools, and merchant listings to help people evaluate purchases. The catalog spans many sellers and categories; breadth alone does not make a listing a recommendation.

Know what you are reading

  • Buying guides: practical decision methods with explicitly labeled illustrative examples. These are not hands-on product reviews.
  • Merchant listings: product information, prices, creative assets, and offers supplied by sellers or affiliate feeds. They may change or expire. Confirm details at checkout.
  • Promotional pages and videos: commercial material from merchants, rather than independent testing.

Evidence and limits

Do not interpret words such as “best,” “premium,” or “ultimate” in merchant material as measured findings by Dealux. A product test needs an identified item, a stated method, and observed results. Where we have not supplied that evidence, we are not claiming to have tested the product. Our cost calculator uses only the numbers you enter and does not verify merchant prices.

Affiliate and advertising disclosure

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you buy through them, Dealux may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Merchant promotions and advertisements are commercial content. A commission does not establish that an offer is the cheapest, that a product fits your needs, or that a seller’s claims are independently verified.

Corrections and maintenance

To flag an expired deal, broken link, incorrect specification, or misleading claim, email support@dealuxapp.com. Include the page URL, the detail in question, and a source or screenshot where possible. Use the same address for editorial and privacy enquiries. Contact the seller directly for physical-product delivery, returns, and warranty service.

Guides display their publication date. A date is not a guarantee that every linked merchant offer remains current. Promotions should always be checked against the seller’s latest terms.
