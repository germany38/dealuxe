Amazon Music Unlimited Subscription: Is It Worth It?

As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.

Ready to subscribe? Get Amazon Music Unlimited here

A music subscription is one of those services that you either use every day or forget you are paying for. Before you subscribe to Amazon Music Unlimited, it helps to know what you get and whether it fits how you listen. Here is a clear look at the service and how to decide.

What Is Amazon Music Unlimited?

Amazon Music Unlimited is a paid music streaming subscription. It offers a very large catalog of songs that you can play on demand, without ads. It also includes podcasts, offline downloads and higher quality audio on supported devices. Pricing and plans vary, and Amazon has offered different options for Prime members, individuals and households. Check the offer page for the current price.

What You Get

  • A huge on-demand catalog. Search for any song, album or artist and play it.
  • No ads. Listen without interruptions.
  • HD and Spatial Audio. Better sound on supported devices and headphones.
  • Offline listening. Download music for flights, commutes and trips.
  • Podcasts. Many episodes are included.
  • Voice control. Ask a smart speaker to play what you want.
  • A monthly audiobook. Members have been able to listen to one audiobook a month from a selection.

Features can differ by plan, device and region.

How to Decide If It Is Worth It

Do you listen every day? If music is part of your daily routine, the cost per hour is low.

Does it replace another service? If you cancel another music subscription, the value goes up.

Do you care about sound quality? The audio upgrades matter more with good headphones or speakers.

Do you want to pick exactly what you hear? On-demand playback is the main difference from limited music benefits.

Do you share a home with other listeners? Check the household plans.

If you answered yes to a few of these, a subscription may be a good fit.

Unlimited vs. Prime Music

Prime Music is included with Prime and offers a smaller selection of music. Music Unlimited has a much larger on-demand catalog and more features. If you only want casual listening, Prime Music may be enough. If you want to play any song you like, Music Unlimited is the upgrade.

Tips for Getting More From Your Subscription

  1. Build and organize playlists. Group songs by mood, activity or season.
  2. Use downloads to save mobile data.
  3. Try the podcasts and the included audiobook offer.
  4. Connect your smart speakers. Voice control is convenient at home.
  5. Review your plan occasionally. Switch if a better option becomes available.

How to Subscribe

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Choose the plan that fits you.
  4. Review the price and terms.
  5. Start your subscription and open the Amazon Music app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Is there a free trial? Amazon has offered free trials to eligible customers. See the offer page.

Which devices work? Many phones, tablets, computers, smart speakers and cars support it.

Is there a plan for families? Amazon has offered household plans. Check the offer pages.

Start Listening

If music is a daily habit, a subscription can be one of the most used services you pay for. Choose a plan, build your first playlist and press play.
