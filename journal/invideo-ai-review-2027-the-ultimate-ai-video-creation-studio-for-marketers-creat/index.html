Core Features: What Makes InVideo Stand Out?

InVideo is packed with powerful capabilities designed to streamline the entire media workflow from concept to completion:

1. Prompt-to-Video and URL-to-Video Workflow

Simply type what kind of video you want to create (e.g., "A 60-second TikTok ad for a fitness app with an energetic voiceover") or paste a link to your blog post or product page. The AI automatically generates a script, selects matching visual clips, synchronizes a voiceover, and adds captions.

2. Extensive Stock Media & Template Library

Gain access to millions of stock video clips, images, and audio tracks via partnerships with iStock, Shutterstock, Storyblocks, Pexels, and Pixabay, alongside thousands of professionally designed templates optimized for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

3. AI Avatars and Voice Cloning

Create digital presenter videos using custom AI avatars or clone your own voice to narrate scripts seamlessly without stepping in front of a physical camera or microphone.

4. Text-Command Editing & Timeline Control

Need to make a change? Instead of manually cutting clips on a complex timeline, you can simply type text commands to instruct the AI to swap visuals, adjust pacing, rewrite script lines, or modify tone.

Who Is InVideo Designed For?

InVideo is built to accommodate various digital content creators and scaling business teams:

  • Digital Marketers & Growth Teams: Rapidly test multiple ad creatives, video hooks, and promotional angles across social media channels.
  • E-Commerce Founders: Turn product listings and URLs into engaging video showcases and user-generated content (UGC) style ads without studio overhead.
  • Content Creators & Influencers: Maintain a consistent publishing schedule on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok with minimal editing friction.
  • Educators & Course Creators: Produce clear instructional explainers, training clips, and dynamic educational content quickly.
