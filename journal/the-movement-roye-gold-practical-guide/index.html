THE MOVEMENT by Roye Gold: A Practical Guide for Buyers

Obstacle course fans often dream of the stage at the top of the course, and many look for guidance from people who have trained for it. THE MOVEMENT, from Roye Gold, is an American Ninja Warrior advisor with more than 1.5 million followers, and the brand centers on training guidance for people who want to build strength, skill, and confidence. If you are considering it, a good first step is to visit the official THE MOVEMENT website and see what is currently offered.

Who Roye Gold's brand is built around

Roye Gold's public profile is tied to American Ninja Warrior, a televised competition that rewards grip strength, balance, explosive movement, and mental focus. An advisor with a large online audience can be useful for learning how a competitor thinks about preparation. A large following, however, is not the same as a program that fits your schedule, fitness level, or goals. Treat the follower count as a sign of interest, not as a measure of whether the training will suit you.

What to look for in any coaching program

Before paying for coaching of any kind, compare a few practical points. Coaching is an education and training service, so the details matter more than the marketing.

  • Clear goals: Does the program explain who it is for and what kind of progress it targets?
  • Format: Check whether sessions are live, recorded, or a mix, and whether you can ask questions.
  • Time commitment: Estimate how many hours per week you can realistically give before you enroll.
  • Background: Look for a description of the coach's experience and how they work with beginners.
  • Policies: Read the cancellation, refund, and billing terms in full.

How Inner Circle coaching fits in

THE MOVEMENT also lists Inner Circle coaching as a more personal option. Higher-touch coaching often costs more than general content, so ask yourself what you need. If you are a beginner who wants structure, a guided plan may help. If you already train regularly and need occasional feedback, a smaller or shorter option may be enough. Compare the included features on the brand's site with what you can get from free resources, local gyms, or community training groups, and ask questions before you commit.

Practical steps before you sign up

Start with a short, specific goal, such as a particular skill or a training routine you can keep for a few weeks. Write down your current fitness level and any health concerns, and talk with a qualified health professional before starting a new exercise program, especially if you have an injury or medical condition. Check the current pricing directly on the brand's website, since prices and offers can change. Finally, keep notes on your progress so you can judge whether the coaching is working for you.

THE MOVEMENT may suit people who want guidance from an advisor with a strong connection to American Ninja Warrior. The best choice depends on your goals, budget, and willingness to train consistently. When you are ready, you can explore the brand's current offerings and compare them against your own plan.
