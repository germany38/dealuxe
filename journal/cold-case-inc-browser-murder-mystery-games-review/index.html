Cold Case Inc: Browser Murder Mysteries for Armchair Detectives

If you enjoy untangling a crime one clue at a time, Cold Case Inc may be worth a look. The brand creates online murder mystery games that you play directly in your web browser, with no separate download needed. Each game puts you in the role of a detective working through a fictional police case file. You can explore the Cold Case Inc website to see how the games are presented before you decide whether they suit you.

What kind of game is it?

Cold Case Inc sits within online gaming, but its games have a lot in common with detective fiction and interactive puzzles. The brand offers eleven fictional police case files, so there is a range of stories to work through rather than a single scenario. The games fall across several areas, including gadgets, toys and games, and digital TV and video-on-demand style storytelling, which gives you a sense of how the experience is built around watching, reading and piecing things together.

How the case files work

The core appeal lies in the evidence. Each case gives you material to examine, such as interviews with witnesses and suspects, CCTV footage, and digital evidence. Your job is to compare these sources, notice what doesn't add up, and reach your own conclusions. Players who like to take notes and test theories tend to get the most out of this format. If you prefer fast action or constant rewards, a slower, investigative pace may feel quite different from what you expect.

Who it tends to suit

Cold Case Inc is most likely to appeal to people who enjoy crime dramas, puzzle solving and reading closely for detail. It can also suit a shared evening, where friends or family compare theories on a case. Because the games are fictional, they are best approached as entertainment rather than as training for real investigations. If you are new to murder mystery games, it helps to start with a case that matches the time you have available.

Trying before you commit

The brand offers a free 10-minute case, which is a practical way to judge the style before spending more time on it. Gift vouchers are also available, which may be useful if you are shopping for a fan of crime stories or puzzle games. Cold Case Inc has a rating of 4.8 out of 5 from 329 players and has won the Bullseye Award twice. These figures are useful context, but your own taste matters most, so a short trial is a sensible first step.

What to check before you play

Before you start, take a few minutes to check the practical details on the brand's site. Confirm which devices and browsers are supported, how each case is accessed, and what the terms say about vouchers, including any expiry dates or restrictions. Look at the description of each case so you know whether it is a short session or a longer investigation. Doing this helps you avoid surprises and makes it easier to choose the right starting point.

Cold Case Inc is a good choice if you want a detective story that you actively solve yourself. Start with the free 10-minute case to see whether the interviews, CCTV and digital evidence hold your attention, then visit the Cold Case Inc site to explore the full range of files at your own pace.
