TEN X YOU Athleisure Review: Sachin Tendulkar’s Play-First Sportswear Brand Redefining Comfort and Fitness

Discover how TEN X YOU blends premium bamboo-cotton comfort, sustainable Indian craftsmanship, and elite athletic design to inspire an active lifestyle.

Introduction: The Rise of India’s Play-First Athleisure Movement

The modern consumer demands versatility from their wardrobe. Clothes need to transition seamlessly from a morning workout or travel journey to casual weekend hangouts without missing a beat. Entering this space with a powerful mission is TEN X YOU—the premier sports and athleisure brand launched by cricket icon Sachin Tendulkar under SRT10 Athleisure Pvt. Ltd.

More than just a clothing label, TEN X YOU champions a vital cultural shift: moving India from a sports-loving nation to a sports-playing one. By combining global-brand quality with precision local craftsmanship, the brand offers dynamic, durable, and stylish apparel engineered to make physical activity more accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

What Does TEN X YOU Sell? Premium Activewear Collections

TEN X YOU features an extensive product lineup tailored for diverse movements, prioritizing functional comfort without sacrificing modern streetwear aesthetics:

1. Lifestyle Tees and Bamboo-Cotton Comfort

Crafted with a specialized blend of cotton and natural bamboo fibers (offering a soft, breathable, and smooth texture against the skin), the loose-fit lifestyle tees and casual wear options deliver all-day ease for travels and casual meetups.

2. Training Tees and Performance Tanks

Engineered for high-intensity workouts and home training routines, these pieces feature moisture-wicking properties, lightweight relaxed fits, and ergonomic stretch to maximize physical performance.

3. Straight-Fit Lifestyle Joggers

Designed with flat waistbands and clean finishes, these joggers offer a sleek, versatile silhouette that balances athletic flexibility with modern minimalist style.

4. Lifestyle Shorts and Active Accessories

Perfect for cafe hangouts, outdoor activities, and warm-weather leisure, the above-knee relaxed-fit shorts provide ultimate airflow and dynamic freedom of movement.

Why Choose TEN X YOU? Key Brand Differentiators

In a saturated sportswear market, TEN X YOU stands out through deliberate engineering and purpose-driven design:

  • Innovative Fabric Blends: The signature 52% cotton and 43% bamboo blend ensures natural breathability, superior softness, and everyday stretch.
  • Long-Lasting Colour Technology: Premium dyes ensure garments retain rich color vibrancy and a fresh appearance even after repeated heavy washes.
  • The Sachin Tendulkar Philosophy: Built to inspire a healthier, active nation, motivating individuals to embrace everyday motion.
  • Versatile Movement-First Design: Clothing curated specifically to transition effortlessly from travel and relaxation to sports and active training.
