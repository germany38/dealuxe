Core Features: What Does StockEdge Provide?

StockEdge is packed with comprehensive modules designed to handle every facet of market research:

1. Advanced Stock Screeners & Scans

Leverage over 500+ combination screeners based on price action, technical indicators (RSI, MACD, VWAP), volume spikes, and fundamental health parameters like low debt and high Return on Equity (ROE).

2. FII-DII & Institutional Activity Tracking

Track daily capital flows of Foreign Institutional Investors (FII) and Domestic Institutional Investors (DII) across cash, futures, and options markets, alongside bulk and block deals.

3. AI-Driven Chart Patterns & Technical Setups

Automatically detect over 15 classical chart formations—including triangles, head and shoulders, and breakouts—saving you hours of manual chart scanning.

4. Investor Portfolios ("Big Bulls")

Monitor the investment portfolios and portfolio adjustments of market veterans and ace investors like Rakesh Jhunjhunwala, Vijay Kedia, and Ashish Kacholia.

5. Sector Analytics & Rotation

Identify trending industrial sectors in real time, analyze sales, EBITDA, profit metrics, and monitor changing promoter shareholding patterns.

Who is StockEdge Designed For?

StockEdge caters to all tiers of market participants:

  • For Beginners: Simplifies market learning with intuitive indicators like Edge Meter scores, basic technical ratios, and curated educational resources.
  • For Traders: Accelerates trade discovery using breakout scans, F&O open interest trends, Put-Call Ratios (PCR), and momentum metrics.
  • For Long-Term Investors: Provides fundamental check-lists, corporate action calendars, earnings call summaries, and comprehensive equity reports.
