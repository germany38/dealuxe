Understanding Sciatica: Why Conventional Solutions Fall Short

The sciatic nerve is the largest nerve in your human body, running from your lower back through your hips and buttocks down each leg. When something irritates, compresses, or inflames this nerve—such as a lumbar disc issue, tight piriformis muscles, or poor spinal alignment—the resulting pain can radiate intensely down your lower extremities.

Too often, sufferers rely purely on temporary band-aids: heavy painkillers that mask symptoms without fixing the structural root cause, or expensive clinical appointments that don't fit into busy schedules or tight budgets. What your body truly needs is targeted, low-impact, consistent movement designed to decompress nerves and restore natural spinal alignment.
