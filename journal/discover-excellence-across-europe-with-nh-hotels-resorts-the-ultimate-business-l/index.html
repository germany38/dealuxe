Tailored Brand Portfolios for Every Traveler Profile

To cater to the varied needs of modern global travelers, NH Hotels categorizes its offerings into specialized brand tiers designed to exceed expectations:

1. NH Hotels (Core Comfort & Convenience)

Positioned as dependable 3- and 4-star city-center properties, NH Hotels offer central locations, comfortable accommodations, reliable high-speed Wi-Fi, and signature breakfast buffets. They serve as ideal hubs for smart business travelers and urban explorers seeking functional quality.

2. NH Collection (Premium & Historic Properties)

Tailored for travelers seeking elevated luxury, NH Collection properties reside in landmark buildings across prime locations. Offering premium amenities, world-class gastronomy, state-of-the-art wellness facilities, and personalized service, these hotels elevate every stay into an extraordinary experience.

3. nhow Hotels (Design-Led & Lifestyle Hospitality)

nhow properties deliver unconventional, design-focused stays inspired by contemporary culture, art, and music. Located in creative hot spots such as Berlin and Milan, nhow hotels feature vibrant atmospheres, custom interiors, and interactive guest spaces.

4. Resorts & Wellness Escapes (Tivoli, Anantara, Avani)

For leisure trips, NH Hotels extends access to luxury coastal and country retreats, including Tivoli and Anantara resorts in the Algarve and Marbella, offering full-service spas, championship golf access, and fine dining.
