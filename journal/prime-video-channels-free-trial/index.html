Trying a new streaming service usually means another app, another login and another bill. Prime Video Channels removes most of that. You can start a free trial of a service, such as Paramount+, and watch it inside the Prime Video app. Here is how it works and how to use trials wisely.

What Is a Prime Video Channel Trial?

Prime Video lets you subscribe to other streaming services through your Amazon account. Many of these services offer a free trial period for eligible customers. During the trial you can watch the service from within Prime Video. After the trial, the subscription renews at its regular price unless you cancel. Offers, lengths and eligibility vary by service, so check the offer page.

Why It Is a Smart Way to Try Streaming

It is convenient. You do not need to download a new app or enter new payment details.

It lowers the risk. You can test a service without paying for the trial period.

You manage it in one place. Your subscriptions are in your Amazon account.

It is easy to cancel. You can generally end a subscription from your account settings.

It is good for one-time interests. Want to watch a single series or event? A trial can cover it.

Who Will Benefit

  • Fans of a specific show on another service.
  • Budget-conscious viewers who rotate subscriptions.
  • Sports and event fans who want short-term access.
  • Prime members who like having everything in one app.
  • Curious viewers who want to test a service.

How to Use Trials Wisely

  1. Pick one service at a time. It is easy to forget several trials at once.
  2. Make a watchlist first. Know what you want to watch before you start.
  3. Set a cancellation reminder. Put a note in your calendar for a day before the trial ends.
  4. Check the renewal price. Know what you will pay if you keep it.
  5. Cancel if you are done. There is no reason to pay for something you are not watching.
  6. Check device support. Make sure your TV or phone works with the service.

How to Start

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Choose the channel you want to try.
  4. Review the trial terms and renewal price.
  5. Start your trial and begin watching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the trial really free? Trials are advertised at no cost for the trial period. After that, the regular price applies unless you cancel.

Do I need Prime? Requirements can vary by channel. See the offer page.

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Can I try more than one? Yes, but it is easy to lose track. Add reminders.
