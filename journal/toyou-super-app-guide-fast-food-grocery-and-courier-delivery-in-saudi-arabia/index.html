What Services Does ToYou Offer?

ToYou is designed as a dynamic, multi-category marketplace that eliminates the need for downloading dozens of separate store applications. By connecting consumers directly to a network of more than 27,000 registered merchants, ToYou covers every daily demand effortlessly.

1. Restaurant & Fast Food Delivery

From local Saudi cuisine and authentic home-style chefs to international favorites like Albaik, KFC, McDonald's, Burger King, Kudu, Pizza Hut, and Starbucks, ToYou brings fresh, hot meals directly to your door in minutes.

2. Supermarket & Fresh Grocery Shopping

Stock up on daily essentials, farm-fresh produce, dairy products, bakery goods, and household supplies from leading hypermarkets such as Danube, Tamimi Markets, and local neighborhood grocers without leaving your home.

3. Pharmacy, Personal Care & Beauty

Access over-the-counter health items, skincare essentials, vitamins, and personal care products from partner pharmacies with rapid doorstep dispatch.

4. ToYou Courier Service (Peer-to-Peer Parcel Delivery)

Need to send a document to an office, forward a forgotten item to a friend, or purchase an item from a store not listed on the main store feed? The ToYou Courier service allows you to request a dedicated rider to transport packages from point A to point B seamlessly.

5. Gifts, Flowers & Specialty Items

Send last-minute birthday presents, floral arrangements, artisan chocolates, and celebratory gifts directly to family members or colleagues using the "Deliver to a Friend" feature within the app.

Key Advantages of Using the ToYou Application

Choosing ToYou as your primary delivery application provides key benefits that make daily living simpler, faster, and more affordable:

  • Flexible Payment Methods: Pay your way using Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Cash on Delivery (COD), ToYou In-App Wallet, or split payments interest-free via Tabby and Tamara.
  • Deliver to a Friend: Surprise loved ones by selecting recipient addresses, sending gifts, or arranging meal deliveries seamlessly through the app.
  • Real-Time Parcel Tracking: Track your driver’s exact location from store pick-up to doorstep drop-off via interactive GPS map updates.
  • Exclusive Promos & Wallet Cashback: New and returning users enjoy frequent promo codes, cashback incentives, and discounted or zero delivery fee campaigns.
  • Kingdom-Wide Reach: Reliable delivery coverage spanning across 57+ cities and regions throughout Saudi Arabia.
