Sometimes you need a gift quickly, and you still want it to feel thoughtful. Here is what it includes and when it works best.

What Is Included

Every Premium Plus gift membership includes:

  • The Plus Catalog. An all-you-can-listen collection of thousands of audiobooks, podcasts and originals.
  • One credit a month. A credit is good for any title in the app, and the title is theirs to keep forever.

With the 1-month plan, the recipient gets one credit and a month of the Plus Catalog. Terms can change, so check the offer page for current details.

When a 1-Month Gift Works Best

Last-minute needs. It is delivered digitally, so you can send it in a few minutes.

Small thank-yous. Teachers, neighbors, coworkers and hosts will appreciate it.

Stocking stuffers and add-ons. It pairs well with a pair of headphones or a gift card.

Trying something new. If you are not sure the recipient will enjoy audiobooks, a month is a gentle test.

Tight budgets. It is the most affordable Audible gift length.

Why It Is a Good Gift

  • They keep the book. The title chosen with the credit stays in their library.
  • There is plenty to explore. The Plus Catalog is available throughout the month.
  • It is flexible. They choose what to listen to.
  • It needs no wrapping. There is nothing to ship or wait for.

Who Will Enjoy It

  • Commuters who are tired of the same radio stations.
  • Walkers and runners looking for something new to listen to.
  • Busy parents who want stories in small pockets of time.
  • Travelers headed on a long trip.
  • Anyone with a hobby that keeps their hands busy, like cooking, crafting or gardening.

Thinking About a Longer Gift?

If you want the gift to last, Audible also sells gift memberships in other lengths, including three, six and twelve months. A longer plan gives the recipient more time to build a listening habit and more credits to keep. A 1-month gift can be a good start, and the recipient can continue on their own if they love it.

Tips for Making a Small Gift Feel Big

  • Write a heartfelt note. Say why you thought of them.
  • Suggest a title. Tell them about a book you enjoyed.
  • Pair it with something physical. Headphones or a cozy blanket complete the gift.
