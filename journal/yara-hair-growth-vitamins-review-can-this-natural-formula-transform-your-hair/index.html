Understanding Hair Thinning and the Need for Internal Nourishment

Hair grows in distinct biological cycles—anagen (growth), catagen (transition), and telogen (resting/shedding). When internal systems experience nutritional deficiencies, high oxidative stress, or hormonal fluctuations, hair follicles can prematurely shift into the shedding phase. Over time, natural structural proteins like keratin and collagen slow down in production, leaving individual strands thinner, weaker, and more prone to breakage.

Topical treatments only address the hair shaft that has already emerged above the scalp. To truly influence thickness, growth rate, and structural resilience, follicles require a consistent, targeted supply of internal nourishment. Dietary supplements bridge this gap by delivering concentrated vitamins, minerals, and plant-based adaptogens directly to the root.
