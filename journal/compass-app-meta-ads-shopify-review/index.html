Compass App Review: Meta Ads and Shopify Store Insights

Visit Compass →Shop for Compass →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

If you sell on Shopify and run Meta ads on Facebook and Instagram, your numbers probably live on several screens. The ad account tells one story, your store dashboard tells another, and piecing them together takes time. Compass is a Shopify-focused app built to help merchants understand how their Meta Ads and store performance relate to each other.

Who Compass is designed for

The brand describes its audience as ecommerce publishers, creators and agencies, and that mix matters. A solo merchant with one product store has different needs than an agency managing accounts for several clients, or a creator who sells merchandise and wants to see which promotions actually move products. Before you sign up, ask how many stores you manage and whether you need reports you can share with a client or a teammate.

What to compare before you commit

Reporting tools vary a lot, so it helps to be specific about what you need. Consider these questions:

  • Does it connect to the store platform you already use?
  • Can you see ad activity and store results side by side, rather than in separate exports?
  • Is the reporting clear enough to share with someone who doesn't live in your ad account?
  • Does the monthly cost make sense for the number of stores and campaigns you actually run?

For the latest details on supported setups and features, check the official Compass website, since these can change over time.

Pricing and the free trial

Compass offers a 14-day free trial, after which the plan costs $49 per month. That structure lets you test the app with your own data before any recurring charge begins. Use the trial deliberately: set a reminder for the end date and decide in advance what you want to verify, such as whether the figures line up with what you already track in your ad account and store admin. If they don't match, investigate the difference rather than assuming either source is correct. You can review the current terms on the Compass site before you start.

Tips for getting the most from a trial

Start with a store and campaign you already understand, so you can judge the output quickly. Compare a single week of data against your own spreadsheet or native reports. Then time yourself answering a real business question, such as which campaigns drove orders last month. If you get the answer faster and with less effort, that's useful evidence. If you don't, the tool may not fit your workflow, and that is a perfectly good outcome.

For Shopify merchants, publishers, creators and agencies who want a clearer view of how Meta Ads connect to store performance, Compass is worth a closer look. The 14-day free trial makes it easy to decide without a long commitment. Visit the Compass website to read the full details and see whether it fits your setup.
