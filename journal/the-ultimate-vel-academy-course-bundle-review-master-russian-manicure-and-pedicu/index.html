Anatomy-Based Technique: Safety Meets Professionalism

One of the standout elements of the VEL Academy methodology is its strict adherence to anatomy-based standards. In many jurisdictions, nail technicians are legally permitted to work exclusively on dead tissue—such as the pterygium and hardened cuticle surface. Cutting or drilling into living skin creates infection risks and compliance violations.

Yuliia’s training emphasizes absolute precision: opening the pocket cleanly, using flame bits at correct angles, and trimming dead tissue without drama or trauma. This protects your clients, preserves skin integrity, and ensures your salon operations remain fully compliant with professional board standards.
