Experience Ultimate Luxury and Entertainment: Why Okada Manila Should Be Your Next Dream Getaway

When it comes to unforgettable travel destinations, finding a resort that combines world-class entertainment, stunning architecture, and unparalleled hospitality can redefine your entire vacation. If you are looking for an iconic, all-encompassing sanctuary that delivers excitement, relaxation, and luxury all under one roof, Okada Manila stands out as one of the premier premier resort destinations in Southeast Asia.

A Magnificent Oasis in Entertainment City

Located in the heart of Entertainment City in Parañaque, Metro Manila, Okada Manila is renowned for its iconic, gold-themed architectural design and its breathtaking views of Manila Bay. As a premier destination for both international travelers and staycation seekers, the resort offers an expansive playground of luxury just minutes away from the Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA) and major commercial hubs like Makati and Bonifacio Global City (BGC).

  • World-Class Accommodations: Featuring lavishly appointed rooms and suites across twin hotel towers (the Pearl and Coral wings), guests enjoy stunning views, plush amenities, and signature bespoke butler service.
  • The Iconic Fountain: One of the property’s crown jewels is its massive, multi-colored dancing fountain—one of the largest of its kind in the world—putting on mesmerizing daily water and light shows choreographed to music.
  • The Cove Manila: Experience the world’s largest ultra-luxury indoor beach club and night club enclosed in a massive, UV-vis-shielded glass dome, hosting international DJs, vibrant pool parties, and dining events year-round.

World-Class Dining and Wellness

A great getaway is incomplete without exceptional culinary experiences and deep relaxation. Okada Manila delivers on all fronts with a diverse array of offerings:

  1. Global Gastronomy: From authentic Japanese omakase and premium Cantonese specialties to rich Filipino comfort food and international buffets, the resort houses dozens of exceptional dining outlets.
  2. The Retreat Spa: A tranquil sanctuary offering bespoke wellness therapies, rejuvenating massages, and hydrotherapy facilities designed to melt away travel fatigue.
  3. High-End Retail Shopping: Explore luxury boutiques, designer fashion, and exclusive retail stores located right within the resort’s sprawling promenade.

Ready to Plan Your Ultimate Escape?

Whether you are looking to pamper yourself with a lavish weekend retreat, catch a breathtaking Manila Bay sunset, or immerse yourself in vibrant nightlife and entertainment, Okada Manila delivers a five-star experience from the moment you arrive.
