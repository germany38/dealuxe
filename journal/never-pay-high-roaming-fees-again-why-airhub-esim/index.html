What is an eSIM and Why Do You Need One?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM card built right into your smartphone’s hardware. Instead of inserting a physical chip, you simply scan a QR code or activate a profile digitally, and boom—you have instant local cellular data the second you land.
