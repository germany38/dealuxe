Comprehensive Product Overview: What Can You Buy at JTV?

JTV boasts one of the most comprehensive digital inventories in the jewelry retail industry. Their offerings span multiple distinct categories, each curated by certified gemologists and master jewelry designers:

1. Natural Loose Gemstones & Rare Minerals

For lapidaries, custom jewelry designers, and gemstone collectors, JTV is an unrivaled destination. Their loose gemstone inventory includes world-class sapphires, rubies, emeralds, amethysts, opals, topaz, garnets, and rare collector gems like alexandrite and sphene. Available in various cuts (brilliant, emerald, oval, cushion, pear) and carat weights, these loose gems allow buyers to create bespoke jewelry heirlooms.

2. Diamond Fine Jewelry (Natural & Lab-Grown)

JTV offers a breath-taking assortment of diamond jewelry, ranging from timeless diamond solitaire engagement rings and tennis bracelets to diamond stud earrings and anniversary bands. Shoppers can choose between ethically sourced natural mined diamonds and high-value lab-grown diamonds set in precious platinum, white gold, yellow gold, and rose gold.

3. Precious Metal Jewelry: Gold, Platinum, & Sterling Silver

Metals form the foundational backbone of fine jewelry design. JTV features robust collections of solid 10k, 14k, and 18k yellow, white, and rose gold chains, bangles, and pendants, alongside high-grade 925 sterling silver jewelry. Their exclusive silver collections feature protective anti-tarnish platings such as rhodium and platinum to ensure long-lasting luster and brilliance.

4. Exclusive Designer Brands & Signature Collections

JTV is home to widely acclaimed proprietary brands and designer collaborations. Popular collections include Bella Luce® (JTV’s luxury line of man-made diamond and gemstone simulants), Moissanite Fire® (exceptional lab-created moissanite with superior brilliance and fire), Artisan Gem Collection of Bali™, and Southwest Style handcrafted sterling silver and turquoise pieces.

5. Jewelry Making Supplies, Tools, & Storage Solutions

Beyond finished jewelry, JTV supports artisans, hobbyists, and jewelers with specialized jewelry-making tools, display stands, protective jewelry boxes, travel cases, cleaning solutions, and gemstone testing equipment.

Key Advantages of Shopping Online at JTV.com

Why do millions of customers trust JTV for their fine jewelry purchases? Here are the standout benefits of shopping on the JTV digital platform:

  • Direct Sourcing Value: JTV works directly with mine owners, gem cutters, and master jewelry manufacturers around the globe, eliminating unnecessary middleman markups and passing substantial savings to consumers.
  • In-House Gemological Expertise: JTV employs Graduate Gemologists certified by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA), ensuring accurate grading, authentic gemstone disclosure, and detailed product descriptions.
  • FlexPay Payment Plans: Budgeting for luxury jewelry is effortless with JTV’s FlexPay option, allowing customers to split their purchase total into convenient, interest-free monthly installments using their preferred credit card.
  • Comprehensive Customer Satisfaction Guarantee: JTV stands behind its product quality with a straightforward return policy, providing peace of mind with every purchase.
