Most gifts do one thing. Prime does many. An Amazon Prime 3-month gift membership gives someone fast, free delivery, streaming, music, gaming perks and member deals for a full season. It is useful for almost anyone, and it is easy to send. Here is what it includes and who will appreciate it most.

What Is a Prime Gift Membership?

A Prime gift membership prepays a set period of Prime for someone else. The 3-month plan covers a quarter of a year. The recipient redeems it with an Amazon account. Eligibility rules apply, especially for people who already have a membership, so check the offer page for details.

What the Recipient Gets

  • Free delivery. Eligible items ship free, with fast delivery options on many products.
  • Prime Video. Movies, series and Amazon Originals included.
  • Prime Music. Ad-free listening on selected music and playlists.
  • Prime Gaming. Free games and in-game items on supported platforms.
  • Exclusive deals. Early access to promotions and member-only pricing.
  • Reading and photos. Rotating ebooks and magazines, and photo storage.

Benefits vary by region and can change. See the offer page for the current list.

Why a 3-Month Gift Works

It is useful right away. Delivery and streaming benefits help from the first day.

It fits many occasions. Holidays, birthdays, graduations, new homes and thank-yous all work.

It is a gentle introduction. If someone has never had Prime, three months is a good way to try it.

It suits a range of budgets. It costs less than a longer plan.

It is easy to deliver. Nothing to wrap or ship.

Who Will Appreciate It Most

  • Students and young adults setting up a first place.
  • New parents who order baby supplies often.
  • Busy professionals who value fast delivery.
  • Family members far from stores.
  • Movie and TV fans who enjoy streaming.
  • Anyone who likes a deal.

Good Times to Give It

Before the holidays. Free shipping on gifts is a great help during busy seasons.

When someone moves. New homes need a lot of small purchases.

For a new baby. Diapers, wipes and essentials arrive without a trip to the store.

During a hard time. After surgery, a loss or a long illness, delivery can make life easier.

Prime Gift or Prime Trial?

A free trial is a great way for you to test Prime for yourself. A gift membership is a way to give someone else the whole experience at no stress to them. If the person is new to Prime, a gift can also show them its value before they decide to subscribe on their own.

How to Send It

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Choose the 3-month gift membership.
  3. Enter the recipient's details and a personal message.
  4. Choose a delivery date.
  5. Complete your purchase. The recipient will receive instructions to redeem it.

Tips for a Thoughtful Gift

  • Add a personal note. Mention a show or a product they would enjoy.
  • Time it well. Schedule delivery for the day of the occasion.
  • Check redemption terms. Existing members may have different options.
  • Remind them about the extras. Many people use only shipping and miss out on the rest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the recipient need an Amazon account? Yes, to redeem the gift.

Will it renew automatically? A gift membership is prepaid for its set period. See the terms for what happens afterward.

Can I choose a longer gift? Amazon offers other gift lengths. See the offer pages.

Can the recipient share it? Some benefits can be shared within a household. See Amazon's help pages.
