The Automated Landscaper Review: How Brett Pieratt’s 7-Minute Proposal System Wins Up to 3X More Jobs

Tired of driving out to properties, measuring yards, and spending your evenings typing up estimates only to lose out to a cheaper competitor? Discover how an award-winning C-27 contractor is using a breakthrough AI proposal system to close bigger contracts without dropping prices.

Let’s be honest about what the life of a residential landscape contractor actually looks like. You spend an hour driving out to a prospective client’s property. You spend another 45 minutes walking the yard, taking measurements, listening intently as the homeowner describes the custom patio or retaining wall they have envisioned in their head.

You snap a few photos on your phone, shake hands, and promise to put a comprehensive proposal together. Then you head home, eat dinner with one eye fixed on your laptop screen, and find yourself sitting at the kitchen table at 9:00 PM still hammering away at line items, calculating square footage, factoring in materials, and trying to arrive at a price that covers your crew’s wages while still leaving enough profit on the bone.

You finally email the estimate over. And then you wait.

Three days pass. You follow up with a polite phone call or text. Radio silence. A full week drags by, and you reach out again—careful not to sound too eager. Finally, an email hits your inbox: “Thanks so much for your time, but we decided to go with someone else. Your price was a little higher than we had hoped.”

That is the exact moment frustration sets in. Because you know your work is superior. You know the contractor who underbid you is going to show up in a beat-up truck, rush the installation, cut corners on base preparation, and be completely unresponsive when things settle in two years. But the homeowner never saw that distinction. To them, they were simply looking at two pieces of paper with numbers at the bottom.

Your proposal said $8,400. The other guy’s proposal said $6,900. And just like that, every hour you invested in the lead—the drive, the consultation, the late-night estimation work—vanished into thin air. If this scenario sounds painfully familiar, you are caught in what industry veteran Brett Pieratt calls The Comparison Trap. And more leads won't fix it. You need a completely different way to sell.

Understanding the Comparison Trap: Why Lower Prices Aren't the Answer

When price is the only variable a customer can clearly differentiate between two competing bids, price naturally becomes the sole deciding factor. It is not because the homeowner is malicious or inherently cheap; it is because they have been handed two opaque documents that look virtually identical on the surface. When faced with a choice they don't fully understand, human nature dictates choosing the smaller number.

For nearly two decades, landscape contractor Brett Pieratt—owner of San Diego Landscape Solutions—faced this exact dilemma. As a licensed C-27 contractor who started with a single Honda mower in 2000 and built a multi-award-winning design-build firm in Coronado, California, Pieratt refused to join the race to the bottom. He knew that being the cheapest contractor in town is simply a slow march toward bankruptcy.

You are ultimately left with two terrible choices: slash your prices and eat into your margins until your business bleeds cash, or hold your ground and watch your closing rate plummet. But what if you could bypass the comparison trap entirely? What if you could present a proposal so compelling, visual, and professional that the homeowner stops looking at price as the primary metric?
