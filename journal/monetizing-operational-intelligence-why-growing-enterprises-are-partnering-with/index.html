Does Your Organization Qualify? The Core Criteria

Unlike massive conglomerates with rigid bureaucracies or micro-startups still figuring out their foundational processes, mid-market businesses possess the optimal maturity profile for advanced AI training. micro1 specifically looks for organizations meeting these enterprise baseline benchmarks:

  • Team Size: Between 30 and 200 employees, ensuring a dynamic yet structured operational scale.
  • Operational Maturity: Strong internal documentation, repeatable playbooks, and well-defined workflows.
  • Modern Tool Stack: Active utilization of cloud software, CRMs, and structured task-execution histories.

"Many companies possess highly valuable operational intelligence without realizing it. Organizations that built modern business workflows should participate directly in the value created by frontier AI systems."

What Types of Data Drive Value?

AI models cannot learn real-world business acumen from generic textbooks or synthetic code. They require authentic execution artifacts generated by professional teams across industries such as financial services, legal compliance, BPOs, consulting, healthcare administration, and technology.

Relevant data contributions include:

  • Internal knowledge bases, SOPs, and structured playbooks
  • Project management workflows and operational frameworks
  • Task execution histories and CRM metadata
  • Specialized templates and business process documentation

Uncompromising Security and Data Governance

A primary concern for any leadership team evaluating data partnerships is data security and competitive protection. micro1’s program is architected from the ground up with enterprise-grade safeguards:

  1. Strict Confidentiality: All sensitive information and proprietary customer identifiers are completely scrubbed before any downstream use.
  2. Temporary Processing: Original datasets are used exclusively for training and evaluation purposes, then securely deleted.
  3. Absolute Ownership: Partner companies retain full ownership of their underlying data assets—nothing is ever publicly shared or exposed.

Rewarding Engagement Built for Busy Teams

Participation is structured to ensure zero disruption to core business activities. micro1 handles all coordination, anonymization, and program management. In return, selected partners receive significant compensation scaling with dataset volume, workflow complexity, and domain expertise.
