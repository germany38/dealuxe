At the very heart of the PandaOffice lineup is Drecov, a professional-grade data recovery solution boasting an industry-leading 99.8% success rate. 1. USB Drive and External Storage Recovery

USB flash drives are notorious for sudden corruption, accidental formatting, and file system errors due to improper ejection. Drecov shines brightly in this arena, easily recovering deleted documents, compressed archives, and media files from any flash drive brand on the market without demanding deep technical knowledge.

2. SSD and HDD Partition Recovery

Losing an entire partition due to accidental reformatting or partition table corruption can cause instant panic. Drecov scans deep sectors of internal and external storage drives to reconstruct lost directory trees, recovering lost documents, photos, audio files, and high-definition video archives safely while preserving device performance integrity.

3. SD Card and Digital Camera Recovery

Photographers and videographers know the heartbreak of getting an "Error Card" notification while on location. Drecov specializes in deep-scanning SD cards used in DSLR cameras, drones, and action cams, pulling raw image files, high-bitrate video clips, and audio tracks cleanly off compromised media cards.

Drepair: Smart File Repair for Photos, Videos, Documents, and Audio

Recovering a file is only half the battle; sometimes, files are successfully extracted from a damaged drive only to find that the file itself is corrupted, blurry, pixelated, or unplayable. This is where Drepair steps in as an indispensable lifesaver.

Photo Repair

Corrupted or half-rendered JPG, PNG, or RAW photo files can ruin treasured memories. Drepair utilizes advanced image-stitching and pixel-analysis algorithms to restore blurry, damaged, or partially missing photos back to crystal-clear high quality.

Video Repair

Choppy playback, missing codecs, unplayable headers, and freezing video timelines can ruin hours of recorded footage. Drepair fixes damaged or corrupted video files across multiple formats, allowing you to relive your important moments without frustrating playback errors.

Document and Audio Repair

Accidentally corrupted Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and PDF files can stall critical business operations. Drepair scans broken document structures to recover text, formatting, and tables with remarkable accuracy. Similarly, distorted or corrupted audio files are repaired to deliver crisp, clear sound.
