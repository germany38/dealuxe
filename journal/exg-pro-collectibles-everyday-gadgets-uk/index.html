EXG Pro: Collectibles and Everyday Gadgets for UK Shoppers

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Some people want a display piece that also earns its place on the desk or shelf. EXG Pro is a UK brand in the toys and games sector that brings together licensed collectibles and practical everyday products. It is aimed at adults who still enjoy gaming, collecting and pop culture, a group often called ‘kidults’. If that describes you, it is worth a look at what the brand offers and how it might fit your interests.

Who the range is aimed at

EXG Pro describes its customers as adults who are passionate about games, collectibles and pop culture. That may suit someone who plays PC or video games regularly, keeps a shelf of favourite characters, or enjoys hunting for items that link fandom with something useful. It can also suit anyone buying a present for a fan whose tastes are well known to them, because a collectible or gadget linked to a favourite franchise is often easier to choose than something generic.

What falls under the brand’s categories

The brand’s listed sub-sectors cover a broad mix: toys and games, PC and video games, computers, gadgets, home and garden, gifts and flowers, and a general entertainment superstore format. In practice, this means you may find items that sit across several of your interests, rather than a single narrow niche. Before ordering, it helps to decide which category you are really shopping for, as that will shape what you compare. A display figure, a desk accessory and a computer peripheral each need a different set of checks.

When you are ready to look at the current selection, you can browse the official UK website and filter by category to see what is available in your area.

Checking licensed products before you buy

Licensed collectibles are only as useful as the details behind them, so read the product page carefully. Look for clear information about the franchise or licence, the materials, the dimensions and any age guidance. For a collectible, check the size against the space you have in mind. For a gadget, confirm which devices or systems it is compatible with, and read the care or safety notes if they are provided.

It is also sensible to review the delivery and returns information on the site before you place an order. Check estimated delivery times, who pays return postage if an item is not right, and how any damage on arrival should be reported. If something is unclear, contact the brand’s customer service team before buying rather than after.

Comparing gadgets and collectibles sensibly

A few simple questions can make comparison easier:

  • Is this mainly for display, daily use, or both?
  • Does it fit the space, desk or setup where it will live?
  • Are the device compatibility and power requirements clearly stated?
  • Does the price reflect the level of detail, finish and packaging shown on the page?
  • Would you still want it in six months, or is it a short-term impulse buy?

Comparing the same item across a few retailers is also worthwhile, as stock levels and delivery terms can differ. Keep the product code or name to hand so you can check like-for-like.

Buying gifts for fans

If you are shopping for someone else, start with what they already own and play. A collectible from a series they follow, or a practical accessory for their gaming or workspace, is usually a safer choice than guessing at a new hobby. If you are unsure about sizing or compatibility, check the product details first or ask the brand directly. Allow enough time for delivery, particularly if the gift has a set date.

Whether you are after a single display piece or a practical everyday item with a bit of personality, the EXG Pro website is the best place to see the full details for yourself. Take your time, compare the specifics and buy the items that genuinely suit how you plan to use them.
