Exploring WAU Global’s Core Product Categories

WAU Global's extensive catalog is structured to address every dimension of personal care, offering targeted solutions for diverse skin types, hair textures, and health needs:

1. Advanced Face Rejuvenation & Phototherapy Devices

The centerpiece of WAU’s skincare innovation is its LED phototherapy series. The flagship LED MASK 3.0 is a wireless 3D phototherapy mask that utilizes multiple visible light spectrums paired with near-infrared wavelengths to penetrate deep dermal layers. This non-invasive treatment stimulates cellular regeneration, boosts collagen production, reduces fine lines, and evens out pigmentation. Complementing the mask are targeted facial devices such as the MIRA microcurrent anti-aging sculptor, designed to lift facial contours and tone underlying facial muscles.

2. Innovative Plasma & RF Technology

For individuals seeking advanced dermatological rejuvenation without injections or downtime, WAU offers specialized devices like the NEO Plasma Technology tool and multi-technology complexes such as VIA GOLD. These tools combine RF warming, EMS stimulation, and plasma energy to refine skin texture, minimize pore appearance, and combat stubborn signs of aging.

3. Hair Care, Scalp Stimulation & Cordless Styling

Healthy hair starts at the scalp. WAU’s ATAMA Scalp Massager uses gentle vibrations and targeted stimulation to enhance blood circulation, supporting natural hair growth and hair follicle health. For daily styling, tools like the KARU Multistyler, YUTO+ modern diffuser dryer, and the KINU cordless straightener combine intelligent heat control with ergonomic mobility to preserve hair shine and protect against thermal damage.

4. Body Sculpting & Professional Oral Care

WAU extends its technological philosophy to full-body care with the KIO body massager and myostimulator, engineered to tone muscles and reduce tension. Meanwhile, their oral hygiene lineup—featuring the MORI 2.0 sonic toothbrush and MIGA series portable irrigators—delivers deep plaque removal and gum care to maintain a healthy, bright smile at home.
