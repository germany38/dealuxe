The Ultimate Guide to Premium Beachwear, Surf Gear, and Coastal Style | Beyond the Beach

There is something inherently liberating about the coastal lifestyle. Whether it is the surge of adrenaline as you catch an early morning wave, the calming rhythm of ocean tides during a sunset walk, or the effortless leisure of a resort getaway, living life near the water demands a specialized wardrobe. Coastal fashion is no longer restricted to simple swim trunks or basic tees; today, it represents a sophisticated fusion of high-performance technical engineering, active UV protection, sustainable textiles, and elevated design aesthetics.

At the forefront of this global movement is Beyond the Beach, a premier retail destination dedicated to curating the world’s most trusted surf, swim, and coastal lifestyle brands. Designed for active adventurers, water sports enthusiasts, and style-conscious families alike, Beyond the Beach delivers an unparalleled multi-brand shopping experience. In this comprehensive guide, we explore the essentials of modern beach culture, the flagship brands shaping coastal fashion, and how to elevate your outdoor adventures with top-tier gear.

1. The Evolution of Beachwear: From Basic Swimsuits to Technical Apparel

Historically, swimwear and beach attire were purely functional items worn during brief visits to the shore. However, over the last few decades, the line between athletic wear, casual street fashion, and beachwear has blurred completely. Modern beachgoers expect versatility—apparel that seamlessly transitions from an intense morning surf session to a relaxed seaside café or a luxury yacht excursion.

Key technological advances in beachwear include:

  • Four-Way Stretch Fabrics: High-performance boardshorts utilize elastane-blend fabrics that move dynamically with the body, eliminating friction and rash during paddling or water sports.
  • UPF Sun Protection: Advanced rashguards and activewear now incorporate UPF 50+ protection directly into the weave, blocking up to 98% of harmful ultraviolet rays without relying on chemical sprays.
  • Hydrophobic Quick-Dry Coatings: Innovative fabric treatments ensure that garments shed water rapidly, keeping wearers dry, lightweight, and comfortable within minutes of leaving the water.
  • Eco-Conscious Materials: Leading global brands increasingly utilize recycled ocean plastics, regenerated nylon (Econyl), and organic cottons to reduce environmental footprints and protect ocean ecosystems.

Pro Tip: When selecting water sports gear, always look for flatlock stitching and seamless underarm panels. These technical construction details prevent chafing during long hours in salt water.

2. World-Class Brands Curated in One Destination

One of the primary advantages of shopping with Beyond the Beach is access to an elite collection of world-renowned ocean and lifestyle heritage brands. Instead of navigating dozens of separate stores, shoppers can access iconic names under one roof.

Oakley

Famous worldwide for optical innovation, Oakley provides high-performance eyewear engineered to handle extreme sunlight and glare reflections off water. Their PRIXM™ lens technology enhances color contrast and clarity, making them indispensable for boaters, surfers, and outdoor athletes.

Rip Curl & Quiksilver

True titans of authentic surf culture. Built by surfers for surfers, Rip Curl and Quiksilver offer industry-standard wetsuits, technical boardshorts, and rugged outdoor accessories tested in the world's heaviest breaks.

Billabong & Roxy

Combining functional performance with vibrant artistic designs, Billabong and Roxy embody the classic beach lifestyle. From flattering bikinis and protective springsuits to breezy linen dresses and woven accessories, these brands capture coastal bohemian elegance.

Seafolly & Tommy Bahama

For those who prefer refined relaxation, Seafolly brings premium Australian swimwear design focused on flawless fit and structural support, while Tommy Bahama offers island-inspired luxury resort wear crafted from premium silks and linens.

Hurley & Insta360

Hurley blends athletic innovation with street style, offering modern compression gear and lightweight boardshorts. To capture every action-packed moment on the water, Insta360 provides cutting-edge waterproof action cameras designed to record 360-degree high-definition video in extreme environments.

3. Complete Family Style: Men, Women, and Kids

A true ocean-focused brand recognizes that coastal living is a family affair. Beyond the Beach offers specialized product ranges meticulously categorized to ensure every member of the family is fully equipped.

Men’s Collection

From high-performance boardshorts and technical rashguards to everyday graphic tees, hybrid shorts, polaroid-lens sunglasses, and durable sandals, the men’s range covers everything needed for surfing, training, or weekend lounging.

Women’s Collection

Offering an extensive range of bikinis, one-piece swimsuits, beach cover-ups, sun hats, activewear, and stylish footwear, the women’s section prioritizes both aesthetic elegance and structural support for active water sports.

Kids & Youth Apparel

Sun safety is paramount for young adventurers. The kids’ collections feature bright, high-visibility UPF 50+ swim shirts, durable swim trunks, protective sun hats, and water-friendly sandals designed to withstand rigorous play on sand and rocks.

4. Specialized Curated Style Collections

To make finding your exact look simple, Beyond the Beach organizes products into themed lifestyle capsules:

  • Pro Surf: Hardcore gear including high-stretch rashguards, durable wetsuits, traction pads, and performance eyewear designed for serious watermen and waterwomen.
  • Seaside Luxe: High-end resort fashion, designer swimsuits, silk cover-ups, and luxury accessories tailored for yachting, poolside relaxation, and five-star coastal retreats.
  • Coastal Casual: Everyday street-to-beach fashion featuring comfortable cotton tees, relaxed linen trousers, casual caps, and comfortable footwear for daily errands or coastal walks.
  • Style Ready: On-trend seasonal outfits and bold patterns designed to keep your vacation photos looking sharp and stylish.
