Amazon Wedding Registry: Build Your Gift List With Ease

Planning a wedding means making hundreds of decisions. Your gift list should not be one of the hard ones. An Amazon Wedding Registry gathers everything you want for your life together in one place and gives guests an easy way to choose a present. Here is how it works and how to build a registry you will love.

What Is an Amazon Wedding Registry?

An Amazon Wedding Registry is a free tool for couples to create a list of gifts. You add items from Amazon, and in some cases from other stores, share the list with guests, and they choose what to buy. Amazon has offered perks for registry owners, such as completion discounts. Perks and terms change, so confirm the current ones on the offer page.

Why Use an Amazon Registry?

A huge selection. From kitchen appliances to bedding to tools and decor, you can find nearly anything you need in one place.

It is easy for guests. Most people already shop on Amazon, so buying from your list is simple.

It prevents duplicates. Gifts are marked off as purchased.

You can mix price points. Include small gifts, big ones and group-gift options.

It works for any kind of couple. Whether you are starting from scratch or merging two homes, you decide what you need.

What to Add to Your Registry

  • Kitchen: cookware, knives, small appliances and dishes.
  • Bedroom and bath: bedding, towels and storage.
  • Living space: lamps, throw blankets, furniture and decor.
  • Home basics: vacuum, tools, cleaning supplies and organizers.
  • Experiences and extras: items for hobbies, travel or entertaining.
  • Upgrades: the things you would love to have but would not buy yourselves.

Tips for a Registry Guests Will Love

  1. Start early and add steadily. A few items each week is easier than a single long session.
  2. Add many price points. Guests have different budgets.
  3. Choose items you will use. Think about daily life together.
  4. Read reviews. Check ratings before you add big items.
  5. Include a few higher-priced items. Guests may pool together for them.
  6. Keep it current. Update the list as your needs change.
  7. Share the link everywhere. Add it to your wedding website and invitations.

How to Create Yours

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Enter your names and wedding details.
  4. Add at least one item to your registry.
  5. Share it with your guests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free to create? Yes. You only pay for items you decide to buy.

Can we both manage it? Registries can generally be shared with a partner. Check the settings.

Can guests find it easily? Guests can usually search for your names or use your link.

Can we add items from other stores? Amazon has offered tools for this. See the offer page.

Start Your Registry

Your wedding is about the two of you. Your registry should make life easier, not harder. Create yours, add the things you want, and share it with the people who want to celebrate with you.
