What Does Italo Jewelry Sell? Explore Their Core Collections

Italo Jewelry boasts a diverse, beautifully curated inventory designed to cater to every taste, style preference, and milestone event. Their core product categories include:

1. Engagement Rings & Bridal Sets

The cornerstone of Italo's collection features magnificent engagement rings, including classic solitaires, halo settings, vintage-inspired art deco styles, and 3-piece wedding ring sets. Utilizing brilliant center stones like moissanite and created white sapphires, these rings replicate the exact fire and brilliance of mined diamonds at a fraction of the cost.

2. Wedding Bands & Eternity Rings

From sleek minimalist metal bands to dazzling multi-row eternity bands and channel-set diamond alternatives, Italo provides perfect companion pieces designed to stack seamlessly with engagement rings or shine brilliantly on their own.

3. Tennis Bracelets & Fine Bangles

Renowned for elegance, their tennis bracelet collection features round, baguette, and alternating colored gemstone layouts. Crafted securely in silver and gold finishes, these bracelets add instant sophistication to any outfit.

4. Necklaces, Pendants & Earrings

Complete any look with Italo's stunning assortment of drop earrings, classic studs, hoop earrings, and personalized pendant necklaces featuring vibrant ruby, emerald, aquamarine, and sapphire stones.
