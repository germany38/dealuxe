Elevating Music and Space: The Scandinavian Artistry of Openhagen

Music is fundamentally an emotional experience, a personal expression that bridges abstract creativity and tangible sound. Yet for millions of musicians, song-writers, and home recording enthusiasts worldwide, the physical items that support their art—such as guitar stands, wall hangers, and audio accessory mounts—have historically lacked artistic soul. Traditional music gear stands have long been dominated by utilitarian metal tubes, black foam padding, and clunky folding joints designed purely for backstages or rehearsal studios rather than thoughtfully decorated homes, creative lounges, or modern working environments.

Enter Openhagen, a pioneering Danish design brand that has transformed how instruments and music accessories exist within our personal living spaces. By merging authentic Scandinavian design principles with functional ergonomics, Openhagen crafts premium display furniture, wall mounts, and acoustic innovations that treat instruments not merely as equipment, but as sculptural art pieces worthy of central display.

The Philosophy of Nordic Minimalism in Music Accessories

At the core of Openhagen’s philosophy lies a commitment to clean lines, organic materials, and intentional simplicity. The brand operates on a powerful realization: when an instrument is stored away out of sight inside a heavy gig bag or tucked into a closet corner, it is played far less often. Conversely, when a guitar or ukulele rests gracefully on a beautifully crafted wall mount or floor stand in a living room, studio, or office, it invites spontaneous creativity and daily practice.

Scandinavian design has long been celebrated for its warmth, light wood tones, and functional restraint. Openhagen incorporates these principles into every product, selecting solid oak, rich walnut, and anodized aluminum to build accessories that harmoniously integrate into Scandinavian, modern mid-century, minimalist, and eclectic interior styles. Rather than competing for visual attention, Openhagen designs frame the instrument softly, allowing the unique craftsmanship of guitars, basses, and ukuleles to take center stage.

The Flagship Collections: Where Engineering Meets Aesthetic Elegance

1. HangWithMe Guitar & Instrument Wall Mounts

One of Openhagen’s signature creations is the HangWithMe wall mount series. Unlike traditional wall hangers with prominent metal arms and visible rubber padding, HangWithMe is designed with a patented folding mechanism. When an instrument is hanging, the mount provides a secure, gentle hold using natural oiled cork padding that safeguards delicate lacquer finishes. When the instrument is removed for a session, the arms gently fold up flush against the wall, turning the mount itself into an elegant, discreet wooden wall ornament.

Crafted from real hardwoods including natural oak, dark walnut, and stained finishes, HangWithMe accommodates both symmetrical and asymmetrical headstocks for electric guitars, acoustic instruments, basses, and ukuleles. It completely redefines the wall-mounting experience, replacing industrial hardware with refined architectural decor.

2. NordicStand & Freestanding Instrument Furniture

For artists who prefer floor solutions or frequently move between recording environments, the NordicStand series offers a masterclass in structural innovation. Engineered from lightweight anodized aluminum coupled with sustainably harvested wood and non-slip cork cradles, these collapsible floor stands fold down in seconds for high portability without sacrificing balance or elegance.

Weighing under a kilogram yet supporting heavy solid-body electric basses and vintage acoustic dreadnoughts, NordicStand provides high stability with a low visual footprint. It brings a calm, composed presence to living rooms, sound stages, and home production studios alike.

3. The Harmoni Acoustic Experience

Pushing innovation even further, Openhagen reimagined the guitar itself with the release of Harmoni. Designed in Denmark with a solid mahogany body, Harmoni integrates self-standing magnetic display legs crafted from the exact same wood stock as the guitar body. This eliminates the need for any separate external stand altogether, creating an self-contained musical sculpture that balances upright on any flat surface while remaining completely flush during play.

Designed for Daily Inspiration and Protection

Protecting valuable vintage or custom-shop instruments requires rigorous attention to material safety. Many traditional stands use chemical-heavy rubbers or synthetic pads that can react negatively with nitrocellulose lacquer finishes, causing permanent discoloration. Openhagen replaces synthetic rubbers with natural oiled cork lining, offering a safe, non-abrasive cushion that grips instruments securely while respecting sensitive finishes.

Furthermore, Openhagen extends its design language to desktop audio accessories, including sculptural solid wood headphone stands, precision-cut wooden guitar picks, and ambient lifestyle equipment. Each item helps reduce clutter in creative workspaces, maintaining an atmosphere of calm, focused inspiration.

Sustainability and Craftsmanship

Modern consumers and creators value ethical production as much as visual beauty. Openhagen places environmental stewardship at the forefront of its manufacturing model, utilizing sustainably sourced timbers and eco-friendly finishes. By creating durable, timeless designs that outlast disposable plastic accessories, Openhagen actively contributes to a culture of long-term utility and mindful consumption.

"Our goal is to create products that honor your passion and your living space, turning everyday instruments into timeless interior art."

Transforming Creative Spaces Worldwide

Whether you are a professional touring musician, an apartment-dwelling hobbyist, an interior designer curating a luxury residence, or someone seeking the perfect gift for a music lover, Openhagen offers an unparalleled fusion of functionality and Nordic aesthetic brilliance. By eliminating the friction between instrument storage and home decor, Openhagen ensures that music remains an active, visible, and inspiring part of daily life.
