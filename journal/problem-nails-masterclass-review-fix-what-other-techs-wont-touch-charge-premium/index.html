Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this course suitable for beginner nail technicians?

No. This is an advanced-level masterclass. It assumes you are already confident with e-file removal, cuticle work, and gel leveling. Graduates of standard shape courses will get the most value out of this material.

2. What is polygel and why is it used here?

Polygel is a hybrid material—thicker than traditional gel and more workable than acrylic. It maintains structural integrity without collapsing, allowing technicians to rebuild missing sidewalls and corners "in the air" before curing.

3. Are these appointments faster than regular manicures?

Initially, no. A first-time hooked nail reconstruction can take 90 minutes or longer. However, as your technical workflow speeds up, you can command significantly higher hourly earnings due to the specialized nature of the service.
