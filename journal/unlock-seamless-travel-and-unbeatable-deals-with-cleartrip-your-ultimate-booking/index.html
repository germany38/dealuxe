The Evolution of Modern Travel Planning with Cleartrip

Founded with a vision to make travel simple, clear, and transparent, Cleartrip has grown into one of the most trusted travel aggregators in the industry. Backed by cutting-edge technology and a deep understanding of traveler behavior, the platform connects users to over 400 airlines, hundreds of thousands of hotels globally, and countless activities. Core Offerings: What Can You Book on Cleartrip?

Cleartrip is a comprehensive travel ecosystem tailored to cater to every kind of traveler. Let us break down the primary services available on the platform:

  • Domestic and International Flights: Compare airfares across leading low-cost carriers and full-service international airlines instantly. With dedicated filters for baggage allowance, layovers, and flight durations, finding the most efficient route has never been easier.

Standout Features That Set Cleartrip Apart

What truly elevates Cleartrip above standard booking engines are its innovative, customer-centric features designed to protect your wallet and your peace of mind:

1. Groundbreaking "Price Trends" Feature

One of the greatest anxieties in travel booking is timing your purchase. Do you buy now, or will prices drop tomorrow? Cleartrip solved this dilemma by introducing its innovative Price Trends feature. This tool analyzes up to 30 days of historical fare data and surfaces dynamic booking signals directly on your search results page for flights booked up to 60 days in advance. It provides immediate data-driven insight into whether you should finalize your booking instantly or wait out potential price movements.

2. Flexibility with Flexifly and EzCancel

Life is unpredictable, and travel plans can change at a moment's notice. Cleartrip accommodates this reality through flexible booking additions like Flexifly and easy cancellation policies. Flexifly allows travelers to reschedule their flights without heavy penalties—you simply pay any applicable fare difference. This level of adaptability ensures you can book early to grab low-cost promotional rates without the fear of losing your investment if your schedule shifts.

3. Cleartrip for Work: Corporate Travel Simplified

For business owners, startup founders, and corporate finance managers, business travel administration is often a logistical headache. Cleartrip for Work is a specialized portal built specifically for GST-registered businesses and teams of all sizes. It unlocks exclusive corporate fares (often significantly lower than retail prices), provides free cancellations on eligible fares, automatically generates GST-compliant invoices for effortless input tax credit claims, and eliminates subscription fees or hidden paperwork.

Booking smart means never paying full retail price. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How can I find the cheapest flights on Cleartrip?

To secure the lowest airfares, utilize Cleartrip's Price Trends feature to evaluate fare history, book domestic flights 3 to 6 weeks in advance (and international flights 6 to 10 weeks ahead), and always check the active offers page for bank promo codes before completing your payment.

2. Does Cleartrip offer customer support if my travel plans change?

Yes. Cleartrip provides dedicated 24/7 customer support to help travelers navigate flight delays, cancellations, rescheduling inquiries, and booking adjustments.

3. Can I use Cleartrip for corporate or business bookings?

Absolutely. Through "Cleartrip for Work," businesses can register using their GSTIN for free to unlock corporate rates, automated GST invoices, and specialized team travel management dashboards.
