What is World of Tanks?

At its core, World of Tanks is a free-to-play 15v15 tactical vehicle shooter. Players take control of historically accurate and prototype armored fighting vehicles spanning from the early 20th century through the Cold War era. Battles take place across dozens of meticulously designed maps representing varied landscapes—from frozen Siberian tundras and scorched African deserts to ruined European cities and lush farmland countryside.

Unlike fast-paced arena shooters that rely purely on twitch reflexes, World of Tanks prioritizes positional awareness, camouflage exploitation, team coordination, armor angling, and precise shot placement. Victory requires understanding your vehicle's strengths and weaknesses while reading the flow of the battle across dynamic terrain.

Understanding the 5 Vehicle Classes

To succeed on the battlefield, every player must understand the tactical roles played by the five distinct tank classes available in World of Tanks:

1. Light Tanks – The Eyes of the Team

Light tanks are fast, agile, and maneuverable. Equipped with superior view ranges and concealment values that remain active even while moving, light tanks serve as scouts. Their primary role is to spot enemy movements, light up targets for allied snipers, and flank isolated opponents.

2. Medium Tanks – Versatile All-Rounders

Medium tanks balance speed, armor, and firepower. Flexible and adaptable, they can push flanking lanes, support heavy pushes, defend compromised bases, or form wolfpacks with allied mediums to overrun enemy defenses.

3. Heavy Tanks – The Frontline Anchors

Heavy tanks feature thick armor, high durability pool, and high-caliber guns. Designed to lead front-line assaults and hold key choke points, heavy tanks rely on side-scraping, hull-down positioning, and armor angling to bounce enemy shells.

4. Tank Destroyers – Deadly Long-Range Snipers

Tank destroyers are specialized ambush predators. Bearing devastating firepower and high penetration cannons, they often sacrifice turret rotation or mobility to lay in wait from concealed positions, punishing overextended enemies.

5. Self-Propelled Guns (SPGs) – Artillery Support

SPGs offer overhead strategic fire support across the entire map. By firing high-explosive shells in high arcs over cover, artillery stuns enemy formations, breaks defensive standoffs, and forces heavily armored enemies out of position.

Key Gameplay Mechanics That Define Victory

  • Armor Angling & Auto-Ricochet: Angling your vehicle's armor relative to incoming fire increases the effective armor thickness, causing shells to bounce harmlessly.
  • Vision & Camouflage System: Understanding line-of-sight, bush concealment, and view range circles on the minimap allows you to strike without being spotted.
  • Crew Skills & Perks: Crew experience boosts vehicle efficiency, providing crucial advantages such as Sixth Sense (spotting indicator), Repairs, and Snap Shot.
  • Equipment & Modules: Customize your vehicles with Rammers, Turbochargers, Coated Optics, and Hardening to tailor performance to your playstyle.
