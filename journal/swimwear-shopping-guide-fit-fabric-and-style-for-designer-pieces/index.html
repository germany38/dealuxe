Swimwear Shopping Guide: Fit, Fabric and Style for Designer Pieces

Swimwear is one of the hardest things to buy. It has to fit closely, stay put in motion and survive sun, salt and chlorine. Veronika Vilim is a New York-based designer swimwear brand featuring custom hardware, recycled fabrics and futuristic silhouettes, aimed at customers who want unique, elevated pieces.

Know what you need

Will you swim laps, lounge by the pool or wear it on holiday? Active use calls for secure straps and supportive cuts. Lounge pieces can be more fashion-forward.

Fit essentials

  1. Measure yourself and compare to the size guide, since sizing varies by brand.
  2. Check coverage and support in the bust, waist and hips.
  3. Look at strap and closure design, which affects comfort and security.
  4. Allow for stretch, but avoid pieces that dig in.

Fabric and sustainability

Recycled fabrics reduce reliance on new materials and can perform well in water. Look for quality indicators such as lining, stretch recovery and colourfastness.

Hardware and details

Custom hardware adds style but should be comfortable and secure. Check that metal parts are well finished and consider whether they suit your use.

Care

Rinse after swimming, avoid wringing, dry flat in the shade and avoid hot water and tumble drying to preserve elasticity.

Choosing a style for your activity

Different activities call for different designs. For swimming laps, look for secure cuts with minimal movement. For beach days and lounging, you can prioritise style and coverage preferences. If you plan to be active in the water, test the fit by moving your arms and bending as you would while swimming.

Mixing and matching

Separates give you flexibility because you can choose different sizes for the top and bottom and combine colours. Check whether pieces from the same collection are designed to coordinate. Neutral shades are easy to pair, while bold pieces can anchor a simple holiday wardrobe.

Caring for hardware and fabric

Metal or custom hardware should be rinsed with fresh water after contact with salt or chlorine. Avoid leaving swimwear in direct sun when wet, and keep it away from rough surfaces that could snag the fabric. With careful handling, quality swimwear holds its shape and colour through many seasons.

Common mistakes

Ignoring sizing guides. Buying without checking returns. Leaving swimwear wet in a bag.

FAQ

How long does swimwear last? With good care, several seasons. Can I mix and match pieces? Often, but sizes may differ between tops and bottoms.

Should I wash swimwear after every wear? Yes. A quick rinse in cool, fresh water removes salt, chlorine and sunscreen that can break down fabric. Can I wear it for sport? Check whether the design is intended for activity, since fashion-led pieces may offer less support during vigorous swimming.

Conclusion

Choose pieces that fit, feel good and match your use. If distinctive designer swimwear appeals, look at Veronika Vilim's collection.
