LinkShortener.io: Short Links and QR Codes for UK Businesses

Long web addresses are hard to read, easy to mistype and awkward to fit onto a flyer, a shop window or a slide. Short links and QR codes solve that problem neatly, and LinkShortener.io is one service that offers both in one place, with a free plan to help you get started.

What the service offers

LinkShortener.io creates short links that send people to a longer destination page, and QR codes that a phone camera can scan to open that same page. This is handy for printed material such as posters, menus, business cards and product packaging, where a shorter address is easier for people to type or scan. It also helps when you want a tidy link to share in an email signature, a newsletter or a social media post.

Who it tends to suit

Small businesses, freelancers, event organisers and marketing teams are typical users of link shorteners, particularly when they share links across several channels and want to see them in one place. If you only send the occasional link, a free option may be all you need. If you are building a brand and want links that look consistent with your website, it is worth thinking about whether a custom domain matters to you.

Free plan or branded domain?

A free plan is a sensible way to test whether a tool fits your workflow before you commit any money. The branded domain option, which the brand lists from $13 a month, lets your short links use your own domain name rather than a generic one. Before paying, consider how many links you publish each month, whether your audience would notice and trust a branded address, and whether the extra cost sits comfortably within your budget. Prices can change, so check the current plans on the official website before making a decision.

What to check before you sign up

  • Pricing clarity: The brand describes its pricing as clear and states that there are no overage fees. Still, read the plan details so you understand what each tier includes.
  • Where the team is based: LinkShortener.io describes itself as having a UK-based team, which may matter if you prefer working with a provider in your own time zone and legal environment.
  • Your printed and shared links: Think about how you will manage QR codes that have already been printed, and whether you will need to keep the same destination for a long time.
  • Your team's needs: If several people share one account, check how access and link management would work for them.

Software and online tools change over time, so treat any single review, including this one, as a starting point rather than a final verdict. Compare two or three options against your actual needs, and make sure you understand how your data and links are handled.

Making the decision

If you need short links and QR codes for business use, LinkShortener.io is worth a look, especially if you want to start on a free plan and consider a branded domain later. Visit the LinkShortener.io website to review the plans, compare them with your current setup and decide what suits your business.
