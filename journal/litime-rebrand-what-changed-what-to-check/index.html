If you have worked with Ampere Time before, you may notice a new name when you visit the company's online presence. The business has rebranded as LiTime and moved to litime.com. The company describes the change as part of its continuing development and evolution, so it is worth understanding what has actually changed before you update your records or sign anything new.

What the rebrand actually changed

The core change is the name. Ampere Time is now LiTime, and the website address has moved to litime.com. The announcement was addressed to the company's existing community as well as to newer contacts, which signals that the company wants current customers and partners to follow the transition rather than start over. Based on the information published, the rebrand is a change of name and web address, and it is presented as a step in the company's ongoing development.

Why a name change matters for business buyers

For a business customer, a name change can affect more than a logo. Vendor records, purchase orders, invoices, and saved login details may still reference the old name. Before you pay an invoice or renew a service, check that the company name on the document matches the name in the announcement. If something does not line up, ask the vendor in writing to confirm the entity name, billing details, and where to send payments.

  • Update your vendor list and accounting software with the new name.
  • Save the new web address and check that bookmarks point to the correct site.
  • Keep copies of contracts and quotes issued under the old name.
  • Ask for written confirmation if your terms or contacts have changed.

How to evaluate LiTime as a B2B provider

Whether a business services company suits you depends on your needs, so start with a clear list of requirements. Think about the scope of work, the delivery timeline, how support is handled, and what documentation you will receive. Compare these points across providers rather than relying on a brand name alone. You can review LiTime's offerings directly on its official website, where service details and contact options are the best place to start.

When you request a quote, ask for a written scope that lists deliverables, responsibilities on both sides, and any fees that may apply. A short call or email exchange can reveal a lot about how responsive a provider will be once the work begins.

Practical checks before you commit

Rebrands are a good moment to double-check details. Look for an official announcement on the company's own site, confirm that the web address you are using is the one listed by the company, and be cautious with messages that ask for payment through unfamiliar channels. Read the terms of service, cancellation policy, and privacy notice, since these may have been updated alongside the new name.

It is also reasonable to ask for references or case examples relevant to your industry. If a provider cannot or will not share enough information for you to make an informed decision, that is useful to know before you sign.

Final thoughts

The move from Ampere Time to LiTime is a name and website change, presented as part of the company's continued evolution. For business readers, the practical steps are straightforward: update your records, confirm the entity name on documents, and compare services on their merits. If you are considering LiTime, visit litime.com to review the latest information and reach out with your specific questions.
