Introduction: The New Era of Fair & Open Digital Commerce in India

E-commerce in India is undergoing a dramatic structural transformation. For over a decade, consumers and small sellers alike were bound to centralized marketplaces that leveraged heavy platform commissions, inflated Maximum Retail Prices (MRP), and algorithm-driven visibility that often sidelined local retail shopkeepers and artisans. Enter DigiHaat—Bharat’s premier ONDC-powered online shopping application built to democratize digital retail. Operated under the banner of Nirmit Bharat (a Section-8 non-profit initiative), DigiHaat connects millions of Indian consumers directly with local farmers, Kirana store owners, direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands, regional craftsmen, and local eateries across more than 500 cities.

By leveraging the Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC), DigiHaat operates without predatory platform fees, hidden surcharges, or arbitrary price markups. This open model guarantees that buyers receive genuine products at factory or store-level prices while local merchants retain 100% of their earnings. Whether you are searching for farm-fresh daily groceries, Swadeshi home decor items, authentic regional handlooms, budget-friendly consumer electronics, or hot restaurant meals delivered fast, DigiHaat provides a single, unified digital shopping destination.

What Can You Buy on DigiHaat? Explore 25+ Product Categories

1. Farm-Fresh Online Grocery & Household Essentials

DigiHaat simplifies online grocery shopping by connecting you directly with local farmers, grain mandis, and neighbourhood Kirana stores. Skip the inflated price tags of dark-store apps and get genuine staples delivered directly to your doorstep.

  • Fresh seasonal fruits, organic vegetables, and greens sourced from regional agricultural clusters.
  • Premium dry fruits, nuts, seeds, unpolished pulses, and cold-pressed edible oils.
  • Daily dairy items, bakery products, packaged snacks, spices, and cooking condiments.
  • Household cleaning supplies, laundry detergents, floor cleaners, and paper goods.

2. Electronics, Mobile Accessories & Smart Gadgets

Looking for authentic tech gadgets at genuine prices? DigiHaat features verified electronics sellers, offering warranty-backed tech products from India’s leading electronics brands and manufacturers:

  • Audio Devices: Bluetooth earphones, TWS earbuds (Pebble, Portronics), wireless neckbands, and portable speakers.
  • Mobile Accessories: High-speed Type-C fast chargers, power banks, wireless charging pads, and magnetic cables.
  • Computer & Workstation Accessories: Multiport USB hubs, ergonomic 360-degree rotating laptop stands, and wireless mice.
  • Wearables: Bluetooth calling smartwatches with HD displays, fitness trackers, and health monitors.

3. Swadeshi Crafts, Handlooms & One District One Product (ODOP)

DigiHaat stands out as India’s primary digital showcase for regional crafts and Swadeshi goods. Under the government's One District One Product (ODOP) mission, DigiHaat spotlights authentic Made-in-India creations:

  • Handcrafted brassware, terracotta pottery, and gold-plated lotus dry fruit gift bowls.
  • Handloom textiles, traditional sarees, ethnic apparel, and regional embroidery work.
  • Eco-friendly home decor, wooden toys, artisan candles, and festive gifting collections.

4. Skincare, Beauty & Personal Care Products

Discover clean, natural, and dermatologically tested skincare products directly from fast-growing Indian D2C beauty brands. Shop face washes, herbal hair oils, organic soaps, sunscreens, and personal hygiene essentials without paying inflated commission fees.

5. Online Food Ordering from Local Restaurants

Hungry? DigiHaat doubles as an efficient online food delivery app. Order fresh pizzas, gourmet burgers, authentic Indian thalis, Chinese noodles, and artisanal desserts from top local eateries and cloud kitchens in your city with real-time order tracking.
