This enables rapid systemic circulation of nutrients through sublingual capillaries, bypassing the harsh stomach environment and liver metabolism for maximum bio-efficiency and instantaneous action.

2. SLOW™ Delayed-Release Capsules

Featuring a unique capsule-in-capsule format with time-release beadlet technology, SLOW™ supplements combine plant-based omega oil matrixes with targeted vitamins and minerals.
