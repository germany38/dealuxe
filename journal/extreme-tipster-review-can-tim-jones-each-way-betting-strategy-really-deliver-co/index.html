Never bet with money you cannot afford to lose. Establish a dedicated "betting bankroll" completely separate from your living expenses, mortgage, or household bills. During this initial phase, focus entirely on understanding how selections are delivered, familiarizing yourself with bookmaker account management, and practicing strict stake discipline.

Phase 2: Level Staking and Data Tracking (Months 3-4)

Resist the temptation to increase your bet sizes after a couple of quick wins. Stick to consistent, fixed-unit stakes (e.g., standard £50 each-way units as utilized in baseline audits). Once your betting bankroll has grown organically through sustained net profits, you can safely scale your unit sizes upward by a fractional percentage of your total capital. This compound scaling approach protects you from catastrophic drawdowns while maximizing your earning potential.
