Carputech CarPlay Modules: What to Know Before You Buy

Visit Carputech →Shop for Carputech →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

If your car is a few model years old and still has a basic stereo and a small dashboard screen, you may feel left out of the phone features everyone else uses on the road. Aftermarket add-ons can help bridge that gap. Carputech is a technology-driven company, founded in 2018, that focuses on CarPlay modules and Tesla-style navigation systems for vehicles that do not come with CarPlay from the factory.

What a CarPlay module does

In general terms, a CarPlay module is a small device that sits between your phone and your car's existing system. Its job is to bring the familiar CarPlay interface to the display you already use, instead of asking you to replace the whole dashboard. Carputech describes its modules as built to integrate with the original car system, which is the part to look at most closely, because a good fit depends on how well the module works with your specific vehicle.

For many drivers, the appeal is practical: navigation, calls, messages, and music in one place, with less need to hold a phone or prop it on a vent mount. That said, the experience will always depend on your car's display, audio setup, and phone, so it helps to set realistic expectations before you order.

Where Tesla-style navigation fits in

Carputech also offers navigation systems described as Tesla-style. The name points to a design inspired by the large central screens common in modern electric vehicles, but the product page is the best place to confirm exactly what is included, how the screen is mounted, and which functions are supported. Treat the name as a style reference, not a promise that your car will match a Tesla in features or appearance.

If you are comparing options, look at whether the navigation unit is a standalone screen, a replacement for an existing display, or an add-on that works alongside your current setup. Each approach has different installation effort and different results.

What to check before you buy

Compatibility is the most important step. Before you add anything to your cart, confirm the following:

  • Your vehicle's make, model, and model year, and whether the product page lists your exact car.
  • Which head unit or display your car uses, and whether the module is designed to work with it.
  • What is included in the box, such as wiring harnesses, mounts, or adapters.
  • The return policy and support options listed on the brand's website, so you know what happens if something does not match your car.

You can find product details and compatibility information directly on the Carputech website. Reading the full specifications there is more reliable than relying on general reviews, which may describe a different model or setup.

Installation and everyday use

Some drivers install these devices themselves, while others prefer a professional installer, especially when wiring is involved. If you go the DIY route, follow the included instructions closely, disconnect the battery before working on wiring, and take your time with connections. If anything is unclear, a qualified car electronics shop can handle the job.

Once everything is set up, use the system while parked whenever you need to adjust settings or check a route. Keep your phone's software updated, and set up your navigation before you start driving. Responsible use matters more than any feature list.

Is it a good fit for you?

A CarPlay module or navigation system may suit you if your car lacks factory CarPlay, you are comfortable checking compatibility, and you want a cleaner, more integrated setup than a phone mount alone can give. It may be less suitable if your vehicle already has a modern display with CarPlay or if you prefer not to do any wiring. Take your time, compare the details, and choose the option that matches your car.

When you are ready to look at the options, visit Carputech to review its CarPlay modules and navigation systems, check your vehicle's compatibility, and read the product details before you buy.
