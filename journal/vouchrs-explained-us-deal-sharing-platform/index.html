Vouchrs Explained: How This US Deal-Sharing Platform Works

Visit Vouchrs →Shop for Vouchrs →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Plenty of people enjoy sharing a good discount, but few have time to track down every live offer, write a clear caption, and find a decent image for each one. Vouchrs is a US platform built around that problem. It gives you access to live deals from more than 200 US brands, and each deal comes with a ready-made picture and caption you can post on your own channels.

What you get with each deal

The main appeal is convenience. Instead of starting from a blank page, you choose a deal, check the image and wording, and share it. Every deal also shows what it could earn you before you post, which helps you judge whether an offer is worth your audience's attention. Treat those figures as a planning tool rather than a promise, since results depend on how your followers respond and on the offers themselves.

Who it may suit

Vouchrs does not set a follower minimum, so it may appeal to people who are just getting started, run a small newsletter, or manage a modest social account. It can also suit shoppers who already write about deals and want a faster way to publish them. If your audience is small, expect modest returns. A platform like this rewards consistent, relevant posts more than occasional big pushes.

Getting started and the early boost

The application takes about three minutes and is free to submit. Have a short description of where you share content and links to your main channels ready before you begin. The site also mentions a Founding boost of about 30% for 30 days. Read the current terms carefully to understand who qualifies, when the boost starts, and how it is calculated. You can review the latest steps directly on the Vouchrs website before you apply.

What to compare before you join

Any deal platform is worth a close look before you commit your time. Consider these points:

  • How deals are listed and whether they match your niche, such as fashion, home goods, or tech.
  • Whether you can edit the captions and images so they sound like you.
  • The expiry dates on each offer, and how quickly the library is updated.
  • Payout terms, including when earnings are paid out and any minimum thresholds.
  • What personal information the platform asks for during sign-up, and how it is used.

Comparing these details against other options will give you a clearer sense of whether the platform fits your goals.

Sharing deals responsibly

If you share links that earn you money, tell your followers. In the US, the Federal Trade Commission expects clear disclosure, so place a plain statement such as "affiliate link" near the link or in the caption. Check that each deal is still live before posting, and read the brand's own terms, since offer conditions can change. Most importantly, only share offers you would genuinely recommend. Your audience's trust is worth more than any single post.

Vouchrs is a practical option for US creators and shoppers who want ready-to-post deals without building everything from scratch. Take a few minutes to review the platform and see whether its library and terms match what you need, then visit Vouchrs to explore it for yourself.
