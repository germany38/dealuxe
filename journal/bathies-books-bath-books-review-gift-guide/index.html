Bathies Books Review: Bath Books With Real Stories for Gifting

Visit Bathies Books →Shop for Bathies Books →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Bath time can be a good moment for reading, but most bath books are short on substance. Bathies Books takes a different approach by offering bath books built around real stories. The brand was founded by a mom of three who previously worked at Google, and it focuses on books that are meant to be read aloud while the kids splash. If you are shopping for a new parent, a baby shower, or a family who already has everything, you can browse the Bathies Books collection on the official website to see what fits your needs.

What to look for in a bath book

Not every book labeled as a bath book works the same way. Before you buy, think about how the book will be used. Bath books need to handle moisture, so check whether the pages are designed to be wiped down and whether they can safely go into the tub. Also consider the reading experience. A story that holds a child's attention for more than a few pages is more useful to a parent who reads the same book every night than a set of simple labels or pictures.

It also helps to think about the child's age and reading stage. Look for a description that makes clear who the book is for, and read the product details carefully rather than relying on the cover alone.

The flagship 3-book set

The flagship Bathies Books offering is a 3-book set priced at $34.99. Compared with buying single books one at a time, a set is easier to evaluate as a complete gift, and it gives the recipient more than one story to start with. Before you purchase, compare the per-book cost against the titles you would otherwise pick up at a bookstore or library sale, and confirm that the set contains the books you expect on the product page.

Why the box matters for gifting

Packaging is often an afterthought, but for gifts it does a lot of the work. Bathies packages its flagship set in a box that is designed for presenting, which makes it a practical choice for baby registries, shower gifts, and holiday gift guides. If you plan to give the set, check the product page for any details about the box and how the books are packed, so you know what the recipient will receive before you wrap it.

For parents who are building a registry, a single well-packaged set can be simpler to add than a long list of individual books. Think about whether the recipient already has books in the same style, and whether a complete set would be a welcome addition to their shelf or bathroom.

Before you buy

Review the basics on the brand's website before you check out. Confirm current pricing, shipping options, delivery timing, and the return policy, since these details can change and are not always obvious in a gift guide. If you are buying for a specific occasion, allow enough time for delivery. Reading a few product descriptions side by side will also help you decide whether the Bathies approach to storytelling suits the child you have in mind.

Bathies Books is a US brand, so check that the shipping details match your address and that the prices shown are in US dollars.

Ready to see whether a story-driven bath book is the right gift? Visit Bathies Books to explore the flagship set and check the latest details for yourself.
