Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Are these government grants loans that I have to pay back?

No! Government grants are essentially free financial assistance provided by federal, state, and local agencies. Unlike bank loans or credit cards, grants do not require repayment.

2. Do I need a high income or perfect credit score to qualify?

Not at all. While many welfare and housing programs cater to low-income households, there are thousands of specialized grants available for middle-class families, entrepreneurs, students, and veterans regardless of personal credit scores.
