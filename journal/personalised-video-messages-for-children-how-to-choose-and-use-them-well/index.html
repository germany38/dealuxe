Personalised Video Messages for Children: How to Choose and Use Them Well

Few things light up a child's face like a message that seems to come from a favourite magical character. Personalised video messages let parents create these moments. Magic Gram describes itself as creating unforgettable personalised video gifts from Santa, the Tooth Fairy, the Easter Bunny and more.

What personalised video gifts are

A personalised video features a character speaking directly to a child, often using their name and details such as age, achievements or a special event. Parents provide the information, and a custom message is prepared for them to share.

When they work best

  • Holidays: Christmas, Easter and birthdays.
  • Milestones: a lost tooth, starting school or learning to ride a bike.
  • Encouragement: praise for good behaviour, bravery or kindness.
  • Special occasions: when a child needs a boost or something to look forward to.

Plan the personalisation

  1. Choose the character that suits the occasion and your family traditions.
  2. Gather details: the child's name, age, interests, pets, friends and recent achievements.
  3. Decide the tone: playful, gentle or celebratory.
  4. Pick a goal: to encourage, celebrate or surprise.
  5. Check spellings and pronunciation, especially for unusual names.

Make the moment special

Watch the video together, or choose a quiet time to share it. Let the child react naturally, and follow up with a related activity such as writing a reply, drawing a picture or setting out a treat. If the message mentions a goal, such as brushing teeth, help the child feel proud of progress.

Be mindful of age and beliefs

Think about whether the child is ready for the message and whether it fits your family's approach to imaginary characters. Some children may be sensitive or sceptical, so adapt the message and the way you present it.

Protect your child's privacy

Providing personal information requires care. Share only what is necessary, avoid sensitive details such as full addresses or school names and check how the company stores and uses data. Read the privacy policy and know how to request deletion.

Check delivery and format

Confirm how long it takes to receive the video, how it is delivered and whether it can be downloaded. Check device compatibility and whether it can be shared with relatives. Plan ahead for holidays, because deadlines may apply.

Use them as gifts for others

Parents, grandparents and friends can order videos for children they care about. Share the details that matter, and coordinate with parents so the message fits their traditions.

Budget and value

Compare prices and what is included. Consider how often you will use it, and whether a video is a one-off treat or part of a seasonal tradition.

Common mistakes

Ordering too late. Giving too much personal information. Misspelling names. Not preparing the child.

FAQ

Are the videos suitable for all ages? Younger children often enjoy them most. Can I preview them? Check the service's options. How long does it take? Check delivery times.

Conclusion

A personalised video can create a memory your child will treasure. Plan the details, protect privacy and enjoy the moment together. To see the options, visit Magic Gram.
