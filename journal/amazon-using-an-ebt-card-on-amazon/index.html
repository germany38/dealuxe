Using an EBT Card on Amazon: How to Add It and Shop for Groceries

Getting groceries to your door can be a big help, especially if you do not have a car, have a busy schedule or have trouble getting to a store. Amazon lets eligible customers add an EBT card to their account and use it for eligible grocery purchases. Here is how it works and how to get started.

What Is EBT on Amazon?

EBT stands for Electronic Benefit Transfer. It is the system many states use to deliver benefits such as SNAP. Amazon lets customers add an EBT card to their wallet, at checkout or through the EBT landing page, and use it to pay for eligible grocery items. Availability can depend on where you live, and eligibility, rules and terms are set by Amazon and your state, so check the offer page.

What Can You Buy?

EBT benefits can generally be used only for eligible food items. Rules follow the rules of the program, and what qualifies can vary.

  • Eligible: Many staple groceries, such as fruits, vegetables, meat, dairy, bread and pantry items.
  • Not eligible: Items that benefits cannot be used for, such as hot prepared foods, alcohol, vitamins and non-food items.
  • Not covered by EBT: Delivery fees, tips and some other charges, which may need a different payment method.

Look for eligibility labels on products, and check Amazon's help pages for current details.

Why Online Grocery Shopping Can Help

It saves travel. Groceries come to you.

It helps people with limited mobility. Heavy items are delivered to the door.

It makes comparing prices easy. You can compare options before you buy.

It supports planning. A list and a budget are easier to follow when you shop at home.

Tips for Shopping With EBT

  1. Check eligibility first. Look for eligible items before you add them to your cart.
  2. Know your balance. Check your EBT balance so you do not go over.
  3. Plan for non-covered costs. Have a second payment method ready for fees.
  4. Compare unit prices. Larger packs are not always cheaper per ounce.
  5. Consider Subscribe & Save for staples you buy often, if eligible.
  6. Keep your PIN private. Treat your card like any other payment card.

How to Add Your EBT Card

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Choose to add your EBT card in your wallet, at checkout or on the EBT page.
  4. Enter your card details.
  5. Start shopping for eligible groceries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this available in every state? Availability can vary. See the offer page.

Can I use EBT for everything? No. Only eligible food items can be paid for with EBT benefits.

Are delivery fees covered? Generally no. Check the terms for what applies.

Is there a discounted Prime option? Amazon has offered Prime Access for eligible customers. See the offer pages.
