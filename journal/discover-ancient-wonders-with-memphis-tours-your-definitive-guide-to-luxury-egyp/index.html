What Does Memphis Tours Offer? Core Travel Services & Packages

Memphis Tours operates as a comprehensive travel platform specializing in bespoke, tailor-made itineraries and pre-designed luxury travel packages. Designed to cater to solo travelers, couples, families, and private corporate groups, their portfolio spans an expansive range of travel products:

1. Classic Egypt Vacation Packages

Egypt remains the flagship destination for Memphis Tours. Packages range from concise 5-day private city breaks in Cairo and Luxor to extensive 14-day grand expeditions. Every package integrates visits to iconic UNESCO World Heritage sites including the Pyramids of Giza, the Great Sphinx, Saqqara, the Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM), the Valley of the Kings, Karnak Temple, and Abu Simbel.

2. Luxury Nile River Cruises & Lake Nasser Voyages

Experience the ultimate in relaxation and historic immersion with a 5-star Nile cruise sailing between Luxor and Aswan. Guests enjoy full-board luxury accommodations, onboard fine dining, cultural entertainment (such as traditional Galabeya nights and Nubian performances), and daily guided excursions directly from the ship to ancient riverside temples like Kom Ombo and Edfu. For a more private or classic experience, Memphis Tours also offers luxury Dahabiya sailings and Lake Nasser cruises to Abu Simbel.

3. Red Sea Beach Escapes & Desert Safaris

Combine historical sightseeing with coastal luxury by adding a Red Sea extension to Sharm El Sheikh or Hurghada. Memphis Tours arranges luxury resort stays, world-class scuba diving and snorkeling excursions in the Red Sea, quad biking across Eastern Desert dunes, and traditional Bedouin dinner experiences under the stars.

4. Multi-Country Combined Itineraries

For global travelers eager to maximize their journey, Memphis Tours crafts seamless multi-destination packages:

  • Egypt & Jordan: Explore Cairo and the Pyramids before traveling to Amman, Petra, Wadi Rum, and the Dead Sea.
  • Egypt & Turkey: Combine ancient Egyptian history with the grand architecture of Istanbul and the surreal landscapes of Cappadocia.
  • Egypt & Dubai / Morocco: Enjoy a fusion of ancient heritage and modern luxury across North Africa and the Middle East.

Why Book with Memphis Tours? Key Competitive Advantages

Navigating unfamiliar international destinations can present unexpected challenges. Memphis Tours distinguishes itself by providing an uncompromising standard of service, security, and flexibility:

1. Private, Licensed Egyptologists

Forget crowded bus tours. Memphis Tours provides dedicated, professional Egyptologist guides for your private group, ensuring customized insights, flexible pacing, and deep historical context.

2. VIP Airport Meet & Assist

From the moment you step off the plane, a Memphis Tours representative greets you inside the airport terminal, assisting with entry visas, passport control, luggage retrieval, and private air-conditioned hotel transfers.

3. 100% Tailor-Made Flexibility

Every traveler is unique. Memphis Tours allows you to customize every aspect of your itinerary—adjusting travel dates, hotel tiers (3-star, 5-star, or luxury gold), cruise ships, and daily excursion activities.

Frequently Asked Questions About Traveling with Memphis Tours

Is Memphis Tours safe for international travelers?

Yes, absolutely. Memphis Tours prioritizes guest safety above all else, operating private transfers in modern, air-conditioned vehicles with experienced drivers, dedicated airport escorts, and professional tour managers throughout your trip.

Can I customize my tour itinerary?

Yes! Every itinerary listed on Memphis Tours can be fully customized according to your travel dates, budget, preferred pace, and specific attractions you wish to visit.

What is included in a Memphis Tours package?
