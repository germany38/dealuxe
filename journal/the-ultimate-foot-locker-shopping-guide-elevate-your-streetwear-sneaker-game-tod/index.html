5. How to Choose the Perfect Sneaker & Apparel Fit

Shopping online for shoes and clothes is seamless when you keep a few key fit principles in mind:

  1. Know Your True Size vs. Brand Variations: While standard running shoes generally fit true to size, basketball performance shoes like Jordans or Kobes often feature snug padding for court stability; consider going up a half size for extra room.
  2. Check Apparel Fits: Private labels like LCKR often feature trendy baggy or oversized cuts. If you prefer a tailored look, size down on hoodies and cargo sweatpants.
  3. Read Customer Reviews: Foot Locker displays real verified customer ratings and detailed fit feedback on product pages, making it easy to determine whether a shoe runs wide, narrow, small, or large.
