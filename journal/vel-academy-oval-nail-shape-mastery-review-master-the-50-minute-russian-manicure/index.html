The 7-Step Vel Academy Oval Sequence

The core breakthrough of the Vel Academy methodology is replacing improvised, random filing with a rigorous 7-step sequence. According to their curriculum, each step creates the structural conditions for the next:

  1. Shorten Length: Establishing the baseline boundary for the final nail plate architecture.
  2. Align Overhangs Visually: Looking at the nail from above to identify coating overhang disparities before any aggressive rounding begins.
  3. Remove Right Overhang: Systematically filing excess width on the right lateral wall.
  4. Remove Left Overhang: Balancing the left side so both lateral walls match symmetrically. (Asymmetry cannot be fixed once rounding starts!)
  5. Round with Rocking Motions: Using a smooth left-right movement across the free edge to build an even curve without flat spots.
  6. Check and Round Center: Spotting and eliminating any remaining straight or flat areas right at the peak of the curve.
  7. Final 45° Pass Over Lateral Walls: Running the file at 45° to clear away the thin ridge or shelf of coating remaining along the sidewalls after rounding.
