Formulated in ISO and GMP-certified facilities, The Trost’s tincture oils utilize full-spectrum leaf extracts suspended in premium MCT carrier oil. For precise, discreet dosing, The Trost offers sugar-free, portion-controlled gummies infused with standardized leaf extract. Designed for daily stress management and athletic recovery, these gummies offer a palatable way to enjoy cannabinoids without confectionery sugar loads.
