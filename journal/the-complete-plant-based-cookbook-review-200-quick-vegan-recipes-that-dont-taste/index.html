Why Most Plant-Based Diets Fail (And How to Fix It)

When people give up on plant-based eating, it is rarely due to a lack of willpower. More often than not, failure comes down to three major friction points:

    - **The Specialty Ingredient Trap:** Having to hunt down imported nutritional yeast flakes, rare seed oils, or exotic mushroom varieties just to make a Tuesday night casserole. - **Time Fatigue:** Spending 60 to 90 minutes prepping complex multi-stage vegan recipes when you only have a narrow window between finishing work and winding down. - **The "Boring Salad" Syndrome:** Eating light, unsatisfying meals that fail to provide genuine comfort or keep your stomach full throughout the evening.

To successfully sustain a plant-based lifestyle, meals need to be fast, familiar, and deeply satisfying. You shouldn't have to relearn culinary arts just to enjoy a hearty Tuesday night dinner.
