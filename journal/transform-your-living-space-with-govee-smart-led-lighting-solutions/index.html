  • Govee Envisual & Camera Sync Technology: Take home entertainment to movie-theater quality. Govee's TV backlights and light bars utilize advanced camera technology and real-time screen matching to project color-accurate backlighting that reacts instantly to movie scenes and video games.

Control your entire home's lighting setup effortless with simple voice commands or quick mobile adjustments.

Explore over 64 preset scene modes, design custom dynamic patterns in DIY mode, sync lights to your favorite music tracks, or use AI-powered scene generators to match your exact mood or decor style.
  • Uncompromised Quality, Safety & Reliability: Govee prioritizes safety through rigorous testing, fire-resistant materials, shatterproof designs, high IP-rated waterproofing for outdoor products, and intuitive energy-efficient LED engineering.
