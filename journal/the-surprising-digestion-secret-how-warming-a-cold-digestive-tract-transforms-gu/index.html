Understanding the Root Cause: Why Digestion Slows Down As We Age

As we navigate through life, our bodies undergo natural physiological shifts. One of the most overlooked aspects of aging and lifestyle stress is the gradual decline in internal digestive efficiency. Stomachs produce fewer crucial enzymes, metabolic fire cools down, and the mucosal lining of the gut can become strained or compromised when exposed to processed foods, environmental toxins, and chronic stress.

When digestion is sluggish, the body struggles to break down complex macronutrients—fats, proteins, and carbohydrates. This incomplete breakdown leads to fermentation in the lower gut, generating uncomfortable pressure, bloating, and unpredictable bowel irregularities. Standard solutions often mask these issues with temporary buffers, but they fail to supply the missing enzymatic support or restore the innate thermal energy required for smooth, rapid nutrient assimilation.
