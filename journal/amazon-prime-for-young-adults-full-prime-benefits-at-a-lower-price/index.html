If you are between 18 and 24, you do not have to pay the full Prime price to get the full Prime experience. Prime for Young Adults is a discounted membership with the same core benefits as regular Prime, aimed at people starting out on their own. Here is what you get, why it is worth considering, and how to sign up.

What Is Prime for Young Adults?

Prime for Young Adults is Amazon's lower-priced membership for eligible customers aged 18 to 24. It includes the main Prime benefits, such as free delivery, streaming and member deals, for roughly half the price of a standard membership. Amazon has also offered extras to this group, such as cash back on eligible purchases and introductory trials. The current price, trial and perks are shown on the offer page, so check there before you join.

What You Get

  • Free, fast delivery. Eligible items ship free, with quick options on many products. This helps a lot when you are furnishing a first apartment or dorm.
  • Prime Video. Movies, series and Amazon Originals are included with your membership.
  • Prime Music. Ad-free listening to a catalog of songs and playlists.
  • Prime Gaming. Free games and in-game items on supported platforms.
  • Exclusive deals. Early access to some promotions and member-only prices.
  • Cash back on eligible items. Where Amazon offers it, this stretches your budget further. Terms apply.

Why It Is a Smart Choice at This Stage of Life

Moving costs add up. New places need basics: bedding, kitchenware, cleaning supplies and storage. Free delivery on those orders saves real money.

Entertainment on a budget. Prime Video and music are built in, so you may not need to pay for another streaming service.

A lower price for the same core benefits. The discount makes Prime much easier to fit into a tight budget.

Flexibility. Memberships can be canceled, so you are not locked in if your needs change.

Who Is Eligible?

The program is meant for customers in the 18 to 24 age range. Amazon may ask you to verify your age or details when you sign up. Eligibility rules are set by Amazon, so confirm yours on the offer page while signed in to your account.

What Happens When You Turn 25?

Programs like this are designed for a specific stage of life. When you leave the eligible age range, Amazon may move you to a standard membership or another plan. Review the terms on the offer page so you know what to expect.

Is It Worth It for You?

A quick way to decide is to list what you buy online in a typical month. If you order a few times a month, or you stream video or music, Prime for Young Adults may well pay for itself. If you almost never order from Amazon, a trial may be a better first step.

How to Sign Up

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Confirm that you meet the requirements and review the terms.
  4. Choose your membership and complete the signup.
  5. Start using your benefits right away.

Tips to Get More From Your Membership

  • Use every benefit. Many people use only the free shipping. Try the video, music and gaming perks too.
  • Choose no-rush delivery when you can. It helps you plan and can reduce packaging.
  • Watch for member deals. Check the deals page before big purchases.
  • Keep track of renewals. Add the renewal date to your calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it the same as regular Prime? It includes the main Prime benefits, at a discounted price for eligible young adults.

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Does it come with a free trial? Amazon often offers trials to eligible customers. See the offer page for what applies to you.

Can students use it? Amazon has also offered discounted Prime for students. Check the offer page for the current options.
