Introduction to The Jockey Club: The Heart of British Horse Racing

British horse racing is more than just a sport—it is a cherished cultural tradition, an economic powerhouse, and a world-renowned social experience. At the center of this thriving industry stands The Jockey Club, an institution governed by Royal Charter that manages 15 of the United Kingdom's most legendary racecourses. From the rolling hills of Gloucestershire during the Cheltenham Festival to the historic turf of Newmarket, the home of thoroughbred racing, The Jockey Club delivers unmatched sporting drama and hospitality.

What truly sets The Jockey Club apart is its founding ethos: operating for the good of British racing. Uniquely, all profits generated through ticket sales, corporate hospitality, and venue rentals are reinvested directly back into the sport. This funding drives elite prize money, world-leading equine welfare standards, and state-of-the-art spectator facilities. Whether you are planning a corporate networking outing, a memorable weekend with family, or a thrilling day at the races with friends, booking through The Jockey Club ensures an authentic, premium experience.

Premier Racecourses & World-Famous Festivals

The Jockey Club's portfolio encompasses the most celebrated venues in the equestrian world. Each venue offers a distinctive atmosphere and hosts signature events throughout the racing calendar:

1. Aintree Racecourse (Liverpool)

Home to the world's most famous steeplechase, the Randox Grand National Festival. Aintree combines elite Jump racing with high fashion on Ladies Day, drawing global audiences and creating unforgettable sporting history every April.

2. Cheltenham Racecourse (The Cotswolds)

The pinnacle of Jump racing. The four-day Cheltenham Festival in March culminates in the prestigious Cheltenham Gold Cup, attracting the finest horses, jockeys, and racing enthusiasts from Ireland, the UK, and beyond.

3. Epsom Downs Racecourse (Surrey)

Host of the historic Betfred Derby Festival, the ultimate test of the thoroughbred horse. Set against stunning views of London, Epsom Downs represents classic British heritage, glamour, and Flat racing excellence.

4. Newmarket Racecourses (Suffolk)

Known as the international home of thoroughbred racing, Newmarket features two famous tracks: The Rowley Mile and The July Course. It hosts two Classic races (the 2000 and 1000 Guineas) alongside the popular July Festival.

5. Regional Gems: Sandown Park, Haydock Park, & More

From Sandown Park's dual-code racing in London to Haydock Park's major North West fixtures, Carlisle, Huntingdon, Kempton Park, Market Rasen, Nottingham, Wincanton, and Warwick offer vibrant racing action all year long.

More Than Racing: Music, Dining, & Corporate Entertainment

The Jockey Club offers a diverse array of leisure and lifestyle options beyond afternoon racing:

  • The Jockey Club Live: Summer racing evenings followed by open-air concerts from global chart-topping music artists on iconic outdoor stages.
  • Fine Dining & Luxury Boxes: Award-winning chefs curate multi-course menus, champagne receptions, and private balcony seating for corporate hosting and celebrations.
  • Annual Membership & Passes: Exclusive access to private member bars, reciprocal racing days, and priority booking for high-demand festivals.
  • Conferences & Event Venue Hire: Flexible exhibition spaces, meeting rooms, and picturesque outdoor grounds available for weddings, corporate galas, and trade shows.
