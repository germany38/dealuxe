Easier Airport Trips with Now Extras: Parking, Lounges and More

Airports can make a journey feel longer than it is, from finding a parking space to waiting at security with a tight connection. Now Extras is a UK-focused provider of airport parking and transfers, with related travel extras such as hotels, lounges and fast track. For holidaymakers and business travellers alike, it is a useful place to start when you want to smooth out the less enjoyable parts of a trip.

Why airport extras are worth a look

Most people budget for flights and accommodation, then forget about the small costs and delays that surround them. Parking for a week, an early start before the trains run, or a long wait between flights can all add stress. Bundling some of these practical elements into one plan can make your travel day more predictable, which matters most when you have an early departure or a meeting on arrival.

Airport parking: what to compare

If you drive to the airport, compare options on the things that matter to you rather than the headline figure alone. Check the distance from the terminal, how you get from the car park to check-in, the opening and closing arrangements, and what happens if your flight is delayed. Look closely at cancellation terms and whether you need to provide vehicle details in advance. A slightly higher price can be sensible if it removes a worry about timing.

Hotels, lounges and fast track: matching extras to your trip

Not every journey needs every extra. A short leisure break may only need parking and a simple plan for getting through security. A longer trip, or one with a late-night arrival, might benefit from a nearby hotel. Business travellers often value lounge access for a quiet place to work before a flight, while fast track can help when time is tight. Think about how much time you have, whether you are travelling with children or heavy luggage, and how much flexibility you need if plans change.

Checking the details before you book

Before you commit, read the terms on the provider's website carefully. Confirm what is included, what counts as an add-on, and what the cancellation and amendment rules are. Check the dates, times and airport terminal, and make sure the confirmation reaches you in good time. If you are booking for work, keep receipts and confirmations in one place so that expenses are easier to claim. Compare the total cost of your plan, not just one element, and remember that the cheapest choice is not always the most convenient one.

Is it right for your next trip?

Now Extras may suit you if you want several airport-related services in one place and prefer to plan ahead rather than sort things out on the day. If you are happy to arrange everything separately, or your route is very simple, a basic comparison may be all you need. Either way, a little research beforehand can help you choose the extras that genuinely add value to your journey. You can explore the full range and check current details on the Now Extras website before you decide.
