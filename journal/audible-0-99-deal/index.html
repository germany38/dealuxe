A good deal is only worth it if you use it. Audible's seasonal offer gives eligible new members three months of Audible Standard for $0.99 a month, plus a $20 Audible bonus, which Audible describes as saving over 80 percent. This guide is not just about what the deal is. It is about how to make the most of it, from choosing your first book to deciding what to do when the intro price ends.

The Offer in Plain Words

For the first three months, you pay $0.99 a month instead of the regular Standard price. The $20 Audible bonus is delivered by email. After the intro period, the plan renews at its regular price unless you cancel. Eligibility rules apply, and the offer page shows the exact terms for your account.

What Audible Standard Gives You

Audible Standard includes one credit a month, which you can spend on almost any audiobook in the store, plus access to a selection of podcasts and Audible Originals. While your membership is active, you can listen to the books you pick. It is a good fit for anyone who wants new releases and bestsellers without the cost of buying each one.

A Smart Plan for Your Three Months

Month one: pick something you will actually finish. Choose a book you are genuinely excited about. Starting with a gripping story makes the habit stick.

Month two: stretch a little. Try a different genre, or a long book that would cost a lot to buy outright. Credits cover any title, so big books give the best value.

Month three: decide. By now you will know how often you listen. If you are finishing a book a month, keep going. If not, cancel before the intro period ends.

How to Choose a Great First Audiobook

  1. Start with a narrator you like. Listen to the sample before you spend a credit.
  2. Choose a story you already love. A favorite author's new book is an easy win.
  3. Try a series opener. Series give you plenty to continue with.
  4. Consider memoirs read by the author. They are often especially engaging.
  5. Match the book to your routine. Short chapters suit commutes. Long, immersive stories suit road trips.

Spending the $20 Bonus Wisely

The bonus gives you some room to explore. Check the terms on the offer page for when it arrives and what it can be used for. Treat it as extra freedom: use it on a title you would otherwise hesitate to buy, or save it for a later purchase if the terms allow.

When Audiobooks Beat Other Ways to Read

  • While driving or commuting. You get something useful out of dead time.
  • During workouts or walks. Stories make exercise go faster.
  • For chores. Cooking, cleaning and folding laundry feel easier.
  • When your eyes are tired. Listening is a break from screens.
  • For learning. Nonfiction and language titles work well on the go.

Avoiding Surprises

Any introductory price is a promise to review your plan later. Put a reminder in your calendar a few days before month three ends. Read the renewal terms. If you want to keep your membership, do nothing. If not, cancel in your account settings.

Is It Right for You?

The deal suits people who have wondered whether audiobooks would fit into their lives. It is also useful for lapsed members who qualify again. If you already know you do not enjoy listening to audio, it may not be for you, and that is fine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is $0.99 the ongoing price? No. It is an introductory price for three months.

Can I keep my books after I cancel? With Standard, access to your selected titles depends on your membership. Premium Plus lets you keep titles you buy with credits.

Is everyone eligible? Eligibility is decided by Audible and Amazon. Check the offer page while signed in.

Can I cancel at any time? Memberships can generally be canceled. Terms are on the offer page.

Start Listening for Less

Three months of audiobooks for under $3 is a low-risk way to find out whether listening is for you. Check that you qualify, choose a first book you will love, and press play.
