Ready to start? Join Audible Premium Plus monthly here

If you love audiobooks but do not want to commit to a full year, the Audible Premium Plus monthly membership gives you the full experience with maximum flexibility. You get a credit each month, a huge catalog to stream and books you keep, billed month to month. Here is what it includes and how to decide whether it suits you.

What Is Audible Premium Plus?

Premium Plus is Audible's full membership. It combines two things: a monthly credit you can spend on almost any audiobook in the store, and unlimited access to the Plus Catalog, a large library of audiobooks, podcasts and Audible Originals. The monthly plan bills you each month, so you can stop whenever you like.

Pricing varies and promotions change, so check the offer page for the current price and any introductory offer.

What Is Included

  • One credit every month. Use it on any title in the store, regardless of its regular price, including many new releases and bestsellers.
  • Books you keep. Titles you buy with credits stay in your library even if you cancel.
  • The Plus Catalog. Listen to thousands of titles without using a credit.
  • Member pricing. Extra titles are generally discounted for members.
  • Listen anywhere. The free Audible app works on phones, tablets and many other devices, with offline downloads.

Why Choose Monthly Billing?

Flexibility. You are not locked into a long commitment, which suits anyone who is not sure how much they will listen.

A lower upfront cost. You pay as you go instead of paying for a year at once.

A chance to test your habits. After a few months, you will know whether Premium Plus fits your life, and you can switch to an annual plan if you prefer.

Easy to pause your decision. If your needs change, you can cancel.

Who Monthly Premium Plus Is Best For

  • New listeners who want to see how audiobooks fit into their routine.
  • Seasonal listeners who travel more in some months than others.
  • Anyone who likes to keep their options open.
  • People who want to build a library at a measured pace, one credit at a time.

Monthly vs. Annual

Annual plans generally cost less per month in return for paying upfront. If you are sure that you will listen all year, annual may be better value. If you want to keep your options open, monthly is the safer choice. You can compare both on the offer pages.

Premium Plus vs. Audible Standard

Audible also offers a lower-priced Standard plan. The key difference is ownership. With Premium Plus, books you buy with credits stay in your library for good, and the Plus Catalog is included. With Standard, access to your selected books depends on keeping your membership active. If you want a library that is yours, Premium Plus is the better fit.

How to Join

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Review the price, terms and any introductory offer.
  4. Start your membership.
  5. Download the Audible app and choose your first book.

Tips to Get More From Your Credits

  1. Spend credits on long books. A credit covers any title, so big books give you the most value.
  2. Use the Plus Catalog for lighter listening. Save credits for titles you want to own.
  3. Keep a wish list. Add titles as you find them.
  4. Listen to samples. Check the narrator before you spend a credit.
  5. Do not let credits pile up. Credits are valuable, but they are only useful when you use them. Check the credit rules on the offer page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. Monthly memberships can be canceled from your account settings.

Do I keep my books if I cancel? Titles you bought with credits stay in your library. The Plus Catalog is available only while you are a member.

Is there a free trial? Audible often offers trials or introductory pricing to eligible customers. See the offer page for what applies to you.

Can I switch to an annual plan? Audible allows plan changes. Check your account for current options.
