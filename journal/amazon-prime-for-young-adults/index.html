Why It Makes Sense for Students and Young Adults

Textbooks and supplies. Free delivery on school supplies, electronics and dorm essentials can save you shipping fees in the first weeks of a semester.

Flexibility. You can cancel before the trial ends if it does not suit you. Set a reminder so you do not forget.

A Quick Example of the Value

Suppose you order a few things a month: a notebook, a phone charger and some snacks. Without Prime you might pay shipping on some of those or wait for a minimum order size.
