Parking Made Simple with Your Parking Space

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Finding a parking space on a busy day can eat up time and patience, which is why many drivers now turn to apps. YourParkingSpace describes itself as the UK's number 1 parking app, with five million registered users choosing from more than 700,000 spaces across the country. Here is a practical look at what it offers and how to decide whether it suits your journeys.

How booking works

The app is built around speed. You search for a space, pick the option that fits your plans and book, and the brand says the whole process takes only seconds. For anyone who has circled a car park looking for a free bay, that quick booking is the main attraction. Before you confirm, it is sensible to check the date, the start and end times and the exact location, so you arrive at the right place when you need it.

Where you can park

The app covers a broad range of trips rather than just one type of journey. Its listings span airport parking and transfers, which can help if you are heading off on a flight and want to avoid last-minute searches near the terminal. It also suits local holidays, where you might be staying in one part of town and need somewhere reliable to leave your car, and visits to tourism attractions, where parking can fill up quickly at peak times. Because the network includes spaces across the UK, it can be useful on unfamiliar routes too.

Comparing the cost

The brand advertises parking from as little as 50p an hour, which is a useful starting point, but the cheapest hourly rate is not always the cheapest overall. Work out the total cost for your planned stay, including any extra time you might need if your plans change. Compare a short-stay option with a longer block if you expect delays, and check whether the price shown covers the full period you have chosen. Price comparisons are easiest when you look at a few options for the same date and time, so it is worth taking a few minutes before booking. You can review current rates directly on the YourParkingSpace website.

What to check before you book

Good planning reduces the risk of surprises. Look at the space details and any access instructions, confirm your vehicle will fit comfortably, and read the booking terms, particularly those covering changes, extensions and cancellations. If you are travelling with a tight schedule, give yourself a margin for traffic and a walk to the terminal or venue. Keep your booking confirmation handy, and if anything is unclear, use the brand's support information before you travel rather than after you arrive.

Who it suits

YourParkingSpace is most likely to appeal to drivers who want to plan ahead, travellers who need reliable airport parking, families visiting attractions, and anyone who prefers to sort parking on a phone rather than hunt for a space on arrival. If you rarely park away from home or prefer to pay on the spot, you may find it less necessary. As with any booking, the best choice depends on your route, your timings and your budget.

If you are planning a trip and want to see what is available in your area, explore YourParkingSpace and compare a few options before you commit. A little preparation can make your next journey noticeably smoother.
