Understanding the Science of Cellular Aging

To appreciate how a supplement like Pep Tonic operates, it helps to examine what happens inside the body over time. Aging is heavily influenced by how efficiently our cells can repair themselves, generate clean energy, and discard damaged components. Three primary cellular components dictate this process:

  • Mitochondria: Often called the powerhouses of the cells, mitochondria generate the chemical energy needed for bodily functions. As we age, mitochondrial efficiency drops, leading to everyday fatigue and sluggishness.
  • Autophagy: This is the body’s natural recycling program, responsible for clearing out old, dysfunctional cellular parts so that fresh, healthy structures can take their place.
  • Cellular Communication and DNA Protection: Maintaining the integrity of genetic structures and supporting balanced inflammatory responses keeps biological systems resilient.
