The Core Methodology: The READ Method

At the heart of the Speak with Confidence course is Dr. Fish’s signature READ method. While many speaking coaches focus exclusively on external mechanics—such as hand gestures, vocal projection, and slide design—the READ method tackles the internal psychological landscape first.

While specific module breakdowns inside the dashboard offer comprehensive video and audio training, the framework focuses on:

  • Recognizing Triggers: Identifying the exact mental thought loops and physical sensations that precede an anxiety spike.
  • Embodying Mindfulness: Utilizing grounded, actionable breathing and centering exercises to anchor yourself in the present moment rather than projecting future fears.
  • Adapting Delivery: Shifting your internal dialogue from self-judgment of anxiety to a service-oriented mindset focused on delivering value to your audience.
  • Developing Resilience: Building long-term psychological strength so that future presentations become comfortable, routine experiences rather than terrifying ordeals.
