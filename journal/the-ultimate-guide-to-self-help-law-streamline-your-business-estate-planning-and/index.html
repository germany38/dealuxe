Core Product Categories Offered by Nolo

Nolo's expansive library covers virtually every major legal need encountered by individuals, families, and business managers. Below is a detailed look at what you can access directly through their online store:

1. Small Business Formation and Operation Guides

Starting and operating a business requires strict legal compliance—from deciding between an LLC and a corporation to hiring independent contractors, executing non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and managing commercial leases. Nolo offers bestselling publications like The Small Business Start-Up Kit, Nolo's Quick LLC, and The Employer's Legal Handbook. These guides detail how to choose the right business structure, meet IRS tax compliance standards, protect personal liability, and draft binding employee agreements.

2. Estate Planning Software and Living Trusts

Securing your family's financial future should not be delayed. Nolo is the developer of the renowned Quicken WillMaker & Trust, an intuitive desktop and online software solution that guides you through creating legally valid wills, living trusts, durable powers of attorney, healthcare directives, and final arrangements tailored to your specific state laws.

3. Real Estate, Landlord, and Tenant Solutions

Whether you are buying your first residential property, managing rental units, or navigating boundary disputes, property law is full of complex regulations. Nolo’s real estate collection includes Every Landlord's Legal Guide, lease agreement forms, security deposit notice templates, and eviction procedures designed to keep property owners compliant with landlord-tenant statutes.

4. Intellectual Property, Patents, and Trademarks

Protecting your brand name, creative works, and technological inventions is critical in a competitive marketplace. Through flagship titles such as Patent It Yourself, Trademark, and Getting Permission, inventors and creators learn step-by-step strategies to conduct trademark searches, file patents with the USPTO, copyright original works, and defend their intellectual property against infringement.

5. Customizable Legal Forms (NoloCloud)

Need a quick, enforceable agreement without purchasing an entire book? Nolo’s online digital form store (NoloCloud) provides downloadable, customizable legal forms covering personal finance, promissory notes, bill of sale, family custody plans, and immigration documentation.

Why Choose Nolo Over Generic Legal Templates?

While generic form downloads exist across the internet, poorly drafted legal documents can result in unenforceable contracts, unexpected tax penalties, or prolonged legal disputes. Nolo stands apart due to several key advantages:

  • Attorney-Reviewed Content: All publications, legal forms, and software tools are created and continually updated by experienced legal editors specializing in specific domains.
  • Plain-English Clarity: Complex statutes and legal procedures are translated into clear, actionable advice free of confusing legalese.
  • State-Specific Precision: Because legal requirements vary widely across jurisdictions, Nolo’s forms and guides account for state-level differences in property, probate, and corporate law.
  • Cost-Effective Independence: Pay only for the specific book, form, or software product you need—avoiding monthly subscription fees or unnecessary legal retainer costs.
