Palazzo Versace Dubai: A Modern Italian Palace on the Dubai Creek

Dubai is a metropolis celebrated for redefining the limits of architectural ambition and luxury living. Standing proudly along the historical banks of the Dubai Creek within the Culture Village (Jaddaf Waterfront), Palazzo Versace Dubai embodies a unique fusion of neoclassical Italian grandeur and Arabian hospitality. Modeled after a 16th-century Italian Palazzo, this masterpiece is a living showcase of high-fashion craftsmanship, where the world-renowned design ethos of Gianni Versace is woven into every archway, mosaic, and textile.

From the moment guests arrive under the striking porte-cochère, they are immersed in a world of curated elegance. The central court displays a hand-laid mosaic floor spanning over 1,000 square meters, crafted with millions of marble pieces imported directly from Italy. Surrounded by landscaped gardens, pristine swimming pools, and sweeping waterfront vistas, Palazzo Versace Dubai is not merely a hotel—it is a destination designed for discerning global travelers, corporate tastemakers, and connoisseurs of fine living.

Architectural Artistry & Versace Heritage

Every element within Palazzo Versace Dubai was tailor-made specifically for the property by the House of Versace. The interior narrative reflects neoclassical architecture, featuring vaulted ceilings, hand-painted gold leaf detailing, and signature Medusa and Greca motifs that define the brand’s global visual identity.

The hotel comprises 215 lavish rooms and suites, alongside 169 high-end private residences. Each floor features distinct color palettes—ranging from soft pastel blue and lilac to warm gold and salmon—reflecting the rich tapestry of Italian art and nature. Natural light floods the expansive hallways through grand floor-to-ceiling windows, highlighting intricate wood paneling, parquet floors, and silk furnishings.

Key Highlights of Palazzo Versace Dubai

  • 215 Hotel Rooms & Suites: Designed with exclusive Versace Home furniture and silk drapery.
  • 169 Exclusive Residences: One to six-bedroom residential units equipped with state-of-the-art kitchens and private terraces.
  • 9 Signature Restaurants & Bars: A culinary journey spanning fine dining Italian, Persian delicacies, and live entertainment venues.
  • 3 Outdoor Mosaic Swimming Pools: Surrounded by palm trees and private cabanas along the Dubai Creek.
  • The SPA at Palazzo Versace: 1,000 square meters of dedicated wellness, featuring hydrotherapy, marble hammams, and bespoke body treatments.

Accommodations: Redefining Ultra-Luxury Living

The rooms and suites at Palazzo Versace Dubai offer a peaceful sanctuary from the bustling city while maintaining an atmosphere of haute couture sophistication. Guests can select from various room categories depending on their preferences for city skyline views or serene waterscapes over the Dubai Creek.

Deluxe & Premier Rooms

Ranging from 50 to 60 square meters, these spacious rooms feature king-size beds, hand-carved furniture, private balconies, and marble bathrooms fitted with walk-in rain showers, deep-soaking tubs, and exclusive Versace toiletries.

Grand & Executive Suites

Designed for travelers seeking extended space and elevated privacy, the Grand Suites feature separate living rooms, dining spaces, and executive work desks. Ideal for business executives or couples, these suites provide personalized butler services and private terrace lounge areas.

The Imperial Suites

Occupying the highest level of the hotel, the Imperial Suites represent the pinnacle of luxury living. Spanning two floors, these duplex suites feature a private rooftop terrace, a private outdoor swimming pool, a jacuzzi, a personal gym, and a dedicated dining room capable of hosting intimate banquets.

A Global Gastronomic Journey

Palazzo Versace Dubai is renowned as one of Dubai's premiere culinary epicenters, boasting nine distinctive venues where gastronomy meets high design.

Vanitas

The hotel's flagship Italian restaurant offers an elegant atmosphere overlooking the Dubai Creek. Vanitas focuses on classic Italian cuisine crafted with seasonal, imported ingredients, pairing refined dishes with an extensive international wine collection.

Enigma

Featuring a concept inspired by Persian culinary traditions, Enigma delivers a sensory dining narrative. Michelin-starred chefs curate reinvented dishes served in an opulent setting adorned with rich textiles and warm lighting.

Giardino

Adorned with jungle-themed green wallpaper from the Versace Home collection, Giardino offers an expansive international buffet with live cooking stations, serving breakfast, lunch, and one of Dubai’s most celebrated Friday brunches.

Q’s Bar and Lounge

The legendary Quincy Jones selected Palazzo Versace Dubai to open his very first live entertainment lounge. Known for its intimate acoustic design, velvet seating, and world-class musical line-ups, Q's Bar offers an unmatched nightlife experience for music aficionados.

Mosaico, La Vita & Outdoor Lounges

Positioned in the heart of the lobby, Mosaico serves high tea, artisanal coffees, and light refreshments throughout the day. For evening cocktails, La Vita offers an intimate setting, while pool bars such as Amalfi and La Piscina provide al fresco dining, private cabanas, and sunbed services.

Wellness, Recreation & Event Spaces

Beyond its rooms and restaurants, Palazzo Versace Dubai is dedicated to holistic wellbeing and high-impact social gatherings. The SPA spans 1,000 square meters, featuring separate relaxation zones for men and women, Moroccan hammams, steam rooms, and specialized beauty therapies utilizing premium skincare products.

For corporate events, gala dinners, and weddings, the hotel provides over 1,500 square meters of flexible event space. The Grand Gala Ballroom accommodates up to 900 guests, offering state-of-the-art audiovisual systems and customized catering tailored to international conferences and high-profile celebrations.

Planning Your Stay

Whether traveling for executive business, planning a romantic retreat, or seeking a luxurious base to explore Dubai's landmark attractions, Palazzo Versace Dubai delivers a timeless stay rooted in design, privacy, and impeccable service.

Guests booking directly enjoy access to seasonal privileges, flexible cancellation options, and exclusive wellness packages.
