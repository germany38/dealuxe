Outerwear That Lasts: How to Choose a Coat or Jacket You Will Wear for Years

Outerwear does a lot of work. It protects you from the weather, anchors your outfit and is often the first thing people notice. Choosing well means balancing style, fit and function. AUTER is a Los Angeles-based outerwear brand founded by stylist Rob Zangardi, known for sculptural silhouettes, considered proportions and pieces designed to be lived in.

Think about your climate and routine

Where do you live, how do you commute and how often will you wear the piece? A lightweight jacket suits mild weather, while colder places call for insulation or heavier fabrics. A versatile mid-weight piece can layer through more seasons.

Understand silhouette and proportion

  • Structured shapes create a sharp line and suit tailored outfits.
  • Relaxed or oversized shapes feel casual and layer easily.
  • Length changes the look: shorter jackets pair with high-waisted trousers, while longer coats add drama.

Proportion matters as much as size. Check shoulder fit, sleeve length and how the piece sits over your usual layers.

Fabric and construction

  1. Material: Wool blends, cotton and technical fabrics each have strengths.
  2. Lining: A good lining adds comfort and helps the coat hang well.
  3. Details: Check buttons, zips, pockets and stitching.
  4. Weight: Heavy is not always warmer. Insulation and fabric quality matter.

Try the layering test

Wear your thickest usual sweater and check that you can move your arms and sit comfortably. If buying online, compare the size guide to a coat you already own.

Care and storage

Follow the label, brush wool regularly, use wide hangers and clean before seasonal storage.

Styling outerwear

A strong coat can be the centrepiece of an outfit, so keep the rest simple. Pair a sculptural jacket with plain trousers and a clean tee, or layer it over a knit for a softer look. Experiment with proportion: a longer coat over slim trousers creates a clean line, while a cropped jacket works with wider legs. Take a photo in a mirror to check how the shape balances.

Buying with confidence online

Outerwear is hard to judge on a screen. Read the measurements, look at product images from several angles and check the fabric details. Confirm the returns policy and keep tags attached until you have tested the piece with the layers you wear most. If you are between sizes, consider how much room you need for sweaters.

Seasonal storage

Before storing a coat for the summer, clean it, check pockets, and hang it on a wide, supportive hanger in a breathable garment bag. Avoid plastic covers that trap moisture.

Common mistakes

Buying a trend piece you will outgrow. Skipping the layering test. Ignoring care requirements.

FAQ

How many coats do I need? One versatile piece can go far; add a second for different weather. Should I buy oversized? Only if you like the look and can still move comfortably.

Conclusion

A great coat is an investment in comfort and style. If sculptural, wearable outerwear appeals to you, explore the AUTER collection.
