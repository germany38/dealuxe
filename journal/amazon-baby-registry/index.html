Amazon Baby Registry: How to Create One and Why Parents Love It

Ready to start? Create your Amazon Baby Registry here

Expecting a baby is exciting, and the list of things to buy can feel endless. A baby registry turns that list into a plan, and gives friends and family a simple way to help. Here is how an Amazon Baby Registry works, what makes it useful, and how to build one that works for you.

What Is an Amazon Baby Registry?

An Amazon Baby Registry is a free tool that lets you collect items from across Amazon, and from other stores through an add-from-anywhere option, into a single list. You share the list with loved ones, who can buy items for you directly. Amazon has also offered perks to registry owners, such as completion discounts and welcome gifts. Perks and terms change, so confirm the current ones on the offer page.

Why Create a Registry?

It keeps everything in one place. Strollers, car seats, diapers, clothes and nursery items all live on one list.

It prevents duplicate gifts. When someone buys an item, it is marked off, so you do not end up with five of the same thing.

It makes it easy for others to help. Family and friends often ask what you need. A registry answers the question.

It lets you plan your budget. Seeing everything together helps you decide what to buy yourself and what to ask for.

You can add what you actually want. Choose items based on your own research and reviews.

What to Put on Your Registry

  • Sleep: crib or bassinet, mattress, sheets and a sleep sack.
  • Feeding: bottles, a breast pump if you plan to use one, high chair and bibs.
  • Diapering: diapers, wipes, a changing pad and a diaper bag.
  • Travel: car seat, stroller and a baby carrier.
  • Bath and care: baby bath, towels and a first-aid kit.
  • Clothes: bodysuits, sleepers and hats in several sizes.
  • Comfort and play: play mat, swaddles and books.

Look for safety information on each product, and check current safety guidance for items like cribs and car seats.

Tips for Building a Great Registry

  1. Start early. Give yourself time to read reviews and compare.
  2. Add a range of prices. Include small items and big ones so there is something for every budget.
  3. Choose sizes thoughtfully. Babies outgrow clothes quickly, so add more than just newborn sizes.
  4. Include everyday items. Diapers and wipes are always useful.
  5. Update it as you go. Remove items you have received or no longer need.
  6. Share the link. Add it to invitations, group chats and baby shower details.

How to Create Yours

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Enter your details, such as your due date.
  4. Add at least one item to your registry.
  5. Share it with family and friends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it free? Creating a registry is free. You only pay for items you choose to buy.

Can I add items from other stores? Amazon has offered tools for adding items from other websites. See the offer page.

Can both parents manage it? Registries can generally be shared with a partner. Check the registry settings.

Can guests buy without an account? Guests usually need an Amazon account to buy. They can find your registry by searching your name.

Start Your Registry

A registry is one of the easiest things you can do to prepare for your baby. Create yours, add a few items, and share it with the people who want to help.
