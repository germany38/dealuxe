Trusty Review: How No Credit Check Catalogue Shopping Works

Visit Trusty →Shop for Trusty →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

If you are looking for a way to spread the cost of electronics, furniture or everyday essentials, you may have come across Trusty. It is a GB catalogue store and mobile phone provider that offers credit on over 10,000 items, with no credit checks, 0% APR and no fees advertised. Trusty runs a physical store in the West Midlands and also sells online, so you can shop in person or from home.

Who the store tends to suit

Trusty covers a broad mix of categories, including audio visual, computers, electronic accessories, furniture and soft furnishings, home and garden, toys and games, and sportswear. That range makes it worth a look if you need several different items at once and would rather manage them through one retailer. It may also appeal to shoppers who want to compare credit terms in one place before committing elsewhere.

Understanding "no credit check" credit

A no credit check arrangement means the provider does not review your credit file as part of the application. That can sound simpler, but it does not mean the agreement is free of obligations. Before you sign anything, work out the total amount you will pay over the full term, the size of each instalment, and the date each payment falls due. Ask what happens if a payment is missed, and whether any late charges apply.

This is general information rather than financial advice, so only borrow what you can comfortably repay from your regular income.

Reading the small print on the brand's site

The headline features of 0% APR and no fees are attractive, but the details matter. Before you buy, check the eligibility conditions, any deposit or upfront payment, the length of the repayment period, delivery charges and what the product warranty covers. Compare the cash price of an item with the total you would pay on credit, so you can see the real difference. You can find the full terms on the Trusty website, and it is sensible to read them in full rather than relying on a summary.

Mobile phones and broadband

Trusty is also a mobile phone provider, offering mobile contract and mobile broadband services alongside its catalogue range. If you are considering a phone bundled with a plan, compare the monthly cost, the data allowance, the length of the contract and the total spend over the agreement. Put that next to a SIM-only deal or a separate handset purchase to judge whether bundling actually suits your budget.

Shopping in store or online

Because Trusty has a physical base in the West Midlands, local shoppers can view products in person before deciding. Customers elsewhere in Great Britain will rely on the online store. Whichever route you choose, check the delivery times, the returns process and the contact options listed on the site before placing an order, so there are no surprises if something needs to be changed or sent back.

Trusty may suit shoppers who want a single retailer for a varied shopping list and who take the time to compare credit terms carefully. Take a look at the current range, read the conditions in full, and set a budget before you go ahead. You can start browsing at trustystores.co.uk.
