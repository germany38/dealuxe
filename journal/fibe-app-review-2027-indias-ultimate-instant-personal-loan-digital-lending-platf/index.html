Core Financial Products and Services Offered by Fibe

Fibe has successfully evolved from a salary-advance app into a comprehensive, one-stop financial ecosystem. The platform caters to a wide spectrum of monetary needs through its diverse product portfolio:

1. Instant Personal Loans

Users can borrow instant personal loans ranging from compact short-term funds up to ₹10 lakhs. With flexible repayment tenures stretching from 6 to 36 months, affordable EMIs, and absolute transparency regarding charges, borrowers can manage debt responsibly. Crucially, Fibe charges zero foreclosure fees, allowing you to clear your dues early and save on interest.

2. Purpose-Driven Financing (PDF)

Life requires specialized investments. Fibe’s Purpose-Driven Financing embeds credit directly at the point of purchase across more than 10,300 partner touchpoints. Whether you are financing higher education, professional upskilling courses, healthcare treatments, rooftop solar installations, or holiday travel packages, Fibe provides structured, affordable EMI options.

3. Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF)

Why break your long-term investments when you face short-term cash requirements? With Fibe’s Loan Against Mutual Funds, you can unlock a credit limit of up to ₹10 lakhs by leveraging over 8,700 approved mutual fund schemes, enjoying interest-only EMI options starting at 10.49% while your original portfolio stays invested.

4. Digital Fixed Deposits & Credit Tools

Beyond lending, Fibe enables users to book digital Fixed Deposits ranging from ₹1,000 to ₹5 lakhs backed by RBI-approved scheduled banks and insured up to ₹5 lakhs by DICGC. Additionally, users can check their credit scores for free in minutes, manage co-branded credit cards, and execute secure UPI transactions.

Key Advantages of Choosing Fibe

Fibe stands out in India's crowded fintech sector due to several distinct competitive advantages designed to maximize user trust and convenience:

  • 100% Digital & Paperless Journey: From initial OTP authentication and DigiLocker verification to video KYC and e-signatures, the application workflow requires zero physical paperwork.
  • Lightning-Fast Disbursal: Once verified and approved, eligible loan funds are transferred directly to your bank account within minutes.
  • Absolute Security Compliance: Fibe is ISO/IEC 27001 certified and PCI DSS compliant, utilizing robust data protection protocols to safeguard user privacy.
  • Zero Foreclosure Penalties: Enjoy complete financial freedom with zero pre-closure charges, allowing you to repay early without financial penalty.
