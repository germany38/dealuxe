Elevating UAE Interiors: The Ultimate Guide to Luxury Living with Chattels & More

Interior Design & Home Living | UAE

In the fast-evolving architectural landscape of Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider United Arab Emirates, homes serve as personal sanctuaries that reflect modern elegance, comfort, and international flair. Furnishing a high-end apartment in Downtown Dubai, a sprawling villa on Palm Jumeirah, or a sleek townhome in Abu Dhabi requires pieces that seamlessly balance functional utility with bold, contemporary artistic statements. Enter Chattels & More—a standout homegrown UAE luxury brand backed by the prestigious Easa Saleh Al Gurg Group.

Whether you are renovating your entire home or seeking single accent pieces to elevate your existing decor, Chattels & More curated collections offer unmatched European craftsmanship, contemporary design, and tailored interior solutions.

The Philosophy Behind Chattels & More

Founded with the core vision of creating gorgeous spaces that serve a genuine everyday purpose, Chattels & More sources its furniture and decor directly from unique European artisans and high-end global vendors. Unlike mass-produced alternatives, each item in their catalog is selected to combine ergonomic practicality with sophisticated visual appeal.

The brand understands the distinct lifestyle demands of UAE residents—where indoor entertaining transitions fluidly into outdoor garden hosting, and open-plan architecture demands structured yet flexible room arrangements.

Key Living Spaces: Crafting the Perfect UAE Home

1. Living Room Sanctuaries: Modular Sofas & Statement Lounging

The living room is the focal point of hospitality in Emirati and expat homes alike. Chattels & More specializes in plush, ergonomic seating designed to accommodate family gatherings and formal hosting:

  • Modular & Sectional Sofas: Customizable layouts upholstered in premium stain-resistant fabrics or supple natural leathers, perfect for grand living areas.
  • Accent Armchairs & Chaises: Statement seating pieces featuring organic curves, sculptural ironwork, or rich velvet textures that create immediate visual interest.
  • Coffee & Console Tables: Crafted from solid walnut, acacia wood, black iron, and natural marble, these core staples keep living spaces organized and elegant.

2. Elegant Dining Sets for Memorable Hosting

Dining rooms across the UAE are built around the concept of warmth, community, and sharing long meals with loved ones. Chattels & More offers dining essentials that anchor your space with sophistication:

  • Solid Wood Dining Tables: Featuring dark walnut, natural oak, and acacia wood finishes designed to endure decades of use.
  • Upholstered Dining Chairs: Ergonomic chairs designed for extended conversations over evening dinners.
  • Sideboards & Hosting Bars: Storage solutions with fluted wood panels, marble tops, and brass accents for effortless entertaining.

3. Outdoor Bliss: Alfresco Furnishings for the Gulf Climate

With cooler months calling residents outdoors, high-grade outdoor furniture is essential in the region. Chattels & More provides weather-resistant outdoor lounge sets, weather-proof dining tables, and poolside armchairs engineered to withstand sun, humidity, and coastal weather conditions while keeping an upscale aesthetic.

Design Tip: Blend neutral textured fabric sofas with warm wooden furniture accents and metallic brushed brass elements to achieve the popular modern minimalist luxury aesthetic featured across top UAE interior spaces.

Why UAE Homeowners Choose Chattels & More

  • Seamless UAE Delivery: Enjoy complimentary delivery on eligible orders above AED 500 directly to your home anywhere in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, and the Northern Emirates.
  • Bespoke Interior Design Consultations: Work directly with expert interior designers in-store or online to plan your home floor plans, select color palettes, and curate complementary decor bundles.
  • Flexible Payment Options: Benefit from convenient payment options including Buy Now, Pay Later installments and cash on delivery.
  • Prime Showroom Locations: Experience pieces firsthand at flagship physical stores located in Mall of the Emirates, City Centre Mirdif, Oud Metha, Nakheel Mall Palm Jumeirah, and Reem Mall Abu Dhabi.
