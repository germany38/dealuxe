What Is the Searl Effect Generator (SEG)?

The Searl Effect Generator is named after its original designer, British inventor John Royal Searl. Dating back to the mid-20th century, Searl’s unconventional research proposed a revolutionary method of combining specially structured permanent magnets and rotating rollers to generate electrical power continuously without burning fuel or relying on external mechanical torque.

According to historical descriptions and modern DIY adaptations, the SEG utilizes concentric rings and cylindrical rollers made of distinct composite materials and rare-earth magnetic elements. When properly configured and initiated, the relative motion and specialized magnetic gradients are said to create a self-sustaining cycle that produces usable electricity.
