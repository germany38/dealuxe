Brandify Review: SEO, AI Visibility and the October 18 Offer

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

If your business depends on people finding you online, you have probably heard the term AI visibility lately. Brandify is a US-focused B2B service that works on how your brand appears in traditional search results and in AI-generated answers. It combines SEO with newer approaches such as AEO and GEO. This guide explains what the service covers, who it may suit, and what to check before you get started.

What the service covers

Brandify describes its work as a mix of AI visibility management, AEO, GEO and SEO, supported by content creation, website improvements and Google Analytics reporting. In practical terms, that means one provider reviewing how your pages are structured and written, what content you publish, and whether you can measure the results. Classic SEO focuses on ranking in standard search listings. Answer engine and generative engine optimization look more closely at how your content is used in direct answers from search tools and AI assistants.

Who it may suit

A service like this tends to fit companies that already have a website and want more structured help than occasional fixes. Small and mid-sized B2B firms without an in-house marketing team often look for a single partner covering content, technical site work and reporting. If you only need a one-time brochure site or a logo, ongoing visibility work may be more than you need.

Understanding the new-client offer

Brandify is promoting a limited offer for new clients: 20% on a new client's first three paid months, capped at $959.40, starting October 18. Before you commit, confirm the exact start date, which plans the discount applies to, and how the cap is calculated. You can also ask whether the offer changes your contract length or what happens after the three months end. Check the current terms on the Brandify website so you are working from the latest details.

How to compare before you commit

Whichever agency or service you consider, a few questions help you compare options fairly:

  • What reporting will you receive, how often, and does it include Google Analytics data?
  • Is content creation included, and who approves what gets published?
  • What exactly does the website improvement work involve?
  • How are AI visibility and AEO or GEO results measured, and what timeframe is realistic?
  • What are the contract length and cancellation terms?

Brandify offers a free website check and instant pricing, which gives you a starting point and a cost estimate before any sales conversation. Treat the free check as a diagnostic, not a promise of specific results, and compare its findings with at least one other provider.

Brandify may be worth a look if you want SEO, AI visibility and analytics handled together and you prefer clear pricing up front. Start with the free website check, read the offer terms carefully, and ask for a clear scope of work in writing. You can visit Brandify here to run the check and see pricing for your situation.
