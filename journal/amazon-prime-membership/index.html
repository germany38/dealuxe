Amazon Prime Membership: Is It Worth Joining?

As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases.

Ready to join? Join Amazon Prime here

Prime has grown far beyond free shipping. Today it bundles delivery, streaming, music, gaming, reading and deals into a single membership. If you have been weighing whether to join, this guide breaks down what you get, what it costs, and how to tell whether it will pay for itself.

What Is Amazon Prime?

Amazon Prime is a paid membership that gives you a set of benefits across shopping and entertainment. The standard price is listed at $14.99 per month plus tax. Amazon also offers an annual option and discounted plans for some groups, such as young adults, students and eligible government assistance recipients. Prices and terms are set by Amazon and may change, so check the offer page before you buy.

What Is Included

  • Free delivery. Millions of eligible items ship at no extra charge, with fast delivery options on many products and same-day delivery in some areas.
  • Prime Video. A large library of movies, series and Amazon Originals, plus some live events.
  • Prime Music. Ad-free listening to a catalog of songs and playlists.
  • Prime Gaming. Free games and in-game items each month on supported platforms.
  • Prime Reading. A rotating selection of ebooks, magazines and more.
  • Amazon Photos. Photo storage with extra space for videos.
  • Exclusive deals. Early access to some promotions and member-only prices, including during major sale events.

Benefits vary by location and may change over time. See the offer page for the current list.

Who Gets the Most Value

Frequent online shoppers. If you order several times a month, free shipping alone can offset the monthly fee.

Households. Families can often share some Prime benefits through a household arrangement, so more than one person gets value from one membership. Check Amazon's rules for what can be shared.

People who stream. If you would otherwise pay for a video or music service, Prime may replace it.

Busy people. Quick delivery of essentials, from batteries to pet food, can save time and extra trips.

Deal hunters. Members often get early or exclusive access to sale prices.

Who Might Not Need It

If you shop on Amazon only once in a while and do not stream, Prime may not pay for itself. In that case, a free trial or a one-off purchase might make more sense. A simple way to check is to add up what you spend on shipping and streaming in a typical month and compare it with the Prime price.

Monthly or Annual?

Monthly billing is flexible. You can stop at any time. An annual membership generally costs less per month overall but asks for a bigger upfront payment. If you know you will use Prime all year, annual billing is usually the better value. If you only need it for a busy season, such as the holidays, monthly billing is safer.

A Simple Way to Estimate Value

Take the last three months of your orders and ask yourself:

  1. How many orders would have charged shipping or required a minimum purchase?
  2. How many subscriptions, such as video or music, would you drop if you had Prime?
  3. How much time did fast delivery save you?

If the shipping savings and dropped subscriptions come close to the monthly fee, Prime is probably worth it. The convenience is a bonus.

Tips for Getting Value From Prime

  • Use all the benefits. Many members use only shipping. Try the video, music, gaming and reading perks too.
  • Group your orders. Choose slower delivery options when you are not in a hurry. Some programs reward this.
  • Use Subscribe & Save for staples. Recurring orders on household items can lower the price on some products.
  • Watch for member events. Big sales often have member-only pricing.
  • Review your membership once a year. Make sure you are still getting value.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel Prime anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Is there a cheaper way to get Prime? Amazon offers discounted plans for eligible groups, as well as free trials for new members.

Does Prime include everything on Amazon? No. Some content and services are separate, and some movies are rentals or purchases.

Can I share my membership? Some benefits can be shared within a household. See Amazon's help pages for details.
