What Is Kindle Unlimited?

Kindle Unlimited is a subscription for readers. Members can borrow from a very large catalog of ebooks, audiobooks and magazines instead of buying each title. They read on a Kindle device or in the free Kindle app. A gift membership prepays a set period for someone else, in this case six months.

What the Recipient Gets

  • Read as much as they want. No per-title cost during the membership.
  • Recommendations that improve the more they read. Suggestions get better over time.
  • Millions of stories. Many genres, from romance and mystery to science fiction, history and self-help.
  • Ebooks, audiobooks and magazines. All included in one subscription.
  • Read or listen. Many titles let you switch formats anytime, and your place moves with you.

Not every book is included, and the catalog can change. See the offer page for current details.

Why Six Months Is a Smart Gift Length

It is long enough to build a habit. Half a year gives time for reading to become part of someone's routine.

It balances cost and value. It costs less than a longer plan while still delivering plenty of reading.

It suits many occasions. Birthdays, holidays, thank-yous and new-job gifts all fit.

It is easy to send. It is delivered digitally.

It encourages exploring. With no cost per book, the recipient can try new authors freely.

Who Will Love It

  • Avid readers who go through books quickly.
  • Students who enjoy reading outside of class.
  • Commuters who switch between reading and listening.
  • Travelers who want a library on a single device.
  • Parents who want to encourage reading at home.
  • Anyone who likes magazines as well as books.

Choosing Between Gift Lengths

Kindle Unlimited gift memberships come in several lengths, including twelve and twenty-four months. Six months is a good introduction for someone who likes to read but may not have tried the service. For a reader you know will make the most of it, a longer plan may be better. Choose by occasion and budget.
