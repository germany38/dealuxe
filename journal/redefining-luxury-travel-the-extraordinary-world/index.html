Redefining Luxury Travel: The Extraordinary World of Anantara Hotels & Resorts

True luxury travel is rarely just about a plush bed, high thread-count sheets, or immaculate room service. For the modern globetrotter, true luxury is measured in context, narrative, and transformation. It is found in the way a hotel integrates into the local ecosystem, respects regional heritage, and opens doors to experiences that standard tourism glosses over. If you are curating a bucket list that spans dramatic desert dunes, crystal-clear turquoise archipelagos, historic European capitals, and misty tropical jungles, there is one name that consistently stands at the pinnacle of five-star hospitality: Anantara Hotels & Resorts.

Derived from an ancient Sanskrit word meaning “without end,” Anantara evokes the freedom, movement, and harmony that comes from a seamless connection with nature and culture. Across a magnificent global portfolio spanning over 50 properties in more than 25 countries—from Southeast Asia and the Indian Ocean to the Middle East, Africa, and Europe—Anantara has mastered the delicate art of luxury place-making.

Let us take an in-depth journey across some of the world’s most mesmerizing destinations where Anantara redefines what it means to travel in absolute style.

1. Tropical Paradises and Island Escapes: Maldives, Thailand, and Bali

When people close their eyes and dream of a tropical paradise, images of swaying palms, powder-white sand, and impossibly blue lagoons immediately come to mind. Anantara turns those daydreams into tangible, breathtaking realities.

The Maldivian Dream: Anantara Kihavah & Dhigu

In the Maldives, properties like Anantara Kihavah Maldives Villas (nestled in the UNESCO Biosphere Reserve of the Baa Atoll) and Anantara Dhigu Maldives Resort offer the ultimate overwater sanctuary. Imagine waking up to floor-to-ceiling glass windows framing endless shades of azure, stepping down from your private sundeck directly into warm, clear lagoon waters, or dining underwater at a specialty subterranean wine cellar and restaurant. Snorkeling with manta rays, cruising into golden hour sunsets alongside pods of dolphins, and unwinding with holistic spa treatments built right over the ocean make the Maldives an unmatched romantic or family retreat.

Thailand: The Cultural Heart of Beach and Jungle Luxury

As the birthplace of the brand, Thailand is home to some of Anantara’s most iconic estates.

  • Phuket: At Anantara Mai Khao Phuket Villas or Anantara Layan Phuket Resort, travelers are treated to secluded pool villas tucked away behind protected national parks and pristine coastlines.
  • Koh Samui & Hua Hin: Anantara Bophut Koh Samui Resort and the historic Anantara Hua Hin Resort capture the essence of coastal tranquility, blending traditional low-rise Southern Thai architecture with lush lotus-filled lagoons and world-class culinary programs.
  • The Golden Triangle: Deep in northern Thailand’s misty hills, Anantara Golden Triangle Elephant Camp & Resort offers an ethically grounded wildlife experience. Perched on a jungle ridge overlooking Myanmar and Laos, guests can watch majestic elephants roam in their natural habitat while staying at one of the world’s most unique luxury safari-style camps.

2. Desert Majesty and Middle Eastern Wonders: Abu Dhabi and Oman

For travelers seeking cinematic landscapes, absolute seclusion, and profound architectural grandeur, Anantara’s footprint across the Middle East delivers jaw-dropping splendor.

Qasr Al Sarab Desert Resort by Anantara (Abu Dhabi, UAE)

Tucked deep within the legendary Liwa Desert and the sweeping orange dunes of the Rub’ al Khali (The Empty Quarter), Qasr Al Sarab looks like a majestic ancient desert fortress. Rising seamlessly out of the undulating sands, this resort marries rugged Bedouin tradition with uncompromising five-star luxury. Guests can spend their days trekking across towering sand dunes on camelback, experiencing archery and falconry displays, or relaxing in private pool villas before dining under a canopy of desert stars.

Oman: Mountain Peaks and Coastal Oases

In the Sultanate of Oman, Anantara captures two entirely different extremes of geographic beauty:

  • Anantara Al Jabal Al Akhdar Resort: Perched 2,000 meters above sea level on the fabled Green Mountain in the Hajar Mountains, this is one of the highest luxury resorts in the world. It provides dramatic canyon views, cool mountain air, and a ruggedly romantic base for exploring ancient stone villages and terraced orchards.
  • Al Baleed Resort Salalah by Anantara: On Oman’s lush southern coast, this tropical oasis features beachfront villas with private infinity pools overlooking the Arabian Sea, blending Frankincense-coast heritage with modern opulence.

3. Historic Grandeur and European Elegance: Rome, Budapest, and Amsterdam

Anantara’s expansion into Europe has brought its signature intuitive service and deep cultural immersion into some of the world’s most historic and culturally rich urban centers and coastal hideaways.

Architectural Masterpieces Reimagined

  • Budapest, Hungary: Housed within a stunning 19th-century palace originally built by an insurance company, Anantara New York Palace Budapest Hotel places guests inside an Italian-Renaissance masterpiece. Downstairs, the legendary New York Café serves coffee and pastries amidst gilded stucco, crystal chandeliers, and centuries of literary history.
  • Rome & Amalfi Coast, Italy: In Italy, properties like Palazzo Naiadi Rome and the cliffside Anantara Convento di Amalfi Grand Hotel—restored from a 13th-century mountaintop monastery overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea—offer a masterclass in Italian romance, coastal art de vivre, and culinary excellence.
  • Amsterdam, Netherlands: Located right on the bustling Dam Square, Grand Hotel Krasnapolsky brings classic Dutch heritage and contemporary luxury together under a breathtaking 19th-century glass winter garden.

4. Wild Africa and Indian Ocean Sanctuaries: Zambia and Seychelles

For the adventurous soul who refuses to compromise on comfort, Anantara bridges the gap between wild frontier exploration and elite sanctuary living.

Royal Livingstone Victoria Falls Hotel by Anantara (Zambia)

Situated right on the banks of the mighty Zambezi River, this colonial-style luxury hotel sits just a short walk from the roaring spray of Victoria Falls. Zebras and giraffes frequently roam freely across the property’s manicured lawns, offering a safari experience right outside your door while providing a sophisticated, historic haven complete with sunset river cruises and supreme hospitality.

Anantara Maia Seychelles Villas

For pure, unadulterated island exclusivity, this secluded sanctuary in the Seychelles offers ultra-luxurious private pool villas nestled amidst lush tropical gardens facing the Indian Ocean. With bespoke butler service designed around your exact schedule, it stands as one of the most private romantic hideaways on earth.

Why Choose Anantara for Your Next Journey?

What sets Anantara apart from other global hospitality groups is its foundational philosophy: indigenous luxury. Every single hotel or resort is meticulously designed to reflect its destination rather than imposing a generic corporate aesthetic.

  • Destination Dining: From private dining on deserted sandbanks to masterclasses in authentic Thai, Vietnamese, or Omani cooking, food is treated as a cultural bridge.
  • Holistic Wellness: Grounded in ancient Asian healing traditions and modern scientific wellness, Anantara Spas offer world-class rejuvenation customized to your physical and mental well-being.
  • Meaningful Travel: Through conservation efforts, local community support, and sustainable tourism initiatives (such as elephant welfare programs in Thailand and marine reef restoration in the Maldives), Anantara ensures that your travels leave a positive footprint on the world.

Ready to Experience the Extraordinary?

Whether you are dreaming of a romantic overwater bungalow in the Indian Ocean, an adventurous desert safari beneath the Arabian stars, or a cultural immersion in a historic European palace, Anantara Hotels & Resorts turns ordinary vacations into lifelong core memories.
