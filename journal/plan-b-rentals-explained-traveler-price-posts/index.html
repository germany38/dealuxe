Plan B Rentals Explained: Traveler Price Posts for Hosts and Guests

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Most vacation rental searches start with a list of properties and a price you may or may not be able to meet. Plan B Rentals takes a different approach. Travelers can say what they are willing to pay for a stay, and hosts can reach out to them. If that flip sounds useful to you, you can look at the platform directly at Plan B Rentals.

How the model works

In simple terms, the site reverses the usual search. Instead of scrolling through many listings and hoping one fits your budget, a traveler posts a price they are prepared to pay, and hosts decide whether to respond. Hosts who want to contact those travelers pay a monthly fee of $99. That cost means hosts are paying to start a conversation, so it is worth thinking about whether a fixed monthly fee makes sense for the number of stays they can realistically fill.

Who it may suit

A model like this tends to work best for people with a clear, firm budget who are flexible about the property itself. Travelers who care about a specific neighborhood, amenity or exact dates may find that narrower criteria are easier to handle on a traditional listing site. For hosts, it may appeal to those with open dates who want direct contact with interested guests rather than waiting for bookings to come through a general search. Neither group should assume results in advance, since availability and demand change.

What to compare before you commit

  • Cost against expected value: For hosts, weigh the $99 monthly fee against how many inquiries you would need to make it worthwhile.
  • Traveler costs: Check on the site whether posting a price or viewing responses involves any charge.
  • Booking terms: Look at cancellation rules, deposits, and what happens if plans change.
  • Communication: Confirm how messages are exchanged and whether contact details are shared before anything is agreed.

Practical steps before you sign up

Read the current pricing and terms on the official website, since fees and policies can change. Save copies of any agreements, and confirm key details such as dates, price, property address and payment schedule in writing before you send money. If something is unclear, it is better to ask for clarification first than to assume the answer.

Plan B Rentals may be a useful option if you want to test a different way of finding or filling short-term stays. Compare it with other platforms, read the fine print, and decide based on your own budget and needs. When you are ready to explore the details, visit the Plan B Rentals website and review the terms for yourself.
