Frequently Asked Questions About Uber Services

1. How do I apply promo codes or discounts to my Uber account?

Applying discounts is simple. Open your Uber or Uber Eats app, navigate to the menu or account settings, select the "Wallet" or "Promotions" tab, and enter your valid promo code or activate your special link offer before requesting your ride or placing an order.

2. What are the benefits of joining Uber One?

An Uber One membership provides ongoing value for frequent users by offering reduced service fees, lower ride costs, exclusive promotional access, and $0 delivery fees on qualifying food and grocery orders.

3. Can I use Uber for scheduled travel and airport trips?

Yes! You can schedule rides up to 30 days in advance, making it exceptionally easy to plan early morning airport runs or crucial business meetings without last-minute stress.
