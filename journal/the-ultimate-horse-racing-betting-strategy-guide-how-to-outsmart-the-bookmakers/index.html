However, professional gamblers operate on an entirely different philosophy. Winning a bet is completely meaningless if the odds do not accurately reflect the true probability of the outcome. Consider these eye-opening statistical realities about market leaders:

  • The 34% Rule: While it is true that favorites win roughly 34% of all horse races over the long term, that simple statistic hides a devastating truth—that leaves a massive 66% majority of races where favorites lose.
