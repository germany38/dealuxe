Core Philosophy: The Pillars of Forest Essentials Formulations

The essence of Forest Essentials lies in its strict adherence to traditional Ayurvedic preparation methods. Ayurveda views skin as a living, breathing organ that reflects internal balance (Doshas—Vata, Pitta, and Kapha). To maintain this harmony, ingredients must be untreated, nutrient-dense, and ethically sourced.

1. Organic Cold-Pressed Oils

The brand uses only organic, cold-pressed vegetable oils derived from the first pressing of seeds or nuts without applying artificial heat. This preserves the natural antioxidants, essential fatty acids, and vitamins, ensuring deep epidermal absorption and nourishment.

2. Steam-Distilled Floral Waters

Pure steam distillation captures the therapeutic essential oils and floral waters of fresh blossoms such as Kashmiri Saffron, Desi Gulab (Rose), Jasmine, and Nargis. These pure hydrosols tone, hydrate, and balance the skin’s natural pH without causing dryness or irritation.

3. Precious Minerals & Ayurvedic Bhasmas

In accordance with ancient Rasa Shastra practices, Forest Essentials incorporates precious metals like 24 Karat Gold Bhasma and Pure Silver. Gold is renowned in Ayurveda for its age-delaying properties, boosting collagen production, enhancing skin elasticity, and imparting a brilliant radiant glow.
