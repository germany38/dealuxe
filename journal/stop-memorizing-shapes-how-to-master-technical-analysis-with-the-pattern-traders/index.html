The Core Problem: Why Most Traders Fail at Pattern Recognition

Retail trading failure usually stems from a fundamental misunderstanding of what a chart pattern actually is. A pattern is not a prophecy or a guarantee. It is simply a visual representation of shifting supply and demand, buyer exhaustion, or institutional positioning.

When you trade based solely on shape recognition, you fall into classic traps: early entries, low-liquidity fakeouts, ignoring broader market context, and failing to define where your trading thesis is completely invalidated. To turn chart reading into a consistent decision-making framework, you need a structured approach that forces every setup through five critical lenses:

  • Context: What is the prevailing market structure?
  • Trigger: What price action confirms the setup is active?
  • Invalidation: What observable event proves the thesis is wrong?
  • Target Logic: Where do measured moves and realistic volatility points lie?
  • Risk Management: How do you protect your capital against common market traps?
