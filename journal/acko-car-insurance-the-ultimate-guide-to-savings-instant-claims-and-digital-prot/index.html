Understanding ACKO Car Insurance Policy Plans

Selecting the right level of coverage depends on your vehicle's age, driving habits, regional risk factors, and budget. ACKO provides structured options to suit every driver's unique requirements:

1. Third-Party Car Insurance Policy

Under the Indian Motor Vehicles Act, Third-Party Liability Cover is legally mandatory for all motor vehicles operating on public roads. This plan covers:

  • Legal and financial liabilities arising from accidental damage to third-party property or vehicles.
  • Medical expenses, permanent disability, or accidental death claims of third parties involved in an incident.

Note: Third-party policies do not cover damages sustained by your own vehicle due to accidents, theft, or fire.

2. ACKO Comprehensive Car Insurance Policy

The Comprehensive Plan is the flagship product recommended for most vehicle owners. It combines mandatory third-party liability protection with extensive Own Damage (OD) insurance coverage. Key inclusions cover:

  • Accidental Damage: Repair expenses resulting from road collisions, turnovers, or impact damage.
  • Theft and Total Loss: Full payout up to the Insured Declared Value (IDV) if the car is stolen or damaged beyond economic repair.
  • Fire and Explosion: Damage resulting from self-ignition, lightning strikes, electrical fires, or explosion.
  • Natural Disasters: Protection against floods, inundation, cyclones, landslides, hail, and earthquakes.
  • Man-Made Disasters: Coverage for losses resulting from riots, strike damage, vandalism, or malicious acts.
  • Transit Damage: Safeguards your vehicle while being transported by road, rail, air, or elevator.

3. Personal Accident Cover

Mandated for owner-drivers, this protection provides financial compensation of up to ₹15 Lakhs in the event of accidental injury, permanent total disability, or accidental death during vehicle operation.

Essential ACKO Add-On Covers for Maximum Vehicle Protection

While a standard comprehensive insurance policy provides foundational security, custom add-ons allow car owners to tailor protection against specific high-cost repairs. ACKO offers powerful add-on options that can be added seamlessly during instant purchase or online renewal:

Add-On Cover Key Benefit & Purpose
Zero Depreciation (Zero Dep) Cover Eliminates depreciation deductions on replaced car parts (rubber, plastic, glass, metal) during claims, ensuring full settlement value.
Engine Protection Cover Covers replacement and repair costs for engine, gearbox, and differential components damaged by water ingress (hydrostatic lock during floods) or oil leakage.
Return to Invoice (RTI) Cover In the event of total loss or theft, RTI bridges the gap between the car's current IDV and its original invoice price, including registration fees and taxes.
24/7 Roadside Assistance (RSA) Provides instant breakdown support, flat-tire assistance, towing services, fuel delivery, battery jump-starts, and key lock-out assistance.
No Claim Bonus (NCB) Protect Protects your accrued renewal discount (up to 50%) even if you file up to two claims within the policy term.
Consumables Cover Pays for items typically excluded from standard claim repairs, such as engine oil, brake fluids, AC gas, nuts, bolts, and oil filters.
Key Replacement Cover Reimburses the cost of substituting lost, damaged, or stolen smart keys and lockset mechanism repairs.

    • Reimbursement Facility: Repair your car at any preferred workshop of your choice, submit the bills online, and receive swift reimbursement.
