Phase 1: Establishing the Baseline (Days 1–7)

During your first week, your primary focus should be mastering movement form rather than rushing through repetitions. Because the program utilizes a 6-day training split, your body will experience mild muscular fatigue as it adapts to daily activity. Focus on clean bodyweight mechanics, deep breathing, and proper recovery during your single rest day.

Phase 2: Driving Progressive Overload (Days 8–14)

By week two, your central nervous system is adapting. To prevent adaptation plateaus, begin pushing your limits on rest intervals between sets. If a standard movement feels comfortable, increase your execution speed with strict form or add micro-pauses at peak contraction points to heighten muscular tension.

Week three is where true cardiovascular and muscular endurance breakthroughs happen. Fatigue will try to convince you to slow down, but this is where mental discipline takes over. As you enter the final stretch of your 28-day mission, your stamina will be visibly higher and your recovery times shorter. Use this week to execute every session with maximum focus.
