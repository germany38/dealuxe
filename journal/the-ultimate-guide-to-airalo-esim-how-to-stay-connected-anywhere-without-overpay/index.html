What is an eSIM and How Does Airalo Work?

To understand why Airalo is a total game-changer, it helps to understand what an eSIM actually is. An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a small, programmable chip built directly into your smartphone, tablet, or smartwatch hardware during manufacturing. It serves the exact same purpose as a traditional plastic SIM card—identifying your device to a mobile network—but operates 100% digitally.

Instead of physically removing and inserting plastic cards, an eSIM allows you to download cellular data profiles over the air. You can store multiple eSIM profiles on a single device and switch between them seamlessly whenever you travel.

Airalo acts as an international marketplace that connects travelers directly with local cellular networks around the world. By partnering with major regional telecom operators (such as Vodafone, Docomo, Orange, and AT&T), Airalo enables you to purchase local prepaid data plans at local prices, completely bypassing expensive home carrier roaming agreements.

Why Airalo is the Smartest Choice for International Travelers

Whether you are taking a brief weekend getaway, embarking on a multi-country vacation, or living as a full-time digital nomad, Airalo solves the biggest pain points of staying connected abroad. Here are the core benefits:

1. Massive Savings on International Data

Traditional mobile network operators charge astronomical rates for international roaming passes. Paying $10 to $15 per day per line adds up quickly, especially on family trips or multi-week journeys. Airalo offers prepaid data packages starting at just a few dollars. You pay for a specific allowance of gigabytes for a fixed number of days, ensuring zero unexpected bills when you get back home.

2. Instant Connectivity Upon Arrival

With Airalo, you don't need to hunt for Wi-Fi or queue at SIM card counters when you land. You can purchase and install your eSIM from the comfort of your home before departing. The moment your plane touches down at your destination, your phone connects to the local network automatically.

3. Keep Your Primary Phone Number Active

One of the largest drawbacks of physical local SIM cards is that taking out your home SIM disables your regular phone number. That means missing important SMS two-factor authentication (2FA) security codes from your bank, emails, or messaging services. With an eSIM setup, your smartphone operates in Dual-SIM mode: your primary physical line stays active for calls and security texts, while Airalo handles all your mobile web browsing, maps, and app data.

4. Flexible Local, Regional, and Global Coverage

Airalo covers over 200 countries and territories worldwide, broken down into three distinct plan types:

  • Local eSIMs: Designed for travel within a single country (e.g., Japan, Italy, Thailand, United States).
  • Regional eSIMs: Perfect for island hopping or multi-border trips across specific continents (e.g., Eurolink for Europe, Asialink for Asia, Latamlink for Latin America).
  • Global eSIMs: Covers dozens of countries across multiple continents under a single digital SIM package.

How to Set Up Your Airalo eSIM: A Step-by-Step Tutorial

Getting online with Airalo is remarkably straightforward. Follow these four quick steps to get connected:

Step 1: Confirm Compatibility and Unlocked Status

Before purchasing, make sure your mobile device meets two basic prerequisites:

  1. Carrier Unlocked: Your smartphone must not be locked to a specific home carrier network contract. Contact your current carrier to unlock it if necessary.
  2. eSIM Compatible: Most flagship smartphones manufactured in recent years (including modern Apple iPhones, Samsung Galaxy S series, Google Pixel phones, and iPad models) feature built-in eSIM hardware.

Step 2: Choose and Purchase Your Plan

Visit the official Airalo platform via your browser or mobile app. Browse by your destination country or region. Select a package based on the data volume you anticipate needing (e.g., 1GB, 3GB, 5GB, 10GB, or Unlimited) and the desired validity duration (e.g., 7 days, 15 days, 30 days).

Browse Airalo Destinations & Plans

Step 3: Install Your eSIM Profile

After checkout, Airalo will present clear installation options inside the app or website. You can install your eSIM through three easy methods:

  • Direct Installation (Recommended): Tap a single button in the Airalo app to automatically configure the eSIM settings on iOS or Android.
  • QR Code: Scan a provided QR code using your phone’s camera via settings.
  • Manual Entry: Copy and paste the SM-DP+ address and activation code into your phone's cellular settings.
Pro Tip for Setup: We highly recommend installing your Airalo eSIM at home or while connected to a stable Wi-Fi network right before heading to the airport. That way, everything is pre-loaded on your device before you enter foreign airspace.

Step 4: Activate Cellular Data Upon Arrival

When you touch down at your destination, navigate to your phone’s network settings:

  1. Turn your Airalo eSIM line to ON.
  2. Select the Airalo eSIM as your primary line for Cellular / Mobile Data.
  3. Ensure Data Roaming is turned ON specifically for your Airalo line (this enables it to connect to Airalo’s local partner networks).
  4. Keep Data Roaming turned OFF on your home primary line to avoid accidental carrier charges.

Frequently Asked Questions About Airalo

Can I make regular phone calls with an Airalo eSIM?

The vast majority of Airalo plans are data-only packages, which are optimized for internet usage. However, you can easily place high-quality voice and video calls using internet-based communications platforms such as WhatsApp, FaceTime, Skype, Zoom, or Telegram using your Airalo data allowance. Some select regional and global plans also include dedicated call and text allowances.

What happens if I run out of data during my trip?

If you run low on data, you do not need to install a new eSIM. Simply open the Airalo app, select your active eSIM, and choose a "Top-Up" package. The extra gigabytes will instantly attach to your existing profile.

Can I use my phone as a mobile Wi-Fi hotspot with Airalo?

Yes! Most Airalo eSIM plans fully support personal hotspot sharing/tethering, allowing you to connect secondary devices like laptops, tablets, or family members' phones to your mobile internet connection.
