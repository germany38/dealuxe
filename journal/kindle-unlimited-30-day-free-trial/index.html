Ready to read? Start your 30-day Kindle Unlimited trial here

Thirty days is a long time when you have a great book in your hands. The Kindle Unlimited 30-day free trial lets you read and listen as much as you like for a full month before you decide whether to keep the subscription. Here is how to make the most of it.

What Is the Offer?

The promotion gives eligible customers a 30-day free trial of Kindle Unlimited. During the trial you can borrow from a large catalog of ebooks, audiobooks and magazines. After the trial, the subscription renews at the regular price unless you cancel. Eligibility rules apply, and terms are set by Amazon, so check the offer page.

What You Can Do

  • Borrow from a large catalog. Many genres and many bestselling authors are available.
  • Read on any device. Use a Kindle or the free Kindle app on a phone, tablet or computer.
  • Listen too. Many titles come with audio, and you can switch between reading and listening.
  • Read magazines. Selected issues are included.
  • Keep your place. Your progress moves with you across devices.

Not every book is included, and the catalog changes.

A Simple 30-Day Plan

Week one: explore. Browse your favorite genres, download a few titles and see how the catalog fits your taste.

Week two: settle in. Start your main read and try listening on a commute or walk.

Week three: branch out. Try a new author or genre. With no extra cost per book, it is a good time to experiment.

Week four: decide. Count how many books you read. If the number is high, the subscription may pay for itself.

Who Will Enjoy It

  • Heavy readers who go through several books a month.
  • Series fans who want to keep reading without buying every volume.
  • Commuters who alternate between reading and listening.
  • Travelers who like to carry a library on one device.
  • Curious readers who want to explore.

Is It Worth Keeping?

Compare what you read during the trial to what you would have spent. If you read several books, the subscription may cost less than buying them. If you only read one or two, or most of your favorites are not in the catalog, it may not be worth it. Either way, the trial lets you find out.

How to Start

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Confirm that you are eligible and read the terms.
  4. Start your trial.
  5. Download the free Kindle app if you need it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I be charged during the trial? No charge is advertised for the 30 days. After that, the regular price applies unless you cancel.

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Do I need a Kindle? No. The free app works on many devices.

Are there borrowing limits? Limits apply to how many titles you can have at once. See the offer page.
