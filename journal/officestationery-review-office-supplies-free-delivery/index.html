OfficeStationery Review: Office Supplies and Free Delivery Over £49

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Running out of printer paper or pens at the worst possible moment is a familiar problem for anyone who works from a desk, whether at home or in an office. OfficeStationery is a UK online retailer that aims to cover those everyday needs, from basic stationery to larger items for the workspace. Here is a practical guide to what the brand offers and how to decide whether it suits you.

Who the retailer is aimed at

The brand describes itself as an online stationery retailer serving both personal and home office use. That makes it relevant to a freelancer setting up a spare room, a family organising a shared study corner, and a small business that wants one supplier for its regular orders. The range spans office supplies, furniture and soft furnishings, and business services, so it is worth checking the categories that match what you actually need before you start browsing.

What to compare before you order

Stationery prices vary more than many shoppers expect, so it helps to compare a few specific items rather than judging a shop on one product. Choose a handful of things you buy regularly, such as paper, toner, folders or pens, and note the price, pack size and unit cost on each site. Check whether the quantities match your usage, because a larger pack can work out cheaper per sheet or per item even when the upfront price looks higher.

Delivery terms also affect the final cost. OfficeStationery offers free delivery on orders over £49 excluding VAT. Because the threshold is quoted before VAT, make sure you check the basket total with the figures that apply to you. If your order is close to the limit, it may be sensible to group purchases together rather than place several small orders.

Practical tips for a smoother order

  • Read the product description carefully, especially sizes, compatibility for printer consumables, and pack quantities.
  • Check the delivery information and any cut-off times on the site before you need items urgently.
  • Keep your order confirmation and receipt, which will help if you need to query an item later.
  • If you are buying for a business, look for any account or invoicing options on the site and check whether they suit your bookkeeping.

Is it a good fit for business customers?

The brand lists business services among its sub-sectors, so it may appeal to companies that want a regular supplier for office essentials. Before committing, look at how the site presents trade or business purchasing, and compare the total cost of a typical monthly order with your current supplier. Price is only one factor. Consider how easy it is to reorder, how clearly products are described, and whether the range covers the items your team uses most.

Final thoughts

OfficeStationery is a straightforward option for anyone looking to buy office supplies online in the UK, whether for a home desk or a busy team. Compare a few regular items, check the free delivery threshold against your basket, and read the product details before you buy. When you are ready, visit the OfficeStationery website to see the current range and prices for yourself.
