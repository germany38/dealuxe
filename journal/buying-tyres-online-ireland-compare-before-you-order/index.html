Buying Tyres Online in Ireland: How to Compare Before You Order

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Shopping for new tyres is rarely exciting, but it is one of those jobs where a little comparison work can make a real difference to your wallet and your safety. If you want a quick way to see what is on offer, Tyres.ie is a product and price comparison marketplace for tyres, rims and related items in Ireland. This guide walks through what to look for before you click buy.

What Tyres.ie actually offers

Tyres.ie is part of TyresNET, an international marketplace and price comparison platform that operates online in 60 countries. For Irish drivers, the focus is on Ireland, so you can browse tyres, rims and other products in one place rather than visiting a series of separate shop websites. Comparing listings side by side can help you spot differences in brand, specification and price, though it is always worth confirming the final details on the retailer's page.

Start with the right size

Before you compare prices, find the exact size printed on the sidewall of your current tyres or in your vehicle handbook. You will see a code such as 205/55 R16 91V. The first number is the width in millimetres, the second is the aspect ratio, the R refers to radial construction, the next figure is the rim diameter in inches, and the final figures give the load index and speed rating. Matching these correctly matters far more than chasing a small saving, because the wrong size can affect handling, braking and how the tyre sits on your wheel.

Compare the details, not just the price

  • Season: summer, winter or all-season, and whether that suits your driving in Ireland's changeable weather.
  • Load index and speed rating: check they meet or exceed the figures in your handbook.
  • Fuel efficiency and wet-grip ratings from the EU tyre label, where these are shown.
  • Delivery, fitting costs and whether fitting is included in the quoted price.
  • Rim compatibility, if you are also looking at alloy wheels.

A low headline price can look very different once fitting, valve replacement or disposal charges are added, so compare the total you will actually pay in euro.

Who it suits

An online comparison service is useful if you already know roughly what you need and want to check several options quickly. It can also suit small business owners and fleet managers who buy tyres regularly and want to keep an eye on the market. If you are unsure about tyre type or fitment, a local tyre specialist can advise you in person, and you may find it helpful to combine both approaches.

Check the retailer before you buy

Whichever site you use, look for clear contact details, a readable returns policy and information on how fitting is arranged. Make sure the seller is clearly identified on each listing. You can review the current terms directly at the Tyres.ie website before placing an order.

Whether you are replacing a single worn tyre or planning a full set for the winter months, a little comparison before you buy goes a long way. Visit Tyres.ie to start browsing tyres and rims for Irish roads, and keep your vehicle handbook close by.
