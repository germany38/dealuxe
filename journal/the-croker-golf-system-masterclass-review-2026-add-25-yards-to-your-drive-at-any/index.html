The Problem With Modern Golf Instruction

Why do so many amateur golfers struggle for years without making meaningful progress? The culprit is usually bad advice.

Modern golf media is flooded with quick-fix tips: "Keep your head completely still," "Shift your weight violently to your back foot," "Bow your wrist at the top," or "Torque your spine as far as possible." For young, hyper-flexible athletes, these complex contortions might work temporarily. But for the average amateur, weekend player, or senior golfer, these unnatural movements create severe tension, ruin timing, and often result in debilitating back and elbow injuries.

When you force your body to move in ways it wasn't designed to, your swing becomes unreliable under pressure. You stand over the ball thinking about five different mechanical checkpoints, tension creeps into your grip, and your swing tempo completely collapses. True power doesn't come from muscle strain and joint torque; it comes from rhythm, balance, and leveraging the ground correctly.
