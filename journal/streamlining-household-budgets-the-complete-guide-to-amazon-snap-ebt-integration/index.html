Frequently Asked Questions

What items can I purchase using an EBT card online?

SNAP-eligible items include fresh fruits and vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, dairy products, breads, cereals, and other pantry staples or non-alcoholic beverages intended for home consumption.

Are delivery fees covered by SNAP benefits?

While SNAP benefits cover the cost of eligible food items, government regulations specify that non-food costs such as delivery fees, tips, or service charges cannot be paid using SNAP funds. However, eligible customers often qualify for discounted Prime membership tiers or specialized access programs.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. Registered platforms utilize high-grade encryption and secure digital wallet protocols to ensure your sensitive financial and benefit details remain fully protected.
