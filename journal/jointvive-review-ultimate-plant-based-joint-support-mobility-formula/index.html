Understanding the Root Causes of Joint Discomfort

Joint flexibility and comfort depend heavily on the delicate balance between cartilage integrity, synovial fluid lubrication, and cellular defense. Over time, excessive oxidative stress generated by overstimulated free radicals can damage connective tissue, trigger inflammation, and diminish lubrication levels between joints.

When connective tissues face persistent stress or age-related wear, mobility declines, leading to stiffness and discomfort. Addressing these challenges requires more than just masking surface symptoms; it demands a multi-pathway approach that nourishes cartilage, neutralizes oxidative cellular stress, and supports natural tissue regeneration.
