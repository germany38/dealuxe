Back to the Office: A UK Buyer's Guide to PCs and Workspace Gear

Buying a new computer, a gaming setup or a more comfortable home office can quickly turn into a maze of specifications, part numbers and compatibility questions. Back to the Office is a GB-based retailer that brings several of these needs together: it is one of the UK's leading suppliers of computer hardware and PC components, and it also offers custom PCs, gaming equipment, office supplies and furniture.

Who this retailer suits

A specialist retailer like this tends to work best for shoppers who already have a rough idea of what they need. If you are upgrading a single part, such as storage or memory, or you want to furnish a workspace and your computer setup in one go, having hardware and furniture in the same place can make comparison simpler. If you are completely new to building or configuring a machine, it is worth reading a few independent guides first so you know which terms matter before you start browsing.

Custom PCs or individual components

The first decision is whether to buy a configured custom PC or to choose the parts yourself. A pre-configured machine saves time and removes the guesswork of matching components. Building from parts gives you more control over cost, upgrade paths and future repairs, but it requires more research.

Whichever route you take, check the core components against each other rather than in isolation. Key points include:

  • Processor and motherboard socket compatibility
  • Graphics card length and case clearance
  • Power supply wattage with headroom for upgrades
  • Memory type and capacity for the tasks you actually run
  • Storage type, such as solid-state versus mechanical drives

Gaming equipment and computer accessories

For gaming, start with the games you play most and their recommended specifications, then choose hardware that meets or exceeds them. Screen resolution, refresh rate and the comfort of keyboards, mice and headsets all shape the experience, so think about how you use the machine day to day. The site's categories for PC and video games, electronic accessories and audio visual equipment are a good place to compare options side by side. Browsing the Back to the Office website lets you see how each product is described, including any stated specifications, before you add anything to your basket.

Office supplies and furniture

A computer is only part of a comfortable workspace. Chair height and lumbar support, desk depth, monitor height and cable management all affect how long you can work without strain. When comparing furniture, measure your room first and check dimensions carefully, as product photos rarely show the full footprint. For supplies, consider how often you will reorder and whether buying in bulk makes sense for your household or business.

Before you order

Whatever you buy, take a few minutes to read the product details, delivery information, returns policy and warranty terms on the retailer's website. These are the details that matter most if something arrives damaged or does not suit your setup. If you are buying for a business, confirm invoicing and VAT requirements before checkout. When you are ready to explore the range, you can visit Back to the Office and compare hardware, gaming gear, office essentials and furniture in one place.
