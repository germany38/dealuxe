2. What Services Does CleanWhale Offer across Europe?

CleanWhale operates a comprehensive suite of maintenance and specialized cleaning solutions designed to handle every aspect of modern property upkeep. Whether you require regular maintenance for a city apartment or intensive restoration after major renovations, CleanWhale provides customizable services tailored precisely to your property size and layout.

A. Regular Apartment and Home Maintenance

Designed for daily comfort and long-term hygiene, standard cleaning packages cover all vital living zones. Cleaners perform thorough dusting of reachable surfaces, complete vacuuming and wet mopping of all floor coverings, detailed kitchen maintenance (including sinks, countertops, and exterior appliance surfaces), and complete bathroom disinfection including toilets, tubs, showers, and mirrors. Waste removal and basic space organization are always included as standard.

B. Intensive Deep Cleaning and Seasonal Refreshes

Ideal for spring cleaning, seasonal transitions, or landlord handovers, deep cleaning addresses hidden grime, accumulated dust on high baseboards, light fixtures, and neglected architectural corners. It ensures every square meter meets high sanitary standards.

C. Specialized Upholstery and Carpet Dry Cleaning

Furniture accumulates allergens, dust mites, and stubborn organic stains over time. CleanWhale utilizes advanced extraction machinery and hypoallergenic chemical agents to deep clean fabric sofas, leather couches, armchairs, double mattresses, and fitted carpets directly on-site, restoring fabric colors and hygiene.

D. Post-Renovation and Construction Cleanups

Post-construction dust and plaster residue require commercial-grade vacuum systems and specialized solvents. CleanWhale’s specialized teams eliminate fine gypsum particles, paint splatter, and sealant marks, turning fresh construction zones into immediately livable homes.

E. Professional Window, Balcony, and Appliance Add-Ons

Customers can easily augment their primary booking with targeted add-ons: deep internal oven degreasing, refrigerator sanitization, interior kitchen cabinet organization, window and frame washing, or full balcony cleaning.
