Your 4-Step Mobile Energy Implementation Roadmap

To get the absolute maximum value out of this system, follow a disciplined implementation path before buying any hardware:

Step 1: Complete Your Energy Audit

Do not guess how much power you consume. List out every single appliance—from your roof AC and residential fridge down to your phone chargers and water pump—and use the Energy Audit Worksheet to map out your true daily amp-hour and watt-hour requirements.

Step 2: Balance Your Storage and Generation

Match your battery bank capacity to your energy audit results, keeping DoD (Depth of Discharge) in mind—especially if you are transitioning to modern lithium batteries. Pair your storage with appropriately rated solar panel arrays and an MPPT charge controller.

Step 3: Map Out Your Wiring and Safety Architecture

Ensure wire gauge sizes, fuse ratings, and breaker placements comply with strict DC electrical standards to prevent voltage drop and fire hazards. Refer to Chapter 6 and your Final RV Power Plan worksheet to double-check every connection.

Step 4: Execute Installation and Test Under Load

Install your components methodically, secure your cabling, and run controlled bench tests before heading out into remote boondocking locations.
