Understanding PLR and MRR Rights

One of the most attractive elements advertised with this offer is the inclusion of Private Label Rights (PLR) and Master Resale Rights (MRR). But what does that actually mean for your business?

  • Private Label Rights (PLR): This grants you the legal permission to modify, rebrand, edit, and put your own name as the author on the content. You can rewrite sections, add your branding, and completely make it your own.
  • Master Resale Rights (MRR): This allows you to sell the product to your own customers and, depending on the specific license terms, even pass along resale rights to your buyers.

Instead of starting with a blank slate, you acquire a pre-packaged business asset that you can customize to fit your unique brand voice and target audience.
