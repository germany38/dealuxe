Streaming has split into dozens of services, each with its own app, login and bill. Prime Video Channels offers a simpler way: subscribe to selected paid services, such as NBA League Pass on Prime Video, and watch them from inside the Prime Video app. Here is how it works and when it makes sense.

What Are Prime Video Subscriptions?

Prime Video lets you add paid subscriptions to other streaming services and watch them in the Prime Video app. Examples include sports packages like NBA League Pass. You pay through your Amazon account, and the content shows up alongside your other Prime Video options. Availability, pricing and terms vary by service and can change, so check the offer page.

Why People Like This Approach

One app. Instead of switching apps, you find everything in Prime Video.

One bill. Payments go through your Amazon account.

Easy to start and stop. Subscriptions can generally be canceled from your account, so you can add a service for a season and end it when you are done.

Simple sign-in. There is no need to create a separate account for each service in many cases.

Good for sports fans. Seasonal packages such as League Pass fit well with a start-and-stop approach.

Who Will Benefit

  • Sports fans who want a specific league package for a season.
  • Movie and series fans who like to rotate services.
  • Households that want to manage fewer apps.
  • Cord-cutters building a custom lineup.
  • Anyone who likes to try a service for a short time.

Tips for Managing Subscriptions

  1. Decide what you will really watch. Only add services you will use.
  2. Check the price and billing cycle. Note whether it is monthly or annual.
  3. Add a reminder to review each subscription.
  4. Rotate services. Add one for the show or season you want, then cancel.
  5. Check device support. Make sure your TV or phone supports the service.
  6. Review live-event rules. Some sports packages have blackout rules in certain areas. Read the terms.

How to Subscribe

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Choose the subscription you want.
  4. Review the price and terms.
  5. Confirm your subscription and start watching.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Prime? Requirements can vary. See the offer page.

Can I cancel anytime? Subscriptions can generally be canceled from your account settings. Terms vary by service.

Is it the same as a Prime Video rental? No. Subscriptions give ongoing access to a service, while rentals and purchases are for individual titles.

Do blackouts apply? Some live sports packages have blackout rules. Check the details for the specific service.
