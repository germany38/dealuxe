When you want something to watch, a free trial of Prime Video is an easy way to find out what is on. The trial is for Prime, so you also get free delivery, music, gaming and more. Here is what to expect and how to get the most from it.

What Is the Offer?

Eligible customers can start a Prime free trial and watch Prime Video during the trial period. After the trial, the membership renews at the regular price unless you cancel. Terms, trial length and eligibility are set by Amazon, so check the offer page.

What You Can Watch

  • Movies. A rotating selection of films across many genres.
  • Series. Dramas, comedies, documentaries and more.
  • Amazon Originals. Shows and films made for Prime Video.
  • Kids and family titles. Content for younger viewers.
  • Rentals and purchases. Some new releases are available for an extra fee.
  • Live events. Certain sports and events are available to members.

The catalog changes regularly, and not everything is included with Prime. Some titles are rentals or purchases.

More Than Video

The trial includes the rest of Prime, so you can also try free, fast delivery on eligible items, Prime Music, Prime Gaming, member-only deals and more.

How to Get the Most From Your Trial

  1. Make a watchlist right away. Add shows and films you have been meaning to see.
  2. Download for offline viewing. Save titles for flights and trips on supported devices.
  3. Create profiles. Give each family member a profile for personalized recommendations.
  4. Check parental controls if kids will watch.
  5. Try the other benefits. Order something with free delivery and explore music and gaming.
  6. Set a reminder a few days before the trial ends so you can decide whether to keep Prime.

Who Will Enjoy It

  • Movie fans who like variety.
  • Series bingers looking for the next show.
  • Families who want content for all ages.
  • Travelers who want to download shows.
  • Prime shoppers who want to see the full benefits.

Is It Worth Keeping?

Think about what you watched. If you found plenty of shows and also used delivery and other benefits, Prime may be a good value. If you watched little and ordered rarely, you may prefer to cancel. The trial lets you find out without paying for the trial period.

How to Start

  1. Open the offer page using the link in this article.
  2. Sign in to your Amazon account, or create one.
  3. Confirm that you are eligible and read the terms.
  4. Start your trial.
  5. Open the Prime Video app or website and press play.

Will I be charged during the trial? No charge is advertised for the trial period. After that, the regular price applies unless you cancel.

Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel from your account settings.

Which devices work? Many TVs, streaming devices, phones, tablets and computers support Prime Video.

Is everything included? No. Some titles are rentals or purchases, and some channels are separate subscriptions.
