What Is an Audible Gift Membership?

An Audible gift membership prepays a set number of months of Audible Premium Plus for someone else. The 12-month plan covers a full year. The recipient redeems it with an Amazon account and starts listening in the free Audible app.

Each Premium Plus gift membership includes the Plus Catalog, an all-you-can-listen collection of thousands of audiobooks, podcasts and originals.
