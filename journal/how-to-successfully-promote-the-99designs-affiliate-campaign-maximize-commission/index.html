Campaign Overview & Commission Structure

Before launching your promotions, it is critical to understand the financial incentives and structural layout of the 99Designs program. Because users must select, configure, and purchase a professional package or launch a design contest, your audience consists of serious entrepreneurs, startups, and small-to-medium-sized enterprise (SME) owners who are ready to invest in their business.

  • New Customer Contest Launch: $75.00 per lead
  • New Customer Project Completed: $22.50 per lead
  • Existing Customer Contest Launch: $37.50 per lead
  • Existing Customer Project Completed: $11.25 per lead
  • Low Value Jobs: $4.00 per lead

Geographic Eligibility: This campaign is active across dozens of lucrative markets globally, including the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, major European Union nations, and select territories across Latin America, Asia, and Africa.

Best Traffic Sources for the 99Designs Campaign

The campaign guidelines permit contextual, display, search, social media, native, and mobile traffic. However, conversion rates vary wildly depending on user intent. Here are the top-performing traffic channels:

1. Niche Websites & SEO Content (Highest Converting)

Because founders and startup owners actively search Google for branding advice, business formation guides, and design solutions, search traffic and contextually optimized niche sites convert the best. Users arriving via informational queries have high purchase intent.

2. Business & Entrepreneurial Social Media Communities

Platforms like LinkedIn, X (formerly Twitter), indie hacker forums, and targeted Reddit communities (e.g., r/startups, r/entrepreneur) are goldmines. Business owners constantly discuss logo creation, brand identity packages, and scaling their visual identity.

3. Contextual Native & Display Ads

Placing native ads or banner graphics on business development blogs, legal formation services (LLC setup guides), and domain registration checkouts captures users right at the exact moment they are building a new company.

Proven Promotional Strategies & Ideas

To capture those high-tier $75 payouts, your marketing must target the pre-launch or rebranding phase of a business lifecycle. Try these actionable promotional angles:

  • "How to Launch a Startup Brand" Case Studies: Write in-depth articles detailing the step-by-step process of naming an LLC, securing a domain, and commissioning professional brand guidelines.
  • Design Platform Comparison Guides: Compare crowdsourced design contests with DIY logo makers. Show why investing in professional creative talent yields a stronger long-term ROI for growing businesses.
  • Resource Pages for Entrepreneurs: Curate a "Startup Toolkit" page featuring essential software, legal setup tools, and brand identity solutions. Embed your affiliate link naturally within the design resource block.
  • Social Media Case Breakdowns: Share visual transformations (before-and-after rebrands) on LinkedIn or Pinterest, explaining how a professional brand identity package elevates customer trust and sales.

Critical Restrictions & Compliance Checklist

To avoid commission reversals or account suspension, always adhere strictly to the advertiser's guidelines:

  • Search Restrictions: Absolutely NO bidding on "99designs", "99designs.com", "99designs.ca", or any brand variations, misspellings, or trademarked terms in paid search engine marketing (PPC).
  • Promo Codes: No incentivized traffic, coupon code sharing, or unauthorized discount codes.
  • Reversals: Keep in mind that cancelled contests or unfinished projects are subject to affiliate reversals, making pre-qualification through quality content vital.
