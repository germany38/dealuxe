What Findlys Is Designed to Do

Who a Platform Like This Tends to Suit

Lead generation services and B2B directories are generally most useful for businesses with a clear offer and a defined target customer. If you can describe your ideal client in one sentence, you will get more out of any platform than if you sign up hoping to be found by everyone. Vendors should ask whether their services are easy to explain to a stranger. Owners should consider whether they have the capacity to respond to inbound inquiries quickly, because slow replies can cost a good lead.

Questions to Answer Before You Upgrade

Signing up for a basic account and upgrading to a paid plan are separate decisions, so it helps to know exactly what changes at each stage. Before you move to a higher tier, check:

  • What the basic sign-up includes, and what the upgraded plan adds
  • How billing works, including renewal dates and the steps to cancel
  • How your contact details and any lead information are handled and shared
  • How refund requests, disputes, and account changes are managed

Save a copy of the terms you agree to, and note the dates you signed up so you can track renewals.

Comparing Findlys With Other Options
