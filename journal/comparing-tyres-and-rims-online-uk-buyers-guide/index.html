Comparing Tyres and Rims Online: A UK Buyer's Guide

Visit Tyres →Shop for Tyres →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Buying tyres can feel like decoding a foreign language, with strings of numbers, speed ratings and competing brand names. It is easy to overpay when you only look at one shop. Tyres.net is an online marketplace and price comparison platform for tyres, rims and related products, and it operates in 50 countries. For UK drivers and businesses, it offers a practical place to see options side by side before committing to a purchase.

What the platform covers

Tyres.net describes itself as a marketplace for all types of tyres and rims. The main appeal is convenience: rather than visiting several websites to check the same size, you can compare what is listed in one place. This can be useful if you are replacing a full set, looking for a specific wheel and tyre combination, or planning a wheel upgrade at the same time as a tyre change.

Start with the correct tyre size

Before you compare prices, find the size currently fitted to your vehicle. It is usually printed on the tyre sidewall and listed in your handbook or on the sticker inside the driver's door frame. You will typically see a format such as 205/55 R16 91V, which gives the width, aspect ratio, rim diameter, load index and speed rating. Getting these details right matters more than saving a few pounds. An incorrect size can affect handling, braking and safety, and it may not comply with your vehicle's specification.

Other things to compare

The lowest price is not always the best value. Consider the following before you add anything to your basket:

  • Season: summer, winter and all-season tyres are designed for different conditions, so choose one that suits where and how you drive.
  • Load index and speed rating: these should meet or exceed the vehicle manufacturer's recommendation.
  • Fitting and delivery: check whether fitting is available near you and whether it is included in the price or charged separately.
  • Returns and warranty: terms differ between sellers, so read them before you pay.

Checking the seller before you buy

When you use any marketplace, take a moment to look at the seller's details. Where a seller's feedback or reviews are available, read them with a critical eye, and check that contact information and delivery terms are clear. If anything is unclear, it is reasonable to ask before you buy. A good rule of thumb is to keep your order confirmation, the tyre specification and any fitting booking together, so you have everything to hand if you need it later.

Whether you are a private motorist, a small fleet manager or someone looking to match new rims with the right tyres, a comparison-led approach can help you make a more informed choice. You can explore the range and compare options on Tyres.net to see what is available for your vehicle, then check the details against your own requirements before you order.
