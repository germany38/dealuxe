What Does Economy Bookings Offer?

Economy Bookings operates as an intermediary broker, partnering with major international car rental powerhouses (such as Hertz, Avis, Enterprise, and Budget) alongside reputable local and regional suppliers. This diverse ecosystem allows users to access an extensive inventory tailored to every conceivable travel requirement:

  • Wide Vehicle Selection: From compact economy cars designed for fuel-efficient city driving to full-size sedans, rugged SUVs, luxury vehicles, and multi-passenger vans.
  • Transparent Side-by-Side Comparison: Compare rental rates, mileage allowances, fuel policies, cancellation terms, and pick-up locations on a single screen.
  • Supplier Ratings & Reviews: Real-world customer feedback helps you evaluate the reliability and quality of individual rental providers before you commit.
  • Global Reach: Access rental pick-up counters at thousands of airports and downtown locations across Europe, the Americas, Asia, Africa, and Oceania.
  • 24/7 Multilingual Customer Support: Assistance is available around the clock to help resolve any communication or logistical barriers with local suppliers.

How to Get the Best Deal on Your Car Rental

While booking through an aggregator guarantees competitive pricing, savvy travelers can maximize their savings and avoid hidden surprises by following a few simple best practices:

1. Read the Rental Terms Carefully

Always review the specific supplier's terms regarding fuel policies (e.g., full-to-full), cross-border travel rules, young/senior driver surcharges, and deductible amounts. Knowing these details upfront ensures zero surprises at the counter.

2. Book in Advance

Particularly during peak holiday seasons or summer tourist rushes, vehicle availability drops and prices rise. Securing your reservation early locks in lower daily rates.

3. Understand Insurance Options

Economy Bookings offers supplemental insurance coverage at checkout to reduce or eliminate your collision damage waiver (CDW) deductible. Check your credit card benefits or personal auto insurance policy beforehand to see what coverage you already possess.
