Core Services Offered by the Way Super App

Way serves as a unified marketplace designed to address every stage of vehicle maintenance and travel logistics. Below is a deep dive into the core verticals available on the platform:

1. Airport Parking Reservations (U.S. & Canada Coverage)

Airport parking fees can quickly inflate travel expenses if not booked in advance. Way offers comprehensive airport parking coverage across all major hub airports in the United States and Canada. Users can browse off-site parking facilities, compare daily rates, view security features (such as 24/7 CCTV surveillance and gated access), and reserve guaranteed spots. Most partner lots include complimentary, direct airport shuttle services with live shuttle tracking capabilities inside the app, ensuring a stress-free transition from lot to terminal.

2. Hourly & Monthly City Parking (U.S. Only)

Navigating downtown parking in metropolitan areas like New York City, Los Angeles, Chicago, or San Francisco often leads to high garage fees or ticket risks. Way provides real-time hourly parking reservations right from your phone or vehicle display (via Apple CarPlay integration). Drivers can reserve garage spaces near major sports venues, concert halls, and business districts in advance. For regular commuters, Way offers discounted monthly parking plans that secure fixed dedicated spots at a fraction of standard drive-up rates.

3. Car Wash Subscriptions & Vouchers

Keeping a vehicle clean is vital for preserving exterior paint and resale value. Way has established a vast network of over 40,000 car wash partners nationwide, with its strongest service density situated across California, Texas, and Florida. Through the app, drivers can purchase discounted single-wash vouchers or subscribe to flexible unlimited monthly car wash plans. App users can pause, modify, or transfer their car wash memberships between partner locations without long-term contracts or cancellation penalties.

4. Auto Insurance Comparison & Policy Matching

Auto insurance premiums represent one of the largest ongoing expenses for car owners. Way simplifies policy shopping by aggregating quotes from hundreds of top-tier insurance carriers covering all 50 U.S. states. Drivers can enter basic vehicle details, analyze side-by-side coverage options, compare deductibles, and finalize insurance coverage directly within the platform—often yielding substantial annual savings.

5. Additional Car Care Services: Gas Cashback, EV Charging, & Mileage Tracking

Beyond parking and washing, Way provides an array of utility tools designed for modern drivers. The app features an automatic IRS-compliant GPS mileage tracker ideal for gig drivers, freelancers, and business expense tracking. Users can also discover nearby EV charging stations, earn cashback on fuel purchases at national gas station brands, and access 24/7 roadside assistance through Way+ membership perks.

Geographic Service Coverage & Regional Availability

While Way continues to expand its partner footprint globally, specific service categories are optimized for key geographic regions:

  • Airport Parking: Broad coverage across both the United States and Canada, spanning international hubs and regional airports.
  • City & Hourly Parking: Available exclusively in major metropolitan cities and urban centers across the United States.
  • Car Wash Network: Nationwide U.S. presence, with heavy partner concentration and premium service availability in California, Texas, and Florida.
  • Auto Insurance & Refinancing: Fully operational across all 50 U.S. states through licensed insurance carrier partnerships.
