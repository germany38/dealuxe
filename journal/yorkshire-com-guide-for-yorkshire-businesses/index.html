Yorkshire.com: A Practical Guide for Yorkshire Businesses

Visit Yorkshire.com →Shop for Yorkshire.com →

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

Yorkshire is one of the most recognisable regions in Britain, and a fair number of local businesses are keen to reach more visitors and customers from outside the county. Yorkshire.com is the online home of Welcome to Yorkshire, a marketing platform that promotes the region and supports the businesses working within it. Here is a straightforward look at what it is, who it might suit, and what to weigh up before you decide.

What Welcome to Yorkshire does

Welcome to Yorkshire is focused on one clear aim: showing the region to the wider world and helping the businesses that make it appealing. Its work sits at the intersection of tourism promotion and business support, so it is less like a general directory and more like a dedicated marketing channel for Yorkshire-based organisations.

Because the platform is built around the region as a whole, its value depends on how closely your business is tied to Yorkshire and whether your audience is likely to be visitors, locals, or both. It is worth reading the current descriptions on the site rather than relying on a summary, as the details of what membership includes are set out there.

Who it might suit

Membership is aimed at businesses that want to be part of the regional story. Organisations that rely on visitors, such as attractions, accommodation providers, places to eat and local experiences, will naturally look at this kind of platform first. Businesses that serve the wider Yorkshire economy may also find the region-wide focus useful.

If you are a sole trader or a very small operator, it is sensible to ask yourself whether you have the time to keep a listing or profile up to date. A marketing platform only works if it is actively maintained, so a realistic view of your capacity matters as much as the audience figures on paper. Visit the Yorkshire.com site to see the membership categories and judge which one fits your size and goals.

What to check before you join

Membership is a paid arrangement, so treat it like any other business expense. Before signing up, compare the cost with what you currently spend on marketing, such as local advertising, print, social media promotion or trade listings. Think about whether the platform reaches the people you actually want to attract.

  • Read the current membership terms, including how long the agreement runs and how renewals work.
  • Confirm what is included at your chosen level and what would cost extra.
  • Check whether your business type and location meet the eligibility criteria.
  • Ask yourself how you will measure value, for example enquiries, bookings or website visits.

Making the most of the platform

Once you are a member, the investment only pays off if you use it. Keep your details accurate, add fresh photos or updates when your offer changes, and link to the platform from your own website and social channels where appropriate. Review your results every few months and be prepared to change your approach if the figures do not justify the cost.

Remember that no marketing platform can guarantee bookings or sales. Treat it as one tool among several, and build a plan that does not depend on a single channel.

Final thoughts

Yorkshire.com offers a region-focused route into marketing for businesses that want to be associated with Yorkshire. It will suit some organisations well and others less so, depending on audience, budget and how much time you can give it. Take a look at the official site, read the details carefully, and compare it with your other options before you commit.
