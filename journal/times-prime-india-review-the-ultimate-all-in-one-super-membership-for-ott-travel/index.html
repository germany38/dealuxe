What Does Times Prime Offer? Key Membership Categories

Times Prime categorizes its benefits into distinct lifestyle verticals, ensuring that every member derives maximum everyday value regardless of their individual preferences or professional background:

1. Entertainment & OTT Streaming

Entertainment forms one of the core pillars of the Times Prime ecosystem. Members gain seamless bundled access to leading video and audio streaming platforms, including Sony LIV, ZEE5, Disney+ Hotstar, Spotify Premium, and Sun NXT. Whether you enjoy live sports broadcasts, international movies, regional blockbusters, or ad-free music, Times Prime provides comprehensive entertainment coverage without requiring separate subscription fees.

2. Premium News, Finance & Business Intelligence

For working professionals, investors, and corporate executives, staying informed with reliable market insights is critical. Times Prime offers complimentary access to premier financial and news publications, including The Economic Times Prime (ET Prime), Moneycontrol Pro, TOI+, and Cricbuzz Plus. These subscriptions deliver ad-free articles, deep market analysis, equity research reports, and exclusive editorial commentary.

3. Travel, Mobility & Commute Perks

Planning vacations or daily commutes becomes considerably more affordable with Times Prime. Members enjoy flat flight and hotel booking discounts on platforms like Flipkart Travel, Yatra, and Paytm Bus, alongside ride-hailing discounts on Uber Premier, Rental, and Intercity trips. In addition, partnerships with airport lounge networks and car service brands ensure stress-free travel itineraries.

4. Dining, Food Delivery & Quick Service Restaurants

Food lovers can leverage exclusive dining privileges across premier restaurants and food delivery apps. Members receive instant bill discounts, buy-one-get-one (BOGO) offers, and complimentary food vouchers with partners such as EazyDiner Prime, Pizza Hut, McDonald's (McDelivery), and Swiggy.

5. Healthcare, Fitness & Personal Wellness

Prioritizing personal health is effortless with integrated wellness benefits. Times Prime includes free or discounted health checkup packages, online doctor consultations, pharmacy discounts, and gym passes via partners like Apollo 24/7, MediBuddy, Netmeds, Anytime Fitness, and Vetic Pet Care.
