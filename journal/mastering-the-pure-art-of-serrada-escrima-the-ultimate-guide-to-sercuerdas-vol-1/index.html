The Lineage: Why Serrada Escrima Stands Apart

To understand the power of SerCuerdas, you must understand its roots. Grandmaster Angel Cabales introduced Serrada Escrima to the United States in 1962. Characterized by its close-range ("serrada" meaning closed or to close) fighting style, economic movements, and devastating efficiency, it strips away unnecessary flash to focus entirely on ending confrontations swiftly.

Master JC Cabiero trained directly under Grandmaster Cabales, absorbing the subtle nuances, timing, footwork, and structural mechanics that make Serrada Escrima uniquely lethal. Through the SerCuerdas instructional series, this master-level knowledge is broken down into structured, digestible, on-demand video volumes designed for dedicated martial artists, law enforcement officers, and self-defense enthusiasts.

How to Scale Your Training with SerCuerdas: A 4-Phase Roadmap

Purchasing a video series is only the first step; true martial prowess comes from deliberate, structured practice. If you want to scale your skills effectively using this on-demand program, follow this evergreen 4-phase training blueprint:

Phase 1: Shadow Drills and Footwork Mastery (Months 1–2)

Start with Volume 1. Do not rush to pick up heavy sparring equipment immediately. Spend your first sixty days perfecting the triangle footwork, 80/20 positioning, and the Cincotero and DeCadena flows in front of a mirror. Muscle memory in your legs and core dictates your speed.

Phase 2: Strike Integration and Solo Partnering (Months 3–4)

Integrate Strikes 1 through 12 until they feel as natural as breathing. Use a heavy bag, striking pads, or air-boxing to build snap and structural integrity into your weapon handling. Focus heavily on the transition between offense and the basic blocking sets.

Phase 3: Lock-and-Block Application (Months 5–6)

Move into Volume 2 and 3 concepts. Practice the lock-and-block mechanics slowly with a trusted training partner. Serrada Escrima relies heavily on geometry rather than raw muscle strength; understanding how to trap an opponent's arm while maintaining structural control is the hallmark of an advanced escrimador.

Phase 4: Live Resistance and Continuous Refinement (Month 6 and Beyond)

Engage in controlled sparring drills outlined in the program. Test your timing, distance management, and defensive reflexes under pressure. Revisit the video modules periodically to correct technical drift and sharpen your form.
