PICCOLOAD Book: Custom Coloring Books From Your Own Photos

Coloring books usually come off a shelf with someone else's artwork inside. PICCOLOAD takes a different approach: it creates custom coloring books from photos you already have, such as family portraits, pets, holiday snapshots, or favorite places. If you're looking for a gift that feels personal rather than generic, PICCOLOAD is one US brand worth a closer look.

How the personalized process works

The idea is simple. You choose the product, and after purchase you upload the images you want included. PICCOLOAD then transforms those photos into illustrated line-art pages, which are printed as a finished book. Because the pages are built from your own pictures, the result is less of a standard product and more of a keepsake that reflects a specific person, pet, or moment.

Since the photos are uploaded after checkout, it helps to have your images ready before you order. Gather the pictures you want to use, decide on the order you'd like them to appear, and make sure you have the files saved on the device you'll be using. That way the upload step goes smoothly and you aren't scrambling for old photos while the order is in progress.

Who this type of book tends to suit

Personalized coloring books can work well for a range of occasions. Grandparents may enjoy a book filled with pictures of grandchildren, while a pet owner might want a collection of their animal's best moments. Couples, friends, and families also use custom books as anniversary, birthday, or holiday gifts. The format is especially appealing to people who enjoy slow, hands-on activities, since coloring can be relaxing for both kids and adults.

Think about the recipient before you choose. A gift for a young child may call for simple, bold line art, while an adult who enjoys detailed work might appreciate more intricate scenes. Consider too whether the book is meant to be used, displayed, or shared, since that can shape how many photos you include and how you arrange them.

What to check before you order

Photo quality matters most when you're working from personal images. Clear, well-lit pictures with a defined subject usually translate better into line art than blurry shots or photos with cluttered backgrounds. Before uploading, review each image and remove any you wouldn't want printed. If you're unsure how a particular photo will look, choose a few test options and see how they compare.

It's also smart to read the brand's own information carefully. Look for details on product options, the photo requirements, how uploads are handled, production and delivery timing, and the policy for order changes or problems. These details can vary by product and season, so checking the current information on the brand's website is more reliable than relying on secondhand summaries. If a gift has a deadline, plan extra time for review and shipping.

How to compare personalized coloring books

When you compare custom coloring books, it helps to look past the cover design. Consider how the artwork is created, how many pages you can include, and how clearly the brand explains its process from upload to finished book. Print quality, paper feel, and the overall binding all affect how the book holds up after repeated use. You may also want to ask whether the book suits your specific goal, such as a memory book, a children's activity, or a gift with a personal story.

Price is only one part of the decision. A slightly higher cost may make sense if the book is a keepsake, while a simpler option might be enough for a casual gift. Compare the product details side by side and choose the option that matches what you actually need.

PICCOLOAD offers a distinctive way to give a coloring book that is built around the people and places in your own photos. If that idea appeals to you, visit the PICCOLOAD website to review the current product options, photo guidelines, and ordering details before you decide. Taking a few minutes to prepare your images and read the information can help you end up with a gift you're happy to give.
