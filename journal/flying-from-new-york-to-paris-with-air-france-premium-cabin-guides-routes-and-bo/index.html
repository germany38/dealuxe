Flying from New York to Paris with Air France: Premium Cabin Guides, Routes, and Booking Tips

Everything you need to know about flying Air France from JFK and EWR to Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), including cabin classes, dining, baggage policies, and transatlantic fares.

Transatlantic Excellence: Departing from New York

For travelers flying out of the New York metropolitan area, Air France provides one of the most comprehensive and luxurious transatlantic flight networks available. Operating multiple daily non-stop flights from both John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) and Newark Liberty International Airport (EWR) to Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG), Air France serves as a premier gateway to Europe, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia.

Whether traveling for international business or planning a European getaway, flying with Air France offers seamless connectivity, state-of-the-art aircraft fleets (including the Boeing 777-300ER and Airbus A350-900), and signature French hospitality from takeoff to landing.

Cabin Experiences tailored to Every Traveler

Air France offers four distinct cabin classes on its New York routes, allowing passengers to customize their journey according to budget and comfort preferences:

  • Economy Class: Features ergonomically designed seating, adjustable headrests, individual touchscreen entertainment systems, complimentary hot meals, and select wine and champagne service included in every fare.
  • Premium Economy: Provides a private cabin with 40% more space compared to Economy, fixed-shell recliner seats, extra legroom, dedicated check-in lanes, priority boarding, and upgraded dining menus.
  • Business Class: Offers fully lie-flat beds with direct aisle access (1-2-1 layout), personal sliding privacy doors on select aircraft, gourmet meals designed by Michelin-starred French chefs, and lounge access at JFK and CDG.
  • La Première (First Class): The pinnacle of luxury travel, featuring private luxury suites, personalized chauffeur transfers, a dedicated check-in lounge, and multi-course haute cuisine.

Smart Booking Strategies for New York Air France Flights

  1. Leverage Flying Blue Miles: Earn and redeem Flying Blue loyalty points across Air France, KLM, and SkyTeam partner airlines on transatlantic routes.
  2. Advance Booking Window: Transatlantic fares generally offer the best value when reserved 6 to 12 weeks prior to departure.
  3. Flexible Dates: Utilizing Air France's interactive calendar during booking allows you to quickly identify lower fare days across peak travel seasons.
