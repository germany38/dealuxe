Lets Buy Books UK: A Practical Guide to Discounted Books

Disclosure: Dealux may earn a commission on purchases made through links on this page, at no extra cost to you.

If your bookshelves are full but your budget is tight, discounted books can be a sensible way to keep reading. Lets Buy Books is a UK online bookstore that sells bestselling collections, children's titles, fiction, non-fiction and gift bundles, with discounts of up to 80% off RRP. It is aimed at value-seeking readers, families and book lovers across the UK.

Who the store suits best

Discount bookshops work well for people who read regularly and would otherwise buy several titles at full price each year. Families buying for children who go through books quickly often find this useful, as do gift shoppers looking for a themed set rather than a single paperback. If you only pick up the odd book, the savings matter less than whether the titles are ones you actually want.

It is worth deciding what you are shopping for before you browse. A clear list of two or three authors, a genre or an age group will help you avoid filling a basket with things you may not read.

Bundles and collections: what to compare

Gift bundles and themed collections can be good value, but only if you would have read most of the books in them. Before you buy, check the full list of titles, the format (hardback, paperback or board book) and the total price. Dividing the price by the number of books gives you a rough cost per title that you can compare with other options.

When you are looking at the collections on the site, it can help to note which items are described as bestsellers and which are general selections, so you know what you are getting.

Children's books and family reading

Children's books are a large part of the range, which makes the store a practical option for parents and carers. For babies and toddlers, look at the age guidance on each listing and consider sturdier formats, such as board books, that handle daily use better. For older children, a themed set can be a simple way to build a home library over time without paying full price for every title.

Always check the recommended age range, as it can vary between publishers and editions. If you are buying a gift, confirm the contents of the bundle carefully so there are no surprises.

Things to check before you order

  • RRP and sale price: RRP means the recommended retail price set by the publisher. Compare the sale price with that figure, and with prices elsewhere, rather than relying on the percentage alone.
  • Delivery costs and timings: Shipping charges can change the value of a discount, especially on a single book. Check them on the website before you pay.
  • Returns and cancellations: Read the returns policy in full, particularly if the purchase is a gift.
  • Edition and condition: Read product descriptions carefully to confirm the edition, format and any stated condition.
  • Stock availability: Popular titles and bundles may sell out, so check that an item is available before you plan around it.

These are general points rather than promises about any particular order, so the details on the brand's own pages should always be your final guide.

Ready to see what is on offer? Visit Lets Buy Books to browse the range, compare prices against the RRP and decide whether the savings suit your reading habits.
