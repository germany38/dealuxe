Understanding Gamma Waves and Brain Health

To appreciate how gamma light therapy works, it helps to look at the brain’s natural electrical rhythms. Our brains operate on various neural oscillation frequencies, ranging from slow delta waves (associated with deep sleep) to fast beta and gamma waves.

Gamma waves are the highest frequency neural oscillations observed in the human brain. They are typically associated with peak concentration, high-level information processing, memory recall, and moments of intense focus or “flow.” As we age or experience chronic stress, maintaining optimal neural synchronization can become more difficult.
