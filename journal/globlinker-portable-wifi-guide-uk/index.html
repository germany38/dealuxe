Globlinker Portable WiFi: No SIM, No Contract, No Roaming Fees

If you dread the roaming charges that arrive after a week abroad, a portable WiFi hotspot could be worth a closer look. Globlinker is a portable device that the brand says works in more than 130 countries without a SIM card, a fixed contract or roaming fees. Here is what to consider before deciding whether it fits your trip or your working life.

How a portable hotspot differs from roaming or a local SIM

A portable WiFi device creates its own wireless network, which your phone, laptop or tablet joins just as it would a home network. Compared with using your regular mobile provider abroad, you avoid unpredictable charges for data used on the move. Compared with buying a local SIM, you skip finding a shop, handling registration paperwork and swapping cards in your phone. Each option suits different trips, so it helps to be clear about how you will really use the connection.

What the brand says about coverage and data

Globlinker states that its service covers over 130 countries, with no SIM, no contract and no roaming fees. It also says customers receive 2GB of data free each month, and that unused data stacks rather than disappearing straight away. Prepaid data is optional, so you can rely on the monthly allowance for light use and top up only if you need more. The brand's wording refers to this allowance as lasting "for life", so read the terms on the official site to confirm exactly what that covers and how stacking works.

Who it tends to suit

  • Travellers who use maps, messaging and social media while on the move
  • Remote workers who want a backup connection between hotels, cafés or co-working spaces
  • Small groups who would like to share one connection across several devices
  • People who visit many countries in a year and do not want to buy a new SIM each time

If you mainly stay in one place with reliable home-style broadband, or you regularly download large files or edit video, compare the allowance carefully before relying on it.

Things to check before you order

Start with the countries on your route. The 130+ figure comes from the brand, so confirm your destinations are included and check for any restrictions. Next, estimate your realistic daily use, including maps, calls made through apps and photo uploads, to see whether the free 2GB each month is enough or whether prepaid top-ups make sense. Finally, look at device details, delivery timescales and returns information on the brand's website, as these are not covered here and can change.

For frequent trips, or occasional ones where roaming charges would otherwise add up, a portable hotspot is a sensible option to compare. Visit the Globlinker website to check the current country list, data terms and any prepaid options before you decide.
