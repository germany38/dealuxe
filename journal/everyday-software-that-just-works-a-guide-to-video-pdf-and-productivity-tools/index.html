Three examples of everyday workflows

Family video. Gather clips in one folder, cut the best moments, add a title and music, and export a version for your phone. Paperwork. Choosing the right licence

Think about how often you will use the software and on how many devices. Short-term licences can suit occasional projects, while longer ones may suit regular use. Always read what happens at renewal and whether updates are included.

Keeping your files safe

Before using any tool that changes or recovers files, make a backup. Keep your operating system updated, use reputable security software and avoid unofficial downloads. A little care protects both your files and your device.
