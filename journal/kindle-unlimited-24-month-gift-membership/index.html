What Is Kindle Unlimited?

Kindle Unlimited is a subscription service that gives members access to a very large collection of ebooks, audiobooks and magazines. Members borrow titles instead of buying each one. They can read on a Kindle device or in the free Kindle app on a phone or tablet, and many titles can also be listened to.

A gift membership prepays a set period for someone else. The 24-month plan covers two full years.

What the Recipient Gets

  • Read as much as they want. There is no per-title cost while the membership is active.
  • Millions of stories. The catalog spans many genres, including mystery, romance, fantasy, science fiction, self-help and more.
  • Ebooks, audiobooks and magazines. Everything is included in one subscription.
  • Recommendations that improve. The more they read, the better the suggestions become.
  • Read or listen. Many titles let you switch formats anytime, and your place is kept as you move between them.

The selection of titles can change over time, and not every book is included. Check the offer page for current details.

Why a 24-Month Gift Makes Sense

It lasts for two years. That is a long time to be thought of every time they open a book.

It removes the cost barrier. Heavy readers can spend a lot on books. This gift lets them read freely.

It encourages new interests. With no extra cost per title, it is easy to try an unfamiliar genre or author.

It is easy to send. It is delivered digitally, with nothing to wrap or ship.

It suits all ages. From young readers to retirees, there is something for everyone.

Who Will Love This Gift

  • Voracious readers who finish several books a month.
  • Students who want to read widely outside the classroom.
  • Travelers who like to carry a whole library on one device.
  • Parents who want to encourage reading at home.
  • Retirees who enjoy having plenty to read, with adjustable text sizes.
  • Commuters who switch between reading and listening.

How It Compares to Shorter Gift Plans

Kindle Unlimited gift memberships come in several lengths, including shorter plans such as six and twelve months. A shorter plan is a good way to introduce someone to the service. The 24-month plan is better for a person you already know loves to read, or for a major occasion like a graduation or milestone birthday.
